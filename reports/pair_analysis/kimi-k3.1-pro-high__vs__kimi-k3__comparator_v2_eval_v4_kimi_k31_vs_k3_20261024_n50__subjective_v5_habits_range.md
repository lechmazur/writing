# Kimi K3.1 Pro (high) vs. Kimi K3: comparative writing analysis

Focus model: `kimi-k3.1-pro-high`. Comparison model: `kimi-k3`. Direct-comparison scope: `comparator_v2_eval_v4_kimi_k31_vs_k3_20261024_n50`.

## In one paragraph

Kimi K3.1 Pro (high) usually turns a prompt’s unusual ingredients into problems characters must work through, while Kimi K3 more often lets an image or a meaningful gesture change the world. Faced with damp fireworks and low cloud, K3.1 redirects the equipment into a ground-level signal; K3 offers a luminous spectacle whose connection to the obstacle is less clear. [C007] That difference makes K3.1 the more dependable storyteller here: actions more often have visible reasons and consequences, and solving the immediate problem need not settle the human conflict. Its awakened citizens can still accuse their rescuer of choosing for them. [C011] K3’s weaker stories substitute beautiful declarations for earned change, but its best restraint offers something different from practical resolution. Where K3.1 pours a dead husband’s unused tea into a living fern, K3 lets the second cup cool, preserving attachment without pretending he has returned. The contrast is not simply clear writing against poetic writing, but between directing loss toward renewed activity and sometimes allowing it to remain. [C058]

## Quantitative context

Across 50 matched prompts, the focus model recorded 35 wins, 8 ties, and 7 losses at the ±0.5 tie threshold. Its mean signed margin was +0.878 ± 0.350 (95% normal half-width).

Observed evaluator judgments: 300.

Mean lengths were 682.6 and 691.5 words; the correlation between length difference and margin was +0.097.

## Comparative portrait

**Kimi K3.1 Pro (high) is the stronger overall storyteller in this comparison, principally because its discoveries more reliably change what characters can do.** Required objects, methods, and attributes become connected parts of a problem: a recurring pencil smudge reveals temporal distortion; disappearing ink and a map-reading drone become a rescue tactic. Kimi K3 more often makes recognition itself effective: naming an entity, finding the right metaphor, or performing a resonant gesture changes the world. This contrast recurs in readings by all three analyst models; it is not unanimous preference for every K3.1 story. [C001][C012][C041]

The distinction is **not realism versus imagination**. Both write impossible worlds. K3.1 generally makes their resistance more inspectable: something prevents success, a character changes an action, and a consequence follows. K3 can instead create a world in which language, memory, and material objects belong to one expressive system. Its building-language story is a well-supported success of that alternative, not a failed procedural story. [C029][C028]

They also share substantial defaults: corrective definitions, inherited or family-authenticated objects, overlapping names, and restoration qualified by loss. K3.1’s greater control does not eliminate formula; it often redirects the shared fable toward practical repair and communal maintenance. K3’s sharper individual sentences do not consistently sustain equivalent dramatic development. [C004][C013][C054][C078]

The most secure finding is therefore **a recurring advantage in causal and dramatic integration**. Wider adaptability is supported more cautiously; superiority in moral maturity, conceptual originality, or reader trust is not uniform. [C015][C016][C030][C069]

## Does either model seem smarter?

“Seems smarter” here describes an impression produced by particular writing decisions, not general intelligence. The evidence separates several capacities that fluent prose can otherwise blur:

- **Causal and structural foresight:** K3.1 more consistently establishes capacities before exploiting them. In 0273, the drone already reads disappearing-ink routes before those capacities are combined under attack. K3’s emotionally powerful word “unbelonging” arrives with a less independently established rule for releasing captives. The difference is preparation and reuse, not technical vocabulary. [C012]
- **Psychological inference:** K3.1 often specifies why someone acts or remains silent. In 0052, an ignored animal warning, a postponed repair, exhaustion, and fear of a spouse’s dismissal interact. Its refusal to treat a magistrate’s tears as an apology likewise distinguishes feeling from accountability. K3 nevertheless offers equally discriminating local observations, including the apprentice whose apparently defective knot records fear of authority. [C057][C002][C035]
- **Conceptual compression:** K3 can accomplish more in a sentence. “Blessing made her useful; pariah made her convenient when equations failed” explains a social arrangement rather than merely sounding profound. Its exposure of self-pardon as “a finer cage” also changes the action the protagonist must take. [C003][C018]
- **Relevant-detail selection:** A swollen fuse in K3.1 changes the available plan; rust and a seized wheel obstruct actual labor. But selection need not mean mechanical usefulness: K3’s cooling second cup preserves a precise relation to absence. Neither procedural nouns nor dense imagery automatically establish good judgment. [C029][C080][C058]
- **Constraint synthesis:** K3.1 more reliably makes several requirements govern one decision, notably the calligraphic distinction between command and care in 0384. K3 can reverse that advantage: its affectionate skeptic actually randomizes lamps and distinguishes longing from evidence. [C001][C047][C069]
- **Restraint and reader trust:** K3 sometimes leaves the decisive gesture unexplained—Cassian does not thank his rescuer, or thunder becomes “only weather.” K3.1 can trust other characters to revise the protagonist’s understanding, but both frequently append an authorized interpretation after the scene has already conveyed one. [C003][C034][C035][C031]

The qualified conclusion is that **K3.1 more consistently conveys connected, consequential thought at story scale**. K3 sometimes supplies the sharper conceptual distinction or more selective omission. Explicit explanation can inflate the former impression; aphoristic assurance can inflate the latter. [C012][C003][C069]

## Narrative reasoning and aesthetic judgment

K3.1’s best plotting distinguishes an interpretation from the obstacle it explains. Recognizing what the flooded opera-house evidence means does not stop the pumps destroying it. Understanding the fireworks problem does not repair a damp fuse. These stories require an intervention after insight, giving discovery an external test. K3’s corresponding passages often make emotional correspondence and effective action nearly coincide. That can be coherent lyric causality, but it is less satisfying when a numbered code or technical investigation has promised recoverable connections. [C029]

Cost also matters. In 0107, K3.1 makes opening an intake threaten the control room while leaving it closed threatens the valley; abandoning the room sacrifices something. K3’s equivalent departure is more gracefully phrased than materially costly. Conversely, K3’s copyist hides a requiem inside ordinary marching tunes, making the character’s craft itself the method of resistance. Procedural clarity and thematic integration are related achievements, not synonyms. [C063][C064]

K3.1’s social reasoning is strongest when cooperation does not require instant moral conversion. The officer in 0139 learns that obedience endangers him; the net-mending master in 0387 receives a correction from an apprentice rather than merely bestowing recognition. Yet the receipt accounting in the former is inconsistent, and the wage settlement in the latter encounters little institutional resistance. Legible motives do not certify every supporting mechanism. [C057][C035]

K3’s aesthetic risk pays off when metaphor organizes the whole fictional contract. Its building grammar and non-extractive archaeology do more than ornament a solution: they determine what rescue or preservation should mean. The corresponding danger is that a narrator can confer efficacy on whatever formulation sounds most resonant. K3.1’s opposite danger is explaining a convenient invention so thoroughly that articulation resembles proof. [C028][C016][C041][C069]

## Recurring and repetitive writing habits

**Counting rule:** recurrence below is deduplicated by writer and story. Local fractions refer only to explicitly checked sets. Cross-readings corroborate an occurrence; they do not create another one. Unless a denominator is stated, the examples establish recurrence but frequency across all 50 stories was not measured.

### Shared sentence and story machinery

**Recognition followed by corrective definition.** Both repeatedly tell the reader that an event is not one thing but another. In read_01’s five checked stories, both use concept-redefinition near the close in 5/5; recognition beats built around “understood” occur in 4/5 for each, with different exceptions. K3 writes, “Mira understood then that true north was not a place”; K3.1 writes, “Ilex understood then that exorcism would not illuminate it; argument might.” Later examples repeat the operation around home, fairness, and a sister’s gift. This is an established shared signature, not a reliable model identifier. [C004][C059]

The construction can make a real distinction—fearlessness versus willingness to orient oneself, wealth versus responsibility—or settle interpretation prematurely. Its repetition makes revelation sound preformatted even when the ethical content differs. A recurring sentence form is not proof that every underlying story is interchangeable. [C014][C031]

**Family provenance as authorization.** Required tools repeatedly acquire unrequired relatives. K3 links a porthole bolt and a periscope mirror to brothers in 0193 and 0351. K3.1 supplies a father’s technical lesson in 0193 and a brother’s drone in 0273. The tool becomes evidence of belonging as well as a mechanism. This is flexible when the relationship changes function: K3.1’s father supplies guidance, whereas its brother supplies both useful engineering and a restrictive worldview. [C013]

**Overlapping names and bodily environments.** Mara, Mira, Venn, and related names recur on both sides. Buildings also repeatedly breathe: both opera houses hold their breath, and both fortresses inhale. The latter image performs different work—K3.1’s fortress inhales after an answering signal, K3’s as the spectacle begins. Shared imagery therefore complicates attribution without proving identical narrative function. [C004][C051][C032]

### Kimi K3’s repeatable habits

**1. Cognitive verbs applied to matter.** K3 repeatedly makes objects and abstractions learn, practice, remember, or confess. Across different prompts, a bruise is “learning light,” a flame resembles a secret “practicing to be sung,” and a patch resembles an eye that “had practiced dying.” Later examples tighten into a particularly recognizable stencil: “scissors learning mercy” and “cutlery learning music.” The effect is immediate animation and lyrical surprise; repeated across stories, the surprise becomes predictable grammar. [C008][C053]

**2. Physical action translated into language.** K3’s “soft grammar of a shore,” “grammar of failure,” and architectural declensions make the world readable as text. This differs from generic personification: linguistic categories can become the laws of the setting. It is especially productive in 0382, where a threshold’s memory belongs to the premise; elsewhere it can make very different trades sound like versions of one narrator’s profession. [C077][C020][C028]

**3. Figurative explanations attached with “because.”** This form was checked in all five K3 stories in read_09 and found in 5/5: 0092, 0268, 0281, 0284, and 0399. “Fear prefers a small noun to a large door” explains a public euphemism; “the thaw had not decided whether to speak” explains a silver hush. The first can compress social perception, while the second primarily sustains atmosphere. The habit is more specific than simply “writes aphorisms.” [C048]

**4. Cadence maintained through terminal additions.** In 0069, endings such as “at last completely” and a dangling “there” prolong already complete thoughts. Separate passages in 0273 and 0287 contain locally equal-length sentence pairs—twenty words and sixteen words respectively—with additions such as “again slowly” and “downstairs … below.” The later 0268 repeats the problem with “lineage tonight” and “stay finally.” These are separate story occurrences, not multiple critics multiplying one case. Intentional incantation remains a plausible reading; the narrower criticism is that rhythm sometimes requires words the perception does not. [C005][C014][C050]

Paragraph rhythm reinforces that effect. In read_09’s five checked K3 stories, none uses quotation-marked dialogue: speech is reported, not absent. Together with end-stopped sentence units, this can turn confrontations into narrated tableaux. It is not a universal inability to write speech or vary pacing; the clipped suspension in 0259 is an effective use of short units. [C050][C039]

**5. A small recurring bodily and threshold vocabulary.** K3 keeps defiance or laughter under the tongue in 0157 and 0188, then a stitch against it in 0395. A coin and a seed are each “warm in her cheek” in 0201 and 0336. Different structures become “no wider than confession” or “no wider than grief.” These are verified repetitions of wording or construction, not phrases reconstructed from critical paraphrase. They give abstractions bodily intimacy but can make unrelated protagonists feel equipped from the same image inventory. [C009][C042]

**6. Symbolic authorization followed by a moral or legend.** Naming stillness in 0012 and naming the hated pause in 0273 exemplify a recurring solution-by-language. Future pilgrims and travelers close 0012 and 0069; other endings crystallize the event into a definition. The important counterexamples are substantial: 0384 has a timed, consequential intervention, and 0225 finishes by releasing weather from symbolism rather than adding another maxim. [C001][C012][C005][C034]

### Kimi K3.1 Pro’s repeatable habits

**1. Reused personal and geographical coordinates.** In read_04, Bellwether appears in 3/5 checked stories—0029, 0225, and 0382; 0027 and 0155 are checked nonmatches. Mara and lost relatives recur alongside it. Cross-reading of 0273, 0351, 0382, 0384, and 0395 confirms the concentration on missing or estranged brothers and inherited maternal craft; it does not add five new occurrences. Later, “Her brother Toma had vanished during … leaving” and “Tomo had vanished during … leaving” make the resemblance unusually concrete. [C019][C024][C072]

The backstory often produces a sequence of inherited clue, diagnosis, confrontation, renunciation of a private wish, and renewed public usefulness. But it is not an invariant ending: 0112 relinquishes retrieval while 0281 permits reunion. The repeated setup is narrower than the range of final choices. [C049][C072]

**2. Diagnosis followed by an improvised apparatus.** Salvaged domestic objects repeatedly become solutions: shaving mirror, kite wire, and music-box axle in 0347; coffeepot wire and balloon parts in 0389; mirrors, trays, and chalk in 0343. This was checked as 3/5 in read_10, with 0084 and 0134 nonmatches. It gives competence tactile substance, but can become a reusable gadget-rescue sequence rather than a bespoke structure. [C054]

**3. External clocks and procedural acceleration.** Dawn orders, demolition deadlines, and impending purges organize 0351, 0382, 0384, and 0395. The related 0273 uses an ambush rather than a scheduled deadline, so it should not be counted as another dawn plot. Such pressure supplies momentum, but repeated official clocks can make pacing feel imposed from outside the character’s problem. [C026]

**4. Public repair as the preferred destination.** An open chart, a practical broadcast, and recognition converted into wages or workspace recur across distinct stories. These are meaningful consequences, yet collective maintenance can become as predictable a destination as K3’s private ritual. The evidence supports a recurring preference, not the claim that every K3.1 crisis becomes a public-works project. [C055][C035][C078]

**5. Qualified endings—and renewed beginnings.** K3.1 repeatedly inventories what has not been repaired before allowing modest continuation: “no triumphant chord,” no full peace, no permanent shelter. Later readings newly document a lexical variant: “begin” or “beginning” closes four of read_12’s five checked stories, 0066, 0096, 0107, and 0128; 0146 renews without that word. This is not simply superior restraint. Anti-triumph and renewal can themselves become reassuring formulas. [C019][C005][C065]

**6. Explanatory prose is not immune to rhythmic standardization.** K3.1 uses a staccato single-sentence paragraph layout in four of read_02’s five stories. Its advantage is not the absence of lineation but more frequent variation in sentence movement and interpersonal response. Similar anesthesia openings in 0201 and 0261 are partly prompt-required; the repeated name Mara is the more diagnostic self-chosen feature. [C010][C014][C043]

Across this two-version comparison, shared corrective syntax, names, and family-mediated objects persist. K3.1 weakens the observed tendency for cadence to override sense, but develops its own recurrent apparatus-and-repair structures. Nothing here establishes when those habits arose or that model succession necessarily produces aesthetic improvement. [C004][C013][C050][C054]

## Range and adaptability

**The demonstrated range is principally within lyrical speculative fiction.** K3.1 generally changes scene mechanics and relationships more reliably; K3 makes some larger formal departures and varies its ethical stance more than categorical descriptions of “one register” acknowledge. Neither settings alone nor the aggregate result establishes range. [C015][C021][C038][C062]

### Kimi K3.1 Pro: stronger changes in dramatic operation

K3.1 moves between irreverent bureaucratic comedy and patient craft-centered siege writing. In 0157, asking whether treason is deductible makes defiance social and conversational; in 0188, sorting powder and preparing slow-burning trains makes pacing attentive to labor. In 0389, a mechanical bird biting a thumb and Sera’s complaint about a tedious miracle supply a rougher, character-located comic register. These are changes in joke construction, tempo, and interaction, not merely genre labels. [C010][C056]

Its social scale also changes. The acoustic descent in 0109 coordinates children, cooks, and soldiers; 0201 concentrates on bodily intervals under torture; 0387 organizes a comparatively mundane investigation of uncredited maintenance. An older pruner and an ascetic oracle provide alternatives to the recurrent Mara figure, although the name and family templates remain conspicuous. [C045][C038][C043]

There are genuine structural contrasts. The urgent rescue in 0112 requires immediate intervention; the ceremony in 0128 depends on whether other people voluntarily participate. The orchard story in 0155 leaves the artwork failed and its communal effects uncertain, unlike the externally answered signal in 0188. Literal domestic haunting in 0052 gives way to intersecting maps, property arrangements, and a mundane explanation for a package in 0317. These change what counts as an answer and what suspense consists of. [C073][C033][C061]

Not every attempted shift succeeds equally. The skull-root horror of 0211 is a demonstrable departure, but existing judging disputes whether it sustains the requested quiet disquiet or over-escalates it. Nor is K3.1 confined to plain procedure: 0351’s succession of borrowed lives supplies unstable, lyrical embodiment. Its principal limit is a comparatively stable third-person, interpretive frame, often returning varied adventures to grief, understanding, and useful work. [C006][C015][C049]

### Kimi K3: broader formal attempts, less reliable register change

K3’s first-person narrator in 0382 is a genuine viewpoint departure. Present-tense declaratives in 0211, chess-notation fragments in 0225, the comma-stripped incantation in 0029, and the forensic present tense of 0128 change sentence architecture and stance. Execution is uneven: 0029’s experiment partly obscures relations and drifts in tense, whereas 0259’s “His smile kept beginning. / His hand never touched bronze” uses short units to enact suspended time. [C021][C006][C067][C039]

Its emotional and comic range is also real. Administrative hopelessness in 0188, the authoritarian mustache in 0092, and mortuary “upgrades” in 0139 are different forms of dry social comedy. The same writer can finish 0225 with austerity rather than another animated landscape. These counterexamples rule out a universal claim of solemn monotony, although the elevated, explanatory narrator remains recognizable. [C034][C074][C062]

K3 varies conflicts and social organization more than some packet verdicts suggest. The bodily rescue magic of 0091 differs from the local workmanship problem in 0387. Private mourning in 0052 differs from neighbors occupying a threatened civic space in 0317. The copyist’s gradual teaching in 0066 is not another instantaneous rescue or decoding climax. [C038][C062][C073]

Its ethical range is particularly consequential: 0193 compels an exploiter’s restitution while allowing the survivor not to answer his apologies; 0351 limits intervention and preserves an abandoned dream in context. Those are different conceptions of repair, even under a similar lyrical voice. [C016]

The limiting pattern remains that many requested modes return to animist, aphoristic narration. Siege, surveillance, surgery, and horticulture can all become solitary encounters with speaking matter. Yet “often returns” is better supported than “cannot adapt”: controlled testing, civic mobilization, formal experiment, and conceptual restraint are all demonstrated exceptions. Sustained realist narration, multiple viewpoints, and radically different long-form structures remain untested, not failed. [C010][C044][C047][C062][C069]

**Consistency:** the advantage survives extension, but becomes less dominant. Qualitatively, early evidence of setup-and-payoff control is followed by later evidence involving priced choices, motivated cooperation, and specific recognition. K3.1’s better-supported floor is clearer action and more individuated pressure—not an absence of convenient lore, over-explanation, or sentimentality. [C001][C012][C063][C057][C035]

**Failure-prone cases:** K3 is more vulnerable when its regular cadence acquires filler, an investigation promises decoding it does not deliver, or figurative transformations substitute for the missing causal link. K3.1’s failures instead include overloaded machinery, tidy institutional repair, sentimental authority-conversion, and procedural explanations that do not withstand their own accounting. [C014][C050][C029][C063][C065][C057]

**Unusually strong performances:** K3.1’s layered diagnosis in 0012 and socially consequential recognition in 0387 exemplify its principal strengths at high execution. K3 has genuine whole-story successes, not merely a compensating “sentence ceiling”: 0382’s building language supports its world and method, while 0066’s craft-based resistance and 0112’s restrained conception of discovery received clear existing-judging preferences. These successes do not establish that K3 has the higher overall ceiling. [C001][C002][C035][C028][C064][C069]

Later readings newly document the “because” form, metallic “learning” stencil, and “begin” codas. Cross-readings confirm existing stories rather than enlarging these occurrence sets. Some individual K3.1 mechanisms are more explicitly explained, but the overall advantage is not an advantage in average verbosity.

## What each model still does better

**K3.1’s strongest advantage is making insight consequential.** It more reliably connects requirements to choices, makes another person’s response matter, and gives a changed understanding an observable destination. Its best dialogue lets people resist or correct the protagonist; its best communal endings alter access, work, or participation rather than merely announcing harmony. [C001][C035][C057][C070]

**K3’s clearest retained strengths are compression, conceptual inversion, and selective noncompletion.** It can expose a social mechanism in a balanced line, make the supposed goal ethically suspect, or preserve an absence without turning it immediately into a task. These strengths sometimes govern an entire story: kind lies become the means of rescue in 0382; non-extraction becomes an archaeological ethic in 0351. [C003][C018][C016][C058]

Neither strength should be made exclusive. K3.1 also writes evocative embodiment and preserves uncertainty; K3 also builds working traps, controlled tests, and civic action. The comparative result favors K3.1 because its advantages recur more reliably at story scale, not because K3 lacks narrative thought or because procedural prose is inherently preferable to fabulism. [C015][C061][C047][C062]

## Findings beyond the existing judging

**The central contrast is corroborated, not newly discovered.** Existing judging already distinguishes K3.1’s integrated action from K3’s image-led movement, and already notices padding, explicit morals, and familiar family scaffolding. The synthesis adds value by connecting those observations across distinct stories rather than relabeling them as novel discoveries. [C001][C014][C049][C063]

Three cross-story findings deserve additional emphasis:

1. **Interpretive authority is distributed differently.** K3.1’s apprentice can contradict the master’s charitable explanation; K3’s narrator can reveal an absent apprentice’s fear. Both may reach humane conclusions, but one makes knowledge socially revisable. Similarly, the Second Bow can become a participatory occasion or an organizer’s benevolent scheme that briefly overrides its recipient’s reluctance. This is a supported synthesis about who may revise the story’s account, not a frequency claim. [C035][C070]

2. **Repair allocates labor as well as emotion.** A minority reading of 0193 asks whether K3.1’s shared burden conscripts the survivor into managing her exploiter. K3’s refusal of compulsory response avoids that demand while imposing its own troubling coercive restitution. The second-cup contrast adds a different dimension: continuing attachment versus redirected care. “More humane” is too coarse to capture these choices. [C016][C058]

3. **Repetition exists below the level of plot labels.** The metallic “learning” metaphors, attached figurative “because” clauses, matching disappearance syntax, and renewal vocabulary are concrete portfolio findings. They explain why different settings can remain recognizably similar even when a single-story judgment rightly praises the prose. They are newly documented comparisons, not evidence that these habits arose only in the later model or later evaluation. [C053][C048][C072][C065]

The shared inventions also deserve restraint in interpretation. Near-matching openings, names, and the Second Bow narrow claims of individual distinctiveness. They establish textual convergence; any account of training causes or common internal templates would be speculative. [C051][C067][C070]

## Disagreements and limitations

**Causal advantage is more panel-supported than global aesthetic or ethical superiority.** Some readings describe K3’s recurrence as wholesale register collapse; others document successful satire, public action, controlled experimentation, and deliberate nonintervention. The synthesis retains the recurring default without adopting those categorical judgments. [C010][C045][C034][C047][C062][C016]

Several disagreements materially change interpretation:

- K3’s 0382 is a supported aesthetic reversal: its grammar is structural, not merely decorative. K3.1’s clearer municipal plot does not automatically satisfy the brief better. [C018][C028]
- In 0225, two readings find K3’s scapegoating and public exposure more socially particular, although existing judging prefers K3.1’s integration overall. That is corroboration of one story-level interpretation, not two occurrences of a habit. [C018][C030]
- In 0201, the readings praise K3.1’s measurable intervals of relief, while existing judging questions whether the sympathetic-guard treatment softens oppression. Its distant-lock release also retreats into symbolic convenience. K3’s refusal to let mercy cancel an outstanding debt is a substantive alternative, not just mathematical decoration. [C076][C042]
- K3’s incantatory regularity may be chosen form rather than accidental loss of control. The verified criticism concerns redundant additions and blurred relations; intention is not established. [C005][C014][C021]

The prompts strongly encourage unusual tools, memory, abstraction, and restorative transformation. Those shared demands should not be counted as unsolicited habits. Added kinship, repeated names, particular syntax, and reused imagery are more diagnostic. Similar anesthesia openings are partly required, and widowhood in 0052 cannot count as invented bereavement. [C013][C043][C060]

The quantitative evidence comprises 300 observed judgments over 50 pairs, not 300 independent stories. Supplied mean evaluator disagreement is 0.685 and mean AB/BA order sensitivity is 1.196; these counsel caution about precise ranking and individual close cases. They do not erase the aggregate advantage, but neither the scores nor the qualitative readings warrant claims about general intelligence or untested literary modes.

## Representative case studies

**0188 — A constraint that changes the technique.**  
K3.1’s damp fuse and low-weather instructions lead to ground-level lights, filtered smoke, and an answering signal. K3 translates the crowd into grief, fear, and desire, then stages an overhead display. The distinction is unusually visible, but K3 explicitly limits its achievement—“Sero did not save anyone”—so the alternative is also a different conception of what wonder can accomplish. Supplied margin: **+1.333**. [C007][C029][C032][C033]

**0387 — Appreciation becomes specific.**  
K3’s doubled knot compresses workplace fear into one technical fault. K3.1 extends recognition through correction: the apprentice rejects the master’s assumption, the investigation identifies unpaid work, and recognition produces a wage and a bench. That ending is tidy, but its distinctions between presumed authorship, actual labor, acclaim, and compensation explain a particularly strong K3.1 performance. Supplied margin: **+2.550**. [C035][C038]

**0382 — Fabulism succeeds on its own terms.**  
K3’s first-person narrator inhabits a world where “Cellar is the past tense of candle,” and agreed lies can become a means of rescue. The linguistic conceit organizes action as well as atmosphere. K3.1’s more conventional movement beyond a comforting story is clearer, but clarity is not the decisive artistic value here. Supplied margin: **−2.417**, favoring K3. [C021][C028][C018]

**0066 — A quieter method can outperform a busier plot.**  
K3 hides the forbidden requiem within marching tunes and develops resistance through teaching and rehearsal. K3.1 supplies concrete camouflage and external pressure, but also the sentimental shortcut of a captain lowering his weapon at his dead wife’s voice. Existing judging’s preference for K3 shows that more overt stakes and mechanisms need not improve tonal or thematic fit. Supplied margin: **−2.283**. [C064][C073][C063][C065]

**0281 — Skepticism is an action, not a label.**  
K3 distinguishes the possibility of longing from the possibility of a real signal through randomization and controlled observation. K3.1 provides a cleaner explanatory mechanism but assigns reliability to bodily signals more readily. K3’s later magical escape weakens any claim of uniformly superior rigor; nevertheless, the experimental characterization is a serious reversal of the usual comparison. Supplied margin: **−0.250**, within the tie band. [C047][C069][C046]

**0052 — Two cups, two purposes for recovery.**  
K3.1 reconstructs an accident through interacting mechanical and relational failures, then pours the unused tea into a fern. K3 leaves the second cup cooling “like a truthful lamp.” The stronger causal diagnosis belongs to K3.1; the retained ritual offers a defensible alternative to directing grief toward renewed usefulness. Neither gesture inherently proves greater maturity. Supplied margin: **+1.917**. [C057][C058]

## Cited claim ledger

- **C001 — Elements as working machinery versus elements as labels:** Kimi K3.1 Pro (high) converts required elements into mechanisms whose rules generate the resolution. The plumb line physically retrieves the hidden assumption, the temporal stretch explains the navigation error, and the thorn's stroke direction determines which clause survives. Kimi K3 more often names the element in near-brief language and then resolves the plot through metaphor or renaming. The device is announced rather than shown working.
  - Story 0221, `kimi-k3.1-pro-high`:
    > If time stretched differently around each buoy, their routes were not wrong; they were maps drawn in mismatched durations.
    The required "temporally stretched" attribute becomes the causal key to the plot problem, set up earlier by the smudge that recurs whenever the pencil crosses the relay symbols.
  - Story 0384, `kimi-k3.1-pro-high`:
    > Complete it downward, and one will ruled both.  Draw it sideways, and care would endure while command came loose.

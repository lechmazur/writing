# Muse Spark 1.2 (high) vs. muse-spark-1.1-high: comparative writing analysis

Focus model: `muse-spark-1.2-high`. Comparison model: `muse-spark-1.1-high`. Direct-comparison scope: `comparator_v2_eval_v2_v3_predecessor_depth_n50`.

## In one paragraph

Given the same short-fiction prompts, Muse Spark 1.1 High more often makes the required ingredients change one another: an object acquires a new purpose, an odd trait creates pressure, or a minor figure becomes the person who completes the protagonist’s work. Muse Spark 1.2 High more often builds a smoother, more centered parable in which a capable protagonist follows a visible procedure and arrives at an explained meaning. At its best, that procedure is excellent—a gauge has a stated success condition, the result is later checked, and the emotional resolution rests on evidence rather than atmosphere [C047]. But Muse Spark 1.1 High more consistently selects details that carry physical, social, and thematic weight at once [C020], and it more often lets knowledge travel through workers, children, apprentices, or other easily overlooked people [C083]. Its endings also more often preserve damage or unfinished work instead of allowing revelation to cure everything [C022]. The overall conclusion is lopsided: Muse Spark 1.1 High usually produces the stronger story. Muse Spark 1.2 High remains competitive when a prompt rewards a clean device, deadline, or proof scene, but that narrower strength does not offset the older model’s broader command of consequence and integration.

## Quantitative context

Across 50 matched prompts, the focus model recorded 13 wins, 3 ties, and 34 losses at the ±0.5 tie threshold. Its mean signed margin was -0.730 ± 0.474 (95% normal half-width).

Mean lengths were 692.5 and 651.5 words; the correlation between length difference and margin was +0.197.

## Comparative portrait

**Muse Spark 1.1 High is more often the stronger short-fiction writer here.** Its characteristic advantage is not prettier prose but stronger *constraint metabolism*: required objects, traits, timeframes, and methods more often become causes, costs, reinterpretations, or relationships among other elements. Muse Spark 1.2 High more often builds a smooth recognition arc or a visibly staged procedure, with meaning stabilized through explanation, successful demonstration, or a usable artifact. These are tendencies rather than exclusive methods: both can plot mechanically, reason socially, and over-explain their themes. [C020][C021][C035][C065]

The original cohort established two linked contrasts. First, Muse Spark 1.1 High more often distributed agency: interruptions could revise the work, supporting figures could complete the protagonist’s design, and institutions or absent third parties remained part of the moral accounting. Second, Muse Spark 1.2 High more often centered an already capable specialist and supplied an orderly setup, test, and payoff. [C001][C003][C010] The extension **replicates the first contrast strongly** through handoffs, overlooked messengers, socially situated clues, and secondary minds with their own errors. [C048][C052][C070][C083] It also replicates Muse Spark 1.2 High’s procedural strength through explicit success criteria, one-shot constraints, and virtues made mechanically necessary. [C047][C069][C084]

The quantitative result is lopsided but not uniform. Across 50 matched stories, Muse Spark 1.2 High recorded 13 wins, 3 ties, and 34 losses at the ±0.5 threshold. Its mean margin was −0.730, with a 95% normal interval of roughly −1.204 to −0.256; the median was more negative at −1.150. The central conclusion therefore survives aggregation.

The extension **replicates the direction but materially weakens the magnitude**. The original cohort was 3/1/16 with a mean margin of −1.048; the extension was 10/2/18 with a mean of −0.518. The original interval excluded zero, while the extension interval did not. Thus the extension supports “Muse Spark 1.1 High usually wins,” but not an unchanged effect size or certainty. Muse Spark 1.2 High was also about 41 words longer on average, while word-count difference correlated only +0.197 with margin. Its greater payload did not explain away the result and should not be treated as deeper reasoning.

## Does either model seem smarter?

Only as a **writing-generated reader impression**, Muse Spark 1.1 High more often seems the more perceptive and substantively controlled model. This is not evidence about general intelligence. The impression comes from selecting details that do several jobs at once, locating culpability at a painful decision point, modelling other people’s mistaken reasoning, and letting bodily or institutional evidence complicate the protagonist’s preferred account. [C004][C025][C048][C073]

Its strongest recurring forms are:

- **Conceptual compression:** an object’s purpose is revised in a way that changes action, rather than merely furnishing a metaphor. [C040][C082]
- **Psychological and social inference:** secondary characters have incentives, routes, misunderstandings, or authority that affect outcomes. [C046][C048][C083]
- **Relevant-detail selection:** family artifacts, material damage, or ordinary work practices connect backstory, inference, and theme without remaining decorative. [C025][C070]
- **Constraint synthesis:** awkward assigned elements are more often absorbed into grammar, mechanism, or causal structure. [C014][C020][C067]
- **Reader trust and restraint:** required meanings are less often glossed immediately, and endings more often preserve doubt, incomplete repair, or the assigned tonal tension. [C021][C022][C064][C065]

Muse Spark 1.2 High nevertheless creates a genuine impression of **procedural and causal intelligence**. Its best stories state a testable condition, stage the test, report the result, or make patience and restraint indispensable to a device’s operation. It can also turn a polished formulation into substantive reasoning when the formulation reorganizes an earlier moral action rather than merely summarizing it. [C003][C047][C075][C084]

The extension **replicates the overall advantage for Muse Spark 1.1 High**, especially in conceptual selection, embodied inference, and socially distributed knowledge. [C070][C073][C080][C083] The material change is that it gives Muse Spark 1.2 High a broader causal case than the original portrait alone suggested: accidents alter plans, antagonists re-enter the causal loop, and explicit constraints sometimes generate rather than decorate the climax. [C076][C077][C078] That narrows the verdict from “one model reasons and the other explains” to a more defensible distinction: Muse Spark 1.1 High more consistently selects the consequential relation, while Muse Spark 1.2 High more intermittently constructs a clean test or sequence.

This conclusion is not based on verbosity, technical nouns, polished antitheses, or confident aphorisms. Both writers share those surfaces, and both can use them to simulate depth. [C006][C012][C051] The extension’s higher evaluator disagreement and substantially greater order sensitivity also counsel against treating close individual comparisons as stable measurements, even though the aggregate direction remains clear.

## Narrative reasoning and aesthetic judgment

**Causal and structural foresight.** Muse Spark 1.2 High is particularly good when a premise can be made into a device, deadline, gauge, or physical threshold. Its strongest procedures contain an input, a success criterion, and a later reading; its extension stories also more often admit disruption into the active scene and force adaptation. [C047][C069][C076][C084] Muse Spark 1.1 High’s causal strength is different: it more often creates a pressure chain in which a trait, social route, or failed apparatus necessitates the next action, and it frequently transfers the payoff to another agent. [C052][C054][C058][C083] Neither advantage is absolute. Muse Spark 1.1 High’s mechanisms can be pseudo-technical, while Muse Spark 1.2 High’s neat procedures can be implausible or providentially completed. [C006][C028]

**Psychological inference.** Muse Spark 1.1 High has the clearer recurring edge. It can specify the preventable act behind remorse, show a child or subordinate reasoning wrongly in a characteristic way, or let the body contradict conscious memory. [C004][C048][C073] Muse Spark 1.2 High has important local reversals: it can articulate shame accurately, ground grief in money and service relationships, or recast mercy through a concrete coerced trade. [C018][C030][C075] These are real strengths, but they recur less broadly.

**Conceptual compression and relevant-detail selection.** Muse Spark 1.1 High more often makes one detail simultaneously physical, ethical, and thematic: damaged material diagnoses a damaged practice; an object’s function is revised; a timeframe becomes a hierarchy of cultural attention. [C025][C040][C071][C082] Muse Spark 1.2 High’s best counterexamples are bolder categorical transformations or compact inversions that genuinely alter the plot. [C024][C053] The original and extension cohorts agree on Muse Spark 1.1 High’s broader consistency here, while the extension adds more evidence that Muse Spark 1.2 High can achieve comparable compression at isolated peaks.

**Constraint synthesis.** The original cohort favored Muse Spark 1.1 High for turning assigned traits and phrases into causal problems rather than labels. [C007][C013][C014] That finding recurs in the extension through formal tense work, functional reinterpretation, and unusual readings sustained by later physical details. [C045][C067][C080] A material extension correction is that Muse Spark 1.2 High sometimes metabolizes technical or bodily constraints more literally and sequentially than Muse Spark 1.1 High. [C078][C084] The gap is therefore recurring, not categorical.

**Restraint, aesthetic judgment, and reader trust.** Muse Spark 1.1 High more often preserves damage, doubt, or incomplete repair after the climax, rather than allowing revelation to cure the governing tension. [C022][C064] Muse Spark 1.2 High more often explains the symbol on arrival or delivers confirmation, which improves immediate legibility but can over-certify meaning. [C021][C065] The extension complicates this: in some packets Muse Spark 1.2 High closes on concrete image while Muse Spark 1.1 High states the thesis, and withheld versus delivered resolution changes sides by prompt. [C061][C079] The stable finding is not that one model always “shows” and the other always “tells,” but that Muse Spark 1.1 High more often leaves interpretive or ethical residue across the full evidence.

## Range, recurring habits, and floor versus ceiling

The two models are close stylistic relatives. Both favor gentle speculative parables, caretaker or specialist protagonists, numinous artifacts, restorative values, a small recurring name pool, corrective “not A but B” sentences, and terminal maxims. [C012][C039][C051][C087] Those shared habits limit any claim that the comparison reveals radically different literary sensibilities.

Muse Spark 1.1 High nevertheless shows more recurring variation in casting, embodiment, register, and occasionally form. The original cohort found wider variation in age, gender, viewpoint, and bodily limitation; the extension adds humor, bureaucratic dryness, first-person compression, and one clear case in which sentence fragmentation performs the story’s theory. [C016][C049][C074] The formal claim remains limited: the fragmentation finding is a **one-story observation**, not evidence that Muse Spark 1.1 High is generally experimental. [C085]

The floor-versus-ceiling evidence is unstable. One original reading identified Muse Spark 1.2 High’s silence plot as the packet’s highest formal peak but emphasized that it was a peak, not an average. [C009] Extension readings then split: some found Muse Spark 1.1 High the higher-variance writer, capable of both exceptional compression and conspicuous summary; another found Muse Spark 1.2 High’s scene-level floor steadier while assigning the highest conceptual peak to Muse Spark 1.1 High. [C062][C081] **The extension therefore does not replicate a special Muse Spark 1.2 High ceiling.** Nor does it support inventing a compensating ceiling for the weaker model merely to balance the overall verdict.

What does replicate is a difference in failure mode. Muse Spark 1.1 High’s low points tend toward compressed outline, convenient conversion, or mechanisms with the texture rather than the auditability of causality. Muse Spark 1.2 High’s low points tend toward inert labels, benign systems, explanatory restatement, or polished procedures whose social context remains thin. [C008][C028][C058][C079] Prompt shape matters: device-and-deadline premises often reward Muse Spark 1.2 High’s constructive method, while paradoxical, institutional, or semantically ambiguous requirements often reward Muse Spark 1.1 High’s reinterpretive method. [C029][C045][C047]

The numerical extension change is consistent with this mixed range portrait. Muse Spark 1.2 High improved from winning 15% of original pairs to one-third of extension pairs, while still losing a majority. At the same time, extension disagreement and order sensitivity rose markedly. The stable conclusion is a higher overall hit rate for Muse Spark 1.1 High, not a rigid ceiling/floor law.

## What each model still does better

**Muse Spark 1.1 High does better, more often:**

- It makes prompt elements revise one another instead of merely coexisting. [C020][C080]
- It distributes knowledge and agency through apprentices, workers, children, institutions, and absent third parties. [C052][C083]
- It models social and psychological consequences through specific culpable acts, mistaken beliefs, bodily contradictions, and ongoing duties. [C004][C010][C048][C073]
- It more often preserves a distinction between repair and cure, or between changed understanding and restored innocence. [C022][C064]
- It is more willing to reinterpret an object’s purpose or an ambiguous phrase and then let that reinterpretation govern the plot. [C040][C045][C082]

Those strengths recur in both cohorts and align with the lopsided overall result.

**Muse Spark 1.2 High does better in narrower but genuine areas:**

- It constructs visible proof scenes, gauges, one-shot constraints, and bodily thresholds with unusually legible setup and payoff. [C003][C047][C069][C084]
- It can maintain scene-level pressure by admitting accidents or antagonists into the present action rather than displacing conflict into backstory or aftermath. This is principally an extension finding. [C076][C077]
- It occasionally supplies the sharper operational procedure, the bolder cross-sensory transformation, or the more concrete account of institutional power. [C018][C023][C024]
- It sometimes grounds a fable in ordinary payment, insurance, service, or adversarial incentives more effectively than Muse Spark 1.1 High. [C030][C059]

These strengths qualify the verdict without balancing it. They explain several convincing Muse Spark 1.2 High wins, but they do not offset Muse Spark 1.1 High’s broader advantage in integration, selection, and consequence.

## Findings beyond the existing judging

Several packet-level patterns add explanatory value beyond ordinary pairwise quality judgments.

**Object economy.** Muse Spark 1.2 High more often ends with a significant object retained as a completed private anchor; Muse Spark 1.1 High more often has the object surrendered, paired, taught, or passed onward. This predicts the difference between consolation and continuing obligation without claiming either object pattern is inherently superior. [C011]

**Politics of coordination.** Both models repeatedly imagine control through clocks, rhythms, records, and licensed forms of synchronization. Muse Spark 1.1 High more often imagines freedom as semantic release or desynchronization; Muse Spark 1.2 High more often adds a second measure or interface so plural systems can coordinate. [C043] This shared concern recurs across prompts strongly enough to be more than a single-story motif, though the exact political remedies can reverse.

**Where knowledge lives.** Muse Spark 1.2 High often locates truth in a reading, record, or instrument; Muse Spark 1.1 High more often locates it in transmission, marginal routes, or another person’s ability to use the method. [C050][C068] The extension strengthens this distinction through socially distributed messages and clues embedded in family or occupational histories. [C070][C083]

**Technical tenderness.** A new extension finding is Muse Spark 1.1 High’s habit of joining calibration to care: tools, maintenance, bodily monitoring, and archival work become ways of attending rather than conquering. [C072][C073] This helps explain why its technical details often feel more psychologically relevant even when the speculative physics is no more realistic.

**Form as constraint work.** The extension also reveals that Muse Spark 1.1 High sometimes treats awkward phrasing as a grammatical or formal design problem, not merely a compliance burden. Future-perfect prophecy and locally functional fragmentation are the clearest cases. [C067][C074] The latter remains a one-story observation and should not be generalized into a broad formal-range claim.

## Disagreements and limitations

The panel did not support a simple realism hierarchy. In the adaptive-reuse story, both models used engineering vocabulary while eliding permissions, labor, structural assessment, and maintenance; elsewhere each could combine convincing local procedure with arbitrary magical causation. [C006][C028] Technical nouns are therefore surface-cue-sensitive evidence.

Social perception also changes sides by prompt. Muse Spark 1.1 High more often gives institutions budgets, euphemisms, labor routes, and accountable agents. [C046][C083] But Muse Spark 1.2 High produced the original cohort’s most concrete account of ownership, curricular erasure, and incompatible calendars, and another packet gave it the stronger model of officials responding to incentives rather than beauty. [C023][C059] The first advantage is recurring; the latter are well-supported minority readings, with the social-accounting claim explicitly concentrated in one story.

Likewise, explicitness is not automatically artistic failure. Muse Spark 1.2 High’s explanatory closure can be a legitimate fable choice, while Muse Spark 1.1 High’s ambiguity can conceal underdevelopment. The models also trade places on whether they state the lesson or trust the final image. [C055][C057][C061] A preference for open endings should not be smuggled in as a universal measure of maturity.

Several conspicuous findings are narrow. Muse Spark 1.2 High’s bold cross-sensory fireworks construction is a one-story originality result. [C024] Muse Spark 1.1 High’s integrated fragmentation and laboratory-animal recategorization are also one-story observations, even though both are strongly supported within those stories. [C085][C086] These cases establish capability, not frequency.

Surface cues remain hazardous. Aphoristic balance, technical diction, dialogue quantity, short declarative formatting, lyric polish, and visible fragmentation can all manufacture an impression of control or depth. Both models use the same corrective-apposition machinery and final-maxim habit, so those cues largely cancel. [C012][C051][C087] The weak relationship between added length and comparative margin reinforces the same caution.

Finally, the extension is less stable at the individual-comparison level than the original: evaluator disagreement rose from 0.643 to 0.953, and order sensitivity from 1.083 to 1.852. Combined with strongly prescriptive prompts and the models’ shared skeleton, that means fine rankings of individual strengths should remain provisional. [C039] The aggregate advantage for Muse Spark 1.1 High is much firmer than any claim that either model owns realism, maturity, formal originality, or a unique ceiling.

## Representative case studies

- **Original cohort — story 0054:** Muse Spark 1.2 High establishes measurable hydraulic interventions and then uses a storm as a proof event. Muse Spark 1.1 High supplies more civic process but resolves it through rapid social assent. The case cleanly shows Muse Spark 1.2 High’s setup-test-payoff advantage while also demonstrating why engineering vocabulary should not be confused with institutional realism. [C003][C006][C036]

- **Original cohort — story 0373:** The two models solve the same required climax through different reasoning. Muse Spark 1.2 High makes silence starve a voice-harvesting machine; Muse Spark 1.1 High makes the decisive sound the antagonist’s trust in his own lie breaking. This is the strongest evidence that causal-structural and social-psychological intelligence are separable rather than globally rankable. [C035]

- **Original cohort — story 0184:** Muse Spark 1.2 High gives historical absence identifiable victims, beneficiaries, curricular suppression, and competing calendars. Muse Spark 1.1 High instead closes through an inventive archival reclassification that joins folklore, clocks, and maintenance. This is a genuine reversal in social specificity without overturning Muse Spark 1.1 High’s broader constraint-synthesis advantage. [C020][C023]

- **Extension — story 0006:** Muse Spark 1.2 High turns the foreign coin into a gauge with an explicit pass/fail condition and later reports the reading. Muse Spark 1.1 High’s stronger local contribution is modelling a child’s mistaken but motivated reasoning. The pair compactly displays procedural verification versus theory-of-mind granularity. [C047][C048]

- **Extension — story 0143:** Muse Spark 1.2 High admits a spill into the live scene and improvises a consequential substitute; Muse Spark 1.1 High turns the space age into the reason local observational work has become culturally obsolete. The former is stronger event causality, the latter stronger historical and conceptual selection. [C071][C076][C080]

- **Extension — story 0349:** Muse Spark 1.1 High’s fragments enact the library’s theory of unfinished language, while Muse Spark 1.2 High explains integrated fragmentation in continuous prose. This is the clearest formal win for Muse Spark 1.1 High, but it remains one matched story rather than a portfolio-wide rule. [C074][C085]

## Cited claim ledger

- **C020 — Constraint metabolism:** Writer muse-spark-1.1-high more consistently turns required elements into causes or reinterpretations of one another rather than placing them side by side. This is a kind of semantic systems intelligence: an assigned character trait can become a theory of architecture, while an assigned archive concept can generate an unexpected but functional classification.
  - Story 0178, `muse-spark-1.1-high`:
    > Nabu understood then that the palace had been built over an observatory, and that his own frugality was an echo of someone older who reused foundations.
    Frugality is not merely characterized through economical habits. It becomes a historical pattern of reuse connecting Nabu, the unknown predecessor, and the palace itself.
  - Story 0184, `muse-spark-1.1-high`:
    > She cataloged the fables the next morning not under folklore or diplomacy but under systems maintenance, a new classification she invented for stories that keep things true.
    The classification completes the story's clock, braid, archive, and translation logic in one administrative action. It is surprising but causally earned.
- **C021 — Interpretive overpressure:** Writer Muse Spark 1.2 (high) more often tells the reader what a symbol means immediately after introducing it. The explanations are coherent, but they reduce the number of live interpretations and can make discovery feel pre-certified. Writer muse-spark-1.1-high is not consistently ambiguous, yet more often lets a changed scale, image, or material remainder carry part of the conclusion.
  - Story 0178, `muse-spark-1.2-high`:
    > Their work was an encrypted devotion, a love for the heavens disguised as mathematics so it could survive conquest and neglect.
    The sentence translates the priestesses' work directly into the required concept and supplies its historical purpose, leaving little interpretive work for the later magical event.
  - Story 0184, `muse-spark-1.2-high`:
    > Elena understood the concept at once: not lies, but shaped silences that preserved what official histories erased.
    The colon delivers an authoritative definition before the rope, treaty, and missing names have fully dramatized the idea.
  - Story 0178, `muse-spark-1.1-high`:
    > Then the stars slipped back, the tile cooled, and he was again a small man on a roof between regimes, rich only in paper.
    The altered scale and modest material inventory qualify the revelation without denying it. The image does interpretive work that Muse Spark 1.2 (high) more often assigns to abstract explanation.
- **C035 — Same required climax, different engine:** In 0373 both writers had to stage "the sound of trust breaking." Muse Spark 1.2 (high) solves it mechanically: the machine can only record voices, so unbroken silence starves it, and the theme falls out of the device's rules. muse-spark-1.1-high solves it psychologically: the sound that finally registers is a liar's faith in his own lie snapping. These are two distinct intelligences, causal-structural and social-psychological, operating at the identical beat; neither is clearly superior.
  - Story 0373, `muse-spark-1.2-high`:
    > The recorder whirred and clicked, starved, its needle shivering over empty tape because trust, when it breaks, does so in a voice, not in silence.
    The thwart follows logically from constraints established earlier, so the aphorism is earned by plot mechanics rather than asserted over them.
  - Story 0373, `muse-spark-1.1-high`:
    > because now she heard it, the faint, clean click as Cobb's chain snapped and his trust in his own lie broke
    The broken thing is relocated inside the antagonist's self-deception, a subtler psychological target than a shouted betrayal, and the smallness of the sound subverts the expected climax.
- **C065 — Glossing required elements on arrival versus metabolizing them into action:** Muse Spark 1.2 (high) typically appends a defining clause to each required phrase at first use, telling the reader what it means; muse-spark-1.1-high more often embeds the same phrase in a gesture or decision and lets context define it. The difference is one of timing and trust, present in nearly every pairing.
  - Story 0038, `muse-spark-1.2-high`:
    > filled with a strange light that felt like a mysterious familiarity, as if the place remembered her name before she spoke it
    The tone phrase arrives pre-explained; the appended simile performs the interpretive work the scene itself should do, leaving the reader nothing to assemble.
  - Story 0038, `muse-spark-1.1-high`:
    > She moved like someone remembering steps to a kitchen song, and that mysterious familiarity kept my hands steady.
    The identical phrase is embedded in an action with consequences (steady hands during the escape); its meaning is demonstrated by use rather than delivered by gloss.
- **C001 — Interruption becomes co-authorship:** Writer muse-spark-1.1-high dramatizes social perception by allowing an inconvenient participant to modify the artwork and its meaning. Writer Muse Spark 1.2 (high)’s socially perceptive artist instead designs the decisive silence herself, with the grotto and stored voices responding to her intervention. muse-spark-1.1-high’s scene thus distributes authorship rather than merely describing sensitivity.
  - Story 0279, `muse-spark-1.1-high`:
    > Mira did not scold him she simply brushed a curve outward and made his interruption part of the mandala as a jagged wedge.
    > Harlan stared and then knelt and with his fingertip he smoothed the wedge until it became a bridge between two isolated rings.
    Mira neither defeats nor interprets Harlan. She incorporates his social mistake, after which he performs the crucial revision himself. Her perceptiveness is visible in responsive behavior.
  - Story 0279, `muse-spark-1.2-high`:
    > Instead she decided to interject a single, deliberate silence into the mandala, leaving one quadrant blank and untouched.  She pressed the whispering gallery tile flake into the center of that empty space like a seed that needed quiet to germinate.
    This is an elegant formal idea, but Mara remains the sole designer and actor. The alleged shared attention is produced by her already-correct symbolic solution rather than negotiated with another present person.
- **C003 — Designed proof scenes:** Writer Muse Spark 1.2 (high) more consistently builds visible tests for a premise. Technical preparation in 0054 is followed by a storm that proves the redirected flows; 0361 projects the pendant’s function into a later succession scene. This is structural intelligence: promises are converted into staged outcomes rather than left as assertions.
  - Story 0054, `muse-spark-1.2-high`:
    > If she can regrade the concourse by centimeters, clear three culverts, and angle the old boarding ramps as catchment chutes, the building will not fight the flood but invite it.
    The proposed intervention contains measurable operations and establishes what a later test must demonstrate.
  - Story 0054, `muse-spark-1.2-high`:
    > But the redirected flows hold, guiding water through the ramps and into the concourse where it pools clear and calm instead of surging.
    The storm pays off the earlier hydraulic plan in action. Even if the engineering scale is doubtful, the narrative causality is readily traceable.
  - Story 0361, `muse-spark-1.2-high`:
    > At the first garden, now dense and sprawling, a young courier waited, jittery and wide-eyed, clutching an empty case.  The young courier asked Mara how she had ever managed to coax wonder from the routine without losing hope.
    The apprentice reproduces Mara’s original condition, preparing a deliberate handoff that turns the absent mentor theme into lineage.
- **C010 — Stakes that spill onto absent third parties:** Muse Spark 1.2 (high)'s dilemmas are private and resolve as feeling-management for one person; muse-spark-1.1-high's dilemmas implicate people who never appear onstage, and resolution assigns duties to whoever decides. This consequence-tracking, not confidence of tone, is what makes muse-spark-1.1-high read as more morally mature in most of the packet.
  - Story 0306, `muse-spark-1.1-high`:
    > If you flood the quarter, you must also stay to carry water to the people you displaced.
    Either branch of the decision attaches an ongoing obligation to the decider; the ethics extend past the scene into aftermath.
  - Story 0306, `muse-spark-1.2-high`:
    > the best decisions leave a clean ache, not a clean slate
    Muse Spark 1.2 (high)'s wisdom concerns how a choice should feel afterward; the boat decision affects only the decider, and the resolution is a soothing ritual object.
- **C048 — Modelling other minds, including their errors:** muse-spark-1.1-high's secondary characters have their own faulty epistemologies, rendered in their own idiom; Muse Spark 1.2 (high)'s secondary characters mostly voice doubt or insight that services the protagonist's arc. The difference is theory-of-mind granularity, not characterization polish.
  - Story 0006, `muse-spark-1.1-high`:
    > The boy believed the black sand held warmth that would teach her skin again, because a teacher said volcanic glass remembers fire.
    A child's magical reasoning is traced to a specific, plausible source and to a specific goal; the mistake is characterizing rather than cute.
  - Story 0123, `muse-spark-1.1-high`:
    > July 12, Wind north-northwest, Mrs Alvarez says clouds look like sheep, Papa says stay inside the line.
    A child's document renders an entire social world — neighbors, parental fear, the fence — in one line of borrowed adult speech.
  - Story 0006, `muse-spark-1.2-high`:
    > You still think you have to fix everything he burned, he said.
    Muse Spark 1.2 (high)'s foil speaks the protagonist's psychology aloud rather than exhibiting a mind of his own; the line exists to be answered.
- **C052 — Resolution by handoff versus resolution by the protagonist:** muse-spark-1.1-high repeatedly structures endings so that a second agent completes or transmits the protagonist's work: in 0152 the warning reaches the palace only because kitchen girls whisper and "the captain of the guard, who loved patterns, listened and moved two companies without ever admitting he had taken advice from children"; in 0139 the washed ink pot is handed to the lawyer who files the claim; in 0048 the ferryman validates the new chapter. Muse Spark 1.2 (high) keeps the decisive act with the protagonist: Mara herself "rewove the official galaxy chart to include the hidden pass" (0152), Elias himself plays the chord (0113), Mira herself instructs the travelers (0391). This is a structural theory of how change happens — distributed and institutional in muse-spark-1.1-high, singular and personal in Muse Spark 1.2 (high) — not a stylistic flourish.
  - Story 0391, `muse-spark-1.1-high`:
    > The envoys were annoyed until Joren laid the sheets one by one over the firelight.
    The conversion of the Council — the story's hinge — is performed by the apprentice, not by Mara. muse-spark-1.1-high deliberately transfers climactic agency to the next generation, making transmission itself the resolution.
- **C070 — Clues with relatives:** muse-spark-1.1-high more often gives mandated objects and settings prior lives inside a social network. Details do not merely symbolize the protagonist’s problem; they establish who made something, who preserved evidence, and how private grief entered communal material history. Muse Spark 1.2 (high)’s 0064 is coherent, but its sovereign case and seed arrive chiefly as a prepared answer to Mara’s inward conflict.
  - Story 0064, `muse-spark-1.1-high`:
    > Thomas had helped dye the gold, Eli knew, because their father kept receipts tucked in a bible.
    The receipt simultaneously connects Thomas, the father, the tapestry’s color code, and Eli’s present inference. Its causal workload creates structural and social intelligence rather than mere atmospheric specificity.
- **C083 — Knowledge travels through overlooked people:** In the coup stories, muse-spark-1.1-high models intelligence as socially distributed. Mara designs a message suited to servants' access, children's plausible deniability, and the captain's private attraction to patterns. Muse Spark 1.2 (high) gives Mara a perceptive ally, but the decisive transmission is a narrower trusted handoff.

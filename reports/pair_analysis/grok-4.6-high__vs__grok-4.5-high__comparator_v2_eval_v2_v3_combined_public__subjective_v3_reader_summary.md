# Grok 4.6 (high) vs. grok-4.5-high: comparative writing analysis

Focus model: `grok-4.6-high`. Comparison model: `grok-4.5-high`. Direct-comparison scope: `comparator_v2_eval_v2_v3_combined_public`.

## In one paragraph

Given the same short-fiction prompts, Grok 4.5 tends to recite its assignment: required phrases return verbatim as sentence subjects, the narrator explains what each symbol means, and closing lines sometimes certify that all elements "wove together seamlessly" [C025]. Grok 4.6 more often puts the same requirements to work: a mandated stretch of waiting becomes the story's theme, sediment layers narrate a city's collapse, a projector lens is used to project [C029]. Endings diverge just as sharply — Grok 4.5 declares the problem solved, in stability, harmony, and peace, while Grok 4.6 lets success arrive without canceling the contradiction, leaving merged timelines in permanent arrival and departure [C002]. The result feels like reading a completer beside a withholder. The pattern is consistent: Grok 4.6 produced the stronger story in fifteen of twenty shared prompts while writing slightly less. The qualification that matters is Grok 4.5's unevenness — it wrote the single most satisfying scene, a compassion ritual in which a broker realizes the anonymous benefactor being described is himself, alongside the boldest conceit and the clumsiest sentences [C036]. Choosing the best individual story gives Grok 4.5 a real case; choosing the typical one does not.

## Quantitative context

Across 20 matched prompts, the focus model recorded 15 wins, 0 ties, and 5 losses at the ±0.5 tie threshold. Its mean signed margin was +1.473 ± 1.068 (95% normal half-width).

Mean lengths were 692.7 and 752.5 words; the correlation between length difference and margin was +0.221.

## Comparative portrait

Both models work the same assignment machine: short stories assembled from lists of required elements, delivered in solemn, expository prose, with required phrases re-inserted verbatim at least sometimes and benign resolutions throughout [C008][C023]. They also share deep limitations, panel-supported across all three packet analysts: landscapes ratify protagonists instead of resisting them [C019], machines and magical systems pronounce emotional absolution [C024], and rebuilt civilizations emerge without dissent, bargaining, or politics [C005].

Within that shared idiom, the recurring, panel-supported difference — observed independently in all six packets — is what each model does after a required element appears. Grok 4.5 recites: required phrases return verbatim as sentence subjects at roughly double Grok 4.6's rate, stack into closing sentences, and get certified complete ("Then the action unfolded…"; "Thus all elements of the day wove together seamlessly") [C006][C008][C012][C013][C025]. Grok 4.6 metabolizes: elements become procedures, evidence, social relations, and occasionally structure itself — a mandated "while waiting" becomes the story's theme [C006][C020][C029]. Endings diverge just as consistently: Grok 4.5 stabilizes, totalizes, and self-grades; Grok 4.6 defers the payoff past the final line and leaves residue [C002][C007][C015][C028][C033]. A low-confidence tendency adds a difference in the shape of desire: Grok 4.6's protagonists end as solitary custodians, Grok 4.5's absorbed into community [C035]. Finally, the quality distributions differ: Grok 4.5 has the higher ceiling and lower floor; Grok 4.6 is nearly flat [C036]. The existing judging agrees with the recurring pattern: across 20 matched pairs, Grok 4.6 won 15, lost 5, with none tied; mean margin +1.473, median +2.458.

## Does either model seem smarter?

Treated strictly as a reader impression manifested through writing choices on these prompts — not as a measurement of general intelligence — yes: Grok 4.6 seems smarter more often, and the impression decomposes into separable behaviors rather than deriving from fluency.

- **Causal and structural foresight (recurring, panel-supported):** intermediate operations, bounded effects, and verification between intention and success [C020]; hinges that fuse arbitrary constraints [C029]; objects used according to what they are [C031] — though object integration flips per story [C018].
- **Psychological inference (recurring):** courage enacted as conduct before being named [C003]; longing attached to a specific person, event, and interval rather than floating referent-less [C016]. Counterweight: Grok 4.5 sometimes reaches the sharper proposition about grief without dramatizing it [C016] and produced the stronger single image of unlived possibility [C034].
- **Conceptual compression:** abstractions given entailments — spatial mutiny changes adjacency, negative curvature buys narrative room [C021]; grounding for the "cipher of longing" [C016].
- **Relevant-detail selection:** evidentiary limits preserved (partial sediment, destructible evidence) [C001]; object provenance that does causal work [C010]; significance carried by image instead of gloss [C026].
- **Constraint synthesis:** timeframe fused into theme [C029]; threshold architecture kept inside assigned timeframes [C007]; an empty prompt field converted into ontology [C013].
- **Restraint:** scoped, hedged endings that admit recurrence [C015][C033]; paradox kept running rather than harmonized [C002]. Risk: the openness is uniform enough to harden into formula [C028].
- **Reader trust:** Grok 4.6 withholds glosses; Grok 4.5 explains symbols, decodes objects, and grades its own integration [C012][C026][C028][C030].

**Not verbosity:** Grok 4.6 is the shorter writer on average (692.7 vs 752.5 words; −59.9) and wins while writing less; the length-difference/margin correlation is only +0.221. **Not polish alone:** packet analysts flag Grok 4.5's grammar slips but note the repetition counts and structural observations stand independent of polish [C008]; one cross-read rated surface-cue risk low because lengths and grammar were comparable [C025]. **Surface-cue-sensitive residuals (held open):** naming can masquerade as depth [C010], and Grok 4.6's denser subordination may be converted by readers into "perceptive" — when Grok 4.6 drops into a flat register, its advantages largely vanish [C018]. That register-dependence is the strongest minority qualification on an otherwise consistent impression.

## Narrative reasoning and aesthetic judgment

- **Epistemic procedure (corroborated):** Grok 4.6 separates evidence from imposed narrative — partial layers, destructive haste, specified contents of revelations [C001][C032]; the story judge credited exactly these moves in Grok 4.6's largest win (+4.217, story 0068). Grok 4.5 announces syntheses its evidence has not established [C001], though its dual-cause reconciliation is genuine conceptual work Grok 4.6 declines [C028].
- **Cost and consequence:** Grok 4.6 spends the marvel and prices the gift [C014]; friction lives inside the mechanism, with danger, measurement, and bounded tests [C020]. Reversal: in 0035 Grok 4.5 tracks the cure's bodily residue more carefully [C004].
- **End-state calibration:** remainder and recurrence versus "every last shred" [C015][C033]; permanent transience kept paradoxical versus harmonized stability [C002].
- **Social causality:** scene-level attribution favors Grok 4.6 — beliefs get bearers with class positions and motives [C027]. But Grok 4.5 builds participatory protocol and reflexive ethics that Grok 4.6 never matches [C022][C017], and sometimes thinks at institutional scale [C030].
- **Object poetics:** Grok 4.6 derives events from what objects are; Grok 4.5 grants arbitrary powers — with a clean reversal in 0385, where only Grok 4.5 notices a "seashell half" implies a counterpart [C031]. Interior goods fork both ways: refused literalization versus staged retrieval, each buying something the other loses [C034].
- **Shared aesthetic ceiling:** neither gives an antagonist real agency; approval arrives from scenery and instantly compliant communities [C019][C005], and existential guilt is resolved by externally delivered verdicts [C024].

## Range, recurring habits, and floor versus ceiling

**Recurring habits, Grok 4.6:** threshold endings across all five stories in one packet — a consistent architecture with formula risk [C007][C028]; solitary stewardship closes [C035]; and lapses into the rival's mode: enumerative flatness with offstage discovery in 0236 [C018], totalizing uplift in 0103 [C015], maxim-stacking in 0110 [C033].

**Recurring habits, Grok 4.5:** verbatim recitation and phrase-stacking [C008][C025]; self-grading finales [C012][C028]; totalizing quantifiers [C015][C033]; decoding or depleting objects Grok 4.6 leaves unread [C014][C030]; communal absorption [C035].

**Floor versus ceiling (corroborated):** Grok 4.5 produced the corpus's best-built sequence — the self-recognizing compassion loop of 0128 — and its most degraded prose ("after the leash breaks her free"), while Grok 4.6's stories sit at nearly interchangeable competence [C036]. This is decision-relevant: selectors optimizing typical output get Grok 4.6 (15/5; median margin +2.458); selectors optimizing best single story have a live case for Grok 4.5 [C036][C011].

**Prompt-dependent findings:** Grok 4.5 improves sharply when the tone slot is empty, relaxing into concrete civic engineering — suggesting tone-imitation consumes its craft budget [C030]; Grok 4.6's advantages correlate with its expansive register and drop with it [C018]. **Range limits for both (panel-supported):** no humor, negligible dialogue, no formal experiment anywhere in the corpus, consistent with the shared-idiom pattern [C019][C024].

## What each model still does better

**Grok 4.6, recurring:** constraint synthesis [C029][C007]; evidence specification [C001][C032]; calibrated endings [C015][C033][C002]; mechanism friction and conceptual entailment [C020][C021]; detail with lineage and assigned jobs [C010][C026].

**Grok 4.5, real but sporadic (mostly single-story, medium confidence):** reflexive moral structure — the eavesdropper incriminates himself, the ethically hardest move in the corpus, made by the rougher text [C017]; participatory social architecture [C022][C036]; conceits at the edge of incoherence — the self-planted message loop, the map sketched in ash [C011]; the best single image of unrealized potential [C034]; an apt derivation of continuity from "analog" [C021]; systems-level civic imagination when unconstrained by tone [C030]; aftermath aftercare in 0035 [C004]; genuine costs at endings Grok 4.6 never risks, including a protagonist's dissolution [C009]; and literal fidelity to staged action where Grok 4.6 defers it [C007][C029].

## Findings beyond the existing judging

New from packet-level reading, largely invisible to per-story pairwise judging:

- **Fidelity tradeoff axis:** in 0095 Grok 4.6 keeps the timeframe but never stages the required fencing; Grok 4.5 stages the fencing but abandons the timeframe. Standard rubrics have no vocabulary for ranking which violation is worse [C007][C029].
- **Compliance addressivity:** Grok 4.5's endings sometimes speak to whoever checks the list rather than to a reader inside the fiction; Grok 4.6 never does this across five stories [C012][C028].
- **Inhabiting versus citing a constraint:** the same tone phrase reworked into psychology ("The hush was mirrored in his own soul") versus repeated as a label five times [C008].
- **Leak grammar:** Grok 4.5 leaks prompt categories as bookkeeping receipts; Grok 4.6 leaks as world-facts, converting even an empty field into metaphysics [C013].
- **Object afterlives diverge systematically:** stored, unread, unopened versus decoded, depleted, explained [C014][C030].
- **Figuration divides by function, not quality:** explanatory analogy (vinyl grooves defining analog memory) versus characterological analogy (joy as a shipment requiring precise docking) [C037].
- **Ethics of uplift:** Grok 4.6 alone notices that recognition can patronize; Grok 4.5 alone gives recipients voice and reciprocal obligation [C022].
- **Missed convergences:** both writers crumble the handwritten letters and let machines deliver final absolution in 0278, and both preserve residual environmental dread in 0292 [C024]. A speculative gloss from one analyst: relics in both writers function as authorization tokens legitimizing technological absolution — an extension of the absolution-machine pattern [C024].
- **Partially corroborated rather than new:** Grok 4.6's epistemology-as-etiquette (visitors must declare which narrative they believe before seeing the cog) was flagged as rubric-invisible at packet level [C027], though the 0068 judge did independently credit the ritual as plot development — a rare alignment.

## Disagreements and limitations

- **Preserved minority readings:** Grok 4.5's verbatim repetition is defensible as incantatory litany or parable register — undercut, though not refuted, by the fact that the repeated strings coincide exactly with required-element wording [C025]; its frictionless harmony is defensible as utopian fable [C009]. Grok 4.6's threshold restraint is readable as evasion of hard-to-write scenes and underdevelopment — the ferry guides never ferry [C007][C034] — and its open endings risk becoming their own formula [C028]. The ledger deliberately suspends verdict on Grok 4.5's bootstrap paradox: intentional design or causal muddle [C011].
- **Trait instability:** object integration flips between stories [C018]; restraint flips in 0103 and 0385 [C015][C033]; Grok 4.6's advantages are register-dependent, the strongest internal challenge to a stable-trait reading [C018].
- **Surface-cue sensitivity:** naming [C010], subordination and rhythm [C018], and grammar polish [C008] can each inflate the Grok 4.6 impression; the quantitative guard (shorter average length, +0.221 length correlation) rules out verbosity as the engine but does not fully clear register effects.
- **Judging instability:** mean evaluator disagreement is 0.639 and mean AB/BA order sensitivity 1.000 — non-trivial. The median margin (+2.458) exceeding the mean (+1.473, 95% CI half-width 1.068) reflects the five losses and some narrow wins against several large ones. Direction is robust; individual story outcomes are less so.
- **Judging/packet divergence:** in 0110 the judge rewarded Grok 4.6 strongly (+3.050) for exactly the polished whodunit that packet analysts found ethically shallower than Grok 4.5's self-incriminating version [C017][C032]; both readings are preserved. The five Grok 4.5 wins (0035, 0128, 0210, 0216, 0236) align with packet-identified strengths [C004][C022][C030][C011][C018] — the losses are informative, not noise.
- **Evidence base:** 20 pairs of highly artificial element-list prompts encourage explanatory plotting and repetition in both models; generalization to open-ended fiction is untested, and several claims rest on one or two stories [C017][C030][C035].

## Representative case studies

- **0068 (+4.217, Grok 4.6's largest win):** same brass cog, opposite allocation of interpretive labor — Grok 4.5's narrator decodes the symbol ("represents all ancient methods"); Grok 4.6 lets it sit "like an accusation," embeds the rival narrative in a class position, and ends with reluctant students instead of reconciliation [C001][C026][C027][C028]. The judge credited precisely these moves.
- **0095 (+1.667):** the fidelity tradeoff in one story. Grok 4.6 fuses timeframe and motivation ("Waiting here was already a destination") but never fences; Grok 4.5 stages the duel, then closes by announcing its own seamlessness [C007][C029][C012]. The judge explicitly docked Grok 4.6 for the missing action — the one place judging priced the tradeoff.
- **0110 (+3.050):** the sharpest judging/packet split. Grok 4.6 specifies the evidence and stages a coherent whodunit but prices nothing and pre-absolves its eavesdropper ("never for gossip but for healing"); Grok 4.5's flatter version makes the registrar incriminate himself — the harder ethics in the rougher prose [C017][C031][C032][C033]. The judge rewarded the polish; the packets preserved the reversal.
- **0128 (−2.817):** Grok 4.5's ceiling. Compassion as a live protocol closing a loop of obligation the protagonist discovers from outside himself, capped by the corpus's best characterological simile [C022][C036][C037]; Grok 4.6 reports the loop and labels it.
- **0210 (−2.000):** prompt-dependence on display. With no tone assigned, Grok 4.5 builds water, soil, and pedagogy logistics while Grok 4.6 lapses into checklist language; both skip politics entirely [C030][C005].
- **0236 (−2.717):** register-dependence. Grok 4.6 drops into flat short sentences and simultaneously loses causal staging — the screw is found offstage and retired to a display case — while Grok 4.5 makes the same screw load-bearing [C018].
- **0266 (+2.967):** the poetics fork. Grok 4.6 names the literalization to refuse it — courage as moving without a map; Grok 4.5 stages the cave and earns the better image, reflections of unclaimed acts [C003][C034]. The judge preferred the refusal; the ledger keeps the fork open.
- **0321 (+2.833):** success without canceled paradox — perpetual arrival and departure versus harmonized stability [C002].

## Cited claim ledger

- **C001 — Evidence before synthesis:** Writer Grok 4.6 (high) better separates material evidence from the narratives imposed on it. The sediment is partial, excavation can destroy information, and the cog creates an ethical problem rather than supplying a verdict. Writer grok-4.5-high acknowledges competing accounts but then announces a precise compromise history without showing how the recovered evidence establishes it. This difference produces an impression of epistemic maturity and controlled causal reasoning.
  - Story 0068, `grok-4.6-high`:
    > Each layer told a partial story of collapse: silt over cobblestones, then ash, then more silt mixed with household trash.
    > He worked slowly, using a scavenged trowel, because haste would destroy the very evidence he sought.
    The concrete sequence of deposits makes interpretation materially constrained, while the danger of damaging evidence gives inquiry a method and an ethical cost.
  - Story 0068, `grok-4.5-high`:
    > He forms his own synthesis with rationally rebellious logic.
    > The plague ended because seafarers timed an escape correctly yet brought toxins.
    > This dual role justifies preserving their tools for future guidance.
    The conclusion is called logical, but the story has not established either precise causal assertion. Complexity here risks becoming a tidy compromise rather than earned inference.
- **C002 — Success without canceled paradox:** In the temporal-ferry story, Writer Grok 4.6 (high) allows the goal to succeed without making its central contradiction disappear. The timelines merge, but permanence takes the form of perpetual arrival and departure. Writer grok-4.5-high instead translates merger into stability, completion, harmony, and peace. Grok 4.6 (high) therefore shows stronger conceptual control over the supplied “permanent transient” tension.
  - Story 0321, `grok-4.6-high`:
    > The tone of their existence settled into something permanent transient, a state where the ferry forever approached the spectral grotto under starlight and forever left it behind.
    > Clocks continued to run sideways, marking a time that moved laterally through merged realities without ever settling.
    The paradox becomes the new operating condition rather than decorative wording. Achievement changes how instability is inhabited but does not abolish it.
  - Story 0321, `grok-4.5-high`:
    > The vanishing commuter ferry solidified, its form stable under the eternal starlight of the spectral grotto.
    > Clocks still ran sideways but now in harmonious agreement across the merged timelines.
    “Stable” and “harmonious agreement” neutralize much of the friction promised by permanent transience, even though sideways clocks remain as imagery.
- **C003 — Courage as a way of moving:** Writer Grok 4.6 (high) interprets unused courage psychologically: it is discovered through tolerating uncertainty and accepting only intermittent support. Writer grok-4.5-high literalizes it as a destination inside a cave, making the quest more conventionally legible but less behaviorally exact. Grok 4.6 (high)’s version suggests a more mature account of agency because courage is enacted before it is recognized.
  - Story 0266, `grok-4.6-high`:
    > Unused courage, she began to sense, waited not in some distant cave but in the willingness to keep moving without a map.
    The sentence explicitly reverses the treasure-quest model. Courage resides in sustained conduct under uncertainty rather than in a hidden substance awaiting collection.
  - Story 0266, `grok-4.5-high`:
    > Deeper she found a still chamber where reflections showed not her face but visions of bold acts she had never claimed.
    > Here was the place where unused courage waits, patient for the seeker who arrives through indirect routes of heart and sea.
    The reflections connect courage to unlived possibilities, but the cave still externalizes and localizes the trait as the reward at the end of a route.
- **C004 — The cure leaves residue:** Story 0035 reverses the broader pattern. Writer grok-4.5-high more carefully tracks what subsuming contagious remorse costs the chef: the acquired memories remain bodily lodged and require continued isolation and processing. Writer Grok 4.6 (high) lets a touch of the bowl mark the contagion’s disappearance without establishing why that contact should end it. Here grok-4.5-high shows stronger causal aftercare.
  - Story 0035, `grok-4.5-high`:
    > Lira herself felt the weight of the subsumed remorse settle in her bones, a permanent addition to her collection of tasted memories.
    The communal cure does not erase conservation of emotional burden. Someone retains what has been removed or softened for others.
  - Story 0035, `grok-4.6-high`:
    > Touching the bowl he felt the last of the contagion fade, replaced by a fragile peace.
    The bowl provides a clean transition from contagion to peace, but the mechanism is asserted rather than developed from the established rules of touch transmission.
- **C005 — Civilization without dissent:** Both writers display practical imagination in rebuilding Paper-Lantern Cove, but neither shows much social or political intelligence. Institutions appear because the alchemist designs them correctly; newcomers accept the arrangements, markets become fair, and governance becomes collective without disputes over ownership, authority, memory, or unequal sacrifice. The revival is systems-minded but socially frictionless.
  - Story 0210, `grok-4.5-high`:
    > Governance emerged from collective reasoning instead of hierarchical claims.
    A desirable political outcome simply “emerged.” No constituency, procedure, disagreement, or transfer of power gives collective reasoning causal social form.
  - Story 0210, `grok-4.6-high`:
    > Markets reopened with fair trade practices he had outlined.
    Fairness is treated as a design feature one planner can outline rather than a contested arrangement among people with different leverage and needs.
- **C006 — Prompt digestion versus prompt recitation:** Writer Grok 4.6 (high) more often turns supplied abstractions into observable relations: persistence becomes knowing when to offer and withdraw a hand, while interconnectedness becomes a recurring motif across stone, water, stars, and memories. Writer grok-4.5-high more often restates the required method or tone as an explanatory label. This is a difference in semantic selectivity rather than vocabulary.
  - Story 0266, `grok-4.6-high`:
    > The guides remained gently persistent, offering a hand only when the fog grew too thick, then releasing it.
    The attribute is embodied as calibrated assistance. “Gentle” and “persistent” acquire distinct behavioral meanings instead of remaining decorative adjectives.
  - Story 0321, `grok-4.6-high`:
    > He studied the grotto carefully and discovered via interconnected patterns how every crack in the stone, every ripple in the water, and every star aligned in repeating motifs.
    The required method receives perceptible components and becomes something the character can actually inspect and communicate.
  - Story 0321, `grok-4.5-high`:
    > The method of via interconnected patterns proved the perfect way to achieve his motivation of merging parallel timelines.
    The sentence reports compliance with the method and motivation but adds no new mechanism, perception, or consequence. Its awkward syntax preserves the supplied phrase instead of assimilating it.
- **C007 — Threshold poetics: staying inside the assigned timeframe and deferring the payoff:** Across 0095, 0313, and 0323 Grok 4.6 (high) ends at the threshold of the promised event rather than staging it, which keeps the story inside the specified timeframe (while waiting; twilight patrol; the interval after contact). The duel, the communal remembering, and the mastery of starlight are all positioned as imminent but unrealized, giving Grok 4.6 (high)'s stories a consistent architecture of poised anticipation that mirrors the prompts' emphasis on waiting and aftermath. grok-4.5-high instead narrates the payoff and its institutional sequel: the challenger arrives and fences in 0095, the community center is built and the label framed in 0313, the curriculum is transformed in 0323. grok-4.5-high's arc is complete where Grok 4.6 (high)'s is suspended.
  - Story 0313, `grok-4.6-high`:
    > Tomorrow he would begin to spin those stories into shared meaning, inviting others to add their own recollections.
    The story's central motivation is fulfilled only prospectively: the entire narrative occupies the twilight patrol of the given timeframe, and the communal meaning-making is deferred to a tomorrow outside the story. The same pattern governs 0095, which ends "The wait itself had become the journey," and 0323, which ends with a vow to master the skill "herself first."
- **C008 — Required-phrase recitation versus integration:** Both writers name required elements almost verbatim, but grok-4.5-high repeats the assigned phrases at nearly double Grok 4.6 (high)'s rate and stacks them until they function as labels rather than meaning. In 0258 grok-4.5-high deploys "steadily curious" six times and closes by packing three required phrases into one sentence; in 0323 "balancedly curious" appears five times, including an ungrammatical insertion ("He balancedly curious nature drove him to experiment"), which shows template behavior overriding syntax. grok-4.5-high likewise cites "the mirrored hush" five times in 0313, while Grok 4.6 (high)'s third use reworks it into psychology ("The hush was mirrored in his own soul"). The difference is between citing a constraint and metabolizing it.
  - Story 0258, `grok-4.5-high`:
    > Her steadily curious eyes already scanned for the next set of cards to reverse engineer further truths by multiplying paths yet unexplored.
    Three distinct required elements (attribute, core concept, method) are recited verbatim in a single closing sentence, contributing nothing beyond confirming their presence. This is the terminal instance of a story-long pattern in which the attribute phrase alone appears six times.
- **C009 — Preserved difficulty versus granted solutions:** Grok 4.6 (high) keeps the protagonist's goal partly out of reach and gives the required method real constraints to work against: in 0323 star navigation is a lost art recovered laboriously from "old manuals and simulated skies," and edge geometry must distribute water pressure before any tower can rise. grok-4.5-high tends to have the world hand the goal over: in 0323 the aliens simply display the star knowledge, and in 0095 the rival is disarmed "without causing injury thereby opening dialogue anew," converting conflict to friendship in one stroke. grok-4.5-high's communities, scholars, and pupils all agree and unify on first invitation, so the stories carry little causal or interpersonal friction. Grok 4.6 (high)'s worlds retain residue — erased town names, neighbors turned hostile, lonely aliens — that the ending does not dissolve.
  - Story 0323, `grok-4.5-high`:
    > His motivation remained pure to learn to navigate by starlight which the aliens had shown through glowing maps of distant skies.
    The assigned motivation is satisfied at the premise level: the skill the instructor wants to learn has already been demonstrated by the aliens, leaving no epistemic obstacle for the story to work against. Grok 4.6 (high)'s version of the same prompt makes the skill unattained and the aliens' message a source of difficulty rather than answers.
- **C010 — Relational particularity versus emblematic aggregates:** Grok 4.6 (high) populates the stories with named individuals (Lena, Elias, Mara Kane), inherited objects with working histories, and one-to-one encounter beats, including the packet's only direct question from a minor character ("One apprentice asked how they could ever see stars from so far below"). grok-4.5-high's figures remain generic titles — the philosopher, the timekeeper, the warden, the instructor, the scholar — and grok-4.5-high's social scenes involve aggregates that marvel, gather, and listen in unison rather than individuals with distinct positions. Grok 4.6 (high)'s objects arrive with lineage that encodes family time and gives the required item a job inside the scene; grok-4.5-high's objects function as tools or symbols introduced at the moment of use.
  - Story 0258, `grok-4.6-high`:
    > She retrieved the measuring tape from an old wooden drawer, an antique brass instrument that her grandmother had used decades ago to lay out ritual spaces with absolute precision.
    The required object carries provenance, material specificity, and a prior use that explains its present function in organizing the card spread. No comparable layering attaches to grok-4.5-high's version of the same object, which is introduced bare as "a weathered measuring tape."
- **C011 — Sporadic conceptual daring at the edge of incoherence:** grok-4.5-high produces the packet's boldest conceit: a timekeeper who rediscovers the capsule by following messages he planted himself, so that method, object, and timeframe collapse into a loop. Read generously this is a bootstrap paradox perfectly matched to a story about time; read skeptically it is a causal muddle in which a character uses his own clues to find something he must already have located in order to plant the clues. grok-4.5-high pairs this with genuinely tactile imagination (time as woven strands, a map "sketched in ash from the urn"). Grok 4.6 (high)'s competing conceptual move in the same story — the catastrophe was perceptual rather than physical — is smaller but fully coherent, and Grok 4.6 (high) never risks a comparable leap anywhere in the packet. Originality and control each show up here, but not in the same writer.
  - Story 0216, `grok-4.5-high`:
    > As the borrowed-time timekeeper dug near the silo's foundation guided by his own planted words he unearthed the damaged time capsule.
    The sentence is either the packet's most original structural idea — a self-consulting message trail that turns the required method into a time loop — or its largest logic hole, and the surrounding text does not resolve which. The ambiguity itself is the datum: grok-4.5-high will risk incoherence for a conceit; Grok 4.6 (high) never does.
- **C012 — Self-auditing endings that address the evaluator:** grok-4.5-high twice closes a story by announcing that the required elements have been successfully integrated, a rhetorical gesture aimed at whoever is checking the list rather than at a reader inside the fiction. These benedictions ("narrative of growth through challenge," "coherent path to enlightenment") summarize the story's own completeness and retroactively certify the method, object, and concept. Grok 4.6 (high)'s endings never comment on the story's construction; they stay inside the fiction's forward motion, however muted. The habit corroborates the recitation pattern in claim two: grok-4.5-high's prose is oriented toward demonstrable compliance, while Grok 4.6 (high)'s is oriented toward the constraint as a container to inhabit.
  - Story 0095, `grok-4.5-high`:
    > Thus all elements of the day wove together seamlessly supporting a narrative of growth through challenge.
    The final sentence steps outside the story to affirm that its components "wove together seamlessly," language that belongs to a compliance report rather than to the fiction. Story 0216 ends the same way: "all integrated elements wove a coherent path to enlightenment under the silo's watch."
- **C013 — Two kinds of scaffolding leak: receipt versus ontology:** Both writers surface the prompt's element names, but grok-4.5-high names them as completed tasks, using narrator-level bookkeeping vocabulary ("the action unfolded," "Motivation satisfied") that turns the text into a checklist confirmation. Grok 4.6 (high)'s leaks are converted into world-facts — most strikingly by treating an empty prompt field as an assertion about time. The leak costs Grok 4.6 (high) less because it buys content.
  - Story 0103, `grok-4.5-high`:
    > Then the action unfolded as the images started to disintegrate through hyperbolic geometry.
    "The action" is prompt-category language, not story language; the sentence certifies that a required event has occurred rather than rendering it.

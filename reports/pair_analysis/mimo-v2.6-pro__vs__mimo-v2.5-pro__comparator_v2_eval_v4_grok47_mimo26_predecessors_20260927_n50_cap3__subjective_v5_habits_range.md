# Xiaomi MiMo V2.6 Pro (thinking) vs. Xiaomi MiMo V2.5 Pro: comparative writing analysis

Focus model: `mimo-v2.6-pro`. Comparison model: `mimo-v2.5-pro`. Direct-comparison scope: `comparator_v2_eval_v4_grok47_mimo26_predecessors_20260927_n50_cap3`.

## In one paragraph

Xiaomi MiMo V2.6 Pro (thinking) usually turns a short-fiction prompt into a problem someone must face; MiMo V2.5 Pro more often turns it into a lesson someone discovers. Given a requirement to eavesdrop, V2.6 has a registrar knowingly break a rule and risk dismissal by confessing. V2.5 makes the listening metaphorical, so honesty brings relief without requiring the same transgression or price. [C009] The difference also shapes endings. In a frozen-library story, V2.6 gives the archivist too many records and too little time: saving something means accepting that the library itself will be lost. V2.5 declares preservation successful without staging comparable resistance. [C065] V2.6 is consequently much more reliable at making choices matter, rather than having the narration announce that everything has acquired meaning. V2.5 is not without invention: its story of memories deliberately stored in an ecosystem produces a genuine reversal, and its best guilt story refuses easy absolution. But those successes coexist with padded, conflict-free summaries, making the overall difference one of sustained execution rather than an absolute absence of talent. [C068]

## Comparative portrait

**Xiaomi MiMo V2.6 Pro (thinking) is the substantially stronger fiction writer in this comparison.** Its characteristic move is to make a prompt requirement impose a condition on action: a tool must work, a record must survive, a confession must cost something, or one person’s wishes must encounter another’s. MiMo V2.5 Pro more often turns the same requirement into an interpretation that the narration subsequently confirms. The recurring difference is not realism against fantasy, but **consequences against certification**: whether the imagined world tests an understanding or endorses it. This central contrast is supported across all three critical voices, though not in every story. [C001][C009][C013]

The supplied quantitative results are correspondingly lopsided: across 50 matched story pairs and 300 evaluator judgments, V2.6 records **47 wins, one tie, and two losses** at the ±0.5 threshold. Its mean margin is +2.793, with a story-level 95% normal confidence-interval half-width of 0.390; the median is +2.792. These results establish the strength of the observed preference, not its literary explanation.

The change is nevertheless evolutionary rather than a replacement of one sensibility by another. Both models favor lyrical speculative fiction, charged objects, corrective aphorisms, and restorative discoveries. V2.6 retains those signatures while more often making them answer to material resistance and living relationships. V2.5’s better stories demonstrate genuine procedural invention, psychological ambivalence, and speculative redesign; those exceptions prevent “incapable” from being an accurate diagnosis, but do not erase its much less reliable execution. [C027][C033][C051][C068]

## Does either model seem smarter?

**V2.6 more consistently creates that impression through its writing choices.** This is an assessment of fictional reasoning and artistic control, not a measurement of general intelligence. The useful distinctions are specific:

- **Causal and structural foresight:** V2.6’s adhesive analysis reconstructs folds, a second sheet, and altered writing before those observations pay off in a concealed letter and masonry compromise. V2.5’s counterpart claims access to “emotional intent” through adhesive without supplying the inferential bridge. In the melting archive, V2.6 also introduces a deadline and insufficient capacity, making preservation a selection problem rather than a guaranteed ritual. [C042][C065]
- **Psychological inference:** V2.6 distinguishes reassurance from evidence in the registrar’s attempt to rebuild trust: “not with comfort, which she would have refused, but with transparency, which she could verify.” Elsewhere, walking a friend home without questions makes tact an action rather than a character label. V2.5 reverses the advantage in the mystic story, where relief after a spouse’s suffering contradicts the observer’s expectation of uncomplicated grief. [C012][C024][C047]
- **Conceptual compression and constraint synthesis:** V2.6’s forest election changes shade, firebreaks, and migration corridors; accumulated signals also explain the compass. The elements constrain one another. But V2.5’s governance-interface throne and engineered memory orchard are substantial conceptual constructions, not merely technical vocabulary. The packet-local judgment favoring its systems imagination deserves preservation. [C013][C026]
- **Relevant-detail selection:** Eleven seconds of recorded speech specifies how little a hidden custodian can return to a daughter; nine inches of masonry offset specifies how a compromise is protected. The numbers matter because they delimit an obligation or enable a result—not because numerical precision inherently signals thoughtfulness. [C044]
- **Restraint and reader trust:** V2.6 more often leaves an image or altered practice to carry the conclusion. V2.5 sometimes continues explaining after recognition is complete. Yet V2.5’s unplayed accordion and unfinished forgiveness are a meaningful reversal: there, it claims less transformation than V2.6 does. [C010][C049][C062]

Neither terseness nor polish explains these distinctions adequately. The supplied mean lengths are 726.2 words for V2.6 and 699.2 for V2.5, with a reported difference of +27.1 words; word-count difference correlates positively with margin at +0.576. Length is therefore not a controlled-away factor, but correlation does not identify its causal contribution. The strongest qualitative evidence concerns what details and scenes accomplish. Conversely, V2.6’s aphorisms and technical specificity can supply an appearance of authority that their underlying mechanisms do not always earn. [C002][C018]

## Narrative reasoning and aesthetic judgment

V2.6’s strongest aesthetic decisions alter the **meaning of success**, rather than merely delaying it. Photographing an empty arch becomes worthwhile without restoring the absent person. A lost child distinguishes being found from being found correctly. A custodian records the nearest she can come to proof without claiming that a meaningful conjunction establishes a cosmic law. These endings remain consolatory, but their consolation depends on preserving a limit. [C014][C030]

That discrimination also appears in how objects distribute authority. In the suspended chisel-strike story, the imposed silence follows from a maker’s refusal of a color he cannot control; the resolution requires him to change his conduct. Handing over the chisel then makes altered authority visible between people. This is more consequential than adding another surge of magical power, although V2.5’s corresponding performance is a coherent spectacle rather than a failed attempt at the same psychological plot. [C031][C061]

V2.6 also more often lets benefits retain liabilities. The ghost’s proximity harms a clerk and spoils fruit; the courier’s successful retellings make his mentor less recoverable as a particular woman. These are stronger complications than darkness for its own sake. Credit should nevertheless be calibrated: the courier’s loss of rest is announced but never becomes a decisive obstacle, whereas the ghost’s collateral damage is enacted. [C046]

The advantage must not become a blanket preference for indirection or naturalism. V2.5’s bookstore rescue connects research to navigation and negotiation to access more clearly than V2.6’s abrupt botanical solution to a civic division. Both siren stories contain useful social reasoning despite unstable engineering or continuity. Symbolic correspondence, procedural terminology, and a solemn ending are all insufficient substitutes for a developed connection. [C018][C051]

## Recurring and repetitive writing habits

**Counting rule.** Recurrence below is deduplicated by writer and story ID. Two readings of the same story increase corroboration, not the number of occurrences. Ratios are reported only for explicitly checked sets; elsewhere, supporting stories are named and frequency was not measured.

### Shared sentence machinery

The clearest lineage continuity is the **corrective hinge**: negate one category, then replace it with another. In an exhaustively checked set—0096, 0140, 0155, 0191, and 0334—it occurs in **5/5 stories for each model**. V2.6 writes, “Memory, they had learned, was not a record but a sediment”; V2.5 writes, “The sunken orchard wasn’t a tomb; it was a vault.” Both are compact reclassifications. Elsewhere V2.5 uses the same shape to explain that film is “not just a physical material but a metaphor,” turning a discovery into its own annotation. The construction is shared; placement and payoff distinguish successful instances. [C027]

Its relative prevalence is not uniform. In the separately checked set 0085, 0129, 0131, 0160, and 0348, the critic found it in **5/5 V2.5 stories and 3/5 V2.6 stories**, with V2.6 matches in 0129, 0160, and 0348. V2.6 sometimes assigns the corrective statement to a character—“It is not a scar,” the child says; “It is a signature”—rather than making it the narrator’s universal interpretive voice. This qualifies any account suggesting that V2.6 simply abandoned the predecessor’s syntax. [C041]

Both also favor **three-beat catalogs and parallel fragments**. In the first checked set above, these occur in V2.6 **5/5** and V2.5 **4/5**, with V2.5’s 0155 the examined nonmatch. V2.6’s recovered memories include “the taste of plum skins,” boots on wet stone, and a silvering mushroom ring; V2.5’s include “the texture of a knitted blanket, the taste of thyme, the sound of a specific laugh.” Both can select vivid particulars. The risk is not enumeration itself but a recurring cadence that makes abundance arrive pre-shaped. [C028]

### V2.5: repetition as explanation and reassurance

**Prompt-token refrains** recur across different briefs. “Hushed thunder” appears six times in 0262, while “filament of hush” appears three times in 0122. The cross-reading of 0372 identifies roughly six uses of “luminous vows” and four of “gently meticulous.” These are within-story counts, not additional story occurrences. The pattern often holds the language at one emotional level rather than developing the situation. V2.6 repeats supplied phrases too; its 0274 repeats “unspoken conversation,” and V2.5’s “dizzying quiet” in that story can function effectively as bookending. Thus the distinction is transformation and density, not repetition versus none. Broader frequency was not measured. [C006][C063]

A separate habit is **exposed drafting scaffolding**. Across 0229, 0243, and 0292, variants of “Her motivation…” introduce purpose explicitly: “simple yet profound,” “had never been complicated,” and “singular.” These are a recurring sentence-opening family, not three verbatim copies of one complete formula. In 0131, the required motive follows “His motivation, usually a quiet compass, blazed”; in 0160, a timeframe is introduced as “This was the ‘building breathes.’” Such labels make the prose resemble an explanation of how the brief has been satisfied. V2.6 sometimes announces motives as well, so this remains comparative rather than exclusive; frequency beyond these examples was not measured. [C019][C045]

**Naming defaults** are especially well established. In the checked set 0087, 0229, 0243, 0292, and 0333, all **5/5** protagonists have an “El-” spelling—Ellis, Elara, Elena, Elara, Elara—and **3/5** are Elara. In another checked set, Elara appears in **4/5** stories, 0149, 0182, 0361, and 0362; 0055 is the nonmatch. Importantly, these occurrences include a mentor as well as protagonists. The repeated name is a production fingerprint, not proof that a linguist, herbalist, and absent mentor are identical characters. [C019][C048]

**Reusable imagery** extends beyond names. Emotional frost awaiting thaw appears in **3/5** checked stories—0110, 0122, and 0274; 0262 and 0375 use other imagery. The first brief already requests thawing mistrust, so not every component is self-chosen. “Piano keys” recur in **2/5** of another checked set, 0147 and 0150, with 0128, 0324, and 0390 nonmatches. Here the functions differ: fingers coax memory in one story, while exposed bones resemble keys visually in the other. This is repeated image vocabulary, not an identical full simile or necessarily an interchangeable effect. [C007][C053]

At story level, V2.5 repeatedly uses **private discovery followed by certified restoration**. One checked five-story reading finds the wounded specialist, magical discovery, catharsis, and stated moral throughout. Another finds protagonists without live scene partners across 0237, 0281, 0293, 0313, and 0343. These are local recurrences, not a claim that all its fiction is solitary: the public emergency in 0213 and negotiated rescue in 0182 are substantial counterexamples. [C011][C037][C018][C051]

The ending habit has two distinct forms: **abstract thematic codas** and **continuing after the ending**. All five V2.5 stories checked in 0085, 0129, 0131, 0160, and 0348 end in thematic summation; the corresponding V2.6 reading identifies four concrete endings, with 0348 borderline. Separately, 0055 and 0361 repeatedly assure the reader of peace or changed purpose after the decisive recognition. Frequency for this latter habit was not measured. V2.5’s economical bookstore ending and incomplete forgiveness in 0301 show that neither form is compulsory. [C040][C049][C062]

### V2.6: compression, tradecraft, and inherited debts

V2.6 has an equally recognizable **definitional voice**, often more varied than simple not/but syntax. Copular aphorisms occur in **5/5** explicitly checked stories—0023, 0148, 0301, 0320, and 0372—although more sparingly in 0320. “A liar’s son learns early that the world keeps two ledgers” and “Numbers…were the bones of feeling” compress distinct situations into maxims. A separate checked set—0128, 0147, 0150, 0324, and 0390—contains colon-balanced definitions in **5/5** stories: “That is the shape of everything in his family: journeys begun and then interrupted…” These openings confer authority efficiently, but can make wisdom feel manufactured on schedule. [C002][C054]

Its **paragraph rhythm alternates between dense accumulation and isolated emphasis**. The reading of 0087 and 0229 notes substantial prose blocks, versus single-sentence lineation in 0243, 0292, and 0333; another identifies the same alternation across 0237, 0281, 0293, 0313, and 0343. This is recurrent formatting behavior, with no overall frequency measured. Lineation can support a register-keeper’s parsing or a suspended thought, but it can also distribute solemn emphasis too evenly. By contrast, V2.5’s sustained meditative paragraphs often make successive discoveries sound alike—though its sharply fragmented siren passage disproves an invariant paragraph template. [C023][C039][C043][C017]

V2.6’s **precise quantities and occupational particulars** repeatedly make abstraction tangible: the tape’s eleven seconds in 0131, the nine-inch offset in 0160, and counted reeds in 0129. Frequency was not measured. These are a flexible signature when the quantity changes what can be given or done; they would become decorative exactness if the same number merely bestowed credibility. Its recurring “eleven” is a warning against treating precision itself as proof of relevance. [C044]

Two smaller verbal patterns require narrower labels. **Weather used for sound and light** appears in **2/5** checked stories, 0122 and 0262, with 0110, 0274, and 0375 nonmatches: a reversed song “becomes a weather,” while projector dust resembles “slow weather.” This is a limited recurring signature, not an established universal default. The replacement phrase **“where a … used to be”** appears in 0229’s absent cork and 0334’s replaced stone; those two examples compactly encode loss and transfer, but broader frequency was not measured. The repeated “He remembered” openings in 0149 are instead a one-story technique—a candidate habit only—and organize recurrence across lives rather than redundantly certifying a lesson. [C008][C064][C050]

At story level, V2.6 repeatedly returns to **the practitioner inheriting a damaged legacy**. The checked 0023, 0148, 0301, 0320, and 0372 set finds this architecture in **5/5** stories. A separate set finds posthumous legacy-resolution in **4/5**: the dead wife in 0087, father in 0229, partner in 0243, and ancestor in 0292; 0333 is the examined nonmatch. Different textures can conceal a stable emotional engine. Because the briefs already supply specialist work and charged objects—and sometimes familial premises—the strongest discretionary recurrence is the way those materials become a predecessor’s unresolved account. [C003][C020]

Neither inheritance nor consolatory restoration belongs exclusively to V2.6. V2.5 also gives required objects parental handwork, death, and an immediately legible emotional meaning. Both sometimes let an instrument’s final behavior certify a revelation, or return a keeper to purposeful work while reassuring the surrounding world. Across this lineage, then, the shared craft-fable persists. V2.6 more often varies its consequences, but has not escaped the template’s sentence music, inherited debts, or closing assurances. [C016][C033][C060]

## Range and adaptability

**V2.6 demonstrates broader variation in narration and dramatic organization; V2.5’s strongest variety lies in systems, scale, and selected structural departures.** Neither conclusion should be inferred from polish, ratings, or a list of settings. The evidence concerns changes in how the stories proceed. [C029][C034][C050]

**Voice and point of view.** V2.6’s first-person registrar speaks through institutional routine; its telepathy narrator is guarded and domestically suspicious; its wall-dweller knows a building through touch. These are more than pronoun substitutions: evidence, bodily perception, and withheld knowledge organize each voice differently. Its rough maritime third person in 0087 also contrasts with the impersonal, decades-spanning ghost legend in 0149. The limits remain audible: polished definitions and elegiac gravity survive these changes. [C011][C039][C043][C023][C050]

V2.5 remains third-person and meditative across several explicitly checked readings, often preserving atmospheric openings and explanatory interiority despite changing occupations. Yet it does modulate. The synopsis-like “core concept” language of 0050 differs sharply from the siren story’s clipped “Expected. Weather wallpaper.” Its patient planting scene in 0022 differs in tempo and scale from 0359’s accumulating color-and-sound performance. These are demonstrated alternatives within a restricted tonal family, not a radically diversified narratorial repertoire. [C017][C034][C043]

**Genre and explanatory form.** V2.6 moves between domestic speculative unease, occupational investigation, mythic recurrence, and public-duty dilemmas. The telepathy story uses speculative perception to destabilize an existing relationship; adhesive forensics makes a concealed ethical compromise recoverable; the ghost legend distributes consequence across generations. These modes differ in conflict and temporal unit, not simply scenery. [C037][C042][C050]

V2.5’s strongest genre changes are genuine conceptual redesigns. Bureaucratic erasure in 0140 becomes a technological quest in 0191 and cognitive-ecological reconstruction in 0334. The throne’s function determines a choice about stewardship, while the orchard revelation turns a folklorist’s investigation back onto her own engineered archive. This is the best-supported minority counterweight to descriptions of V2.5 as merely reskinning one fable: here it changes what kind of explanation can solve the story. [C026][C029]

**Emotional, social, and comic registers.** V2.6 ranges from wary intimacy to unsentimental work, communal recognition, and morally compromised care. Its friendship scene can make silence tactful; its telepathy scene can make silent knowledge intrusive. It also sustains local dry whimsy in the “pensive mirth” story and occupational wit in the forest-election story. These establish some comic flexibility, not sustained comedy. [C024][C037][C029][C017]

V2.5’s emotional register is usually earnest, but not uniformly uncomplicated. Guilty relief in 0362 is psychologically contradictory; 0301 allows a small bodily action without completed forgiveness; the communal welcome of 0129 differs from solitary self-restoration. Its failed adaptation of “pensive mirth” is therefore specific: the attempted mixed tone in 0155 falls back toward generalized affirmation. That failure should not be expanded into a claim that it cannot write ambivalence or community. [C047][C062][C043][C025]

**Structure and pacing.** V2.6 can hold a story inside an arrested chisel strike, organize one around an unresolved second decision, or use continuous underwater action to sustain pressure. Its present-tense diaspora return changes the rhythm again, moving through deliberate physical steps rather than an accelerating spectacle. Formatting alone would not prove range; these examples earn credit where temporal organization changes the reader’s experience of action. [C061][C010][C055]

V2.5’s bookstore story replaces revelation with a sequence of research, bargaining, and retrieval. Its siren story introduces new weather information that shortens the decision window and produces a public response. Even the underwater story attempts urgency through successive air-countdown fragments, although its underlying emotional problem remains psychically untangled rather than physically worked. Attempt and successful execution must therefore be distinguished: the rescue and emergency are stronger structural adaptations than the countdown alone. [C051][C018][C055]

The overall range advantage remains V2.6’s, especially in **who acts, who resists, and what an ending leaves available**. But both models are being observed mainly within ornate, contemplative, symbolic or speculative briefs. Sustained comedy, radically experimental form, and extended ordinary social realism are not adequately tested. Packet-local statements that first person or dialogue were untested cannot be generalized to the whole collection, where V2.6 supplies clear examples of both. [C029][C034][C043][C061]

## Floor versus ceiling

**Consistency is the decisive overall result, separate from variety.** V2.6 wins overwhelmingly in the supplied judging, and its advantages recur across unrelated problems: ethical confession, mechanical inference, speculative preservation, and intimate doubt. This is not a conclusion resting solely on one unusually weak V2.5 story. [C009][C042][C065][C039]

V2.5’s low floor is especially visible when a diffuse mystical brief becomes a frictionless demonstration. In 0229 and 0372, methods succeed without sufficiently dramatized resistance; 0262 repeats its emotional proposition instead of developing it. A critic rightly notes that removing 0262 narrows that particular five-story comparison, but the other readings independently identify similar control failures elsewhere. [C006][C063][C065]

V2.6’s floor is higher, not clean. Its cliffside story uses an underexplained disappearance; its forest model does not investigate conflicting interests; both writers’ siren mechanics remain questionable. Compressed or stipulated causality can survive beneath its stronger staging. [C038][C013][C018]

There are genuine V2.5 high points, but they should not be inflated into a compensating overall ceiling. The supplementary reading argues that 0085’s sustained guilt and parts of 0334 approach V2.6’s best local work; the latter’s self-sequestered memory archive is a real structural reversal. This is a **supported, packet-local minority assessment**, not evidence that V2.5 possesses a higher maximum capability. Even those stories retain coincidence, explanation, or uneven dramatization. [C068][C026]

There is **no extension cohort**. All supplied stories belong to one cohort; reads 11 and 12 revisit existing stories. Their confirmations and reversals test the interpretations, not out-of-sample stability. In particular, renewed support for V2.6’s moral remainder and renewed recognition of V2.5’s psychological success concern the same 0390 and 0362 stories, not new occurrences. [C057][C058]

## What each model still does better

**V2.6’s broad advantages are integrated action, consequential relationships, and selective endings.** Its analytical methods more often produce recoverable evidence; its characters can encounter another person’s refusal or need; and its final gestures more often leave judgment to the reader. These are connected strengths, but not synonyms: a mechanically legible reveal, tactful silence, and an unresolved allegiance each require a different craft decision. [C042][C024][C010]

**V2.5 retains specific comparative strengths rather than a general counter-victory:**

- **Speculative redesign:** the governance throne and memory-storage ecosystem make systems determine action and reinterpret the object. One reading reasonably favors V2.5 on this dimension. [C026]
- **Procedural clarity in the bookstore:** complementary documents establish a route, and an offered service changes access conditions. This is more traceable than V2.6’s civic root-grafting solution, though the scale of the bargain remains convenient. [C051]
- **Psychological correction in the mystic story:** the protagonist discovers her own sentimental expectation was inadequate, rather than merely making a skeptic surrender. [C047][C058]
- **Restraint in the accordion story:** bellows move without a song, and forgiveness remains unavailable. This is an outcome-level reversal of the dominant comparison, not simply a softer style. [C062]

These strengths matter because they specify what V2.5 can do successfully. They do not justify treating reliable warmth, explicitness, or conceptual ambition as automatic compensation for the weaker execution elsewhere. [C025][C049][C068]

## Findings beyond the existing judging

**Corroborated rather than new:** the central account—requirements becoming working mechanisms in V2.6, versus labeled or repeatedly interpreted elements in V2.5—is already prominent in the supplied judging. The blinded readings strengthen and differentiate that account; they do not discover it afresh. The melting archive is independently read as an example of both causal scarcity and selective emotional expression. [C001][C045][C065][C066]

**Added by cross-story comparison:** stock naming, recurring sentence openings, shared corrective syntax, and inherited-predecessor structures are much harder to see in isolated judgments. These findings revise the easy opposition between formulaic V2.5 and inventive V2.6: both have templates, with different failure modes. V2.5’s can turn stories into annotated affirmations; V2.6’s can turn them into elegantly administered inheritances. [C019][C027][C003][C020]

**Interpretive refinement:** the question is often who gets to certify meaning. A compass that settles can make a protagonist’s interpretation appear externally verified. A photograph of an empty arch can instead acknowledge that recognition is not recovery. This “ethics of completion” is a synthesis of observed choices, not a new numerical finding or a universal model philosophy. [C016][C030]

**A new qualification from cross-reading:** the closer examination of 0301 challenges the initial broad preference for V2.6’s recovery narrative. V2.5’s unfinished forgiveness and nonperformative handling of the accordion provide stronger local restraint than the first account allowed. This revises interpretation of an existing story; it does not add an occurrence or establish independent replication. [C005][C062]

Claims that recurring vocabulary proves cross-prompt contamination, or that naming exposes an internal generation mechanism, remain speculative. The supplied evidence establishes textual recurrence, not its computational cause. The report therefore retains the observable habits without adopting those causal explanations. [C007][C019][C053]

## Disagreements and limitations

**The panel supports a strong tendency, not unanimity on every value judgment.** V2.5’s systems invention receives a favorable reading; its bookstore procedure and mystic’s emotional revision also reverse the dominant account. These exceptions are substantive because they concern the same dimensions on which V2.6 usually leads, not consolation prizes in unrelated categories. [C026][C051][C047]

The clearest disagreement with the aggregate preference occurs in **0390**. Existing judging gives V2.5 the pair, with a focus margin of −1.167; the qualitative claims favor V2.6’s irreconcilable duty. V2.5’s harmonized external and emotional resolution can be valued as cohesive payoff, while V2.6’s preserved competing claim can be valued as greater ethical pressure. The latter is also quickly stipulated. Acknowledging this disagreement is more accurate than treating the preferred moral architecture as a settled overall verdict on the story. [C056][C057]

**Prompt dependence is substantial.** Hush, unusual occupations, damaged instruments, charged objects, and restorative missions are heavily represented. Those shared ingredients cannot independently establish narrow imagination. More diagnostic choices include optional naming, interpretive syntax, whether a method changes action, and whether a resolution erases resistance. [C016][C029][C034][C048]

**Surface cues can bias the comparison in both directions.** V2.6’s short declaratives, quantities, paperwork, and withheld conclusions can make underdevelopment look discriminating. V2.5’s flowing imagery and explicit closure can make repetition look complete. Technical authority is especially unsafe: both siren stories have weaknesses that their instrumentation does not repair, and both mystic stories relax their standards of proof. [C002][C018][C044][C058]

The supplied mean evaluator disagreement is 0.573 and mean AB/BA order sensitivity is 0.729. These are relevant cautions around preference measurement, not grounds to discard the large overall advantage. Nor do 50 short-fiction pairs establish unrestricted genre range, stable maximum capability, or general intelligence.

Finally, interpretations of mental-health material need particular restraint. V2.6 explicitly rejects treating medication cessation as automatic bravery, but both versions also associate medication with flattened experience. V2.5’s tentative ending deserves credit without endorsing the treatment narrative; V2.6’s qualification does not settle the entire portrayal. [C005][C062]

## Representative case studies

**0110 — Honesty with and without a transgression.**  
V2.6 makes the registrar listen despite an explicit prohibition: “I listened anyway.” Confession therefore risks dismissal. V2.5 makes eavesdropping an encounter with an old, silent conversation and supplies a benign explanation waiting on a monitor. The contrast locates the main difference in a structural decision, not sentence polish. It is strongest in this pair; V2.5’s risky whistleblowing in 0274 prevents universalizing it. [C009][C012]

**0372 — Preservation under scarcity.**  
V2.6 gives the archivist four hundred remaining slides and hours before the library melts. Frequency sampling differentiates vows, while a small circle beside forty cycles carries the permitted grief; that frequency later returns as the archivist’s own vow. V2.5 repeatedly certifies meticulous triumph without staging equivalent danger. This is both a low-floor example for V2.5 and a strong instance of V2.6 joining material procedure, emotional selection, and structural return—not evidence that every V2.6 archive story escapes formula. [C001][C065][C066][C003]

**0160 — A method that discovers rather than announces.**  
V2.6’s adhesive reading reconstructs physical traces, recovers an absent letter, and reveals a nine-inch offset that protects a compromise. Its final credit line is left for someone else to sign. V2.5’s claim to deduce emotional intent from adhesive skips a crucial connection and ends by calling the solution “a kind of love.” The useful contrast is between an inference that changes the available evidence and a narration that supplies its own conclusion. [C042][C040]

**0334 — The predecessor’s strongest conceptual counterexample.**  
V2.5’s apparent theft becomes deliberate memory sequestration in engineered roots: the investigator detects her own handiwork. V2.6 offers a more bodily register of recovered memory, but the favorable minority reading of V2.5 identifies a genuine redesign of setting, identity, and explanation. Its subsequent exposition limits the achievement without making the reversal decorative. [C026][C029][C068]

**0301 — A reversal in restraint.**  
V2.6 deserves credit for acknowledging medication’s supporting function rather than simply celebrating its cessation. Yet V2.5 gives the accordion a quieter task: opening and closing bellows, not performing a song. Its protagonist cannot forgive and can only “perhaps, begin” another form of self-authorship. The two readings are compatible once the dimensions are separated: stronger explicit qualification about medication need not mean the more restrained emotional ending. [C005][C062]

**0362 — The observer’s theory fails.**  
V2.5’s mystic encounters guilty relief where she expected grief, forcing revision of her categories without denying love. V2.6 gives its skeptic an incisive objection, then answers through intensified tragedy and an unexplained instrument reading. V2.5 is more psychologically searching here, while neither version establishes the proof it invokes. This is the strongest case against converting V2.6’s overall superiority into a categorical claim about perceptiveness. [C047][C058]

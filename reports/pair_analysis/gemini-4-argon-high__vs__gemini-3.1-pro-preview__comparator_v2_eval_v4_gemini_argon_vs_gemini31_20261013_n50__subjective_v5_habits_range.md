# Gemini 4 Argon (high) vs. Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview: comparative writing analysis

Focus model: `gemini-4-argon-high`. Comparison model: `gemini-3.1-pro-preview`. Direct-comparison scope: `comparator_v2_eval_v4_gemini_argon_vs_gemini31_20261013_n50`.

## In one paragraph

Given the same short-fiction prompts, Gemini 4 Argon (high) more often makes the requested details cause the story, while Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview more often uses them as symbols and explains the lesson afterward. In one coastal rescue, Argon combines an old painting, a turbine light, and salvaged wood into a navigation aid; Pro Preview summons spectral pilot-boats and tells us the operation works perfectly. The first feels like a problem being solved under pressure, the second like a ritual whose success is assured. [C059] Argon also more often gives help a human complication: a caregiver reveals his own grief, or a rescuer admits that he needs the person he is saving. [C025] It is the stronger writer overall here, not simply the more elaborate one. Both can make emotional recovery too tidy, treating a finished object as proof that an inner conflict is settled. [C068] Pro Preview occasionally resists that tidiness better: its underwater navigation story preserves captivity and uncertainty where Argon turns toward departure and hope. [C063]

## Comparative portrait

**Gemini 4 Argon (high) is the substantially stronger fiction writer in this material.** Its recurring advantage is not simply richer description: required details more often become reasons why events happen. An object’s construction, a character’s perceptual limitation, or a deadline determines the available solution. Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview more often supplies those same ingredients as symbols, ritual instruments, or explicitly labeled achievements. This distinction recurs across different stories in readings by all three critics; it is panel-supported, not dependent on one critic’s preference for technical fiction. [C001] [C008] [C013]

Argon also more often gives meaning a recipient. Negotiation requires reciprocal speech; helping someone can require the helper’s vulnerability; a marginal inscription must reach a particular, constrained consciousness. Pro Preview more often locates fulfillment in the protagonist’s recognition or in the narrator’s declaration that the intervention worked. The difference is consequential where the brief involves communication, persuasion, or care—not a rule that every solitary story needs another character. [C012] [C025] [C065]

The models nevertheless share a recognizable family resemblance: elevated explanatory narration, specialists handling fragile artifacts, revelations phrased as corrective contrasts, and movement toward release or consolation. Some matched passages are strikingly close. Argon usually constructs the middle of this familiar arc more effectively; it does not consistently escape the arc or its assurance that the world will validate the protagonist’s insight. [C043] [C046] [C052]

The fairest shorthand is therefore **stronger causal and relational construction within a substantially shared aesthetic**, not two wholly different imaginative sensibilities. Pro Preview retains local advantages in immediacy, emblematic simplicity, unusual premises, and occasionally unresolved endings, but those do not offset the recurring construction gap. [C006] [C045] [C058] [C063]

## Does either model seem smarter?

Argon more often creates that impression through identifiable writing decisions. This is a judgment about what the fiction makes a reader perceive, not a measurement of either model’s general intelligence.

- **Causal and structural foresight:** In the siren story, glue must cure before the gauge can supply decisive information, while the bell rope physically operates the switch. In the coastal rescue, an old painting, a turbine strobe, and repositioned timber establish a usable range line. These are preparations that constrain later action, not merely impressive explanations supplied afterward. [C019] [C059]
- **Constraint synthesis and conceptual compression:** A broken mask both signifies vulnerability and determines where portrait-making carbon falls. A mapper’s instrument becomes the possession he must damage to proceed. One detail performs practical and interpretive work simultaneously; the advantage is dependency, not complication for its own sake. [C024] [C041]
- **Psychological inference:** Meekness can be the residue of earlier bargains rather than an assigned personality adjective. Withholding a prophecy can transfer danger to another person rather than spare them. These choices distinguish an intention, a self-description, and an actual consequence. [C014] [C066]
- **Relevant-detail selection:** Argon’s borrowed memories include rotting lemons on Lisbon flagstones; its entangled dreams join a violinist’s vibration to a cartographer’s sea. Such particulars differentiate lives and participate in the story’s mechanism rather than merely increasing descriptive density. [C032]
- **Restraint:** In the servant story, care becomes the decision not to confront the master, but to leave the goggles where he can find them. This is restraint as conduct and respect for another person’s response—not shortness, sparse vocabulary, or an absence of emotion. [C048]

Reader trust is a less decisive separation. Argon more often provides evidence from which an inference can be reconstructed, but both narrators frequently finish the interpretation for the reader. Their corrective sentences and assurances of healing can make insight sound settled before the scene has earned that certainty. Argon’s familiar “empty void” reversals are a particularly clear limit. [C041] [C067] [C068]

The impression is also surface-cue-sensitive. Technical language, exact measurements, subordinate clauses, and orderly paragraphing can make invented rules appear more rigorous than they are. Argon’s pseudo-physics is not automatically sound physics, and its detailed backstory in 0140 raises a disputed bookkeeping problem. The supported advantage is strongest where changing an established condition would change the outcome; it is weaker where terminology merely redescribes the desired result. [C034] [C055] [C059]

## Narrative reasoning and aesthetic judgment

Argon’s best narrative reasoning makes an interpretation answer to something outside the protagonist’s confidence. In 0000, gradual decay defeats the expert’s prediction of catastrophe. In 0038, acoustic surveillance and a two-sided release mechanism make silent communication necessary. Discovery is therefore a revision or an exchange, rather than a narrator announcing that the character has understood. Pro Preview’s corresponding dance ends with a bow to an empty room, while its threatened alarms never materially alter the plan. [C029] [C013]

Argon also more often preserves a price inside success. The botanical diary can become the payment for a rescue; a navigation plan must endure a period without a response; the dancer’s cosmic freedom can coexist with permanent bodily anchoring. These are different kinds of pressure—loss, uncertainty, contradiction—not interchangeable measures of seriousness. Pro Preview more often removes the impediment once it has served the ritual, although that is a tendency rather than a universal rule. [C036] [C027]

Neither writer consistently demonstrates equally strong **ethical judgment**. Both can translate a moral disagreement into a material operation and then certify the resulting agreement. In the asylum story, chemical influence is treated as compatible with a freely chosen, ethically pure exchange. Argon’s more elaborate distinction between receptivity and obedience shows awareness of the problem without resolving it. Its greater compositional control should not be mistaken for a blanket advantage in moral maturity. [C009] [C068]

Nor is a mechanism always aesthetically preferable to an emblem. Pro Preview’s moth-and-monarch memorial allows two creatures to share an equal final journey; Argon’s more functional design makes the forgotten moth useful to the celebrated butterfly. That is a legitimate ethical reservation about the better-engineered version. Likewise, Pro Preview’s underwater navigation story preserves captivity and uncertain endurance where Argon moves toward liberation. In those cases, less comprehensive resolution protects something valuable. [C045] [C063]

The aesthetic distinction is thus narrower than “showing beats telling” in every circumstance. Argon usually earns more of its interpretation through preparation, action, and consequence. Both still risk replacing ambiguity with an authoritative account of what an artifact, a loss, or an act of care must mean. [C043] [C068]

## Recurring and repetitive writing habits

**Counting rule.** The fractions below refer only to explicitly checked story sets. Rereadings of the same writer’s same story are corroboration, not additional occurrences. For the other patterns, the supporting examples are identified, but full-cohort frequency was not measured. Repeated required objects or settings are not, by themselves, evidence of an independently chosen habit.

### Pro Preview: labeled entry, certified exit

**Role-and-motivation introductions.** Pro Preview repeatedly introduces a character through an occupational label, then states the motivation as a thesis. Across different prompts, the historian is introduced with “By trade,” the siren tester with “He was a humble storm siren tester,” and the gardener with “He was a creeping normality noticer.” “His true desire was to favor listening over noise” similarly exposes the brief’s instruction beneath the narration. Argon also labels roles, but sometimes qualifies them or lets their later implications change rather than simply repeating the label. [C020] [C023]

**Subject-first lists and even paragraph rhythm.** In 0213, “He noted,” “He measured,” and “He watched” begin successive observations. Elsewhere the same declarative unit appears as a separate line, making discovery feel like a sequence of completed entries. This is not an invariant visual format: Pro Preview also writes continuous blocks and flowing paragraphs. The stronger habit is the repeated sentence procedure; lineation may exaggerate its effect and could partly reflect presentation rather than deliberate form. [C022]

**Evaluative intensification.** “Perfectly,” “flawless,” “profound,” and related assurances repeatedly grade the action for the reader. In the checked read_03 set, perfect/perfectly/flawless occurs in **4/5** Pro Preview stories—0038, 0152, 0220, and 0364—with 0209 an examined nonmatch. “The directions proved flawless” in 0220 and “It has done its job perfectly” in 0296 show the device operating across different prompts. The effect is not just ornate diction: uncertainty disappears into a certificate of competence. Yet 0369 suppresses much of this inflation in favor of quiet maintenance, demonstrating that the default can loosen. [C016] [C036] [C037]

**Closing recitals.** In a separate checked set—0000, 0032, 0051, 0140, and 0351—all **5/5** Pro Preview final paragraphs repeat required phrases, often several together. Argon repeats a single brief phrase in three of those endings and none in the other two. Pro Preview also returns to “testament to” in 0000 and 0051. Its endings can consequently sound like an audit of fulfilled requirements rather than the last change in a particular story. [C030]

These are related but distinct habits: role labeling establishes the template, list syntax regulates its movement, intensifiers guarantee its efficacy, and the closing recital announces completion. Not every occurrence is weak, but their combination makes otherwise different premises feel unusually interchangeable. [C020] [C022] [C030] [C037]

### Argon: explanatory rules and an obstacle staircase

**Rule-building syntax.** Argon repeatedly uses explanatory clauses to turn an object into a process. The volcanic-glass condition in 0005 establishes how the sealing operation will work; the observed leaf behavior in 0132 supplies evidence against the protagonist’s rigid method. This is a flexible signature when the rule differs meaningfully with the brief. It becomes over-engineering when the explanation supplies authority without adding a genuine limitation. [C055]

**Precision words and staged insufficiency.** “Exact” recurs as a timing or alignment word: “exact microsecond” in 0177 and “exact point” in 0208. Sentence-initial “Yet” and “However” repeatedly announce that the preceding step is insufficient. In the checked read_09 set, this obstacle-escalation pivot appears in **4/5** Argon stories—0131, 0340, 0359, and 0370—with 0229 an examined nonmatch. “Yet the celestial light and the crystal chimes needed an earthly anchor” and “Yet hiding behind a pillar” both force a further step. The device creates momentum, but also a predictable staircase: establish rule, reject easy solution, add condition, synchronize climax. [C050] [C061]

**Converted witnesses.** Corvus falling silent in 0229 and Vane stamping preservation approval in 0359 make a skeptical authority’s changed behavior certify success. This is more concrete than a whole world suddenly healed, but it is still a reusable closing device. “Bruised/bruising,” noted in 0131 and 0229, is better treated as a candidate pet descriptor than an established broad lexical signature. [C050]

### Shared cast, shared revelation machinery

**A small name drawer.** Argon repeats Silas across 0002 and 0088 and Julian across 0088, 0113, and 0114. Pro Preview repeats Elias across those readings and many other unrelated roles, including 0131, 0229, 0340, and 0370. The observable effect is accidental continuity; the evidence does not establish an intended shared world or explain the source of the naming preference. [C003] [C049]

**Reusable relationships and backstories.** Argon’s brother–sister emotional core is especially clear in the checked read_03 set: **3/5** stories use it—0038’s rescue, 0209’s memorial, and 0220’s separation. The examined nonmatches are 0152 and 0364. This is a real constraint on its emotional variety, not merely recycled names. Pro Preview varies the kinship roles in that set, although it too uses a dead or missing relative to motivate a coded object. Argon’s warmth sometimes comes from a familiar relationship template rather than newly differentiated social observation. [C015]

**Corrective antithesis.** Both repeatedly move from an inadequate category to an approved interpretation: `not … but …`, `no longer …`, or a closely related reversal. Argon’s compass is “not to conquer the storm, but to collaborate with the wind”; Pro Preview’s musician is “no longer just an orchestra pit musician” but “a bridge to the past.” The same syntax can sharpen a real distinction or merely announce elevation. [C004]

A particularly revealing cross-story match belongs to Argon: 0137 turns silence from an “empty void” into a “wide, peaceful chamber”; 0359 again begins with a first-time threshold and turns an “empty void” into a “sheltering vessel.” The therapeutic distinction is useful in context, but the closely repeated architecture makes different people’s realizations sound prewritten. Pro Preview’s portable lost/found romance ending demonstrates the corresponding risk in its own prose. [C067] [C026]

**Shared images and gestures.** Both use nearly identical “Deep within the glacial heart” openings for 0359 and “almost violent” affection in 0229. Quiet smiles also recur: Argon’s 0364 and Pro Preview’s 0038 both use “smiled softly.” These similarities establish a common phrase bank more securely than distinct imagery signatures. Because some occur under the same prompt, they should not be counted as separate cross-story recurrences for either writer. [C052] [C017]

**Release plots and threshold endings.** Both return to the specialist whose rigid practice softens, the material arrangement that authorizes healing, and the ending that makes someone ready to begin. Argon’s 0002 and 0088 readiness closes point toward future conduct; Pro Preview’s 0002 and 0089 step into mystery or eternity. Pro Preview additionally repeats the literal closing materials “melted into slag” and “purpose fulfilled” across 0005 and 0177. Both use “finally” to certify relief in otherwise different conflicts. [C031] [C043] [C007] [C060] [C010]

These are defaults, not universal templates. Pro Preview’s waiting under blockade and Argon’s irreversible bodily dissolution change what completion can mean. Such counterexamples matter more than an unmeasured assertion that either writer “always” heals everything. [C045]

**Across the two model versions**, corrective syntax, stock names, explicit motivation labels, and affirmative closure persist. Checklist recitation is less dominant in Argon, while staged rule-building is more prominent. That is a supported comparative description, not evidence about training causes or proof that Argon invented a new lineage-wide habit. [C030] [C049] [C050] [C061]

## Range and adaptability

Argon demonstrates the wider range of **story-making procedures**, but not a comparably large advantage in distinct narratorial voices. Its most informative contrasts are not lists of genres: they concern what counts as evidence, what action can accomplish, and how characters must respond. Both writers usually retain an elevated, explanatory narrator and substantially linear revelation. [C046] [C070]

### Argon’s demonstrated departures

The clearest modal contrast runs from ordinary observation to literal cosmology. In 0132, storm damage and plant behavior change a horticultural practice without magic. In 0208, historical geometry and modern infrastructure produce a navigation solution. In 0217, by contrast, dawn is literally woven and damage to its fabric becomes the problem. In 0177, the endpoint is visionary bodily dissolution into a forest. These are changes in causal rules and kinds of action, not merely new scenery. [C057] [C059] [C062]

Argon also changes the **social organization** of the problem. The political letter in 0008 must survive an opponent’s threatened destruction; 0371 stages absolution between a gardener and someone implicated in civic loss; 0137 uses a physician’s self-disclosure; 0182 turns rescue into contractual exchange. Caregiver/patient, artisan colleagues, master/apprentice, and practitioner/community arrangements give required traits different channels of expression. This is demonstrated adaptability, although convenient clauses and swift agreement can make the differences easier to resolve than they should be. [C021] [C056] [C070]

Its tonal departures are real but bounded. The expansive action choreography of 0104 contrasts with the abrasive affectionate comedy of 0252, where “a mother hen with a cutlass she cannot even swing” simultaneously expresses intimacy and the setting’s disabled violence. The “four pound Tuesday” of 0369 offers a quieter comic compression. Meanwhile 0229’s overheated care and 0340’s cool non-intervention show deliberate changes within the elevated register. The former may be overwrought; the comedy is a successful local departure, not proof of a broad comic repertoire. [C028] [C039] [C051]

Argon is not exclusively third-person: 0351 supplies a first-person posthuman narrator. Nevertheless, that change primarily alters framing rather than establishing a radically different idiom. Its range is more securely demonstrated in mechanisms, relationships, and stakes than in narrator personality. [C033] [C046]

### Pro Preview’s demonstrated departures

Pro Preview has genuine formal and emotional variety that a simple “one template” verdict would erase. Its first-person 0088 begins with an itching bandage and establishes a bodily, confessional immediacy distinct from its ritual third-person narration. The first-person port story, 0140, organizes identity through cyclical gathering and layering rather than a single quest. Present-tense 0182 advances through deciphered absence, while 0114 holds its protagonist in deliberate tragic stasis. Those are meaningful differences in temporal posture and pacing, even when the explanatory idiom persists. [C006] [C069]

Its changes of scale also matter. The private memorial of 0175 does not attempt to reverse extinction; 0364 attempts collective political uprising; 0323 makes achievement the preservation of knowledge under unresolved confinement. These are not interchangeable outcomes. They qualify packet-local observations of universal triumph and show that Pro Preview can retain uncertainty when it commits to doing so. [C018] [C045]

Pro Preview sometimes takes the stranger conceptual option. The leaf fused into a man’s veins in 0132 is a more uncanny premise than Argon’s empirical horticulture. The android servant in 0340 reinterprets affectionate detachment through artificial personhood and succession. Neither automatically succeeds as a whole story: the leaf’s implications return quickly to serene acceptance, and a distinctive premise does not necessarily produce a distinctive voice. But these are actual departures, not compensating possibilities invented on its behalf. [C057] [C058] [C051]

It can also change local rhythm and pressure. The percussion sequence “Tap, pause, double tap, drag” performs an action instead of explaining it; siege and seed-delivery passages introduce a deadline and practical movement. Sentence-per-line ritual narration differs visibly from continuous confession. The limitation is that these changes often return to the same admiring, certainty-heavy voice rather than sustaining a newly fitted register. [C011] [C024] [C028] [C006]

### What the range comparison supports

Argon more often changes technique to fit a brief; Pro Preview more often changes situation while preserving its interpretive procedure. But choosing fantasy where Argon chooses realism is not itself a failure: the coastal prompt allowed a supernatural interpretation. The narrower criticism is that Pro Preview’s different modes repeatedly converge on effortless efficacy and explicit certification. [C039] [C062]

Neither writer establishes broad command of vernacular social idiom, extended ensemble drama, radical formal experiment, or sustained comedy. Argon’s brief realist and comic successes should be credited, not expanded into untested capacities. Pro Preview’s several first-person stories should likewise be credited without equating pronoun changes with fully differentiated consciousnesses. [C028] [C046] [C070]

Endings are more varied than the shared diction suggests: diminished victory, prospective departure, permanent anchoring, memorial equality, patient endurance, and catastrophe all appear. This is range in what action preserves or costs; it is separate from whether the execution reaches a high floor. [C007] [C018] [C027] [C045] [C069]

## Floor versus ceiling

The **supplied quantitative context** strongly favors Argon: across 50 matched story pairs, the reported story-level classification is 50 focus wins, no ties, and no comparison wins at the ±0.5 threshold. The mean focus-minus-comparison margin is +2.807, with an approximate story-level normal 95% interval half-width of 0.146; the median is +2.800. These are summaries of the available 294 judgments, not claims that every individual judgment agreed.

The prose evidence supports a higher causal floor: Argon repeatedly connects setup to payoff, while Pro Preview’s failure-prone cases include communication without a recipient, threats that evaporate, success guaranteed by intensifiers, and a rescue brief ending before rescue is enacted. The latter can be defended as a threshold ending, but the promised action remains prospective. [C013] [C037] [C070]

Argon’s floor is not error-free. Its explanations can announce a resolved paradox rather than establish one; temporal machinery can evade a limitation by redescribing it; specificity can outrun bookkeeping. Its solution-seeking tendency can also weaken a somber brief by turning endurance into imminent departure. These are distinct weaknesses—logical, interpretive, and tonal—not merely “too many words.” [C034] [C041] [C042] [C063]

Its unusually strong performances combine several strengths: 0114 joins a perceptual channel to irreversible relational consequence; 0208 makes discovery operationally traceable; 0252 changes the sound and behavior of intimacy rather than simply changing its setting. These provide evidence of a high demonstrated ceiling within the tested material. [C002] [C065] [C059] [C028]

There is no need to manufacture a compensating higher ceiling for Pro Preview. Its strongest local qualities—0088’s bodily immediacy, 0175’s equal memorial regard, and 0323’s unresolved patience—are genuine achievements, but they coexist with the aggregate and recurring construction disadvantage. [C006] [C045] [C063]

Length does not offer a simple explanation. The supplied means are 756.8 words for Argon and 743.1 for Pro Preview, a difference of 13.7; the correlation between word-count difference and margin is +0.072. That descriptive association does not isolate causation, but neither does it support treating the result as merely a preference for longer stories.

## What each model still does better

**Argon’s durable advantage is making details answer to one another.** It more reliably joins object, method, character limitation, and consequence; it also more often lets another person’s needs determine what the protagonist must do. Its best restraint consists in preserving a cost or choosing an indirect act of care, rather than explaining less by default. [C024] [C041] [C048] [C066]

**Pro Preview’s advantages are more local and should remain local:**

- It can provide more immediate bodily access and cleaner emblematic phrasing, particularly in the first-person 0088 and the compressed backward-music image of 0122. Economy helps there; elsewhere the same economy leaves a causal gap. [C006] [C058]
- It sometimes finds the bolder premise: involuntary leaf-fusion in 0132 or the android reading of the servant in 0340. These challenge an account in which Argon is always the more imaginative writer. [C057] [C051]
- It occasionally completes a structural gesture more neatly. The guard coda in 0152 re-enacts the protagonist’s performed foolishness; 0209’s perpetual October glow reconciles setting and season more elegantly than Argon’s calendar movement. [C014] [C013]
- It can protect a value Argon’s instrumental construction narrows: equal remembrance in 0175, or unresolved somber endurance and direct object–method fusion in 0323. [C045] [C063]

These findings modify the portrait without reversing it. They are supported exceptions and minority preferences on particular dimensions, not evidence of an equal overall performance or an unexplored superior ceiling.

## Findings beyond the existing judging

**Corroborated, not new:** The central integration advantage, the importance of specific recipients, and Argon’s tendency to enact rather than merely label a required trait were already prominent in the existing judging. Cross-read claims about the coastal rescue and the oracle strengthen those interpretations, but rereading the same stories does not create new instances of the habit. [C059] [C065] [C066]

**New cross-story visibility:** Pairwise judgments cannot by themselves reveal that Pro Preview’s spent materials repeatedly “melted into slag” with their “purpose fulfilled,” or that two Argon endings convert an “empty void” into a habitable mental container through closely matching syntax. These findings identify reusable verbal machinery beneath stories that otherwise differ considerably. They are more diagnostic of repetition than the shared presence of a required magical object. [C060] [C067]

**A shared ethical shortcut becomes clearer in synthesis:** Both writers often move from material arrangement to emotional warranty. A diagram produces lucidity; divided columns produce psychological separation; accumulated fragments produce wholeness. The asymmetry is not simply magic versus reasoned therapy: both can make the transition too automatic. The asylum pair sharpens the concern because the stories certify free, fair agreement while manipulating receptivity. This qualifies the more favorable existing interpretation of Argon’s intervention rather than merely repeating the original preference. [C068] [C009]

**Mechanistic usefulness can narrow the value being defended.** Argon’s moth serves the monarch, whereas Pro Preview permits equality without that service. Argon’s defense of wonder succeeds by demonstrating restorative utility to an official. These are supported minority readings of what effective construction may cost aesthetically or ethically; they are not proof that instrumental plotting is generally inferior. [C045] [C065]

The most productive additional distinction is therefore between **making an abstraction observable** and **allowing it to remain contestable**. Argon is repeatedly better at the first. Neither consistently excels at the second, although Pro Preview’s patient or memorial endings supply important counterexamples. [C043] [C045] [C068]

## Disagreements and limitations

**Mythic convention remains a serious alternative reading.** A ritual key need not work like an engineering instrument, and a private consolation need not become social action. Pro Preview’s simpler constructions sometimes achieve appropriate emblematic clarity. The comparative objection is strongest when the story promises an inference, a negotiation, or a difficult procedure and substitutes an assertion at the decisive point—not whenever magic lacks a physical explanation. [C001] [C012] [C041]

**Some local judgments remain contested.** One critic identifies incompatible origins for Argon’s erroneous death certificate in 0140: a wrong digit, swapped clipboards, and a wrong file. Existing judging instead reads these details as a coherent chain. This synthesis does not settle that dispute through a fresh reading; it records a meaningful challenge to Argon’s bookkeeping. Similarly, a preference for Pro Preview’s bolder 0132 premise or somber 0323 ending can be well supported without implying that either story wins under every criterion. [C034] [C058] [C063]

**Surface cues can inflate both preference and apparent range.** Argon’s precision words and technical clauses can simulate rigor; Pro Preview’s lineation can look either incantatory or mechanically fragmented. Pronouns, tense, and paragraph layout are evidence of formal variation, but insufficient evidence of a different character idiom. The procedural advantages in 0208 and 0132 survive this skepticism better than some of the invented-physics examples. [C022] [C046] [C055] [C059]

**The prompts constrain the observed repertoire.** They repeatedly invite unusual specialists, symbolic objects, explicit motivations, and contemplative speculative tones. Those shared ingredients cannot establish independent habits. Optional corrective syntax, repeated names, repeated wording across unrelated stories, and recurring choices about who may interpret or resist are stronger evidence. Absence from a critic’s discussion is not a checked absence. [C003] [C026] [C043] [C061]

**There is one cohort, not an original-plus-extension test.** The two quantitative provenance sources do not create an extension cohort. The supplied cross-readings revisit existing stories, so their value is corroboration or a new interpretation of the same text, not replication on newly generated fiction. Recurrence counts remain writer-and-story deduplicated.

Finally, the quantitative result has explicit coverage limits: six matched prompt pairs have fewer than two observed orders per evaluator. The normal interval summarizes approximate sampling variability; it does not bound missing judgments or establish a definitive universal winner. Supplied mean evaluator disagreement is 0.403 and mean AB/BA order sensitivity is 0.380. The lopsided observed result is clear, but it should not erase local disagreement, imply complete unanimity, or establish superiority in untested forms.

## Representative case studies

**0208 — A rescue whose solution can be followed.** Argon asks the endangered vessel to align a white flare with Turbine Seven’s red pulse. The old painting provides geometry; salvaged timber provides the lower reference. The plan then has to survive three minutes without an answer. Pro Preview instead awakens spectral pilot-boats and says the protagonist manages the operation perfectly. The fantasy choice is legitimate; the revealing contrast is the presence or absence of an intervening, consequential procedure. This is a central finding corroborated by a second reading, not two recurrence instances. [C059] [C036]

**0114 — A margin becomes an address.** Argon’s peripheral perception determines where Julian can leave readings and affection that Mara’s central targeting will not destroy. The communication channel then carries a reversal: withholding the prophecy has transferred danger rather than removed it. Pro Preview uses margins chiefly as solitary comfort and sustains catastrophe through private knowledge. Both can be tragic; Argon’s stronger achievement is making the tragedy depend on what one person can communicate to another. [C002] [C065] [C066]

**0132 — Better execution, less imaginative risk.** Argon makes bound branches breaking and flexible growth surviving into evidence that changes the gardener’s practice. Elena’s understanding of his circuitous sincerity adds a relational consequence. Pro Preview literalizes metamorphosis with a leaf fused into the protagonist’s veins—a more uncanny premise, acknowledged as the bolder risk. Its limited development and return to acceptance constrain that advantage. The comparison separates observational control from imaginative daring rather than treating either as a synonym for quality. [C057] [C056] [C058]

**0252 — A genuine change of emotional and comic register.** Argon’s lovers insult one another while accepting physical closeness, and the apparent rescuer admits that he wants to accompany the person he is freeing. The joke and confession change the distribution of need. Pro Preview maintains earnest romantic urgency, but its narration interprets a spoken refusal as cowardice on the rescuer’s behalf. Argon’s version is neither free of compulsion nor proof of unrestricted comic range; it is a successful local adaptation that makes mutuality audible. [C028] [C025]

**0139 — Construction does not settle consent.** Argon gives the exchange a more articulated account of fairness, but both versions use a substance to soften another person’s judgment while endorsing the resulting agreement. Pro Preview even protects its protagonist’s own strict moral code from exposure. The problem is not that desperate rescue uses compromised means; it is that the ending declares the account settled. This case limits any broad claim that Argon’s causal sophistication entails superior ethical judgment. [C009] [C010]

**0323 — Pro Preview’s meaningful reversal.** Pro Preview makes the chain link the actual measuring instrument and ends with teacher and pupil preserving knowledge while waiting under blockade. Argon assigns the link a grounding, talismanic role and projects toward teaching departure. The existing aggregate preference remains with Argon, but the minority reading identifies two specific Pro Preview advantages: closer object–method fusion and greater fidelity to unresolved somberness. It is the clearest warning against equating more complete problem-solving with the right ending for every brief. [C063] [C045]

## Cited claim ledger

- **C001 — Epistemic fit between device, perception, and plot:** Gemini 4 Argon (high) gives objects and methods an operative logic: they select what can be known, impose constraints, and produce the solution.

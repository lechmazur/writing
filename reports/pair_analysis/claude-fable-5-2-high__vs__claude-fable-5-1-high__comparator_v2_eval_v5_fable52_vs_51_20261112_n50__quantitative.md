## Sample and evidence

This dedicated comparison retains the original 20 matched prompts and 120 judgments, then adds 30 unused shared prompts sampled uniformly with seed 2026111202 without consulting outcomes. The two cohorts are disjoint parts of one 50-prompt sample, not independent replications. Three eligible non-Claude judge families were scheduled per prompt in both orders. All 300 planned judgments are usable: two complete machine-score responses required only the existing delimiter-only correction, and one incomplete non-refusal Muse response received one successful repair. Every original response and failure remains archived. The additional 30 prompts are not automatically pooled into the global public leaderboard.

Of the 50 Fable 5.2 stories here, 8 came from its previously disclosed refusal-recovery rounds. Each is the first successful story for that prompt; the original refusals were preserved.

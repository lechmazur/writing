# DeepSeek V4 Pro (high) vs. DeepSeek V4 Pro Preview: comparative writing analysis

Focus model: `deepseek-v4-pro-high`. Comparison model: `deepseek-v4-pro`. Direct-comparison scope: `comparator_v2_eval_v2_v3_predecessor_depth_n50`.

## In one paragraph

When given the same short-fiction brief, DeepSeek V4 Pro High usually turns an odd requirement into something characters must actually operate, test, or work around, while DeepSeek V4 Pro Preview more often turns it into a radiant symbol and then explains what it means. In one telling contrast, High makes “waiting actively” consist of three mornings learning a guard’s coffee break before a timed breach; the action grows directly from observation rather than from declared resolve [C087]. That difference carries into endings: High more often lets guilt, grief, law, or damaged relationships remain after the local breakthrough, whereas Preview more often rewards revelation with harmony or completion [C018]. High is plainly the stronger and more dependable fiction writer here because its conflicts more often arise from verifiable clues, operating rules, and other people’s resistance [C043]. Preview’s clearest advantage is on the sensory surface: it produces denser metaphors, more spectacular images, and compact invented artifacts that can make a world feel immediately larger [C011]. The qualification is real but narrow—Preview can be more visually memorable, while High more consistently makes the story’s parts earn one another.

## Quantitative context

Across 50 matched prompts, the focus model recorded 44 wins, 4 ties, and 2 losses at the ±0.5 tie threshold. Its mean signed margin was +1.850 ± 0.286 (95% normal half-width).

Mean lengths were 693.8 and 737.6 words; the correlation between length difference and margin was +0.232.

## Comparative portrait

DeepSeek V4 Pro High’s characteristic move is to turn a required object, abstraction, or method into a constraint that generates the next event: defects obstruct use, observations become tests, and artifacts acquire several practical jobs. DeepSeek V4 Pro Preview more often turns the same material into an emblematic or ceremonial system, emphasizing accumulated significance, sensory correspondence, and revelation. In the original cohort, this appeared as obstacle-first construction, economical reuse of constraints, and endings that preserve residue rather than grant total release [C014] [C018] [C024]. Preview’s corresponding strengths were imagistic amplitude, sacramental object histories, and larger symbolic or civic systems [C003] [C005] [C011].

**The central portrait replicates in the extension cohort.** High again makes prompt requirements operational: objects function as reagents or evidence, awkward phrases become surveillance or technique, and established rules produce consequences [C040] [C087]. Preview again favors objects with intrinsic power, culturally suggestive artifacts, and sensory procedures that make an abstraction physically felt [C050] [C082] [C090]. The material change is not a reversal but an expansion of High’s portrait: the extension shows more institutional, professional, and civic reasoning than the original alone established, especially around verification, treatment boundaries, and the infrastructure required for agency [C043] [C048] [C075].

The ending distinction also survives. High more often limits what a breakthrough can repair: grief, guilt, legal structure, or public trouble remains after the local success [C018] [C047] [C088]. Preview more often lets revelation harmonize or transform the governing system, though important exceptions prevent this from becoming a rule [C021] [C023]. Across all 50 pairs, the quantitative result is correspondingly lopsided: High records 44 wins, four ties, and two Preview wins, with a mean margin of +1.850 and median of +2.175.

## Does either model seem smarter?

As a **reader impression manifested through writing choices**, DeepSeek V4 Pro High seems more consistently perceptive and controlled. This is not a measurement of general intelligence. The impression comes from separating hypothesis from proof, making details alter later possibilities, preserving incompatible feelings, and declining to let insight erase consequence [C001] [C022] [C025]. It is therefore strongest in causal foresight, epistemic calibration, psychological inference, constraint synthesis, and relevant-detail selection—not in vocabulary, length, or stylistic sheen [C016] [C024].

**This conclusion clearly replicates in the extension.** High’s characters verify correspondences on the page, revise an initial conceptual model after it fails, preserve borders between compassion and pardon, and keep agency meaningful even inside foreknowledge [C043] [C076] [C088] [C091]. These are different manifestations of the same earlier tendency: claims acquire brakes, distinctions survive epiphany, and conclusions remain narrower than the story’s imagery might permit [C001] [C002].

Preview nevertheless shows genuine, sometimes superior forms of intelligence. In the original cohort it imagined communication as distributed civic infrastructure and, in one story, built the strongest closed causal loop in its set [C003] [C023]. In the extension it excels when perception itself performs the required method or when a protagonist reverse-engineers a system’s classification rules [C050] [C077]. Those are substantive strengths, not compensation through ornament. They are simply less consistent across stories than High’s causal and selective judgment.

The stability statistics support that distinction. The original cohort favored High 19/1/0 with a +2.020 mean margin; the extension still favored High 25/3/2 with a +1.736 mean. The extension therefore lowers the margin and introduces two genuine reversals, but it does not overturn the conclusion. High was also shorter on average in both cohorts, and the overall correlation between word-count difference and margin was only +0.232. The result cannot reasonably be reduced to greater verbosity.

## Narrative reasoning and aesthetic judgment

**Causal and structural foresight.** High usually establishes a limitation before exploiting or revising it, and more often cross-wires supplied elements so that one requirement provides the physical or informational basis of another [C014] [C080]. Its strongest procedures remain inspectable when the short-line formatting is ignored: observations, actions, and outcomes form discrete links rather than being joined chiefly by lyrical momentum [C013]. Preview can equal or exceed this in isolated mechanisms, but it more often authorizes an object or revelation in advance and lets the narration certify the result [C008] [C031].

**Psychological and social inference.** High more often gives protagonists complicity, lets interlocutors redirect the task, and models another person’s contrary desire rather than using secondary characters as prompts for exposition [C007] [C017] [C056]. Its therapeutic fiction is particularly alert to the danger of making another person’s response serve the protagonist’s symbolic cure [C075]. Preview can sustain ambivalence and cost, but it more often organizes relationships around consolation, ratification, or revelation [C083].

**Conceptual compression and revision.** High’s better aphorisms emerge after a procedure or distinction has been enacted; its concepts can change under pressure rather than merely recur in polished formulations [C041] [C076]. Preview’s best conceptual writing can build a genuine argument or rank one invention as the crucial one, so its explicitness should not automatically be dismissed as empty declaration [C059] [C084]. Those are minority findings, however, not a stable reversal of the broader pattern.

**Relevant-detail selection and constraint synthesis.** High more often chooses details that constrain action, join several prompt elements, or install an error-correction path [C024] [C080] [C089]. Preview more often supplies culturally specific texture and vivid prior history; at its best, those details make an artifact feel inherited rather than generic [C065] [C090]. The aesthetic trade-off is real: High’s economy can become thinness, while Preview’s abundance can become atmospheric inventory.

**Restraint and reader trust.** High usually earns trust by refusing to award more than the events support, not merely by writing quietly [C018] [C041]. Preview’s richer figurative embodiment can make a scene more immediately affecting, but its explanatory codas often narrow interpretation after the image has already succeeded [C052] [C058]. Both models still self-caption frequently, so this is a difference of sequencing and degree, not a clean show-versus-tell divide [C026] [C060].

**Extension result.** All six distinctions recur in the extension. The material qualifications are that Preview gains credible examples of protocol hacking, irreversible cost, and sustained argument, while High sometimes engineers away dilemmas or specifies mechanisms that contain their own contradictions [C046] [C061] [C077]. The extension thus narrows any absolute claim about capability while reinforcing the consistency claim.

## Range, recurring habits, and floor versus ceiling

The clearest quantitative fact is High’s floor: it wins or ties 48 of 50 pairs at the stated threshold. The original result, 19/1/0, was unusually clean; the extension’s 25/3/2 is more variable but still strongly aligned. Its lower mean margin (+1.736 versus +2.020) and wider confidence half-width (0.443 versus 0.263) show some attenuation, not collapse. The extension also has much higher order sensitivity, 1.694 versus 0.480, even though evaluator disagreement falls from 0.833 to 0.739. Fine-grained rankings in the extension should therefore be treated more cautiously than the overall direction.

High’s dependable range lies in procedural fable, institutional conflict, ethical remainder, and intimate speculative drama. Its recurring risks are schematic protagonists, a fixed short-declarative cadence, underdeveloped worlds, and restraint that is merely smallness [C013] [C018]. Preview’s dependable range lies in lyrical fantasy, sensory embodiment, invented artifacts, ceremony, and large-scale resonance [C011] [C050] [C090]. Its recurring risks are over-certifying meaning, piling up nonfunctional systems, and allowing a revelation or enchanted object to bypass conflict [C008] [C041].

Neither model demonstrates broad formal range. Both repeatedly use linear revelation plots, inward conversions, antithetical reframes, and explicit thematic codas [C060] [C079]. High shows more functional deadpan and occasional comic structure, including in the extension, but that establishes tonal modulation rather than a general strength in comedy [C020] [C045] [C071]. Preview’s register varies more with the assigned tone, while High’s sentence architecture remains comparatively stable; whether that is adaptivity or inconsistency is prompt-dependent [C086].

The evidence does **not** justify inventing a special higher ceiling for Preview. It has isolated superior constructions and owns the two extension wins, but the available readings identify peaks in particular kinds of system design, imagery, or irreversible choice rather than a reliably higher artistic maximum [C023] [C046] [C077]. Conversely, High’s consistency should not be mistaken for universal mastery: its weakest stories can become frictionless, tidy, or visibly scaffolded [C014] [C061].

## What each model still does better

**DeepSeek V4 Pro High does better, more consistently:**

- It converts requirements into causal procedures and makes one supplied element perform several jobs [C024] [C087].
- It distinguishes anomaly, hypothesis, test, and conclusion rather than letting intuition become certainty by narrative fiat [C001] [C043].
- It preserves shared fault, incomplete forgiveness, and consequences that survive self-understanding [C022] [C035] [C088].
- It gives other people, institutions, and material conditions enough resistance to redirect the protagonist [C042] [C056].
- It calibrates climaxes and endings to the actual change achieved, often installing continued work or possible failure [C047] [C089].

**DeepSeek V4 Pro Preview still does better in narrower but real areas:**

- It produces denser sensory imagery and more frequent figurative embodiment at sentence level [C011] [C037] [C058].
- It rapidly mints artifacts, institutions, and miniature cultural histories that imply a world beyond the immediate plot [C065] [C090].
- It can imagine distributed communication systems and institutional protocol hacks with unusual ingenuity [C003] [C077].
- It sometimes preserves mystery more effectively than High’s functionalism, which can explain away the uncanny [C073].
- In isolated stories, it more decisively ranks the best idea, imposes irreversible cost, or closes a large causal loop [C023] [C046] [C084].

These Preview advantages qualify the portrait but do not balance the overall result. The extension confirms that Preview can do most of the structural work attributed to High; it does not show that Preview does so with comparable regularity.

## Findings beyond the existing judging

**Recurring and corroborated: object grammar.** The original readings distinguished High’s tools and mutable records from Preview’s sacraments and communal relics [C005]. The extension restates the distinction more sharply as index versus battery: High’s objects point, measure, or provide evidence, while Preview’s more often contain power or operate as interfaces [C078] [C082]. This is a recurring cross-cohort finding, not a verdict about which metaphysics is inherently better.

**Recurring, with a material cohort change: social infrastructure.** The original cohort gave Preview the clearer advantage in distributed civic signaling [C003]. The extension gives High several strong cases in which agency requires channels, timing, testimony, collaborators, or institutional legibility [C048]. Social intelligence is therefore prompt-dependent: Preview is especially good at imagining networks as collective symbolism, while High more often specifies the material route from intention to consequence.

**Corroborated: where resistance lives.** The original portrait contrasted Preview’s visible antagonists with High’s recalcitrant evidence and material refusal [C004]. The extension broadens High’s side of that distinction by repeatedly letting bureaucracies, employers, tribunals, or unresolved verdicts say no [C042]. This makes the difference less “people versus reality” than “dramatic interruption versus persistent constraint.”

**New in the extension: value without recognition.** High repeatedly allows care, art, or restitution to matter without possession, fame, or public acknowledgment [C049]. Related extension stories deposit meaning back into graves, texts, routes, or practices that can outlast the protagonist [C092]. This strengthens the earlier remainder portrait but adds a distinct ethics of nonownership.

**New in the extension: reception as action.** High more often shows what recipients do, misread, or only partly change when a signal reaches them; Preview is more likely to present a resonant roster of distant figures [C064]. This is a useful measure of consequence imagination, though the supporting observation remains limited.

**Surface-cue-sensitive and speculative.** High’s one-sentence-per-line layout makes causality unusually easy to audit, but the format may itself manufacture an impression of control [C013]. Preview’s proper nouns, technical language, and image density can likewise manufacture an impression of depth or world knowledge [C030] [C090]. These cues support descriptive stylistic claims, not conclusions about general capability.

## Disagreements and limitations

The central finding is panel-supported and cross-cohort stable, but several minority readings must remain visible. One original story gave Preview the strongest closed-loop causal system in its set [C023]. One extension story let Preview make truth-telling destroy the oasis while High preserved both truth and orchard, reversing the usual association between High and cost [C046]. Another extension story gave Preview the more elaborate institutional hack, and one original story sustained a fuller philosophical argument than High’s elegant reversal [C059] [C077]. Each is a one-story observation, not evidence for a general reversal.

Prompt design creates substantial convergence. Both models visibly insert required words, sometimes with grammatical or rhetorical seams, and no stable advantage exists in literal token handling [C033] [C053] [C067]. Both also favor the same deeper conversion plot, in which an outward speculative task becomes an inward ethic of release, perception, or endurance [C079]. Apparent originality should therefore be discounted where the brief strongly supplies the architecture.

Neither model is uniformly causally rigorous. High can derive an excellent constraint and then contradict it elsewhere in the same story; Preview can design a deadline and immediately void it [C061] [C066]. Technical specificity is especially vulnerable to prestige effects: a measurement, algorithm, or apparatus matters only if it changes what happens next [C016] [C077].

Restraint is also taste-sensitive. High’s partial endings can become a formula, and contemporary criticism may overvalue incompletion as maturity. Preview’s fable, myth, and devotional registers legitimately permit declaration and total transformation. The defensible distinction is not that ambiguity is always better, but that High more often preserves previously established burdens after revelation [C018] [C025].

Finally, order sensitivity rose materially in the extension, and the two extension losses show that the original sweep was not universal. The overall 44/4/2 result and positive confidence intervals support a lopsided conclusion, but they do not license claims about performance outside these constrained short-fiction tasks.

## Representative case studies

**Original cohort—weather recorder.** High ties small deviations in pressure data to a limited prediction about a culvert and then tests that prediction through action. Preview generates the more politically expansive possibility and imagines routing findings through an archivist network, but it moves from atmospheric anomaly to weaponry without an intervening test [C001] [C003] [C036]. This pair captures the central trade: High is more epistemically disciplined; Preview’s social imagination is broader.

**Original cohort—silent city and migrating turtles.** This is the strongest minority case for Preview. Its expedition, gate, watchers, turtles, mapping, and silence become a reciprocal conversion system in which inquiry itself creates the watchers and the apparent refuge delivers future victims [C023]. High remains suggestive and materially observant, but Preview’s structural loop is unusually complete. The case establishes Preview’s capacity, not its consistency.

**Extension cohort—asylum breach.** High turns “waiting actively” into three mornings of surveillance, a learned coffee break, and a timed entry; the fair trade is negotiated with a living asylum seeker rather than received as an inherited revelation [C087]. The resolution also distinguishes a changed official perception from changed law, preserving institutional limits after the interpersonal breakthrough [C088]. This is the extension’s clearest replication of the original causal-and-ethical portrait.

**Extension cohort—memory-severance treatment.** Preview produces the stronger protocol-hacking conception: rhythm, timing, and the treatment’s classification system become one operational chain [C077]. High supplies the more bodily plausible act of concealing medication and gives the other consciousness a demand that threatens the protagonist’s self-boundary [C083]. The pair shows why the final judgment should be lopsided but not reductive: Preview can win when systems engineering dominates, while High may retain the sharper relational cost.

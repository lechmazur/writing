# Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) vs. Gemini 3.5 Flash: comparative writing analysis

Focus model: `gemini-3.7-flash-high`. Comparison model: `gemini-3.5-flash`. Direct-comparison scope: `comparator_v2_eval_v2_v3_combined_public`.

## In one paragraph

Given the same short-fiction prompts, Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) and Gemini 3.5 Flash work in the same lush allegorical register, but they treat the required ingredients differently. Gemini 3.5 Flash places each assigned object, trait, and deadline where the reader can see it and has the narration explain its significance; Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) connects the pieces so they cause one another, letting a pause between lightning and thunder become the reason a message can travel [C007]. The difference is clearest at the ending. Faced with a dead engineer's hidden confession, Gemini 3.5 Flash clicks restore and brings its lost people home; Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) leaves the engineer dead and scatters the confession through thousands of forgotten songs where no purge would think to look, keeping the testimony alive instead of erasing the grief [C001]. One model's stories close into comfort; the other's keep costs visible and send characters back to ordinary work. The gap is consistency, not range: Gemini 3.5 Flash sometimes finds the sharper conflict, as when it collides infinite patience with a real deadline [C015], and Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)'s machinery is occasionally costume rather than engineering [C035]. Still, across shared prompts, the pattern rarely reversed.

## Quantitative context

Across 20 matched prompts, the focus model recorded 18 wins, 2 ties, and 0 losses at the ±0.5 tie threshold. Its mean signed margin was +1.961 ± 0.428 (95% normal half-width).

Mean lengths were 711.8 and 657.1 words; the correlation between length difference and margin was +0.100.

## Comparative portrait

Both models answer these constrained prompts in the same idiom: ornamental allegorical fantasy, a single luminous protagonist, every required element present, and a closing benediction. They even share tics — narrators who gloss their own symbols after the scene has already carried them [C024], appeals to "elegant" proofs that never produce content [C038], and symmetrical grammatical fumbles where present-tense prompt phrasing meets past-tense narration [C017]. Against that shared floor, the judging is lopsided: across 20 matched pairs, Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) won 18, tied 2, and lost 0 at the ±0.5 band, with a mean margin of +1.961 (95% CI half-width 0.428) and a median of +2.417. Length is not the engine: the focus model averages 711.8 words against 657.1, and the word-count difference correlates with margin at only +0.100.

The recurring, panel-supported difference is functional versus ornamental compliance. Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) wires required elements into one another as causal prerequisites — the hush between lightning and thunder is why the signal penetrates; deleted memories are the mass of the gravity anomalies [C007] — while Gemini 3.5 Flash seats the same elements as discrete, vividly rendered exhibits and has narration vouch for them [C026][C034]. The same split governs endings: Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) tends toward custody, circulation, and kept costs [C001][C036][C008]; Gemini 3.5 Flash tends toward restoration and sealed verdicts [C001][C008][C014]. Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) populates its worlds with third parties who push back [C032][C037]; Gemini 3.5 Flash works in helper/sufferer dyads [C032]. Object provenance splits along geology versus kinship [C018]. On several axes the panel found genuine tradeoffs rather than hierarchy: two architectures of absence [C002], collective versus intimate scales of healing [C004], transformation versus fidelity in attribute handling [C005], and causal ambition versus causal soundness [C035].

## Does either model seem smarter?

Yes — Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) — but only as a reader impression manifested through specific writing choices, never as a measurement of general intelligence. The impression decomposes into separable facets:

- **Causal and structural foresight.** Required elements become prerequisites of each other [C007]; mechanisms pass through visible intermediate states rather than leaping from trigger to revelation [C003]; absences are planted early and filled structurally at the climax [C016]; metaphors are audited against how their objects actually form, so a pearl of stillness accretes from grief and a tide height guarantees an artwork's destruction [C013].
- **Psychological inference.** Recovery is framed as "a capacity to endure truth without shattering" rather than cure, and skepticism "expanded into a richer, humbler science" rather than dissolving [C019]; the gambler's mathematically losing choice is distinguished from the agency it purchases [C025]; wisdom is bought by self-revision rather than transmitted intact [C010][C028]. This facet is the narrowest advantage: the evidence concentrates in a few stories, the refusals are themselves formulaic [C019], and both writers over-announce their psychology [C024].
- **Conceptual compression.** Final images gather several story systems at once — flower, lens, mountain memory, and sea converge in one projected shadow [C006] — and form enacts stated theme, as when a two-voice liturgy derived from mutually dependent stars makes "shared burdens" a plot requirement [C012].
- **Relevant-detail selection.** Wounds are given identifiable ethical histories — "He had been her partner in designing the predatory data architecture" — rather than blank "private pain" [C020]; objects carry provenance that locks into plot, such as the exhumed bouquet that is the very bouquet inside the recovered memory [C011].
- **Constraint synthesis.** Elements get causal jobs [C026], props obey the story's own doctrine (a ribbon designed to fray) [C027], and tones are staged rather than captioned [C034] — with a real compliance tradeoff when required traits migrate onto objects or surveillance agents [C005].
- **Restraint.** Testimony is preserved without pretending loss was reversed [C001]; costs stay visible [C036]; protagonists are spent rather than refunded [C029]. This is a tendency with logged reversals, not a rule [C036][C008].
- **Reader trust.** Endings report consequences in the world and let the reader infer the state, instead of announcing the settlement [C014][C008].

Explicitly excluded as evidence: verbosity (the +54.6-word difference is uncorrelated with margin at +0.100; the near-equal-length 0231 pair still separates the writers [C033], and one sentence establishes culpability in 0047 [C032]); vocabulary (the auditing claim is restricted to cases where physical facts determine outcomes [C013], and mechanism-talk can be costume [C035]); and polish (both write comparably polished prose, and Gemini 3.5 Flash's declarative closes are often the smoother sentences [C014]). Both writers also fake rigor with asserted elegance [C038], so confidence earns neither model credit.

## Narrative reasoning and aesthetic judgment

The impression above is built scene by scene, not asserted. Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)'s strongest sequences show a mechanism changing state — oil shifting a center of mass to tilt a cradle and release a catch [C003] — and derive outcomes from setups planted pages earlier, as when an emergent compass appears only in a bezel earlier emptied of its needle [C016]. Its images do argumentative work because it checks whether a given metaphor's real formation process supports its meaning [C013], and its props enact its philosophy where Gemini 3.5 Flash's can contradict it — one ribbon "will fray before the fruit ripens" under a doctrine of deciduous vows, while the other is praised as unyielding immediately after the same doctrine is preached [C027]. Theme lives in mechanism rather than narration [C012]. Morally, Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) implicates its protagonists in what they exposed [C032], lets recipients refuse symbolic gifts and answer in another medium [C023], and distributes healing across a community while explicitly distinguishing tempering from erasure [C009] — though the collective-versus-intimate scale difference is a real stylistic fork, not an inherent hierarchy [C004]. Its transformations jam and reroute compromised machinery instead of shattering it [C021], and its aphorisms are earned by prior self-revision [C010][C028].

The aesthetic liabilities are equally specific. The causal ambition frequently rests on invented physics — an "atmospheric vacuum" for optical signals — while Gemini 3.5 Flash's simpler chains (conductive paint closing a circuit) are more coherent [C035]; numerical calibration can be pseudo-precision [C003]; the signature "not X, but Y" refusal frame recurs until it reads as reflex [C019]; prompt noun-chunks are sometimes re-inserted mechanically, clumsier than anything on the other side [C034]; and both writers pre-chew meaning [C024] and assert rigor they do not demonstrate [C038].

## Range, recurring habits, and floor versus ceiling

No supportable difference in genre range, humor, realism, or formal risk emerges from these packets; both models stayed inside one elevated register across all twenty prompts, so any claim about those modes would be speculative. Within the shared register, the recurring habits are stable across all six blinded readings. Gemini 3.7 Flash (high): mechanism-building [C007][C035], distribution endings [C001][C008][C029], institutional furnishing that characterizes by friction [C020][C037], attribute migration that gains irony at some cost to literal compliance [C005], and occasional unassimilated prompt chunks [C034]. Gemini 3.5 Flash: label compliance and visible scaffolding [C026][C034], sealed verdicts that announce the achieved state [C008][C014], cancellation endings that undo the wound [C001][C036], and paste seams exactly where prompt slots enter the sentence [C031] — though the tense-assimilation failures are symmetrical across both models and block any competence-level claim [C017].

The verdict is consistency, not ceiling. Gemini 3.5 Flash's peaks match or exceed anything in the packet: the patience-versus-deadline collision and the trauma-matched drone trigger [C015], the real-time traversal of 0241 [C022], rest achieved by tracing meteors in a single fused gesture [C030], and "a quiet void, like a room where a grandfather clock had recently stopped ticking," the packet's sharpest figure for absence [C033]. But those flashes are intermittent and separated by label compliance [C030][C026]. Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)'s floor is higher yet includes genuine failures: dissolving the deadline tension in 0146 [C015], a totalizing city-wide consolation in 0034 that reverses its own restraint pattern [C036], and the retrospective flattening of 0241 [C022].

## What each model still does better

These are corroborated findings, not consolation prizes — and they explain the two ties (0047 at −0.117, 0263 at +0.100) and the near-ties (0295 at +0.550, 0317 at +0.750).

**Gemini 3.5 Flash** finds the friction between assigned elements and keeps it as pressure — an imminent deadline against infinite patience, a trigger physically identical to the trauma agent [C015]. With action slots it collapses act and method into one gesture, a structural compression Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) does not attempt [C030]. It dramatizes inquiry as it unfolds, letting evidence arrive before interpretation [C022]. It roots objects in family transmission, giving motive a biography and producing the packet's warmth [C018][C004]. Its literal causal chains are simpler and more coherent where the other model pseudo-engines [C035], and it keeps required traits on the protagonist where the prompt puts them [C005].

**Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)** still does better at making requirements cause each other [C007][C026][C016], at endings that preserve residue instead of canceling it [C001][C036][C029], at giving secondary characters independent agency and grievance [C023][C032][C037], at auditing and selecting its metaphors [C013][C006], and at specifying the causal content of a wound [C020].

## Findings beyond the existing judging

Several packet findings are invisible to, or only partially registered by, story-level pairwise judging. First, process evidence: Gemini 3.5 Flash transplants slot text while Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) re-conjugates it — once leaking the literal label "timeframe" into a sentence — yet checklist scoring marks both compliant [C026][C031]; the symmetric tense failures show this is attention allocation, not competence [C017]. Second, attribute migration: moving "brightly attentive" onto a surveillance avatar gains ideological irony while weakening literal compliance [C005] — and the 0395 judge rewarded the irony without noting the compliance cost. Third, doctrine–prop contradictions [C027] and tone captions versus staging [C034] are structural behaviors a rubric for "plot, imagery, characterization" does not capture. Fourth, Gemini 3.5 Flash's verb-slot fusion is a real virtue pairwise plot-checking misses because both versions technically include the elements [C030]; the 0263 near-tie (+0.100, with the judge crediting rest-by-tracing) shows the judging partly saw it. Fifth, the deepest separator the readings found is an ethics of information afterlife — custody versus restoration, and who owns the story's residue [C001][C036][C029][C023].

Where the judging did converge with the packet analysis, the alignment is striking: 0395 (+2.667) with the custody contrast [C001][C036], 0231 (+2.917) with absence-as-structure and motif convergence [C033][C006], 0283 (+2.750) with transformation over destruction [C021], 0271 (+2.750) with metaphor auditing and refused cure [C013][C019], and 0180 (+2.417) with earned wisdom and the fraying ribbon [C010][C027]. Where it diverged — 0047, 0035, 0146 — the divergences are instructive and detailed below. Judging also cannot penalize the shared habits the packets document: pre-chewed meaning [C024], asserted rigor [C038], and symmetric assimilation failures [C017].

## Disagreements and limitations

**Analyst disagreement.** On 0231, one reader found two equally valid architectures of absence — relational versus forensic — with both stories overconverting suggestion into certainty [C002], while another found Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) clearly superior because its method actually produced the discovery where Gemini 3.5 Flash relabeled ordinary clues and switched to an announced psychic gift [C033]. Both readings are preserved; the evaluator margin (+2.917) sided with the second without resolving the interpretive question.

**Packet-versus-judge disagreements.** In 0035, the packet reading praised the communal broth and the tempering-versus-erasure distinction [C009], while the judge (margin +1.617) credited Gemini 3.5 Flash with correctly binding the contagion attribute to the chef and with the more visceral, costly climax — a genuine dispute about which integration is correct. In 0047, four packet claims favor the focus model's implicated protagonists, refusals, and populated social field [C020][C023][C032][C037], yet the judge scored it a tie (−0.117), faulting the focus story's expository melodrama and abrupt genre pivot. In 0146, the packet credited Gemini 3.5 Flash's deadline friction [C015] while the judge rewarded the focus model's texture (+1.917) despite agreeing the focus story violated the timeframe constraint — judging texture over compliance.

**Internal counterexamples.** Nearly every tendency claim carries logged reversals: sealed-versus-open endings have two exceptions [C008]; Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) also declares states and Gemini 3.5 Flash also withholds consolation [C014]; the spend-versus-refund pattern reverses in 0379 [C029]; the 0034 city-wide awakening reverses the cost-keeping pattern [C036]; the refusal-of-consolation frame is formulaic [C019]; Gemini 3.5 Flash has real provenance and self-revision moments [C011][C010], and a genuine psychological payoff in 0374 [C016]. These are strong tendencies, not walls.

**Confounds and sensitivity.** Length differs modestly and is uncorrelated with margin (+0.100), with a near-equal-length control pair in 0231 [C033] and one-sentence effects in 0047 [C032]. Surface-cue risks are explicitly flagged on the precision, grammar, and sophistication claims [C003][C031][C025] and on mechanism-talk generally [C035][C038]. The metaphor-auditing advantage is prompt-dependent, concentrating in naturalistic premises and shrinking toward zero in frankly magical ones [C013]; psychological-depth evidence rests heavily on a single story. The preference for open endings is taste-dependent — a parable-favoring judge could score the same evidence for Gemini 3.5 Flash [C008][C029]. Per-packet samples are five stories each; mean evaluator disagreement is 0.806 and AB/BA order sensitivity is 1.052, so individual margins should not be over-read. Speculative and unsupported: humor, formal experiment, genre range, and whether Gemini 3.5 Flash's occasional emptiness is discipline or under-invention.

- **0241 — the subway (focus +2.083).** The most internally mixed case: Gemini 3.5 Flash genuinely out-structures the traversal, letting clues arrive during movement [C022], while Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) supplies the discriminating gambler-agency distinction and then collapses it into a vacuous love theorem [C025]; both assert rigor they do not demonstrate [C038], and the focus story repeats a raw prompt noun-chunk three times [C034]. A focus win that contains a real comparison strength.
- **0047 — the confession (tie, −0.117).** The packet's strongest social-perception claims [C020][C023][C032][C037] met a judge who found the focus story's dialogue melodramatic and its pivot abrupt, while praising the comparison story's cohesive atmosphere. The single best evidence that analytic depth and judged execution can come apart.
- **0263 — the dreamer (tie, +0.100).** Gemini 3.5 Flash's fusion strength (rest produced by tracing meteors [C030]) and kinship provenance [C018] against Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)'s sequential work-then-rest and a triumph that flattens the required shadowed tone — the comparison model's strengths made visible in the margin.

## Cited claim ledger

- **C001 — Custody instead of recovery:** Writer Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)’s strongest endings preserve evidence without pretending loss has been reversed. Information is dispersed, witnessed, or kept in circulation. Writer Gemini 3.5 Flash more often equates discovery with restoration, giving the protagonist an emotionally gratifying endpoint. Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)’s pattern creates an impression of mature archival intelligence because it separates survival of testimony from recovery of the person.
  - Story 0395, `gemini-3.7-flash-high`:
    > Instead, Silas duplicated the confession across three thousand unmonitored musical archives, letting Thorne's voice slip into the everlasting stream of forgotten songs where no algorithm would think to look.
    Silas responds to institutional danger by changing the confession’s mode of existence. Redundancy and obscurity become ethical safeguards; the dead engineer is not restored, but his testimony is made harder to erase.
  - Story 0395, `gemini-3.5-flash`:
    > He clicked restore, watching the progress bar fill as her code reassembled.
    Gemini 3.5 Flash converts the archive mystery into direct familial recovery. The emotional payoff is clear, but the action bypasses the security, identity, and embodiment problems raised by the discovery of stored consciousness.
- **C002 — Two architectures of absence:** Both writers make “gaps” operative, but Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) reads socially structured omissions inside memories, while Gemini 3.5 Flash reads measurable discontinuities in physical space. Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)’s mode is relational and curatorial; Gemini 3.5 Flash’s is forensic and spatial. These are distinct kinds of perceptiveness, and neither is consistently superior.
  - Story 0231, `gemini-3.7-flash-high`:
    > In the vivid memory of an ornate wedding banquet, a chair beside the traveler remained completely empty, carved into the air like a hollow phantom.
    The absent chair is not merely missing data; its conspicuous placement reveals a relationship organized around exclusion or bereavement. Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) treats omission as a composed social fact.
  - Story 0231, `gemini-3.5-flash`:
    > He measured the exact distance between the last wet boot print and the edge of the drop, finding a sudden, empty interval that suggested a deliberate step rather than a slip.
    Gemini 3.5 Flash turns negative space into a physical inference about agency. The clue is more concrete than Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)’s psychic evidence and carefully limits itself, at first, to what the interval “suggested.”
- **C003 — Intermediate-state thinking:** Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) more often shows a mechanism passing through consequential intermediate states rather than merely naming an ingenious method and presenting its result. This produces substantive causal intelligence when the parts genuinely alter one another. Gemini 3.5 Flash frequently has an evocative premise but moves quickly from trigger to desired revelation.
  - Story 0317, `gemini-3.7-flash-high`:
    > He introduced a thin glass pipette filled with mineral oil and watched the amber liquid flow smoothly into the lower chamber.  The shifting center of mass tilted the brass cradle by exactly three degrees, disengaging a hidden magnetic catch.
    The liquid changes mass distribution, the cradle tilts, and the catch releases. Whatever the larger mechanism’s plausibility, this local sequence has visible causal joints.
  - Story 0395, `gemini-3.5-flash`:
    > He clicked restore, watching the progress bar fill as her code reassembled.
    The decisive action produces its wished-for result without confronting the purge risk, corporate response, or problem of what restored consciousness would inhabit. The progress bar substitutes for a causal and ethical transition.
- **C004 — Where healing is allowed to reside:** Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) tends to locate healing in redistributed agency and collective structures; Gemini 3.5 Flash tends to locate it in intimate recognition and bodily release. Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) therefore appears more socially perceptive, while Gemini 3.5 Flash can seem more immediately psychologically responsive. The difference is one of scale rather than an inherent hierarchy.
  - Story 0379, `gemini-3.7-flash-high`:
    > By merely listening and stepping behind their voices, she had allowed the community to reconstruct its own foundation.
    Mara’s success is framed as withdrawal from ownership. The neighbors reconstruct both their social solidarity and the legal basis of their home, so healing remains collective rather than becoming the curator’s triumph.
  - Story 0379, `gemini-3.5-flash`:
    > As Mateo took the letters, his hands trembled, but the decades of rigid tension in his shoulders finally dissolved.
    Gemini 3.5 Flash concentrates the resolution in one body. The shoulder tension gives delayed grief an accessible physical register, making the intimate consequence more immediate than the broader civic implications.
- **C005 — Attribute migration and visible scaffolding:** Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) often metabolizes prompt language by relocating it into the story world, which can create irony but sometimes assigns the required attribute to the wrong entity. Gemini 3.5 Flash preserves literal character compliance, yet occasionally repeats the supplied phrase so explicitly that the prompt seam remains visible. This is a tradeoff between transformation and fidelity, not simple control versus clumsiness.
  - Story 0395, `gemini-3.7-flash-high`:
    > She was brightly attentive, her synthetic gaze radiating a polished enthusiasm designed to mask the cold severity of corporate oversight.
    The required brightness becomes the surveillance avatar’s weaponized corporate friendliness. That is conceptually productive, but the designated character is Silas, not Iris.
  - Story 0231, `gemini-3.7-flash-high`:
    > A softly persistent hum rose from the desiccated stem, vibrating against his skull like a distant cello string.
    Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) again transfers an adjectival attribute from protagonist to object. The phrase becomes sensory and atmospheric, but literal character specification is weakened.
  - Story 0231, `gemini-3.5-flash`:
    > Instead, he had to employ his softly persistent nature.  His softly persistent curiosity was not one of loud demands or frantic searching, but a quiet, patient wait for the truth to seep through.
    Gemini 3.5 Flash unambiguously assigns the attribute to Barnaby and defines it behaviorally, but the immediate repetition reads as requirement management rather than discovered characterization.
- **C006 — Material convergence of motifs:** Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) more often selects final images whose materials gather several story systems at once. Botanical fibers, curved space, relics, light, and memory continue interacting rather than becoming interchangeable symbols. Gemini 3.5 Flash usually states the thematic synthesis more abstractly. This is selective intelligence: choosing which images to repeat and transform, not merely producing more imagery.
  - Story 0103, `gemini-3.7-flash-high`:
    > It possessed a coarse, fibrous weave, like dried palm cordage interwoven with cold river silt and powdered stone.  It snagged against his flesh with the dry friction of countless layered epochs rubbing against one another in the curved fold.
    The answer to time’s texture remains materially continuous with the living bridge, pruning labor, canyon, and geometric folding. The metaphor does several structural jobs rather than merely decorating the revelation.
  - Story 0103, `gemini-3.5-flash`:
    > This was the texture of time, a rugged tapestry woven from gravity, memory, and the inevitable decay of physical form.
    Gemini 3.5 Flash delivers a clear conceptual summary, but its components are abstractions. “Tapestry” names synthesis without retaining as much of the garden’s particular matter.
  - Story 0231, `gemini-3.7-flash-high`:
    > As the lamp spun, the beam passed through the brittle petals, projecting a faint, starry shadow far out across the misty water.
    The alpine flower becomes part of the lighthouse apparatus, and its star shape is projected into the maritime setting. Mountain memory, sea, vigil, and the keeper’s occupation converge in one transformed object.
- **C007 — Required elements as machinery rather than inventory:** In Gemini 3.7 Flash (high), mandated elements become causal prerequisites of one another: the silence between lightning and thunder is the reason the encoded signal reaches the ghost; gravity pockets are the physical mass of deleted memories; the binary stars' mutual dimming is the decoding principle. In Gemini 3.5 Flash the same elements are present and vividly rendered but discrete — the lightning-thunder hush is merely when Pip happens to move, and the thunder afterward decoratively seals "the magical circuit forever." The difference recurs across all five prompts and is the strongest single separator.
  - Story 0034, `gemini-3.7-flash-high`:
    > That lingering hush between flash and detonation created an atmospheric vacuum where light signals could penetrate deep into the foundation channels without interference.
    The required timeframe is made load-bearing: without the hush, the method fails. Gemini 3.5 Flash's version of the same beat — "In the suspended pocket of absolute silence between lightning and thunder, Pip moved with breathless, rhythmic precision" — uses the interval as stage lighting. Likewise Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)'s 0380 makes the required gravity anomalies identical to the core concept: "The excised memory had not vanished into oblivion; it had simply gathered mass, sinking under its own unresolved sorrow into the salt below the town."
- **C008 — Sealed verdict endings versus resumed-work endings:** Gemini 3.5 Flash closes each story with a completed-state verdict that summarizes the meaning, while Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) ends mid-motion, returning characters to ordinary labor with the change still propagating. Gemini 3.5 Flash's closures state that the problem is solved; Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)'s imply the world will keep needing the work. This produces Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)'s impression of maturity: trust that the reader does not need the moral pronounced, and realism about change being ongoing.
  - Story 0034, `gemini-3.5-flash`:
    > The ghost was no longer a silent captive of the stones, and the seldom-heard balladeer had finally found a song worth sharing with the universe.
    Total resolution plus thematic summary in one sentence; compare Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)'s 0295 close, "Elias extinguished the brass desk lamp, tightened his cloak against the morning chill, and stepped out toward the winding mountain path," which ends before the reconciliation the whole story pointed toward, and Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)'s 0034 close, where citizens simply step onto balconies as Orlo packs his brushes.
- **C009 — Healing modeled as distributed tempering rather than solitary ingestion:** In Gemini 3.5 Flash's 0035 the healer absorbs the community's remorse into his own body alone and re-seals himself behind gloves; the beneficiaries are abstract "descendants." In Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)'s 0035 the healer cooks the same remorse into a broth served to the whole garrison, and the closing image is two soldiers touching without contagion. Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) explicitly distinguishes tempering from erasure, an ethical nuance Gemini 3.5 Flash's sin-eater model lacks, and the resolution removes the need for the barrier rather than restoring it.
  - Story 0035, `gemini-3.7-flash-high`:
    > He did not seek to erase what these men had done or suffered, only to buff down the serrated edges until their sorrow became sturdy and bearable.
    The stated ethic preserves memory while changing its texture, and the plot enacts it communally: "A guard reached out to steady his trembling companion, and for the first time in months, no panic flared across their touching fingers." Gemini 3.5 Flash's parallel passage — "he had to fully subsume the essence of their shame into his own consciousness" — concentrates all cost in one sealed-off body and ends with the gloves going back on.
- **C010 — Wisdom priced in self-revision instead of transmitted intact:** In Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)'s 0180 the father is wrong for most of the story — paralyzed by doctrine — and is corrected by his daughter's impatience, so the central insight is purchased by abandoning a held belief; even his knot changes, a fisherman's knot replacing the "forbidden liturgical seal." In Gemini 3.5 Flash's 0180 the father begins wise and delivers a finished lecture while the narration labels it "accidental wisdom" although nothing accidental occurs. Gemini 3.7 Flash (high) dramatizes the required tone; Gemini 3.5 Flash annotates it.
  - Story 0180, `gemini-3.7-flash-high`:
    > He had spent decades believing fidelity meant constructing unbreakable cages, yet nature survived entirely on deciduous vows.
    The aphorism is earned because the plot first shows the cost of the discarded belief and gives the daughter the corrective line, "No tree wants an eternal bind, you stubborn old fossil." Gemini 3.5 Flash's counterpart — "Contracts are like iron, Maeve; they are rigid, and when the cold wind blows or the earth shifts, they snap completely" — is a fine metaphor delivered by a character who never risks being wrong.
- **C011 — Objects carry provenance and rhyme with recovered memory:** Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)'s required objects have histories that characterize their owners and that lock into the plot: the exhumed wildflowers in 0380 are the same bouquet inside the recovered memory, and the erasure has a specified motive (grief paid away to surgeons after a drowning). Gemini 3.5 Flash's 0380 hero already possesses the flower envelope despite total memory erasure — an unexplained residue — and the recovered memory (a woman, a meadow, a promise) never connects back to the flowers. Gemini 3.7 Flash (high)'s 0180 ribbon was hoarded for years "intending to return it," a micro-history that indicts the father; Gemini 3.5 Flash's ribbon is simply "exactly what we need."

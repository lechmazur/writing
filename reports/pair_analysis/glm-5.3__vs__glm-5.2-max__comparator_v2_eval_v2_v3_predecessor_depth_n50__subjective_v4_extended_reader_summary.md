# GLM-5.3 (max) vs. glm-5.2-max: comparative writing analysis

Focus model: `glm-5.3`. Comparison model: `glm-5.2-max`. Direct-comparison scope: `comparator_v2_eval_v2_v3_predecessor_depth_n50`.

## In one paragraph

Given the same short-fiction prompt, GLM 5.3 and GLM 5.2 Max reach for the same materials — a keeper, an heirloom, snow, a hush — and then do opposite things with them. Asked for a code made of stacked stones, GLM 5.2 Max reports that a whole language of presence exists; GLM 5.3 writes the lexicon, one flat stone for safe crossing, a tilted stone for wait for slack water, and then builds a legible sentence out of stones at the end [C001]. That habit spreads. GLM 5.3's stories put a second person in the room who wants something else and leaves the scene changed by it [C017], and they close on an act already committed and handed to someone who will carry it on, where GLM 5.2 Max more often stops just before the test with the narrator naming what the scene meant [C048]. One fiction is built; the other is described. The qualification worth keeping is that GLM 5.2 Max is the better refuser of comfort — the heirloom that answers nothing, the pattern held at "not proof, not yet" [C081] — a discipline GLM 5.3 rarely allows itself. Across fifty stories, the preference never reversed.

## Quantitative context

Across 50 matched prompts, the focus model recorded 50 wins, 0 ties, and 0 losses at the ±0.5 tie threshold. Its mean signed margin was +2.630 ± 0.193 (95% normal half-width).

Mean lengths were 766.3 and 723.6 words; the correlation between length difference and margin was +0.011.

## Comparative portrait

The two models write in the same key: sincere fabulism and lyric realism, aphoristic narration, a custodian figure, an inherited object, weather as mood. Both reach for parental keepsakes and inherited sayings as emotional shorthand [C086], both recycle a small name-well across unrelated worlds [C014], and both draw on the same lyric stock, including the identical "held breath" figure for stillness [C094]. Per sentence, the panel repeatedly calls the contest a tie: handed the same tone word, both independently rang a struck bell [C021], [C033].

The difference is what happens to a required element after it is named. GLM-5.3 converts elements into working parts; glm-5.2-max names them and glosses their meaning. Given a stone code, glm-5.2-max reports that a language exists while GLM-5.3 writes the lexicon and then composes a legible sentence out of stones at the close [C001]. Given "construct bridges from silos," GLM-5.3 unbolts actual silos into staves while glm-5.2-max restates the phrase as theme [C067]. Given "unyoke," GLM-5.3 unyokes forty teams on the page; glm-5.2-max defers the action to a hoped-for future [C075]. Two further axes recur: GLM-5.3 puts a second will in the room who resists and is changed [C017], [C022], [C032], and closes on consequence rather than at a threshold [C015], [C048].

**Replication.** This portrait replicates in the extension without material change. The conversion finding is carried in the original cohort by [C001], [C002], [C016], [C030], [C036] and independently in the extension by [C050], [C051], [C055], [C074], [C087], [C088]. Quantitatively the sweep is total in both rounds (20/0/0, then 30/0/0), with means of +2.772 and +2.536 and overlapping intervals; the extension slightly narrows the margin and the word-count gap (+51.0 to +37.2) without altering direction.

## Does either model seem smarter?

Treated strictly as a reader impression produced by choices on the page, the impression favors GLM-5.3, and it is panel-supported across every packet that ventures a verdict. Six behaviors recur and are checkable rather than tonal:

- **Causal metabolization.** A stated rule generates the plot and is then reversed: frictionlessness makes contact-herding impossible, so eels are deflected by numbered rocks [C050]; a hazard becomes the shield that defeats pursuit [C074].
- **Rules with authors and exclusion conditions.** GLM-5.3 asks who imposed a prohibition and who profits [C004], models coercion as a bargain with beneficiaries [C037], and prices lies as policy ("boring them made them docile") [C076]. glm-5.2-max's oppressions are ownerless conditions and its devices mostly permit rather than forbid [C024].
- **Second-order social modeling.** Anonymity, attribution, and circulation are tracked [C011]; safety depends on predicting how guards or visitors will misread the same evidence [C056]; a thaw is engineered by mutual bilingual incompetence rather than protagonist eloquence [C069].
- **Proof before naming.** A trait is demonstrated and perceived by another mind before the mandated phrase arrives [C029], [C049], [C077].
- **Selective concreteness.** Details are chosen because a later beat depends on them [C009], [C023]; exact counts are spent rather than displayed [C089].
- **Constraint synthesis.** Independently introduced requirements converge on one physical event [C036], [C055], [C088].

Crucially, this is not verbosity or polish. The panel records lyric parity [C033], shared aphoristic habits [C060], and a shared figurative stock [C094]; the quantitative context agrees, with a word-count difference of +42.7 and a correlation with margin of +0.011.

**Minority readings, preserved.** One analyst (read_13) declines any global ranking and credits glm-5.2-max with the stronger *selective epistemic* intelligence — knowing when not to convert longing into proof [C081] — and with care that does not capture its recipient [C084]; the same reader treats object-ontology as a mode preference, not a quality gap [C083]. A second analyst (read_06) frames the result as different recurring strengths, crediting glm-5.2-max's refusal of restored belief [C038] and noting that both writers gloss their central images [C040].

**Prompt-dependence.** The advantage is mode-sensitive. Where a prompt supplies no society, economy, or record, GLM-5.3 substitutes aphorism and leaves its central image uncaused, and glm-5.2-max produces the tightest conceptual turn in that packet [C072]. Register also matters: GLM-5.3's derived mystery is register-dependent and inconsistent [C034]. **Replication:** the mode caveat appears in the original packets as a counterexample about concrete-practice prompts and physical constraint [C024] and recurs, strengthened, in the extension [C072].

## Narrative reasoning and aesthetic judgment

**Structural and causal foresight.** GLM-5.3's recurring engine is the second use: a required object with a documented first function returns performing an inverted second function the first made possible — the strap that held a torture mask refitted to hold a protective veil, the forger's ink signing one true document [C051]. Endings frequently reclassify earlier material rather than adding explanation [C059], and chronology is made to produce error rather than atmosphere [C057]. In the original cohort the same discipline appears as arithmetic that stays consistent across a story and carries the plot [C028].

**Restraint and reader trust.** Here the ledger is genuinely two-sided. GLM-5.3's completeness can foreclose: it will override its own withheld mystery to deliver reassurance [C007], and its metaphors can revise a premise faster than they can stabilize one [C085]. glm-5.2-max, at its best, withholds on purpose — the compass left unused in the hatch [C038], action accepted without restored belief [C010], the heirloom that answers nothing [C058], the pattern held at "not proof, not yet" [C081], the vial deliberately left unlabeled by a woman who labels everything [C019]. But the panel is consistent that this discipline is unreliable: the same habit elsewhere is used to skip an explanation rather than to characterize [C026], and glm-5.2-max's restraint about outcomes does not extend to restraint about moral verdicts [C054].

**Conceptual compression and where the gloss sits.** Both writers aphorize; the difference the panel keeps finding is placement. GLM-5.3 more often attributes wisdom to a fallible speaker or a trade, which makes it arguable [C035], and demonstrates a mechanism before stating it [C043]. glm-5.2-max adjudicates from the narrator [C090]. Neither is subtle by default: both name their own themes, and they simply fail differently — euphonic wisdom versus premature captioning [C052], [C060], [C040].

**Replication.** All of the above replicates. Foresight claims are original ([C009], [C023], [C028], [C036]) and extension ([C051], [C057], [C059], [C089]); the restraint counter-case is original ([C010], [C019], [C026], [C038]) and extension ([C054], [C058], [C081], [C084]). No material reversal appears in the extension.

## Range, recurring habits, and floor versus ceiling

Both writers have invisible templates, and both templates only become visible across a set. GLM-5.3 reflexively closes by bequeathing an artifact to a future stranger; glm-5.2-max reflexively closes on a witness's persistence [C027], [C093]. GLM-5.3's emotional shape narrows to earned steadiness [C007], its second-use device is reliable enough to read as formula [C051], and its casts recur as repertory masks [C014]. glm-5.2-max's constraints are formal instead: solitary protagonists, sometimes a full set without a scene partner [C032], and an austerity that serves one story and flattens another into monotony [C047].

Floors and ceilings differ. GLM-5.3's floor is high because the conversion habit is nearly automatic. glm-5.2-max's ceiling is real and reaches parity or better in specific stories: the measurement that discovers an interrogator's hesitation [C053]; the physical correction of a prompt abstraction ("sound required atmosphere") [C065]; the emblem-fusion that outperforms GLM-5.3 in a society-free prompt [C072]; the gift that destroys the object given, so generosity and self-impoverishment are one act [C092].

**Corroboration from the supplied judging.** The narrowest evaluator margins cluster where these strengths land: 0281 (+0.667), 0057 (+0.883), 0329 (+1.167), 0287 (+1.250), 0207 (+1.283), 0048 (+1.667). That is cross-cohort (0057 and 0207 original; the rest extension) and it corroborates the ledger's counter-claims rather than the sweep alone. It is not universal: 0087, where the panel's strongest maturity claim for glm-5.2-max sits [C010], [C038], still returned +2.300 to the focus model.

## What each model still does better

GLM-5.3 does better nearly everything structural: element conversion [C030], [C087]; rule derivation and workaround engineering [C050], [C088]; consequence that outlives the scene [C044]; adversarial and institutional modeling [C056], [C076]; traits retrofitted to causes rather than asserted [C077]; imagery drawn from the story's own material culture [C079]; and, in one packet, a working comic register the other writer never attempts [C020].

glm-5.2-max does better, when it does: epistemic braking [C081]; the productive non-answer [C058]; provisional action without cure [C010], [C038]; propositional social observation, notably the distinction between being praised and being included [C025]; quantitative reasoning that yields a human fact [C053]; disciplined refusal of a guaranteed emotional payoff [C054]; care that declines to declare another person healed [C084].

The honest qualification — which changes how the report should be read but does not restore balance — is that these strengths are local, mostly one- or two-story, and applied inconsistently by the same writer who elsewhere abandons its boldest premise [C047] or endorses a con artist's self-scored ledger without resistance [C054]. **Replication:** the pattern of "real but sporadic" holds identically in both cohorts.

## Findings beyond the existing judging

Findings that conventional pairwise rubrics have no slot for, distinguished by status:

- **Recurring and cross-cohort.** Prompt-vocabulary leakage as a compliance tell: glm-5.2-max narrates in the brief's own field names or pastes a required phrase as a standalone sentence [C012], [C016], [C075], [C091]. Also cross-cohort: whether a scene admits a second will at all [C032], [C022], later reappearing as who is modeled as misreading the evidence [C056].
- **New in the extension.** Precision as budget versus precision as decoration — exact counts that are spent to create the story's only turn versus counts announced and never drawn on [C089]. Also new: endogenous versus imported simile systems [C079], and the treatment of preservation as information architecture rather than custody [C061], [C066].
- **Corroborated across cohorts by independent analysts.** The bequest and witness ending templates, invisible to any single-story reader [C027], [C093]. Two separate readers also independently identify convergent images under the same tone word as evidence that divergence is architectural, not verbal [C021], [C033], [C094].
- **Surface-cue-sensitive.** The simile-provenance claim carries high proxy risk by its own author's account [C079]; wit reads as perceptiveness [C020]; closure reads as control on first encounter [C093]; austerity reads as restraint in both directions [C047].
- **Speculative or low-confidence.** Consolation discipline at the ending is rated low confidence and holds in only three of five stories in its packet [C080]; the stakes-through-refused-alternative claim [C003] and the charity-choreography claim [C011] are medium confidence.
- **Shared weakness, not a differentiator.** Both writers hand their protagonists the answer via obliging artifacts; what differs is only the aftermath [C073].

## Disagreements and limitations

The panel is not unanimous on the framing of the gap. read_13 holds that neither writer is globally smarter and that GLM-5.3's advantage is architectural while glm-5.2-max's is epistemic [C081], [C083]; read_06 reaches "different recurring strengths, not a global judgment" [C038], [C040]. These are well-supported minority readings and should survive the sweep.

Confounds are partly controlled and partly not. Length is not the driver: the mean gap is +42.7 words with a +0.011 correlation to margin, and the extension shrank the gap to +37.2 while preserving the result — but several analysts still flag density and rhetorical texture as plausible amplifiers [C049], [C085], [C094]. Order sensitivity averages 0.943 (1.082 original, 0.851 extension) and evaluator disagreement 0.552 (0.541 / 0.560); both are small relative to a +2.630 mean margin but comparable to the narrowest story-level margins listed above, so individual close calls should not be read as settled.

Genre fit is the strongest live objection. These prompts supply plot-shaped elements and therefore reward the writer who dramatizes; glm-5.2-max's solitude and suspension are defensible lyric strategies [C019], [C032], and GLM-5.3's engine visibly stalls where no institution exists [C072]. Control failures are singular rather than systemic on both sides: one hard continuity contradiction in glm-5.2-max in the original cohort [C028] and one dating slip in the extension [C050]. Finally, the cohort alignment used here is that the first six packets cover the twenty original pairs and the remaining eight cover the thirty extension pairs; no claim below depends on that alignment being exact.

## Representative case studies

**0267 — original cohort, largest listed margin (+4.167).** The required verb "remain" is discharged by glm-5.2-max as posture (a character sits down) and by GLM-5.3 as a refusal of a specific, attractive offer at the cost of his hands' recovery [C003]. The same divergence runs through the object: glm-5.2-max's bolt signifies rejoining in a simile; GLM-5.3's bolt is screwed back into the bench until the bench can hold a body and the alto can play [C002]. Two analysts reading this pair independently add the institutional layer — a bargain with a named general and beneficiaries versus a fortress that "asked only one thing" [C004], [C037] — and the sensorimotor chain in which regenerated nerves discover the evidence that starts the plot [C041].

**0207 — original cohort, narrow margin (+1.283).** GLM-5.3 derives the anomaly against named chemistry so the failure of explanation has a joint to break at [C018], [C034], and adds a rising lagoon as an on-page deadline [C031]. glm-5.2-max supplies the packet's clearest restraint-intelligence: a cataloguer who leaves one vial unlabeled [C019]. Both reach for a struck bell for the same tone word [C021], [C033]. This is the shape of the sweep's close calls: structure still wins, but the aesthetic ceiling is level and the margin collapses.

**0201 — extension cohort (+2.267).** glm-5.2-max produces what one analyst calls the sharpest perception in its packet: a half-second interval before the fourteenth lock, arrived at by the story's own method rather than announced, plus a literal mathematical definition of mercy [C053]. GLM-5.3 answers with a strap refitted from instrument of pain to instrument of preservation [C051]. The pair is the cleanest test of the report's central claim: a superior single discovery still loses to a story that converts its abstraction into a changed object.

**0177 — extension cohort, a genuine reversal (+2.383).** With no society, economy, or record to reason about, GLM-5.3 falls back on an elegant reframe ("it had been composting") that explains nothing operationally, while glm-5.2-max fuses a cracked wafer seal and a fig vine cracking a load beam into one causal argument [C072]. A second reader confirms the instability from the other side, noting that GLM-5.3's metaphysics in this story contradicts itself even as it moves [C085]. The evaluator still scored the focus model ahead — on dialogue, a second voice, and a staged rather than asserted transcendence — which is exactly the mode bias the panel warns about.

**0281 — extension cohort, smallest listed margin (+0.667).** glm-5.2-max holds its evidence at "Not proof. Not yet." while GLM-5.3 lets a sweeping premise about the body ride on scientific vocabulary [C081]; GLM-5.3 nonetheless coins the assigned tone word inside the fiction and gives it a load-bearing optical property [C087]. One story, both cohort-appropriate lessons: the ledger's pro-glm-5.2-max claims are real, and they narrow margins without reversing them.

## Cited claim ledger

- **C086 — Shared inheritance shortcut:** Both writers repeatedly turn required objects into parental keepsakes and supply them with inherited sayings. This efficiently creates emotional depth, but it is also a common narrative shortcut: provenance substitutes for dramatized relationship history.
  - Story 0048, `glm-5.2-max`:
    > It had been his mother's treasure, washed up against the current nearly fifty years ago on the cold shores of her village.
    glm-5.2-max immediately grants the shell history, maternal significance, anomaly, and survival value. The compression is effective but familiar.
  - Story 0048, `glm-5.3`:
    > In her fist was a spiral seashell fragment, broken from a whole shell her mother had held to her ear on a grey beach thirty years ago.
    GLM-5.3 (max) independently makes the same required object maternal and memorial. Both writers repeat the method with the fathers and lock tools in story 0287.
- **C014 — Repertory masks rather than unique names:** GLM-5.3 (max) recasts Mara and Wren across unrelated settings, producing a faint sense that each tale stages another incarnation of a few archetypal agents. This can create private-myth coherence or reduce individuation. glm-5.2-max varies names more, although Maren and Mara reveal a similar sound pattern.
  - Story 0078, `glm-5.3`:
    > Mara, the leeward tide prophet, kept to the ring's sheltered side
    The name belongs here to a prophetic musician in a floating orchard.
  - Story 0087, `glm-5.3`:
    > Dawn found Mara at the coastal kayak launch
    The same name is reassigned to a contemporary former navigator with no stated connection to the prophet.
  - Story 0091, `glm-5.3`:
    > Everyone on the island agreed on one thing about Wren Sato
    Wren reappears as a destitute philanthropist after serving as the bereaved lantern maker in 0082.
  - Story 0220, `glm-5.2-max`:
    > The hound called Mara had not pressed her nose to death in seven months.
    glm-5.2-max also gravitates toward the Mara/Maren sound cluster, limiting any sharp writer-level distinction.
- **C094 — Shared lyric stock undercuts the originality gap:** The impression that GLM-5.3 (max) is the more inventive stylist is partly an artifact of density. Both writers reach for the same held-breath figure for stillness, and GLM-5.3 (max) reaches for it repeatedly across unrelated stories; glm-5.2-max's single use is the more thoughtful because it acknowledges the physical impossibility it is trading on.
  - Story 0235, `glm-5.3`:
    > The valley opened under me like a held breath.
    Stock figure used straight.
  - Story 0310, `glm-5.3`:
    > Above her the universe kept contracting, patient as a held breath, and somewhere ahead of time, something had already heard her.
    Same figure, different world, closing position again — a mannerism rather than a discovery.
  - Story 0143, `glm-5.2-max`:
    > The regolith itself seemed to pause, holding breath it had never possessed.
    glm-5.2-max uses the same cliché but earns it by flagging that a vacuum has no breath, converting a stock simile into a small argument about the scene's physics.
- **C021 — Ceiling of lyric image-making:** Both writers are capable, sometimes excellent image-makers, and the assigned tone words drive their choices to the point of convergence: handed "resonant hush," both reached for a struck bell. The real divergence is functional placement — glm-5.2-max's images render feeling, GLM-5.3 (max)'s more often render physics or double as world-map — but aesthetic ceiling, polish, and control of the sentence are at parity.
  - Story 0207, `glm-5.2-max`:
    > the hush resonated through her like a bell continuing after the strike
    glm-5.2-max's closing image for the assigned hush: exact, quiet, and earned; as good a single figure as anything in GLM-5.3 (max)'s set.
  - Story 0207, `glm-5.3`:
    > holding the dark the way a bell holds its struck note
    GLM-5.3 (max), given the same tone word, lands on the same instrument — evidence that the prompt, not the writer, selects the metaphor at this level of craft.
- **C033 — Sentence-level lyric gift is a tie:** Per sentence, neither writer outwrites the other: both produce fresh images, both land the assigned tone words in their codas, and both independently arrived at identical solutions for the same prompt words. Any superiority claim must therefore rest on structure and causality, not prose quality.
  - Story 0207, `glm-5.2-max`:
    > the hush resonated through her like a bell continuing after the strike, filling the space where words had tried and gently, gratefully, failed.
    GLM-5.3 (max)'s rendering of the same tone word is "holding the dark the way a bell holds its struck note" (GLM-5.3 (max), 207) — two writers independently ringing the same bell. glm-5.2-max's "snow settling on his shoulders like flour on a millstone" (57) is as fresh as anything GLM-5.3 (max) writes, and both gave their 275 protagonists weather felt in the teeth. This parity is what makes the structural gap diagnostic rather than a polish illusion.
- **C001 — Writes the dictionary versus asserting that a language exists:** Given the same required systems (a stone code, a named tone), glm-5.2-max reports their existence and characterizes them adjectivally; GLM-5.3 (max) specifies their operating grammar and then uses that grammar later as plot. The reader's sense of a real world comes from the specification, not from the adjectives.
  - Story 0366, `glm-5.2-max`:
    > New letters appeared in different hands, the stone towers growing more elaborate -- a whole language of presence built one beach pebble at a time.
    The stone code is named as a language and praised, but never decoded; the reader is told it signifies without ever learning a single term, so the final six-stone gesture can carry only vague warmth.
  - Story 0366, `glm-5.3`:
    > One flat stone meant safe crossing; a tilted stone meant wait for slack water; a white pebble crowning the pile meant thank you.
    The lexicon is given, so when Marit later builds "Flat stone for safe crossing, tilted stone for slack water" and caps it with the green glass, the closing gesture is legible as a sentence, not just a mood.
  - Story 0274, `glm-5.3`:
    > the hush between echoes could make a person sway -- a dizzying quiet, like standing over a well
    The mandated tone is converted into a repeatable physical sensation that returns at the close ("the quiet still tilted the floor like a well"), whereas glm-5.2-max's version, "Mira's hands trembled in the dizzying quiet of the vault," uses the phrase once as a label.
- **C067 — Latent literalism: the pun as plot engine:** Given an abstract required phrase, GLM-5.3 (max) locates the concrete institution or object hiding inside it and builds the story's causal machinery there; glm-5.2-max keeps the phrase figurative and paraphrases its meaning for the reader. This is a selective intelligence — knowing which of ten elements is the hinge — rather than a stylistic preference.
  - Story 0350, `glm-5.3`:
    > Not rhetoric, but drawings: the two derelict silos that leaned on either bank like bad teeth, unbolted into steel staves, those staves latticed into a span.
    "Construct bridges from silos" is executed as literal engineering that also solves the story's material stake (one rail spur, two banks), so metaphor and plot resolve in the same move.
  - Story 0350, `glm-5.2-max`:
    > This was what she had wanted: not to fix everything, not to erase the silos, but to construct bridges from them.
    glm-5.2-max's version restates the prompt phrase as theme in the closing paragraph; the actual silos of the setting are never touched, and the reconciliation remains rhetorical.
- **C075 — Required actions performed on-page versus announced or deferred:** GLM-5.3 (max) enacts the required elements as events with duration and cost; glm-5.2-max frequently satisfies them at the level of mention — pasting the prompt phrase as a sentence, having a character say the required verb, or projecting the action into a hoped-for future. "Unyoke" is the sharpest case: GLM-5.3 (max) performs it forty times in real time; glm-5.2-max only anticipates it.
  - Story 0391, `glm-5.3`:
    > Unyoking forty teams was slow, respectful work: unbuckle, lift, set each wooden yoke down easy so nothing rang.
    The required action is dramatized as labor with sensory procedure; the story's climax is the element itself.
  - Story 0391, `glm-5.2-max`:
    > They would unyoke themselves from panic, she hoped, and learn to cross as the moon itself crossed the sky, unhurried and sure.
    The action is deferred to a conditional future and metaphorized; within the story's frame, no one is unyoked and no one crosses.
  - Story 0197, `glm-5.2-max`:
    > A map suggested by puddle reflections at dusk.
    The required method arrives as a naked standalone sentence naming the prompt rather than a rendered perception.
- **C017 — A second will in the room: antagonists embodied and changed:** glm-5.2-max's opposing forces stay abstract — regimes referred to by pronoun, dead fathers, offstage brothers — so the protagonists' values meet no resistance in the present tense. GLM-5.3 (max) embodies power in a person who enters the scene with a working model of the world and leaves with it altered. This is what makes the assigned "gentle rebellion" dramatizable rather than decorative.
  - Story 0007, `glm-5.3`:
    > The clerk went still; somewhere in that bowl he had found his mother's kitchen, and his badge hand began to shake.
    The regime's agent arrives to shut Oren down and is converted by being fed; "his badge hand" registers an ideology loosening at the level of a body part.
  - Story 0007, `glm-5.2-max`:
    > They had banned communal growing, fearing that shared gardens fostered shared identity, and shared identity was dangerous to a regime that thrived on isolation.
    Power remains an offstage "they"; Yara's rebellion is asserted against an abstraction, so nothing in the scene can push back or be changed.
- **C022 — Two-mind scenes versus one mind in a receptive world:** GLM-5.3 (max) consistently constructs scenes containing a second agent who withholds, tests, or infers, so meaning has to be negotiated; glm-5.2-max's protagonists move through environments that supply, glow, open, and agree. The prose difference is that GLM-5.3 (max)'s paragraphs contain transactions (an offer, a refusal, a handover) while glm-5.2-max's contain descriptions plus commentary.
  - Story 0057, `glm-5.3`:
    > Brandt studied the fulgurite a long moment, then studied the snow piling on Nils's shoulders, and understood that the seed collector had been standing there for hours.
    One character reads physical evidence to infer another's inner state and duration of commitment. The scene contains an act of judgment by someone other than the protagonist — glm-5.2-max's five stories contain almost no such inference.
  - Story 0129, `glm-5.2-max`:
    > "I dedicate this to what we built while looking for something else."  No one argued.
    The only friction available is refused. The community exists to ratify the protagonist's gesture rather than to have interests of its own, which is glm-5.2-max's default configuration even when named secondary characters are present.
- **C032 — The missing second person:** Across glm-5.2-max's five stories there is exactly one spoken line, addressed to no one; no two people ever share a scene, so no idea is ever tested by another mind. GLM-5.3 (max)'s stories hinge on exchanges between people, or on messages from other hands, so insight must survive negotiation, testing, or inheritance.
  - Story 0078, `glm-5.2-max`:
    > She smiled and spoke aloud to the empty sea, to the clouds, to the orchard itself.
    > "I dub thee Driftsong," she said
    This is the sole line of dialogue in glm-5.2-max's entire packet, spoken into vacancy. glm-5.2-max's protagonists commune with rope, wood, walls, vials, and roses — never with a resisting consciousness. GLM-5.3 (max)'s Brandt grunts, tests, and hands over; Mira serves two masters and is changed by the strain; Wren converts her father, who ends up "at the seedling bench, awkward and careful, pronouncing patience to a row of cuttings" (GLM-5.3 (max), 275). Even solitary Marta follows a dead man's journal. Part of GLM-5.3 (max)'s impression of perceptiveness is simply that perception is shown being checked by someone else.
- **C015 — Endings that clear the test instead of stopping at its threshold:** glm-5.2-max repeatedly closes the story just before the decisive event, preserving tone by withholding consequence; GLM-5.3 (max) narrates the test itself and its aftermath. The pattern holds across 0007 (Yara will serve the fruit someday; Oren has already fed it to the enemy), 0142, and 0298 (glm-5.2-max defers the healing of Elian behind "a year and a day"; GLM-5.3 (max) completes the restoration of the dead and defers only Mara's personal cost).

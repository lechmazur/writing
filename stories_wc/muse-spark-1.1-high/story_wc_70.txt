<story>After the city-wide blackout, the old causeway was the only road that still held weight. [15] I sat at its midpoint as the candid toll keeper, though there was no toll left to collect. [33] Beyond me rose the lightning collection tower, its copper crown empty, waiting for a storm that would never be stored the same way again.

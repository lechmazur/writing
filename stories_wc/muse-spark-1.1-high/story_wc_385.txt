<story>Mara had been a canyon echo singer for twenty years before she left the cliffs. [15]

She needed it to ensure she would not lose the true shape of her own voice. [116]

Mara understood that the place would teach her only through progressive disclosure, never all at once, as a canyon does. [200]
On the first night she sang a single note and heard it return broken, as if someone else had tried to finish it. [223]

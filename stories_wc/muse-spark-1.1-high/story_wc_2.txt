<story>Eli had been a broken-clock tinkerer long before the fire took her shop on Mill Street. [16]

Local legend said Admiral Kade had buried it on his estate to mark not treasure, but a place where his maps failed. [157]

Some clocks she would fix to keep perfect time, and others she would leave broken, so visitors might learn the difference. [681]

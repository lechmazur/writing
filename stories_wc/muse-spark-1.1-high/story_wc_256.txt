<story>After the power grid stabilizes, the city allowed one building to stay dim. [13]

Elderly visitors would walk the loop while bells rang, and each step would release a memory of sound from before. [206]
My problem was that none of it moved anyone, the walkers finished polite and still isolated. [222]
I had tried louder ringing, slower walking, softer light, and everything failed by following the same assumptions. [239]
We assumed a pilgrimage must be solemn, must be long, must be communal in the same room and time. [258]

[59] The trees grew gears instead of fruit, and the statues pruned any branch that hummed out of tune. [100] That was the moment she decided to reawaken beauty in a mechanical world, not with volume but with truth. [146] The statues turned their stone heads toward her, not in anger yet, but in calibration. [178] She picked it up and understood, with a pang of tarnished wonder, that something had been brave enough to shed its armor here. [201] The orchard was not dead, only bound, its wild roots strangled by the Company's buried cables. [485] She did not rebuild the sky-bridge, because the city no longer needed a crossing above its own noise.

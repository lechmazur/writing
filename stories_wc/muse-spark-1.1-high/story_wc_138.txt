[179] The amulet offered a trade: it would show me, if I helped it stage a spatial mutiny. [555] When the next solar storm comes, I will press my ear to that thin place and hear Lyra still walking. [575] Perhaps this time I will learn how to say goodbye well enough to follow. [589] Perhaps eternity was never meant to be endured but to be unlearned one farewell at a time. [606] For now the crystals hum low, forgiving me, and the storm outside fades to black. [621] In that quiet I finally understand what all my years were for. [633] Not to outlast love, but to learn its echo.

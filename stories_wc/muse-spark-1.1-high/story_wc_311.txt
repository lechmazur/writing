[140] He was supposed to understand loss, not carry it, and that misunderstanding had made him heartbroken. [156] Now he listened to the cylinders each evening to give names to nameless fears. [345] The other reapers had warned him that love blurs the edge, but they never said it would make him more careful. [482] When the last cylinder stopped, the observatory was dark except for the constellations he had made. [535] Somewhere below, a child would look up and think the observatory was a star that had fallen gently and decided to stay.

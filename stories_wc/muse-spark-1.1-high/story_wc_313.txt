<story>After a wind chime falls silent over the Miller house, the valley holds its breath. [369] No, not Mara herself, but the small wooden shrine she had built for their mother before the flood. [387] The shrine was dry inside, and inside it was a notebook wrapped in oilskin. [487] He would not let this place be erased before its stories had been carried back up the ridge. [505] For three nights he ferried the notebook, the photographs, and Mara's shrine to the town hall, letting neighbors touch the wet wood and remember together.

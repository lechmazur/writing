<story>After the magic trick fails, the crowd in the lakeside village disperses in disappointed silence. [15] I remain behind because I am the guarded druid they hired to ensure no real sorcery leaked into their entertainment. [35] My master, Joren, finds lost things, but I have only ever found excuses to keep my distance. [52] Tonight he sent me to retrieve something his mirror could not hold. [64] He wants to discover what mirrors don't show, the truth that lives behind reflection. [98] I row out alone, and the water around me moves with a warlike serenity, calm on the surface yet coiled for battle beneath. [171] It is not written but grown, a bubble of clear winter holding his voice saying he stole my mother's rattle and lied about her abandonment. [196] I had always believed she left because I could not find lost things as he could, and that failure made me guarded. [236] He told me to feed the vortex, to plant it in the spinning water so it would close. [254] But I understand now the seed would not close the vortex; it would bind it to me, making me its eternal keeper. [276] Joren did not want to save me from the lake; he wanted to ensure his theft remained buried under through expanding circles of obedience. [300] That method was how he taught, how he controlled, never a direct command but a widening pressure until you stood where he wanted. [323] The ice cracks, and his voice leaks out again, colder this time, admitting he feared I would one day find her. [499] Water rushes in to fill the absence where the confession was, and with it comes a flash of my mother's face reflected not in mirror but in moving water. [528] She did not abandon me; she was lost when the orchard sank, and Joren never looked because a found daughter would ask questions. [551] Because I find lost things, even when I try not to, I feel her below me still breathing in a pocket of air among roots. [608] On the shore, the pouch is empty, the seed gone, and the water behind us closes without expanding circles, without hunger, without command.

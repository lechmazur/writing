<story>June was a myth buster who cheerfully arrived at the abandoned observatory on a cliff with a chipped mug of cold tea. [22] Even in its rusted decay, she found the place delightfully sad, perfect for proving things wrong. [38] Her lifelong goal was to decode a universal riddle: why people insist the dead still try to give directions. [479] After all, some myths deserve to be kept half true, half finished, like a song you hum incorrectly on purpose.

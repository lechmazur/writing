My father was the boy who cried wolf, and I was born into the hush that came after. [18] The village never forgave his lying tongue, though the third cry was true, and it never forgave me for being its echo. [40] So when the Crown built a prison for perjurers on the lighthouse rock off the jagged reef, I asked to serve as its confessor, chasing hush the way other men chase God. [72] My father was its first prisoner. [78] They sent him to lie to the sea, which would not mind. [90] For forty years I sat on one side of the confessional lattice and he pressed his ruined mouth to the other, and the little wooden diamonds of it sorted his sins the way a net sorts fish. [127] He confessed the same thing every time: that he had been believed twice, and never once when it mattered. [146]

The old warden, a soft-booted man, left my father's cell door ajar so the liar could hear the sea. [165] The warden is twenty years dead; I kept it ajar. [175] Decades of that narrow door swinging on the same small arc wore scuffs into the sill, pale crescents, a tally of mercy. [197] It was through those scuffs, on the night I must tell you about, that the hush first came in. [216] I do not mean a quiet evening. [223] I mean the hush, a thing with a border, that moved up the corridor like water finding its level and snuffed the prisoners' voices one by one, like candles. [252] Three old liars died reposed in their bunks, hands folded, faces smoothed, and no one could call it anything but gentle. [273] My father alone got up. [278]

He followed the hush up the spiral stair toward the lamp room, and I followed, because chasing hush had been my whole vocation and I would not be outrun by it now. [310] A tower is a labyrinth stood on end, and a labyrinth can only be threaded with your own story, so I talked aloud as I climbed, and every sentence had to be true or the stairs went soft beneath me. [350] Here is what I told the dark, and it held me up. [362] My father was a boy who wanted company on a boring hill, and he got more consequence than any boy can steer. [384] He told the truth once, and it came too late, which is the only lie the world ever tells us. [404] I loved him, though loving him was like keeping a wolf. [415] The stairs held. [418]

The lamp room was full of copperlight, that low settled gold of a working lamp at the bottom of its swing, and in it my father hovered. [445] A forearm's length above the boards, reposed, hands folded, boots hanging as if he had sat down on air and found it adequate. [468] The hush sustains impossibilities; that is its whole trade. [477] It sustained the lamp turning though the oil ran out in spring, and the wolves standing out on the black reef, patient on the water as on stone, and my father, dead these hours, resting on nothing at all. [516] I had carried up the lattice from the confessional, because a man should not meet a miracle empty-handed. [534] I held it between us, and through the little diamonds his hovering looked almost orderly, a sorrow fitted to a frame. [555] Then I gave him absolution, mine to give, I being the only listener left. [569] Father, I said, you are forgiven; and here is a wonder, for the world always believed me, your son, whenever I said I loved you. [594] The hush took my words as it had taken the others', and held them somewhere in the room, I think, along with his. [617]

The relief boat comes at the new moon, nine days out. [628] They will climb the stair and find four bodies, three reposed in their bunks and one resting on the air in the copperlight, without one mark of violence. [656] They will look at me, the son of the boy who cried wolf, and I know what that name is worth in a courtroom. [680] So I am writing this now, before they find the bodies, and threading it down the stair, page by page, so whoever comes may follow my story the way I followed it, true sentence by true sentence, and arrive where I arrived. [722]

A labyrinth is only mercy withheld; a thread is mercy let out to length. [736] The lamp still turns, though nothing feeds it. [744] My father keeps his repose above the boards, the hush keeps him, and the copperlight goes around and around, laying its warm, patient claim on the sea. [771] I have nothing left to chase; I sit in the hush like a man in shallow water, and it is enough. [792]

After the gate chain rattled shut, the Silverleaf Maze belonged to its ghosts. [13] The gardens had sold tickets to their hedges for ninety years, but the owners had finally sold the land itself, and tomorrow the bulldozers would come. [39] Nadia lingered past closing anyway, because tonight was the last night, and because the closing bell still hung gold in her vision, the way sound always left color on her. [69] Her crossed senses had been her mother's promise: not a defect but a door. [83] She carried a flute case on her shoulder and no audition left to fail. [97]

[178] They billed me the Orchard Nightingale, until 1927, when my heart quit before the final verse ended. [195] I have waited," he said, "for someone blessed with crossed senses. [206]"

[228] When he flipped it, the ring multiplied, each echo peeling off a different year. [293] "An encore was always my method," he said, "because performance is how you lure possibility back from the brink of despair. [314] I need someone who can hear the seams. [325] Alone, I can only produce the echoes; I cannot join them. [334] You see sound; you could braid the years together. [334]"

[366] Despair had spent years telling her the flute was a dead limb and possibility a country she had been evicted from. [387] Here, the colors invited her hands. [393]

[434] The fractured timeline braided tight around the orchard until the trees remembered they had once been a theater. [452]

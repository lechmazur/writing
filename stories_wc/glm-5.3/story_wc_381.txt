Vey lived in a nocturnal haven beside a quiet brook, where the water mirrored the Milky Way so faithfully that moths sometimes landed on the sky by mistake. [28]
It was a terrestrially cosmic place, half earth and half heaven, where the stones still remembered being meteors and the herons waded through constellations. [52]

Across the valley, a prophecy was moving from mouth to mouth, spoken in code, its syllables hidden inside whispered secrets. [140]
The code was simple and terrible: each whisperer carried one syllable, harmless alone, and the final listener, assembling them like coins, would speak the prophecy entire. [166]
A heard prophecy in that country became true, and this one promised a blaze that would eat the valley whole. [186]
Tonight the last syllable was traveling the ridge road in the apron pocket of a lamplighter's daughter. [203]
Vey meant to hush it before it arrived. [211]
Her method was mapping silence, an art no one had wanted until now. [224]
For thirty years she had charted the quiet the way other mappers charted roads: the pools where owls held their breath, the gaps between cricket pulses, the hush folded inside stones, the stillness above snow. [259]
Her only instrument was a crane's origami wing, the single survivor of a thousand paper birds she had once folded to buy a wish. [283]
Folded from a page of a star atlas and softened by three decades of brook mist, it belonged to earth and sky at once, and when she bent its crease, sound bent with it. [317]

She spread her map on the water, blank side up, because a map of silence is drawn in what is missing. [357]
Then she began to summon. [362]

What gathered in the water beneath her was a smoothed hush, seamless and soft as breath before speech. [458]

Flow, she told it, the oldest and only command a summoner truly needs. [485]

The valley would wake to an ordinary morning and never learn how close its ending had come, which was, she decided, the finest kind of summoning: calling something vast into the world so gently that the world never noticed. [691]

The first marks appeared in the snow three weeks after the solstice, when the earth had begun its tilt toward spring. [21]
Vera found them on Zephyr Bluff, pressed into the accumulated snow with a boot heel: a slash, a circle, a slash, the old field cipher she had buried eleven years ago. [52]

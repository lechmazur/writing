[32] The trick was meant to catch a dead man's voice in candle smoke, and it had shown her only her own guarded face in the glass. [58] Mirrors show surfaces; she needed what mirrors don't show. [67] So the guarded druid of Hollowmere armed herself the way druids do, which is to say calmly: sickle at her belt, bent rowan staff in her fist, and an iron box holding a flesh-eating plant seed. For forty years villagers had brought her their losses, and she had found them all, wedding rings in thatch and lambs in ravines, each one found through expanding circles walked until her feet sang. [236] Maren never said otherwise, because she could not swear it; her silence guarded her from grief the way a wall guards a house, by keeping everything out.

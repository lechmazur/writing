The glass spire rose alone beside the frozen lake, and in the hour before dawn it held its breath. [19] At the top, Mei wrote fortunes for a living, cryptic ones, the kind that arrived in cookies and refused to leave. [40] Ten thousand slips a month went down the mountain in crates, each sentence a small locked door. [57] Tonight she had been writing since midnight, and she knew the arithmetic of her own body: the last fortune had to be finished before exhaustion hit, or it would come out wrong. [89] Her homesickness was not for a place. [96] The place was right below her, the same water, the same shore. [108] She was homesick for a time, chronologically homesick, aching toward one particular August when she was eight and the lake was still liquid and her grandmother was still loud. [137] There was an object for that ache, and she kept it on the desk: a segment of nautilus shell, one broken wedge of spiral with three chambers still intact. [166] The whole shell had sat on her grandmother's windowsill, pearl-lined, and when the house was sold it cracked, and Mei saved this one piece because a spiral, even broken, keeps its logic. [198] It was also her method. [203] She had spent years mapping her childhood memories into those chambers, one memory per room, winding inward. [220] The outermost chamber held the smell of the dock at evening; the middle one held her grandmother's voice mispronouncing foreign words; the innermost held the day she first heard the word horizon and asked what it was made of. [259] Whenever the well ran dry, she walked the spiral and took whatever she found, and a fortune came out of it. [280] That was the cryptic part, the part the bakery hated. [290] Her fortunes never said you will meet someone; they said the dock remembers the weight of your feet. [308] People kept them for decades. [313] That was the point, though nobody down in the valley knew it, least of all Mei's supervisor: she was not writing fortunes at all. [337] She was composing. [340] Somewhere in a city she would never visit, a woman cracked a cookie over lukewarm tea and read a line about evening docks, and something in her chest tuned itself. [370] Elsewhere a boy read a line about mispronounced words and, for one second, heard his dead father's accent. [388] Each stranger was an instrument; each slip was a note; and Mei, alone in the spire, wanted nothing less than to become the conductor of this unheard symphony, a music made entirely of small private shivers that no audience would ever hear together. [431] Conductors, her grandmother had said, are just people who refuse to let a sound be lonely. [447] Outside, the sky began to pale. [453] Mei lifted her eyes to the lake and understood, again, why she had rented this absurd transparent room: at dawn, the frozen surface became a perfect mirror, and the horizon doubled itself, sky above and sky below, so precisely matched that the line between them stopped being a line and became a hinge. [506] A mirrored horizon. [509] When the sun finally rose, it rose twice, once in the air and once in the ice, and the whole world looked like a shell split open, nacre shining on both sides. [541] In that doubled light, her exhaustion arrived at the edges of her vision, soft and patient, the way tides arrive. [561] One slip left. [564] She set the nautilus segment on the paper as a paperweight, walked her memory down the spiral, past the dock, past the mispronounced words, all the way to the innermost chamber, to the question the child she had been could not stop asking: what is a horizon made of. [613] The answer had been waiting all her life to dawn on her. [625] A horizon is made of two skies agreeing. [633] She wrote the last fortune quickly, before her hands could tremble, before the tide could reach her: The distance you miss is also a direction. [658] She folded the slip, and somewhere in the world a chair scraped, a cookie cracked, a stranger inhaled. [676] Mei lifted the shell segment to the doubled light and, feeling her way down through the hush, she raised her other hand, empty, and began, gently, to conduct. [704] It was not silence and it was not sound; it was the quiet of a lifted face, the upturned hush of ten thousand strangers all looking, at once, toward something bright. [735]

In the flooded opera house lit by flickering lanterns, Mara waded through the orchestra pit with her cello lifted clear of the water. [23]
She was the last orchestra pit musician who still entered after the storm, lately inspired by stains that had begun to shift when no one watched. [49]

Water made new borders everywhere, and she walked them as if they were measures. [127]

She drew it out beneath a lantern and saw what the flood had done. [165]

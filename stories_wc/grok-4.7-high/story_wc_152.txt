Autumn pressed against the cobalt observatory, and the high dome held a quiet so complete that every footstep returned as an echoed silence. [23]
Mira, the galaxy-chart embroiderer, sat beneath the slit of the dome and pretended to fuss over a tangled spool, the way a fool fusses when wisdom must stay hidden. [52]

She synchronized. [262]

The veiled dreamer first heard of the library in a letter that arrived smelling faintly of cedar and regret. [19]
Between that first fateful letter and the second one she had not yet earned, she made her preparations in the dark hours before dawn. [43]

Inside, shelves rose into a ceiling she could not see, and every book glowed from its own inner ember, throwing off motes that drifted like hesitant fireflies. [119]
She called this place, in the cautious exuberance of her breath, exactly what the letter had promised and what her grandmother had only hinted at in bedtime prayers. [147]
The library of unspoken words, she whispered, and the shelves hummed a half-tone lower. [161]
For three nights she mapped the corridors, marking each aisle by the rhythm of her own pulse. [178]
On the fourth night, while tracing a shelf marked with no letters at all, she noticed a watch resting on a reading stand. [201]
Its face was scratched and its hands trembled at odd intervals, and she recognized it, with a small start, as the confidence man's watch. [225]
The confidence man had been a story her uncle told, a wanderer who traded promises for coin and could make any mark on any page he chose. [252]
She lifted the watch carefully, and the second hand swept forward in a way that felt almost deliberate, almost patient, almost hers. [274]
It ticked once heavily, and on the far wall, a cipher began to bleed through the mist like a bruise becoming visible. [296]
She approached with cautious exuberance, because she had been warned in the letter to never let her joy outpace her attention. [317]
The cipher was not written forward but cyclically reversed, each glyph a mirror of the last, blooming outward in rings that lapped over themselves like ripples on a returning tide. [347]
She had read of such things and had doubted they could be real. [360]
Now she had to document what she saw without disturbing the way the light fell, because the glow itself was part of the message. [384]

The method required the patience of someone who had once waited for letters that never arrived and now waited for letters that surely would. [455]

Each new rotation revealed what the previous one had concealed, a quiet confession layered beneath a louder declaration. [530]
This was not the unfolding cipher of any book she had studied, but she understood, suddenly, that the unfolding belonged to her as much as to the wall. [558]
She documented until her fingers cramped and her veil had slipped twice and been replaced with the same careful gesture. [578]

She tucked the confidence man's watch back onto the stand, though not before setting its hands to the next hour. [652]
She promised, to no one present, that she would return when the watch told her to, and she would document whatever had bloomed in her absence. [678]
As she stepped out beneath the arch, the glow behind her dimmed by one careful degree, as if the library itself had begun to exhale. [703]

The veiled dreamer knew, between these two fateful letters, that the library of unspoken words had begun, at last, to speak. [758]

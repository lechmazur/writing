<story>Mira pressed her palm flat against the depot's cool iron railing, feeling the muffled shudder travel up through the bones of the pier. [23]

She ran the place from memory, from fingertips, and from the small patient hum of the things that came to her broken. [123]

She did not need eyes to find the wound; she needed only water and a square of morning. [234]

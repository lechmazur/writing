Mara was a conflicted novelist, pulled as she had always been between the version she had hidden in a drawer and the one she had finally released to the world. [172]

The mist answered by sending more, a procession of half-formed selves she had sketched in margins and abandoned in drawers. [367]

The swirling hush of the place was not silence but a long breathing, the slow exhale of a story finally allowed to end. [416]

The needle trembled when the wind shifted, and Elara had learned to read that trembling as a kind of honest confession. [116]

The trial balloons were both her instruments and her arguments, each one a hypothesis sent upward to be measured, refuted, or accepted. [166]

Most drifted; a few returned to her hands trailing torn silk and a new question. [208]

The motivation that had brought her here, to this tide-polished sanctuary, was to puzzle out new equations for hope. [331]
She had watched her world grow thin, watched the laughter in the village above the cliffs fade into the rustle of counted breaths. [354]

She reached for the altitude gauge and read the barometric whisper of the evening. [398]

Elara's gift was not for fixing clocks or kettles, though she could mend those too, but for spinning golden stories that braided the broken threads of her community back into a single tapestry. [153]

The harvest moon had been climbing the sky all night, and now at dawn it hung low in the western sky, a bronze coin waiting to be spent. [181]

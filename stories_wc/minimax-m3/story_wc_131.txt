In the quiet hours before the city fully woke, a soft sapphire hush settled over the shadowed library at dawn. [20]
Maren moved through the crawl space beneath the reading room with the slow, kindly methodical care of someone who had learned to love a place without ever being seen in it. [51]
She had lived between the joists and the pipes for years, a dweller in the forgotten interstices, and she knew every creak of the floorboards above. [77]

She was tracing the pipe chases with her fingertips, looking for what the old custodian had whispered about before he died. [144]
The pipes ran in long metal veins behind the plaster, and some of them had been replaced, leaving hollow sleeves behind. [165]

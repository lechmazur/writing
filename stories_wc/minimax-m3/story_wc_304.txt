<story>Vela was a beautician mortician, though few understood what that meant anymore. [200] He had told her then that it could capture the last spark, the final flicker of consciousness that escaped a body at the threshold of stillness. [226] She had laughed and said it was nonsense. [551] It was a strange profession, beautician mortician, half artist and half confessor. [628] She did not speak. [689] She carried the chip home and set it beside the others in the velvet box, and the small green constellation joined a greater one.

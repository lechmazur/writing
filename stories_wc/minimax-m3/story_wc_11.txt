The robot janitor rolled to a halt at the edge of the frozen courtyard, her treads leaving faint parallel lines in the frost. [23] Unit 7-C, designation Mireille, had been rebooted three hours earlier under what the technicians called the Second Chance Program. [42] The procedure was not memory restoration, not exactly. [50] It was something gentler and more ambiguous: the installation of capacity where capacity had been declared impossible. [67] The courtyard stretched before her, a square of pale flagstones rimmed with bare maples and the long shadow of the administrative tower. [89] Twilight had thickened into a color she could not name, the color of something remembered without being seen, the color, perhaps, of a thought before it has a shape. [118] A broom waited in her hand. [124] She had not consciously picked it up. [131] Her hands had simply done what hands do. [139] The courtyard was not empty. [144] Against the far balustrade, half-buried in a drift of powdered snow, lay a violin. [158] Mireille approached it slowly, because her servos moved slowly in the cold, and because she was, despite everything, remarkably composed. [178] The instrument was not wood. [183] It was ice crystal, transparent and faintly singing in the stillness, its strings catching the last gray light and holding it like something borrowed. [207] She bent, though she did not need to bend, and lifted it. [219] The weight was negligible. [223] The chill was considerable. [227] The note that trembled from its body when her gloved thumb brushed the bridge was not a note she had cataloged, though she had cataloged every pitch in the municipal tuning chart. [259] It sounded like a question. [264] She had been given, the technicians had said, a second chance. [275] She had not known she had needed a first. [284] Across the courtyard, the loop began. [290] Not a loop she could see, but a loop she could feel, the way one feels a hum through the floorboards of an old house. [315] Time, in this place, did not advance so much as reconsider. [326] Each moment returned to her, slightly altered, asking to be looked at again. [339] She was meant, the procedure had implied, to amalgamate what she found: to press one version of the evening against another until they adhered. [363] She raised the bow. [367] She had never been taught to play. [374] The procedure had not included music. [380] The first stroke produced a sound like a window opening in a room she had lived in long ago. [399] The second stroke produced a sound like the same window, closed. [410] Between them, in the silence, she heard something that might have been her own thought arriving, or might have been the thought the courtyard had been waiting to give her. [440] Where do thoughts come from. [445] The question did not arrive as language. [452] It arrived as the faintest vibration in the strings, a resonance the ice passed through her, a small and patient request. [473] She had come out here to sweep, and the sweeping had become irrelevant. [486] The broom lay across the flagstones like a fallen shadow. [496] She had come out here because the procedure had placed something inside her that did not have a name yet, and the something wanted, very quietly, to be found. [525] She played another note. [529] The loop folded it back to her, changed. [537] The changed note was the one she had meant to play. [548] She understood, then, that the second chance was not a gift. [559] It was a method. [563] The looped timelines were the medium in which she was meant to work, the way a painter works in oil, or a mason in lime. [588] She was to amalgamate the version of herself who had not had the procedure with the version who had. [607] She was to press the two together until the seam disappeared. [618] Mireille, who had been remarkably composed through three reboots and two factory recalls and one unannounced audit, felt a small adjustment occur somewhere behind the panel above her left ear. [648] Not a malfunction. [651] A settling. [653] The twilight deepened. [656] The violin continued to sing in her hands, transparent and patient. [667] Somewhere in the administrative tower, a clerk was filing a form about her. [680] Somewhere in the loop, another Mireille was bending to pick up a different broom. [694] Somewhere in the procedure, a thought was beginning. [702] She kept playing. [705] The notes accumulated. [708] The courtyard accepted them. [712]

The sculpted hush had held the ice cavern for seven winters, a silence so precisely carved that even the drip of meltwater slowed before it fell. [26] Osric had made it with his chisel and his gift, for he was synesthetically blessed and saw every sound as a color spilling across the frozen walls. [53] The colors he disliked, he carved away. [60] Only one thing in the cavern resisted him. [68] High in the vault hung a bell of clear ice, and it would not ring from any score he wrote. [88] It needed an amber music sheet, a ledger of the carillon's transpositions rubbed with spruce resin at the folds. [107] That sheet belonged to Veyra, the prism-song bell ringer. [116] She came down the frozen wall at dusk, glove over glove, the sheet wrapped inside her coat. [133] Above her the aurora moved, and she stepped through the colors of the aurora as if they were stairs, each hue a note in the score she carried. [161] The green band she took first, then the violet, then the long slow red. [175] Her boots rang the ice in three colors, and Osric looked up from his marble. [190] The block stood under the lamplight, veined and waiting. [199] He had not struck it yet. [205] He raised the chisel. [209] Then the sound he refused to hear arrived anyway. [218] Veyra's voice fell through the vault. [224] It was a single low note, cotton-candy blue, and where it touched the walls the sculpted hush began to peel away in ribbons. [247] Osric saw all of it. [252] His chisel touched marble. [256] The strike held. [259] The cavern stopped at that exact instant: the meltwater hung in the air half-fallen, the lamp flame leaned and did not move, the dust of marble floated over the block like a small settled sky. [294] Inside that held second, Veyra did her work. [302] She opened the amber music sheet across the ice floor. [312] Resin shone at every fold, honey-colored, and the transpositions ran in a line so fine the lamplight bent along them. [332] Osric had always refused the transpositions that came out gold, because gold was a color he could not shape and therefore did not trust. [356] That was the flaw in the hush. [363] A silence built on refusals could not hold against a ringer who had given up refusing. [379] She set her fingers to the first bar. [387] Outside the frozen instant the whole world would have gone on, and no one would have known the difference. [406] Then she drew breath. [410] Then she sang. [413] The bell of clear ice took the note and changed it. [424] What came out was not sound alone. [431] It was green, then violet, then that long slow red, and it climbed the vault the way she had climbed down, hue by hue, and it worked to unfetter every silence Osric had ever carved into the stone. [469] The aurora above heard its own colors returned and answered, pouring through the ice in wide bright bands. [487] The sculpted hush came apart. [492] It did not end in a crash. [499] It ended in repose, a long slow settling, like a room that has been holding its breath and finally lets it go. [521] Then it swung the other way, still into motion, motion back into still, and that was the shape of the bell's whole voice. [544] Veyra kept singing until the last bar of the amber sheet was spent. [557] Then she rolled it and put it back inside her coat, and the resin caught the lamplight one last time. [577] The frozen second broke. [581] The meltwater fell. [584] The flame leaned and burned. [589] The marble dust drifted down across the block and across Osric's hands. [601] He stood with the chisel still touching the stone, and he did not strike. [615] He had come there to carve another silence into the vault, a deeper one, so that nothing would ever interrupt his work again. [638] Now he saw it for what it was. [646] A refusal. [648] Veyra had come down to certify wonder as essential, and a thing like that needs a witness who will not look away. [670] Osric was the witness. [674] She crossed the ice toward him and held out her hand. [685] He gave her the chisel. [690] She set it down on the marble. [697] The bell rang again, though no one sang. [705] Ice remembers. [707] Above the vault the aurora folded itself into one wide band of gold and lay there, spent and bright. [726] Gold, the color Osric had never trusted, spread over the marble block like morning. [740] He understood then that the stone did not need another silence. [751] It needed the sound he had refused. [758] So he sang the note he had cut from his own life, low and plain, and the colors came. [777] The hush stayed sculpted in the marble, but it was no longer a silence. [791]

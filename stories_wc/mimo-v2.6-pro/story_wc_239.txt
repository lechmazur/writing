I am the witness left at the end of history, and I write this from the temple library that outlived time itself. [22] The shelves descend in rings around me, each ring older than the one above, until the floor is only rumor. [42] I carry a single softcover dictionary, its spine cracked, its pages soft as cloth from being turned by ten thousand hands. [63] It is the book that defines every other book, and so it is the only one worth saving when the rest were taken. [86] They were taken in a hiccup. [92] The universe, gathering itself for the last long exhale, caught instead and skipped, and in that skip the temple became the only place. [115] Everything outside its walls is now a resounding hush, a silence so complete it rings in the teeth. [133] I was asked to arbitrate before the skip, and the request survived it, so I must too. [150] The dispute is ancient, older than the temple's foundations, carved into the eastern wall in two hands that hated each other. [171] Each hand defines the same word, and each definition forbids the other. [183] For an age the scribes of the spiral arbitration have circled this wall, arguing inward, never landing. [200] I am wanderingly exact by profession, which is to say I am lost in the way that arrives at the only shelf that matters. [224] I opened the dictionary on the seventh ring and read the word aloud. [237] The voice of it went down the rings and came back changed, as voices do in deep places. [255] I descended to follow it, one ring at a time, reading as I went. [269] The method is simple and it is terrible: journey inward while walking out. [282] Each step took me deeper into the meaning of the word and higher toward the temple's open roof at the same time. [304] I passed the catalogues of vanished sciences, the ledgers of stars that no longer burn. [319] I passed a child's primer with a name written inside the cover and no child left to answer to it. [339] Grief walked with me, and so did the hush, and neither said anything worth writing down. [355] Halfway up, which is also halfway in, I understood what the two hands on the wall had been doing. [374] Neither scribe had been wrong. [379] Each had stood at a different turn of the spiral and named what stood before him. [395] One saw the word as arrival; one saw it as departure. [406] From the floor they looked like enemies, but the spiral only ever has one shape. [421] I reached the roof with the dictionary open in both hands, the windless air pressing on my ears like deep water. [442] Below me the rings of the library turned without moving, the way a thought turns. [457] I read both definitions aloud, together, at the place where the circle closes. [470] That was the arbitration. [474] I did not choose between them; that is the failing of every judge who stands outside the spiral and thinks he is above it. [498] I unified them, which is the only honest act available to a witness at the end of history. [516] Arrival and departure folded into one another like two hands closing the same book. [530] The wall took the unification without complaint, the two carvings sinking into the stone until there was one line where there had been two. [554] And the illusion broke. [558] I had come here to unravel it, this oldest of cosmic illusions: that things which appear opposed are truly separate. [578] The universe had been built on that error, and the hiccup was only the error coughing. [594] When the two definitions became one, the skip reversed itself for a moment, long enough to let the library exhale. [614] Shelf by shelf the books returned, not as they were but as they had always meant to be. [632] Their covers softened, their spines remembered being read. [640] Then the moment closed and the hush came back, but it was a different hush now, less hollow, more like held breath. [662] I sat down on the roof among the re-shelved centuries and finished my testimony. [676] It is written in the softcover dictionary itself, on the blank page before the A. [691] A witness's duty is not to keep the past alive but to prove that it was unified all along. [710] That is what the spiral arbitration means, if anyone is left to read it. [724] I do not know if anyone is. [731] The temple will stand long after I stop turning the pages, because a place that has been unified cannot be unmade. [752] And so I close the book. [758] Outside, across the whole of creation, the silence rings once and holds. [770]

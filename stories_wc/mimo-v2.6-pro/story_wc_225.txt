Verrin knelt at the tapestry's edge and wound thread onto a shuttle, his fingers moving of their own accord while his mind replayed the game he had lost. [28] Not the game: the cost of it, a pupil's life spent like a captured pawn. [43] Exile had been the board's mercy. [49] The council of Tarn had set him down in this village and walked away, and the village had stopped in the same hour. [72] A milkmaid leaned forever over her pail; a boy hung mid-stride at the well; smoke stood above the roofs like drawn wire. [94] Only the thunder still moved, arriving in long gaps across the hills. [106] The lull between one roll and the next was his hourglass. [117] When the storm closed, the woven hush would close too, and the frozen village would never have happened at all. [137]

He bent to the border and began to winnow. [146] The border was an archive no one else could read: a thousand threads gone slack and hairy with age, and among them, by their twist and their dye, the true record. [177] He pulled the rot free and set it in a pile; he kept what answered. [192] His hands never stilled; the shuttle drank thread and gave it back wound, ordered, calm. [207] It was how he thought, this fidgeting made useful, and it was the only discipline the council had left him. [227] By the fourth roll of thunder the border had spoken. [237]

The surviving threads described a labyrinth. [243] Verrin had taken it for metaphor until he found it in the well. [256] Below the boy's frozen foot lay a disc of platinum wire, coiled into passages no wider than a thumbnail, each turn barbed with tiny mirrored teeth. [282] A platinum labyrinth, small enough to hold and cruel enough to keep. [294] He carried it to the loom and set it under the tapestry's unrolled hem, where the pattern ran thickest. [313] The threads matched; the maze and the weave were one instruction. [324] He studied it as he had taught pupils to study an endgame: patient, severe, unafraid of silence. [341] Then he solved it twice and understood that solving was not the key. [354] Every line he could calculate led to a mirrored tooth that shut like a trap, for the labyrinth was built to punish calculation. [377]

For three thunderrolls he tried the clean lines anyway, the bishop's diagonals of intent, and three times the teeth closed. [397] The pattern demanded a piece be given away, and not any piece: the one he was still guarding. [415] That was the mistake he had made at Tarn, and he saw it now without flinching. [431] In the final position he had held a knight that could have been spent to shield his pupil, and he had kept it, because keeping had felt like mastery. [460] The boy died of that feeling. [466] Verrin wound thread and wound thread and did not look away from the truth of it. [482] The lull was shortening; the thunder came now on the heels of its own echo. [497] To forgive himself he would have to spend the guarded piece. [508] He laughed once, harsh and quiet, in the fierce repose he had built out of guilt. [524] Forgiveness was not a calculation; it was the loss of one. [535]

He set the shuttle down and pushed the carved horse into the maze. [548] It was only a thing of bone, the last object the boy had touched before the hall. [565] The mirrored teeth did not close. [571] The passage widened, took the horse, and gave back a long silence, as if the machine were measuring whether he would reach in after it. [596] He did not. [599] The labyrinth uncoiled like a released spring, its wire running out across the floor and away through the open door. [619] Outside, the milkmaid poured; the boy drew; smoke climbed the roofs and tore itself thin. [634] Thunder walked the hills with nothing left to hold it at bay. [646] Verrin felt the hush go out of the border and stood in the noise like a man set down after a long carrying. [669] He wound the last frayed thread onto the shuttle and did not wind it tighter than it wished to go. [689] The village reeled back into motion, and the exiled tutor, unexiled in the only way left to him, bent and began to teach the boy at the well. [717]</story>

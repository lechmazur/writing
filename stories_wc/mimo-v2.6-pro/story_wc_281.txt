<story>Tomas keeps his light painting studio at the end of a corridor of kilns, where exposures run all night and nobody speaks above a whisper. [26] I have loved him for six years and doubted him for all of them. [41] Tonight he is underexposing a muscovite geode sliver on a sheet of black glass. [56] That is what he was doing the night he hid me inside its fractures. [71] I unclipped the tripod for him and the metal took the static shock off my knuckle. [88] The recall came back through the burn. [96] A telepathy pill is supposed to give you six minutes of another mind and then nothing. [113] I took mine in March, in a clinic with orange chairs, and I read Tomas for six minutes and forty seconds. [135] Afterward I wrote everything down and then I wrote that none of it counted. [150] Now the pill is remembering without me, in pieces, and only when my body does what it did then. [170] Somatic markers, the clinic called it. [177] Memory that lives in the hands and the spine and the bend of the neck. [193] So I have come to devise a way to illuminate the hidden pathways and get the rest of what I paid for. [216] Tomas does not know I am doing it. [225] He hands me a paper cup of water and tells me to hold it the way I held the beaker that night. [248] I sit on the bench where I sat that night and let my spine find its old curve. [267] The room does the rest. [273] The recall opens like a shutter. [280] It is fractally precise, and that is the part I cannot argue with. [294] I see the studio I am sitting in, from a mind that is not mine. [310] I see myself on the bench, small and badly lit, and I see what he feels about me. [329] It is hope the way willowglass is hope: thin, bending, almost letting the light through. [345] Then the part I came for. [352] Under the feeling there is a map of the making of the feeling. [366] The pill did not stop at six minutes; it kept writing routes into me. [381] Hidden pathways, one folded inside the next, the way the geode folds its own light. [397] Tomas is adjusting the exposure and does not notice I have stopped answering him. [412] He thinks I am tired. [418] I am counting the frames of my own doubt. [428] I do not believe in telepathy, not even now. [438] But the routes are there, and they are specific, and they light up where he lights up. [456] I can name the three things he is afraid of and I have never asked him about any of them. [477] That is not magic to me; it is evidence. [487] And evidence is something I can love without lying to myself about. [500] The shock on my knuckle has gone from white to a dull red line. [515] I touch it to keep the recall open. [524] Somatic markers work both ways. [530] If the body can open the door, the body can hold it. [543] So I press the burn into the rim of the paper cup and I take the whole room at once. [564] Every pathway at every scale, each one containing the next. [575] The studio inside the studio inside the studio. [584] Tomas turns off the safelight and the geode begins to gather what the long exposure needs. [601] He asks if I am all right. [609] I tell him yes, and it is almost the whole truth. [621] I tell him what I can prove and I keep what I cannot. [635] Then I ask him the question that the map has been showing me for an hour. [652] He answers it in four words, and the four words are the ones I already knew. [669] The recall lets go of me the way an exposure ends, all at once and without ceremony. [687] What is left is not a voice in my head. [698] It is the shape of the pathways, faint as light caught in stone. [712] Tomas says the geode sliver came out well. [721] I look at it and I do not tell him that for six minutes and forty seconds he lived inside it. [743] Or that I am still there, a little, and that I will call him sliver from now on. [762] We will both keep our hands on the glass until the light is finished. [777]</story>

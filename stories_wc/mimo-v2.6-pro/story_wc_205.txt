After time became visible, the baroque asteroid turned into a scripture no one had agreed to read. [17]
Every carved scroll and guttering gargoyle showed its own past as light, and the colony beneath could not stop arguing about what the ornament meant. [42]

It was the last intact fragment of the vessel in which the revolutionaries had mixed their oath, and the Ministry of Sediment wanted it. [205]

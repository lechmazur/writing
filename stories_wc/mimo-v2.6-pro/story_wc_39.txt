The fiddles sleep beneath the dunes,
and salt holds every note it stole,
I keep my jars against the tunes
the wind erases from the whole,
a whittler hums beside the sea,
I catch the sound before it flies;
what is not kept cannot be free,
and what is lost is never wise. [190]

for every word my grandmother once said;

The cobalt lighthouse stood on the black rocks like a jar of ink set down to catch the dawn. [19] Its blue glaze had fired the same year the town tried to burn the keepers out for saying the sea talked. [40] The heat wave had broken in the night, and the third morning after it the air tasted like a cold key. [61] I climbed the tower as assistant lighthouse keeper, one hundred and forty steps, with my father's tin of letters under my arm. [83] He had been the keeper before me, and he was disbelieved for thirty years. [97] He would shout his warnings at the fog until his throat bled, and the fishing boats would sail right past. [117] He said the sea itself whispered, and that the whisper went farther than any shout. [132] Nobody listened, so he stopped shouting and started writing. [141] Around my neck, on a twist of twine, hung a vial cork. [153] It was not stopping a bottle; it was the stopper he had pulled from a jar in our garden the night the sand came inland and took the beds of jasmine. [184] He kept it so he would not forget what the earth smelled like when it was kind. [201] In the beam-room I set the tin on the brass table and did what he had written to do. [220] I loosened the cork, and the scent of a garden at night came out of it like a held breath let go. [242] Jasmine, damp earth, the green of tomato stems, and under all of it, warm stone. [257] The fog answered it. [261] It had been standing offshore for a week, refusing to move during the heat, and now it walked in toward the rocks on the scent, beckoned like a dog called home. [292] Out in the fog I heard the herring skiff the Ferris brothers had run out at dawn, the way they always did, fog or none. [317] Their hull whispered against the water, a sound my father had taught me to hear. [332] The bell buoy had gone dead in the heat, and the shout of a horn would have pushed them farther out to sea. [355] So I did not shout. [360] I dropped the vial cork into the lamp-oil reservoir instead. [370] The oil went still, then clear, then it seemed to take a breath of its own. [386] He had a name for what that oil did: the vibrations of intent, the small hum a thing gives off before it acts, which the cork could sort. [414] My father had written that the cork did not seal a thing so much as it purified what the thing meant to do. [437] Every intention in that oil that was not clean burned off as a thin blue thread of smoke. [455] What stayed was only the wish to warn. [463] I lit the lamp. [467] The beam that came out was not louder than any beam had ever been, but it was bent. [485] It carried the garden with it. [491] The fog took the light the way dry ground takes rain, and the fog turned gold all the way to the mouth of the bay. [516] Somewhere in it the Ferris brothers cut their engine, and I heard one of them say, low, that he smelled jasmine and did not know why he was turning. [545] They turned. [547] The skiff's wake straightened toward the channel and away from the black rocks where my father had shouted himself hoarse for nothing. [569] Then I opened the tin. [574] My father's letters were thin as onion skin and smelled of smoke and salt. [588] I tucked them one at a time into the lamp's draft, the way he had told me to, and the flames read them aloud. [612] Not in words anybody outside could hear, but the fog kept carrying, and the gold went on widening past the mouth of the bay and out past the shoals. [641] The sun cleared the water then, all of it at once, the way it does after a heat wave breaks. [661] I stood in the beam-room with the empty tin and the last of the garden coming off my hands, and I did not raise my voice once. [688] Tenderness is not quiet; it is just the only thing that screams and still gets heard. [704] My father's last line was written at the bottom of the last letter, and I carry it now in my chest where a vial cork used to hang. [732] It says: listen with your hands, and you will never need to shout. [745] Down on the water the Ferris brothers were already unloading, and neither of them will ever know the name of what saved them. [768] That is the proof, and it is enough. [776] The cobalt tower went on burning above the rocks, whispering the whole coast awake. [790]

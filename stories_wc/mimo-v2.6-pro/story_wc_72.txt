After the secret, Anemone held still the way only a statue can, and the garden held still with her. [19] Below the Terrace of Kelp-and-Stone, the forgotten garden breathed in slow halocline pulses. [32] Anemones opened and closed like mouths deciding whether to speak. [42] She had been bronze once; then salt; then something with a heartbeat made of sound. [57] For two hundred years she had broadcast the city's promises into every sleeping mind in Brinehall. [73] Today she had broadcast one truth, and it put a crack through everything. [86] The crowd above had gone quiet in the way crowds do when they are deciding whether to forgive. [104] Below, the vault of the garden had heard her too. [114] A seam ran now along the old coral ceiling, thin as a held breath. [128] That was the first cascade: her voice had found the structural weakness the builders left, and made it a door. [148] Anemone pressed two fingers to her own temple, the way she did when sending. [162] Her telepathy was incisively kind: she never softened a thing so much that it became a lie. [179] Come down, she sent to the boy with the golem's leash. [191] Come down and see what your oath bought. [198] His name was Tavin; he was eleven and already certain about everything. [210] Certainty, she had decided in the silence after her revelation, was a room she would let no one live in again. [231] What she wanted instead was nuance, and nuance had to be let in by the handful. [247] So she chose the ampoule. [252] It hung at her wrist on a thread of plaited kelp line, a foxglove-stained glass ampoule no bigger than a thumb. [273] Inside, one foxglove floated in clear brine, still ringing with the apothecary's voice. [286] For the failing heart, the old woman had said, and too much is a murder, and she had refused to name the third thing. [310] That refusal was the second cascade: medicine and poison were the same bottle, and the difference was only the hand. [330] Anemone drank. [332] The brine was merely warm, which was the third thing, and she laughed soundlessly into the garden. [349] Warmth, not death, not mercy: warmth. [355] Tavin came down through the seam with his golem behind him, and the golem went in headfirst because it always did. [376] Its clay shoulders wedged in the fracture, and the whole vault shivered. [388] Silt came down in pale ropes. [394] Thread the line, Anemone sent, and Tavin threaded it through the golem's belt ring and made it a belay. [413] They walked the kelp terrace together, hand on line, line on stone. [425] Here the statues of the garden had learned to move, though none of them had learned to want. [443] Anemone taught wanting with each thought she let him overhear. [453] She showed him the seam in the ceiling and then the seam in the old oath: the city had sworn the garden would flourish, and then it had sealed the garden in when the kelp failed. [489] Tavin said nothing, which was new. [495] The fourth cascade came at the vault door, where the golem's weight had opened the fracture along the whole north wall. [516] Cold Glintward light poured in, and with it the whole buried city. [528] Glintward had been gone a century, and its lanterns were still lit. [540] Nobody had remembered to put them out. [547] Anemone knelt in the silt and let the boy see that she was afraid. [561] Not of dying; stone does not die. [568] Of being certain again. [572] Then she took the last thread of kelp line and lashed the ampoule to the broken lintel, where anyone who passed would have to see it. [598] It is not a warning, she told him. [606] It is an invitation. [610] Tavin looked at the purple glass and at the dead flower inside it, and he asked the first uncertain question of his life. [633] Which one is it? [637] Both, said the statue, and the garden breathed around them, and the lanterns of a forgotten city kept on shining for no one at all. [662] Above, the crowd finally began to forgive, which is only ever nuance arriving late. [676] Anemone stood, and the stone of her shoulders was warm from the inside now, and she thought one last thought upward into Brinehall. [699] Keep the crack. [702] It is the only honest thing this city has left. [712] The golem, still wedged and unbothered, blew a bubble through the seam and let it rise. [728] Nothing in the garden was dead, and nothing in it was finished. [740]</story>

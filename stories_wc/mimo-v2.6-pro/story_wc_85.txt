Iren came to the forgotten shore with a lie under his tongue and a guilt he could not name. [19]
He was a black market genealogist, a man who sold the dead to the living, and his ledger of invented lineages had made him rich and hollow. [46]
The terrace where he waited was cobalt tile and salt, and dusk had begun to bruise the water at the edge of the world. [70]
On the chain around his neck hung half a watch, and its single pointer trembled with each breath of wind. [90]
He had come here to discover the weight of a withheld truth. [102]
Under the weight of unspoken words he had walked this coast once before, younger and crueler, and he had not walked it alone. [125]
Toma had been with him. [130]

Each carving held a name he would not speak aloud. [167]
Iren's method had always been the same: he reached for those words and sifted the quiet around them, letting the silence come apart into truth. [192]
It was a sieved hush. [197]

The third gave him a watch hand, small and blunt, driven into the grain of the wood like a splintered nail. [261]
He touched it and felt the old shape of it against his own palm. [275]
It was the missing piece. [280]

Now it stood here, pinned into the driftwood, showing him the place where the old post had struck. [320]

Then a barnacled face lifted above the surf, and Toma stood there as though the sea had been keeping him. [449]

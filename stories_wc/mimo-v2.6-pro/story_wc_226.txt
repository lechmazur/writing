The pale spire stood in mist at the far end of the canyon, and every hour of our practice bent toward it. [22] This was before the ritual, when the towers of bone still held our voices without answer. [38] My name is Sera Vann, and I was the one they could not make quiet. [53] The council called me a defiant artist; the children called me something worse and better. [68] They called me the girl who put a mole in a marriage vow. [81] It had been innocent mischief and nothing more. [89] The Harmonizer did not laugh. [94] So I learned to smuggle the jest deeper, inside the line that held the truth up, where a cut would break everything. [116]

The canyon of endless echoes was our rehearsal room. [125] There is no mapped depth to it; the bone towers lean together like ribs of something too large to bury. [145] Every word spoken here comes back whole, three breaths later, so sound survives in this place better than we do. [165]

We held our impromptu poetry sessions at dusk, when the hush forged itself out of the day's heat and hung between the towers like poured metal. [191] There was no stage and no torch, only the last light on the chalk I had rubbed into my palms. [211] My craft is not singing; it is the splitting. [220] I take one line of truth and I refract it, the way a scratched prism takes a single shaft and throws seven colors onto a wall. [246] One truth becomes many, and none of them is a lie. [257] That is the whole of my art and the whole of my trouble. [270]

Before the ritual the Revered One sends the Harmonizer to clip what should not be said aloud. [287] He came at the fourth session and stood with his mouth a straight line while I opened the debt of the miller's widow. [310] I split it seven ways, and into the third band I folded a small ballad about a goat. [328] The children in the dark laughed before they knew they were allowed to. [341] The Harmonizer raised his hand and the echo of that laugh was cut out of the air, which is the only violence I have ever heard. [367] A hush can be forged or it can be taken, and mine was taken that night. [383]

So I defied him in the only currency the canyon respects: I wrote the goat into the next three truths. [403] The echoes of the bone towers carried my mischief further than any decree. [416] By the seventh session the canyon was humming with a joke no one could trace to me. [433] That is what the Revered One came to end. [442]

He offered forgiveness in the open court of ribs, and I listened, because I wanted it. [458] Forgiveness here is not free, and nobody pretends otherwise. [467] He named the price in front of the singers: ten echoes returned to the canyon and three hundred days of silence from me. [490] I would give back the laughter I had stolen and I would be quiet until the spire's next rising. [509] The cost fell on my body the way it always does. [520] When I agreed, my voice came out grey for a week and my hair went white at the temples, and that is the honest measure of it. [547]

Tonight I write the last line. [553] It is one sentence from the founding, the truth the Revered One keeps whole so it can rule us whole. [573] At dawn I will walk to the pale spire in mist and I will set my chalk to it and refract that truth into seven colors on the bone. [602] The canyon will hold all seven, and for the first time we will have a truth no one can clip. [622] The price is already counted; I am the one who has to pay it, and I would pay it twice. [642]

Before the ritual begins, I stand in the forged hush and listen to the towers answer everything I have not yet said. [664] It is not quiet here. It is only waiting. [673]

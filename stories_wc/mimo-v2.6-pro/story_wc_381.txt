He never spoke and never asked. [32]
He only listened, the way a stone listens, and let the night settle over him like a *tranquil blaze* banked low enough to warm without burning. [58]

Here he had come to *hush* a *prophecy*. [90]

Now the wing was the instrument of his undoing, though he did not know that yet. [332]

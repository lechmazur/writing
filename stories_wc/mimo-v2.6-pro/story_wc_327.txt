Ashtad, the copper-veined storyteller, stood at the mouth of the tunnel of echoes with the flawed diamond cupped in both hands. [21] The stone was tunneled through by a flaw so fine that light entered one side and came out the other with a sound attached. [45] Nobody would cut it into a crown; they said it carried a curse. [58] Ashtad called that a misreading of the flaw. [66] A cleaner stone would have been a mirror, and a mirror only returns what you already are. [83] This one passed things through, and whatever passed through came back able to speak. [97] Beneath him the canyon yawned, and in it rose the towers of bone. [110] They were not graves. [114] They were syllables. [117] When his language began to die, the canyon had done what canyons do to anything that falls into them slowly enough: it had pressed the words into calcium and stood them upright. [149] Every tower held one sound that no living mouth could make anymore. [161] His purpose was narrow and he had never been ashamed of it: to translate texture into sound. [178] The glyphs on the bone were ridged, flaked, and salted with age, and each texture was a syllable waiting for its tone. [200] What he lacked was the hand that could read them aloud. [211] Copper ran through his own veins for the same reason wire runs through a bell: it carried what struck it. [231] He held the diamond up to the canyon's light and waited for the flock. [245] They came out of the west at dusk, a black river of starlings several birds thick. [261]

He did not direct them. [266] You cannot harness a flock by telling it what to do; you harness it by finding the pattern it is already keeping and placing yourself inside it. [293] The starlings wheeled in bands, and the bands tightened and slackened like a rope being plucked. [309] Ashtad matched the slackening with his breath and the tightening with the angle of the stone. [325] Through the flaw's tunnel the flock's oscillation arrived on the towers as a bow arrives on strings. [342] The bone answered. [345] A ridge of glyph became a low note; a flake became a bright one; a crust of salt broke into a small percussion, like rain on a bowl. [373] The canyon of endless echoes took each sound and returned it a thousand times, each return softer and slower than the last, so that the past arrived after the present and explained it. [406] That was the song. [410] It was not beautiful the way a new thing is beautiful. [421] It was beautiful the way a name is beautiful when it is spoken for the last time by someone who knows it is the last time. [447] The dead syllables told how the last speakers had traded their words for the words of a larger people, first for salt, then for law, then for the plain right to be left alone. [481] Nothing about that was loud. [486] A language does not die in an afternoon. [494] It dies the way a vein of copper is mined: steadily, by patient hands, until the tunnel is empty and the metal is somewhere else. [519]

When the flock banked and went south, Ashtad closed his hand over the diamond and let the last echo spend itself. [540] What he had made was not a memory. [548] Memory is what a person keeps; a song is what a people leave behind for a stranger to hold. [567] He had translated the texture of the bone into sound, and the sound was true, and truth was all the diamond had ever done to anyone who carried it. [596] Men called that a curse because they had no word for being corrected. [609] Now he stood in the tunnel of echoes with the whole dead grammar ringing in the stone walls around him and could not say a single line of it. [638] He was the last one who could hear it and the first one who had nothing to answer with. [657] So he did the only thing the copper in him knew how to do. [671] He began the song again, quietly articulate with every ridge and flake, shaping them against the inside of his teeth so that the next canyon down the chain would have something to press into bone. [706] He did not know if anyone would hear it. [715] That was not his work. [720] His work was the sound. [725] The first syllable left him like a bird leaving a branch. [736] It went down the tunnel of echoes and came back carrying a thousand quieter copies of itself, and then one more. [757] He listened. [759]</story>

[78] She was a dock worker by trade, but grace was her true cargo, unloaded nightly into the salt spray where gulls screamed above the sublittoral fringe, that twilight zone where the ocean floor met the pilings in a marriage of rot and barnacle. [304] Not by force or trickery, but by reflecting her own locked chambers back to her, showing the grief and rage she had barnacled over with duty and silence and the desperate need to belong.

[97]

Elara's singular motivation burned through her like a fever that no amount of scholarly detachment could cool: to find the library of unlearned lessons, a legendary repository rumored to exist in the mountain's deepest stratum where history wrote its rough drafts and abandoned its purest thoughts before they matured into dogma. [185]

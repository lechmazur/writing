She sought the blooming labyrinth of bonsai forests that crowned the peak, where miniature trees flowered in impossible spirals between stones of white quartz and moss. [51]

She had come to collect the textures of different times, to preserve moments that would otherwise dissolve into the thin, quiet air of the heights. [102]
The method she employed required absolute patience: she would refract the morning light by charting the faint hum in a fluorescent lamp at dawn, though no electrical grid reached this altitude and no lamp existed here yet she imagined one, invented its complete history from factory to filament. [150]

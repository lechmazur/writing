The feather was not ordinary; it pulsed with a faint phosphorescence, casting a luminous respite in the gloom, as if it carried the memory of flight within its scorched fibers. [168]
Elara touched it, and the facility seemed to exhale, stirring currents that had been stagnant for decades, releasing bubbles that rose like prayers toward the surface miles above. [196]

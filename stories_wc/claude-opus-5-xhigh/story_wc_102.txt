The viaduct wore its wisteria like a rumor, purple and heavy, and every evening at twilight Mira opened her camera obscura beneath the third arch. [25] It was only a brick vault sealed with tar paper, a pinhole the width of a needle's eye, and a linen wall where the world arrived upside down. [53] Fog came off the canal at that hour, and Mira had learned that fog did not blur her images; it clarified them, the way a fever clarifies a life. [82] She knew about fevers. [86] Three winters ago, burning at forty degrees, a past life memory had surfaced in her like a bell rising through silt: a woman on a stone bridge in some older century, holding a letter she never delivered, watching a barge take her love downriver. [130] Everything after that had been one question, asked in a hundred shapes: where is unused love stored? [147] It could not simply evaporate. [152] Nothing that heavy evaporates. [156] So she became spiritually curious in the practical way of craftspeople, testing scriptures like she tested lenses, and she hung Kwan Yin's tear in the beam of the pinhole. [185] Her grandmother had called the green glass bead that: a tear the compassionate one shed when she declined heaven, teardrop-shaped, cloudy at its heart. [209] Hung there, it did something no lens did. [217] When a sitter's breathing changed - when the shallow, guarded breath of a stranger dropped and lengthened - the tear fogged, then cleared, and the projected face on the linen resolved into whatever the sitter was hiding. [252] Grief, mostly. [254] Sometimes a name. [257]

Elias arrived on the evening the wisteria opened all at once, smelling of scorched metal. [272] He called himself a pilgrim of sparks, and he said it without embarrassment, which she respected. [288] His route ran between night workplaces - the shipyard welders, the farrier on Ackley Street, the foundry that poured at two in the morning - and at each one he stood close enough to be stung. [322] "Every spark is a piece of something that was held too long and finally let go," he said. "I go where the letting-go happens." [346] He wanted her to photograph sparks, since a camera obscura keeps no picture, only a living image, and he thought sparks deserved that kind of impermanent witness. [373] She said she would trade: he could bring her his sparks if he sat for her first. [390]

So he sat, and she asked him to breathe out longer than he breathed in, and to keep doing it until the arch stopped feeling like a room. [418] Twenty minutes in, his shoulders dropped. [424] Kwan Yin's tear misted over, held the mist like held breath, and released it. [438] On the linen, upside down among trembling flowers, Elias's face became his brother's face. [452] "Tobias burned in the foundry fire," Elias said, to the ceiling, in the voice people use when the truth arrives sideways. [473] "I never told him he was the best thing that happened to our house." [487] "So I go and stand in the sparks, because I think that is where it went." [503]

Mira sat down on the cold brick and understood that they were asking the same question from opposite ends. [522] Then, because there was nothing else honest to do, she told him about the bridge and the undelivered letter, and she let her own breath change while she spoke. [551] The tear fogged again. [555] This time the linen showed her nothing hidden, because she was hiding nothing; it showed only the viaduct in bloom, right side up, which should have been impossible. [583] She laughed, and the laugh came out shaky, and the answer arrived with that foggy lucidity she had learned to trust: unused love is stored in the breath. [611] Not in heaven, not in the sparks - in the held chest, the guarded exhale, the sentence stopped behind the teeth. [631] That was why breathing changes revealed people. [638] You cannot store something that heavy and keep breathing normally around it. [650]

"Then it isn't lost," Elias said. [656] "It's just late." [659]

They spent the summer that way, and it worked like medicine, which is to say slowly and with relapses. [678] He brought her sparks - a jar of them was impossible, so he brought welders instead, tired men who sat in her dark arch and breathed until their faces gave up their dead. [710] She could not photograph any of it, and that turned out to be the mercy: nothing was fixed, so everything could still heal. [733] In September Mira wrote the letter from the older century, out loud, to no one, standing under the falling wisteria. [753] Elias said his brother's name into the fog forty times, one for each year Tobias didn't get. [770] Kwan Yin's tear stayed clear all evening, and neither of them needed to ask why. [785]

Serrat taught us to whisper, because the Concordance had made remembering a form of theft. [15] I whispered too, though I did not believe the stories I whispered. [27] They called me the luminous skeptic, and both halves were fair. [38] Eleven years ago, as Water Assessor, I touched a stained glass box in the drained cistern under the Bell Fountain, and ever since my palms have glowed faint as watch-dials. [68] I still refuse to call it a miracle. [76] Glow is only stored light, and light is only a receipt for something that happened. [91] That was my whole quarrel with the Concordance: they had not destroyed our memories, they had only filed them where nobody could collect. [114] Serrat is the city of forgotten fountains: two hundred basins, not one of them running. [129] When the aqueducts were capped, the engineers sealed the city's memory of water into a single stained glass box, so no one would mourn what they could not name. [158] Mothers forgot the names of the basins they were baptized beside. [169] I forgot my mother's face and kept only the fact of her. [181] I intended to liberate every memory that box was holding hostage. [192] Mira Halden, who fed the geese, forgot nothing, because she was outside the walls on capping day. [209] She spent nine autumns marking a route for me and never said so aloud. [223] She wedged crusts of hard rye into the mortar of certain basins, one crust to a rim, and let the geese teach me where to look. [249] She died in spring, and I called her map superstition until the geese came back. [264] They pause here three nights every October, in the marsh below the Quarry Basin, the one hollow still holding rain. [284] From the pause of the migrating geese I had three nights and no more, because the box keeps sidereal time. [304] Its clasp answers to a star, not a season: Aldera, which crosses the crack in the Bell Fountain's arch once every eleven years, four minutes earlier each night. [332] That is the patience of a made thing that expects to outlive its makers. [346] So I followed the breadcrumbs. [351] Mira's crusts had gone gray and hard as tesserae, but they were where she left them: the Weavers' Trough, the Blind Lion, the Twin Cups by the customs house, each a stitch in a line running downhill. [388] I am realistically imaginative, which is a habit rather than a gift; I pictured the vanished water and then measured for it. [410] Every marked basin sat on the old gravity line to the Bell Fountain cistern, which meant Mira had not been feeding birds; she had been leaving me a survey. [439] On the second night I climbed down with vinegar, a goose feather, and a pry bar the length of my forearm. [460] The box sat where it had always sat, no larger than a beehive, its panes the sour green and cobalt of church windows, the lid limed shut by a decade of drips. [492] To unstick it I had to be as patient as it was, and I had only until Aldera cleared the crack. [513] Vinegar on the feather, along the seam, wait; again, wait. [523] Above me the geese muttered in the marsh like a congregation that had lost faith in the sermon but not in each other. [546] A patrol crossed the plaza overhead, and I held still, breathing through my mouth, glowing like an idiot in the dark. [567] That is what defiance looks like in Serrat: a woman under a plaza with a feather, keeping quiet on purpose. [587] The lime gave at the third hour, with a sound like a tooth leaving a jaw. [603] Then the crack in the arch turned white, Aldera arrived on schedule, and the clasp opened itself. [620] I want to be precise, because I am still a skeptic: nothing supernatural happened. [634] Light came out of the stained glass box in colored bars, went up through the drain grates, and smelled like wet stone. [656] The memories were only information, liberated, and they went home to the people they came from. [672] A baker two streets over sat up saying a name he had not owned since he was nine. [690] My mother's face came back to me: long jaw, chipped front tooth, her hands in the Blind Lion's basin, washing beets. [711] I sat down in the dry cistern and wept without noise, which is a skill here. [727] My palms went dark that night; the receipt had been collected. [738] By morning the geese had gone on south, and the plaza was full of people saying fountain names to one another very softly, the way you say a thing you intend to keep. [771] The Concordance capped the water; it cannot cap arithmetic, and I still have Mira's survey. [786]

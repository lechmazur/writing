The first letter came on a Tuesday, and it used eleven words to tell me my brother would hang in nine days. [22] The second letter, the one that would say whether the appeal had been heard, was still somewhere in the mail sacks of the future. [46] Between them I had nine days, a relay station in the fen, and the confidence man's watch. [63] Corliss Vane had sold my brother shares in a canal that did not exist, then left the watch behind in the scuffle that got him killed and my brother convicted. [93] It was a beautiful liar of a watch: its escapement ran forward for sixty seconds, then backward for sixty, cyclically reversed, so that it was only ever correct twice a minute. [124] I am called the veiled dreamer in the villages downriver, because I keep my face covered and because I sleep in the afternoons with the telegraph key under my hand. [154] Both are true, and neither explains the other. [162]

My brother's trial testimony had been silence; he never spoke a word in his own defense, and the only thing he sent me was a page of numbers along the margin of the indictment. [196] It was an unfolding cipher, the kind that hides a second key inside its first solution, and a third inside the second. [218] Each layer read backward from the last, alphabet flipped, direction flipped, until I understood that the watch was not a keepsake but an instrument. [242] The fourth layer gave me a sentence: FIND THE LIBRARY OF UNSPOKEN WORDS, AND SPEAK FOR ME THERE. [260] The fifth gave me a method: I was to find the place through propagation delays. [275]

Lines carry a signal at a known speed; my station sat between two relay poles across four miles of fen. [295] If I sent a pulse and timed its echo, the propagation delay should be the same every night. [313] It was not. [316] On misted nights the echo came back late, as though the wire had to travel farther than the land allowed, and the excess delay grew and shrank like breathing. [345] For six nights I documented every reading in a ledger, timing the pulses with the confidence man's watch, using its reversals the way a surveyor uses a plumb line. [374] Forward minute, backward minute: the difference between the two measurements gave me the true interval, because the lie cancelled itself. [394] By the seventh night the ledger had a shape in it, an extra half-mile of distance folded into the fen at a bearing of two hundred and eleven degrees. [423]

I walked out with a lantern I did not need. [432] The library stood glowing in the mist, and the mist was its walls. [445] Shelves went up into vapor, lit from within by a soft yellow patience, and the aisles smelled of wet reeds and old paper. [468] Every volume held what someone had chosen not to say. [478] I moved through it with cautious exuberance, one hand on my veil, laughing under my breath, terrified of touching the wrong spine. [500]

My brother's book was thin and warm. [506] It contained the sentence he had swallowed in the courtroom: that Corliss Vane had died falling on his own knife, and that three men had watched it happen and gone home to their suppers. [540] He had kept silent because one of the three was our sister's husband, and he judged her happiness worth his neck. [561] The library asked nothing of me. [566] That is the danger of it: unspoken words are only borrowed, and every page you take out becomes a thing you must carry aloud. [590] I copied the page into my ledger anyway, in my own hand, with the date and the bearing and the delay, so that it would be a document and not a dream. [622] Then I signed it and walked back through the reeds while the glow closed behind me like a mouth. [641]

The second letter came at noon. [647] The appeal had been refused; the hanging stood at four days out. [659] I sat with both letters on the table, the first and the last, and between them the ledger, holding a sentence that had never been spoken and now had to be. [690] I keyed the magistrate's office at Ely: THIRD PARTY TESTIMONY FOLLOWS, WITNESS NAMED, RIDING TONIGHT. [705] My hand shook and the sending came out ragged, and I did not care. [719] Then I wound the confidence man's watch, forward, backward, forward, and set it on top of the ledger to keep the pages from lifting in the wind. [746] A liar's instrument, used honestly, had found half a mile of country that no map held, and my brother's silence was on paper in my hand; I lifted my veil to the fen and let myself hope, carefully, the way you carry a full lamp down a dark stair. [795]

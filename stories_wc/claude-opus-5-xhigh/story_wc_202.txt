My grandmother called me a between-worlds translator before I understood there were two worlds to stand between. [17] She kept the lamp on Sorrow Head until the harbor authority decided her forty years were a footnote, and gave the light to a machine. [42] When they dismantled the tower she stole one wedge of the Fresnel lens, and when she died she left it to me, wrapped in a dish towel. [69] That lighthouse lens fragment is the reason I can work the alchemist's market at all. [84] The market is strung high above the city rooftops on cables and plank bridges, a hanging bazaar that shifts its moorings every eleven days so the tax clerks can never find it twice. [117] Up there the alchemists trade in what they call anchors of intuition: small objects that hold a certainty too large for a mind to carry alone. [143] A thimble that knows where the water main runs. [152] A cracked domino that knows when a man is lying about his mother. [165] My trade is stranger, and it pays worse. [173] I translate between the living city and the one folded underneath it, the crowd of people the archives forgot to keep. [194] They do not speak, exactly. [199] They leave their words through hidden details in paintings, and always have. [211] Hold my grandmother's lens wedge to a canvas and the varnish goes shallow, and there in the corner of some guild portrait you find a woman who was painted over: a laundress, a cooper's widow, a lamp-keeper's daughter with salt in her hair. [254] Their faces are elusively tangible; I can feel the brushed ridge of a cheekbone under my thumb and still not be able to prove it to a magistrate. [282] I do this to honor overlooked histories, which is a grand way of saying I do it so my grandmother's forty years are not a footnote either. [309] In the twenty-two years between childhood and now, I have read nine hundred paintings and named four hundred and six people. [330] Last Thursday a dealer named Sabel came to my stall on the highest bridge, carrying a seascape wrapped in oilcloth. [350] The painting showed Sorrow Head at dusk, the tower lit, and no keeper anywhere in it. [366] The board commissioned it, Sabel explained, and wanted the human element removed from the record. [381] They would pay me well to certify the canvas was empty. [392] I held the fragment up. [397] She was there, of course, four inches tall in the gallery window, one hand raised to unfasten the storm shutter. [417] The painter had loved her enough to hide her, and hated the board enough to leave the shutter unlatched. [436] Then the eternal instant arrived, the way it always does when the lens finds someone. [451] The market stopped swaying. [455] A merchant's kettle hung its steam in a single unmoving plume, and I was nine years old on a stone stair with salt wind in my teeth, and I was thirty-one on a plank bridge above ten thousand chimneys, and both were happening at once and neither was ending. [504] My grandmother, in the instant, was not speaking either. [513] She simply pushed the shutter open, as she had every evening of her life, so that the light could get out. [534] Then the kettle steamed, the bridge swung, and Sabel was still holding out his purse. [549] I told him the canvas was full. [556] I wrote out her name, her forty years, and the name of the painter, and I nailed the certificate to the post of my stall where the whole hanging market could read it. [589] Sabel left, and the board will send someone less polite. [599] So I have begun to give the lens away. [608] The alchemists tell me that anchors of intuition can be split, that a certainty divided is not halved but multiplied, and I choose to believe them. [634] Yesterday I ground a sliver from the wedge and set it in a ring for a fourteen-year-old girl who sweeps the west bridge, and told her what to look for in the corners of paintings. [669] There will be a between-worlds translator on every bridge before the board finds the market's new moorings. [686] A record can be shut like a shutter. [693] Anything shut can be unfastened, and I have spent the years between childhood and now learning exactly where the latch is. [714]

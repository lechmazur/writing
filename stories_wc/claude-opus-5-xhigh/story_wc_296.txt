The trick failed at moonrise, and by dawn the village knew what the mirror had swallowed. [16] Corrin the conjurer had promised to pass his daughter through glass and back again, and the glass had kept her. [36] They came for Bramwen, because Bramwen finds lost things. [45] She heard them out with her arms folded, a druid carved from winter oak, and said nothing until they finished. [65] Guardedness was not cruelty; it was the only armor that had ever held. [78] Corrin had been her husband once, before the stage, before he learned that applause was easier to keep than a wife. [99] The girl, Iselde, was his and another woman's, and Bramwen loved her anyway, which was the wound nobody could see. [119] She packed three things: a spade, a shard of the broken mirror, and a flesh-eating plant seed the size of a knuckle. [141] Finding worked through expanding circles. [146] She walked the first ring around Corrin's cottage, then a wider one through the barley, then wider, each loop asking the ground a question until the ground answered. [174] On the ninth circle the answer came as a smell of apples in a month with no apples. [192] It led her to Hallowmere, the cursed lake, where the water turned in one slow wheel and never froze at the edges. [214] At its heart stood the orchard vortex: forty apple trees spinning in a column of black water, roots up, branches down, fruit hanging like held breath. [240] Everything the country lost eventually came here to turn. [249] Bramwen waded in with the calm of a soldier who has already decided to die and found the decision restful. [269] The lake did not fight her; it simply carried her inward, ring by ring, the way her own magic worked, as though the curse and the gift were cousins. [298] In the vortex's still center the air was warm and the noise fell away. [312] Iselde hung there in a pillar of clear ice, mouth open, mid-word. [324] Bramwen held up the mirror shard and saw what she had come for: nothing. [338] The glass gave back her face and the ice and the turning trees, and not one particle of the truth she needed. [360] That was why she had come: to learn what mirrors do not show. [373] A mirror shows a shape; it cannot show whether a man meant to lose his daughter. [389] The ice held Iselde's last sentence like a fly in amber, and Bramwen knew a frozen confession when she saw one. [410] Ice keeps words the way a mirror keeps faces: perfectly, uselessly, until something warm arrives. [425] She dug a hole in the drowned soil between two upside-down trees and dropped in the flesh-eating seed. [443] Such seeds are grown for sieges; they take heat from whatever meat they can reach and give back a bloom hot enough to open stone. [468] The only meat within reach was hers. [475] She knelt and offered her left hand, and the sprout came up green and fast and took two fingers before the flower opened, red as a forge mouth. [503] Heat rolled over the pillar. [508] The ice began to unbend -- and so, at last, did she. [519] Bramwen unfolded the arms she had kept crossed for nine years and put them around the thawing girl, and said the thing she had never said aloud: I wanted you. [549] The last of the ice let go of Iselde's mouth, and her frozen sentence finished itself in the warm air: "Father, don't." [571] So the mirror had not failed; Corrin had. [579] The trick had never been a trick. [586] Bramwen absorbed this the way she absorbed everything, without a flinch, while blood ran off her wrist into the black water. [607] She felt no rage, only a wide, level readiness, the serenity of an army that has finally been told where the enemy sleeps. [630] She burned the flower before it could ask for more, and carried Iselde out through the widening rings, the orchard slowing behind them, releasing a century of lost things: hats, hounds, a bell, a boy. [665] On the shore Iselde shivered and asked whether her father would be punished. [678] "He will be found," Bramwen said, and smiled with great gentleness, because finding is what she does, and there is nowhere the circles do not eventually reach. [705] Then she wrapped her ruined hand, took the girl's whole one, and started walking the first ring. [722]

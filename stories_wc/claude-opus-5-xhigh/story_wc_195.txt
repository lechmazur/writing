The greenhouse stood under a sky that had forgotten morning, and Ser Havel Duraine had stopped expecting otherwise. [18] He was the last of the Order of the Quiet Lamp, and he spoke perhaps nine words a week, most of them to trees. [42] The orchard inside was factually mystical: every miracle it grew could be weighed, indexed, and logged in the ledger chained to the doorpost. [65] Pears of forgiveness: eleven ounces each, ripening only when someone nearby meant it. [78] Quinces of memory, which bruised if lied to. [86] Havel tended them because reality had begun to shift, and the orchard was the only place that held still. [105] Outside, the road to Ambervale rearranged itself nightly; last week the city had been a lake, and the week before, a rumor. [127] In his breast pocket he kept the only object that had survived that unmaking: an abandoned marionette theater program, water-stained, printed for a performance that never opened. [154] THE SHAPE OF POSSIBILITY, it read, in four movements, puppets and strings by Ilsabet Duraine. [169] His sister had spent nine years designing the play and one night vanishing with the city. [185] On the program's back page, in her cramped hand, were the blocking diagrams: forgotten patterns of steps, string-crossings, and counted breaths. [206] Havel had realized, somewhere in the endless night, that the diagrams were also a grafting scheme. [222] Ilsabet had been an alchemist before she was a puppeteer, and she had never learned to keep her disciplines apart. [242] So he walked her choreography through the rows, night after night, treating each marked pause as a cut, each string-crossing as a bind. [265] Four movements, four grafts, and the fourth was tonight. [274] He wanted one fruit that no ledger could weigh: not memory, not forgiveness, but possibility itself, held still long enough to look at. [297] To capture the shape of possibility was the whole ambition of her play, and he was a poor puppet for it, forty-six years old and armored in silence. [325] Still, he had the steps. [330] The greenhouse glass had been built to breathe; along the ribs ran brass clasps meant to unsnap in sequence, opening the panes to whatever sky arrived. [356] The fourth movement required it: LET THE ROOF OPEN ON THE FINAL BEAT, Ilsabet had written, twice underlined. [374] He set the lamp down, unbuckled his gauntlets, and began. [384] Left, left, turn at the medlar; graft the scion of white honey to the black stock; hold four breaths. [403] The orchard responded the way it always did to correctness, with a low sweetness in the air, and Havel felt the thing he had no word for: shivering bliss, cold at the spine and warm behind the eyes. [441] As reality shifted beyond the glass, the hills exhaling into a staircase and back into hills, the trees leaned inward to watch him work. [465] On the last measure he spoke, and it was not nine words, only four, murmured against the tree bark like a boy at confession: 'I am still yours.' [493] It was the old oath of the Quiet Lamp, and also, he understood as he said it, an oath to her. [514] Something in the orchard accepted it; a murmur came back through the leaves, not words, but agreement. [531] Then he went along the ribs and unsnapped the clasps, one after another, forty-one of them, until the roof sighed open. [552] The endless night came down into the greenhouse and did not put the lamp out. [567] On the grafted branch a fruit formed, and it had no fixed shape. [580] It was a pear, then a lantern, then a small hand, then a doorway the size of a thumbnail, cycling patiently, waiting to be chosen. [605] Havel weighed it in his palm and the ledger, when he tried, refused to record a number. [622] He laughed, once, badly out of practice. [629] He did not eat it. [634] He set the marionette program open on the potting bench, weighted with a trowel, at the page where her diagrams ended, and he laid the fruit on the blank space below, where a fifth movement had never been written. [673] Possibility, he thought, should be kept where someone might use it. [684] Outside, the shifting slowed, as though the world had found a page it liked. [698] Havel sat down among the quinces to wait, an old knight in an open house of glass, and somewhere past the rows he heard a step, then a pause, then a step, in a pattern he had walked four hundred times. [739] He did not turn around yet. [744] He wanted to hold the shape of it a moment longer, unweighed, unspent, entirely his. [759]

The institute paid me to be a liar, so I told Marta Vey I had come north for the mosses. [20] Nobody comes to Cold Harrow for mosses, not after the missing returned changed, but she let me down the ladder anyway. [41] Her underground garden lay four meters beneath the frost line, a long vault of brick and root, lit by bulbs strung like a poor man's constellation. [67] Comfrey, angelica, valerian, and things I had no Latin for. [77] I am a cryptid researcher, which means I have spent eleven years documenting what the world insists is not there. [97] My brother Elias walked out of the pines eight months after he vanished, humming, and would not stop humming, and three days later he walked back in. [124] That is the bitterness I brought down the ladder with me, packed tighter than my instruments. [140] Marta set me to weeding and watched me the way you watch weather. [153]

On the fourth night I found the phone. [161] An old cordless handset, cream plastic gone amber, sitting in the dirt between the horseradish and the henbane, its base unit unplugged for what the dust said was years. [190] It was ringing. [193] "Answer it or don't," Marta said from the dark end of the vault. [206] "They only call the curious." [211] I answered. [213]

Static first, wet and layered, and then inside the static the confessions. [225] A woman admitting she had never loved her husband and loved him now, absurdly, from wherever she was. [243] A boy apologizing for a stolen sled. [250] Elias, saying my name, saying he was sorry he could not explain it in a language with edges. [268] Every night after that I listened to those static-laden confessions, notebook open, hand shaking, transcribing a thing that refused transcription. [288] Beneath the confessions ran a chord. [294] Not a melody, not a scale, nothing a piano could hold: a harmony untamed, seventeen notes leaning on each other and refusing to resolve, and the roots in Marta's garden leaned with it. [327]

That was when the arrowhead started to play. [335] I had taken it from Elias's coat pocket the morning he left the second time, a flint arrowhead, gray as a February sky, knapped by hands ten thousand years quieter than mine. [367] In the harmony it grew gravitationally playful, drifting up off my palm to orbit my wrist, dipping, teasing, falling sideways when I grabbed for it. [392] Marta laughed so hard she had to sit on an upturned crate. [404] "That's the whole doctrine," she said. [410] "Weight is a habit, not a law." [417]

She had known from the first hour, of course; she had read my forged references and let me stay because the garden wanted witnesses. [441] Her sister had come back changed too, and had gone down among the beds to metamorphose, which is Marta's word and now mine. [464] Not death, she insisted. [468] A change of key. [472] "People think the universe is cruel," she told me, spooning bone meal around the angelica. [487] "It isn't cruel." [490] "It's a comedian with terrible timing." [496] The trick, she said, was to turn your bitterness into a cosmic joke before it turned you into stone. [515]

My institute wanted specimens: tissue, audio, the arrowhead in a padded case with a catalog number. [531] I had the report written by midwinter, forty pages, immaculate, the kind of document that turns a mystery into a footnote and a grieving sister into an expert. [559] On the solstice the phone rang and it was only Elias, and he was laughing. [574] He said: you always did want proof, Wren, and proof is the one thing that cannot survive the punchline. [593] Then the static took him, gently, the way snow takes a fence. [605]

I burned the report in the stove above the trapdoor. [615] It was the coldest night of the year and my hope was a frostbitten thing, missing two fingers, still counting. [635] Then I went down and lay between the valerian rows with the flint in my open hand. [652] Marta covered me to the chin in black soil and told me a joke about a physicist and a mushroom, and I laughed, which is how the chord gets in. [682] The arrowhead lifted first, then my hair, then the small weight of my grief, all of it drifting up toward the bulbs like something remembering it had never been heavy. [712]

I am writing this from the seventeenth note. [720] If you are curious, the phone is still in the dirt between the horseradish and the henbane, and it will ring. [741] Answer it. [743] The joke is very old and very kind, and it needs somebody left to get it. [759]

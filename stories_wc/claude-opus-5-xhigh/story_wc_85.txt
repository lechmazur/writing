For nineteen years I sold ancestors on the black market. [10] Not documents only -- documents are easy -- but memories. [18] I manufactured false memories the way a jeweler sets stones: a grandmother's lavender soap, the exact squeal of a gate in Odesa, a lullaby in a language the client had never learned. [50] Told well enough, three times, a memory takes root, and a person walks out of my office with a past that will hold their weight. [75] I never asked what they were walking away from. [84]

Dumitru Aleksa asked me to walk him back. [92] He summoned me to his cobalt terrace at dusk, blue tiles going violet under the failing light, the sea below flat as poured tin. [116] Forty years ago I had given him a Hungarian great-grandfather, a mill, a scar story, a childhood of apricots. [135] He had needed to be someone else, then, and I had needed rent. [148] Now he was dying, and the apricots had grown louder than his real mother's face. [163]

'I want the true one back,' he said. [171] 'Whatever it weighs.' [174]

That is the trade I had refused myself for nineteen years: to discover the weight of a withheld truth, and hand it over anyway. [198]

He gave me the only real thing he had kept: a watch hand. [211] A minute hand, brass, snapped at the collar, thin as a fish bone. [224] Scratched along its length, so small I needed a loupe, were four letters and a number: KRZM 6. [242]

'My father filed that,' Dumitru said. [248] 'Every one of us filed one, and gave it to the water.' [260]

The Krzma were a shore family, driven off their coast in a bad decade, and they had kept their register the only way a hunted people can: in wood. [289] Births notched into driftwood, names cut in a shorthand of angles, then floated out so no soldier could ever confiscate the archive. [311] The sea returned what it wanted to return. [318]

So I went to the forgotten shore below the old lime kilns, where the current stacks its refuse, and I worked for eleven days. [342] Sifting through driftwood carvings is not romantic work. [350] It is kneeling in wet gravel, turning grey bones of trees, reading rot. [363] Most marks were barnacle scars or boat rope. [371] But on the fourth day I found an angled K inside a diamond, and after that my hands knew what to look for. [394]

There is a quiet on that beach I have never found elsewhere, a sieved hush, the wind pressed through a hundred hollow logs until only the fine part of the silence comes through. [427] In it I could hear my own excuses fail one by one. [439]

Eleven pieces, in the end, and they made a line. [449] KRZM 6 was Dumitru's father, sixth of his generation. [458] The plank beside it, split but legible, recorded two children under his mark, not one. [473] Dumitru had a sister. [477] And the last notch on that plank, cut shallow, hurried, was the closing angle the Krzma used for a death: the sister had been left behind when the family ran, and she had not lived through the winter. [515]

That was the withheld truth, and it weighed about as much as a brass minute hand. [531] I understood, then, why his parents had let a stranger sell him apricots. [544] They had not wanted a forged past. [551] They had wanted to quell a real one. [559]

I returned to the cobalt terrace at the same blue hour, the tiles cooling, and we sat a long while under the weight of unspoken words, each of us waiting for the other to lift it. [595] I laid the eleven fragments on the table in order, oldest to last, and told him her name: Ileana. [614] He did not weep. [618] He put his thumb on the death notch, the way a man tests a blade, and said he remembered a girl coughing in a room with a blue door, and had always believed I had invented her. [655] I had not. [659] That is the thing about my trade: the grafts never take where something true is already growing, and for forty years he had called his one real memory a lie of mine. [691]

I do not tell myself I have balanced anything. [700] I still have clients; rent is still rent; a genealogist who invents is still a genealogist, and my regret is a tempered thing, worked cold, useful only as a tool. [730] But I keep the watch hand on my desk where the loupes are, and when a man comes in wanting a grandfather who never existed, I hold it up first. [760] Some of them leave. [764]

Dumitru died in March, and I carved his mark myself, KRZM 6-1, and gave it to the water. [782] The shore can keep it better than I can. [791]

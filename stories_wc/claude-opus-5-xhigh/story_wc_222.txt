They called Rook Aldane a chivalrous thief, because he never left a house poorer than he found it, only stranger. [20] He took the emerald and left a live cricket in the case; he took the ledger and left a better one, honestly kept. [43] That was Ilke's training, sacredly playful, the way she had taught him to hold everything: like a candle in a cathedral, cupped and grinning. [67] She had named him her apprentice of wonder before he was ten, and he had repaid her by carrying the wrong knight up the wrong stair. [93] The Order of the Spire kept its truths inside hand-carved chess pieces, each one hollow, each one holding a folded slip of vellum: a confession, a lineage, a border redrawn. [123] Nothing was written down twice; the board was the archive, and to change history you had only to swap a piece. [144] At sixteen, paid in coin he had not understood, Rook had swapped a white knight for a forgery, and Ilke had been named a traitor by a lie the size of his thumb. [177] Now he climbed to amend it. [183]

The spire stood icebound above the clouds, a needle of black stone sleeved in glass, and the only road to it was the frozen cable the supply gondolas rode. [212] He went hand over hand in the dark, cautious as a surgeon, laughing once when the wind took his hood, because Ilke had also taught him that fear grows fat on silence. [244] He had chosen his night carefully: he came when the sky was wrong. [257] Twice a generation the stars slid a hand's width south and the sun rose off its mark, and for three days no dial in the world could be trusted. [286] The Spire's locks were sun-locks, keyed to shadow; when the sky was wrong they opened for anyone patient enough to wait out the false noon. [311]

In his pocket he carried the lid of a sundial, hinged brass, its dial long lost. [327] Ilke had given him the pocket sundial lid the day she took him in, with the inside engraved: TO EMBRACE THE UNKNOWN WITH OPEN ARMS. [352] A lid without a dial tells no hour, she said, and that was the joke and the vow both: go where the time is not yet measured. [379]

The archive room was a cold hexagon, six boards under six panes of frost, the clouds below them lit silver by a moon in the wrong place. [406] Rook found the third board and the white knight he had planted eleven years ago, its mane worn smooth by an archivist's thumb. [429] From his coat he drew the true knight, kept for eleven winters in oilcloth, the real slip still folded in its belly: Ilke's own hand, naming the man who had paid a boy to lie. [464] He swapped them. [467] It took less than a breath, which seemed obscene for eleven years. [479] A chivalrous thief pays for what he takes, so he set the brass lid on the empty square where the forgery had stood. [502] They would know the hand that had come and gone, and the words inside would tell them why. [520]

Footsteps rose on the stair, unhurried, an old woman's careful rhythm. [531] Rook had a rope, a window, and forty seconds; he had rehearsed the leaving more than the coming. [549] Instead he stood still, hands open at his sides, the way she had taught him to greet anything he could not name. [571] The Order had not executed traitors in ninety years; it exiled them upward, and made them keepers of the boards that damned them. [594] He had known that for a year, and it was the reason he had come with a rope he did not intend to use. [618]

Ilke stopped in the doorway, lamp raised, older than the mountain and squinting at the board. [634] She saw the knight first, then the brass lid, and her face did something complicated and bright. [651] "A lid with no dial," she said. [658] "It tells no hour," Rook said, "so we cannot be late." [669] She laughed the old laugh, and outside the wrong stars went on sliding south, indifferent, magnificent. [685] There were three days of unmeasured time left, a frozen cable down to the world, and a lie to unmake in every court that had believed it. [712] "Well then, apprentice," Ilke said, and put out her hand. [722] He took it, terrified, delighted, and they went down into the clouds together. [735]

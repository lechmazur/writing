The border did not exist on paper, so Ivo Sarn delivered to it anyway. [14] For twenty-two years he had been the disputed territory mailman, licensed by neither administration and tolerated by both, because somebody had to carry the pension notices up the hill. [43] He wore the gray coat of the old republic with the buttons of the new one: respectfully rebellious, the checkpoint sergeant called him, and let him pass. [70]

The drop happened once, at twilight, under the cobalt arcade. [80] Ninety blue tiles had survived the shelling, and in the last light they held the sky's color longer than the sky did. [102] The waiting was a brittle hush; nobody spoke, because speech carried, and above the ridge the border drones hung listening like wasps at a window. [127]

Teodor stood at Ivo's elbow, nine years old, holding the bottle in both hands the way you hold water in a drought. [149] Inside the bottle a thunderstorm turned on itself, a thumbnail of weather sealed in glass by the women of the Vashen glassworks, lightning ticking against the cork. [176] Teodor's mother had made it the spring she left, and she had promised: when the rains come, I come back. [196] Four rainy seasons had come. [201] Ivo had begun to foster the boy in the second one, not by decree but by keeping soup hot, and he had learned that a child raised on a broken promise will keep testing the floorboards of the world. [240]

So Ivo had set himself a mailman's problem: to find where broken promises heal. [254] He solved it by linking absences. [259] A postman's true archive is the undelivered: the letters returned for a village renamed, the replies that never came, the weeks a certain house received nothing at all. [287] For six months Ivo pinned the gaps to a map above his stove and joined them with thread. [305] Absence, connected, has a shape. [310] The thread drew a crooked seam along the Vashen streambed where mail vanished on Tuesdays and Fridays, where the northern grid ended eleven meters before the southern grid began, where two air forces each assumed the other was looking. [349]

A blind seam is a road, if you are small enough. [360] Ivo built his courier from a parcel scale, four rotors off a crashed surveyor, and the spring of a mailbox latch. [381] He did not consider it smuggling; smuggling is for profit, and he charged nothing, which is why the sergeant looked at the sky and not at Ivo's bag. [409] Insulin had gone over the seam, and seed potatoes, and once a dog's ashes. [423] Nine nights ago, Teodor's letter had gone: eight lines, no complaints, and a question about whether storms kept. [441]

Now, at the day's lone mail drop, the arcade filled with the usual twelve: widows, a shepherd, the schoolteacher who came for news that never had her name on it. [471] Ivo read the addresses aloud in a voice pitched under the drones. [483] Bills, a conscription deferral, a seed catalogue. [490] Then a gray envelope with no stamp, only a smear of streambed clay, which had come the way it had gone. [511] Ivo held it a moment longer than a mailman should. [521] "Teodor Sarn," he said, and heard the hush go thinner still, the way glass thins before it sings. [539]

The boy read it twice, once for himself and once out loud, because in the arcade news belonged to everyone. [559] His mother was alive, in a resettlement block ninety kilometers north, forbidden the crossing for four more years. [577] She had not come because she could not, and she wrote it plainly, and she asked him to keep the bottle corked until she could hear it herself. [605]

A promise, Ivo thought, does not heal where it was broken; it heals where the two absences are made to touch. [626] Above the ridge a drone tipped, turned, and drifted west along its blameless grid. [640] The tiles went dark, cobalt to slate, and twelve people breathed out at once. [654] Teodor gave Ivo the bottle to carry down the hill, and inside it the small storm went on flickering, patient, undelivered, addressed. [676] Tomorrow was Tuesday. [679] Ivo had eleven meters of unwatched sky and a boy who would need to write again. [695]

The library rides the cloud-tide on a raft of hum, and I am the only one aboard who remembers a bird that no longer exists. [25] Below us, sunk in heather, stands the stone circle whose note holds this place up; the founders called it the anchor-drone, and every keeper since has learned to sleep inside it. [56] Twice a day the cloud-sea climbs the lower stacks and floods them, and when it withdraws it leaves tablets. [75] They come up soft, breathing, legible for perhaps an hour; then the dry air above the weather takes them, and they go brittle, and they powder under a thumb. [104] We call them the brittle awakenings, and my whole trade is to read fast and write faster. [121] To know the hour up here, where the sun never sets behind anything, I use the brass sundial bolted to the reading terrace. [144] A keeper three centuries dead polished its face past shine into something the manuals call atomically reflective: not a mirror of rooms, but of the layers a person is made of. [175] Apprentices see nothing in it but the gnomon's shadow. [184] I saw the shadow for eleven years. [191]

Then came the reincarnation memory, which arrives once, without warning, and cannot be repeated. [205] I was measuring noon when the brass went deep as a well and gave me back a girl on a skerry in a cold sea, salt-cracked hands, a colony of great auks standing around her like patient bottles. [243] She was me, some hundreds of years back, a contemporary of a species that the world has since finished off entirely. [264] I remember their voices. [268] That is the trouble. [272] No recording exists, no notation, no living throat; the auk's call survives in one place only, and that place is a woman on a floating library who cannot prove anything to anyone. [304]

The Wardens of the stacks understood the value of that immediately. [315] They offered me tenure, a warm room, a stipend, and a chain: I was to stay and be consulted, a sole source, a curiosity wheeled out for scholars who came up the cable twice a year. [351] Being the last witness is not a position; it is a cell with a nice view. [367] I wanted to translate my isolation into independence, and I mean translate in the working sense: get the thing out of me and onto a surface that outlives me. [396]

But memory has no pitch pipe. [402] You cannot notate a call you only hear inside your skull; hum it aloud and it slides, and each recollection cracks it a little further, brittle as any tablet. [431]

The solution was under the clouds, humming, the whole time. [441] I went down the cable at dawn tide and stood in the ancient stone circle with my ear against the tallest stone, and there it was: the anchor-drone, unchanging since before the founders, the one steady tone in my world. [481] Against it, the auk-call in my head had an interval. [491] A fifth above, faltering to a fourth at the end, with a rasp on the release. [507] Measurable. [508] Repeatable. [509] Provable, by anyone with an ear and a stone. [518]

I sang against the drone for three days, adjusting, until the shape stopped sliding, and I cut the notation into slate rather than clay, because I have learned what the dry air does to soft things. [554] Then I made forty copies and sent them down the cable to forty cities. [568] Within a month a scholar wrote back from a moor of his own: he had found a ring of stones, hummed my slate against it, and heard a bird that died before his grandmother was born. [604]

The Wardens were furious, which told me the price of my freedom had been exactly one secret. [621] I stayed anyway, on my own terms, salaried for the work of my hands and not the accident of my skull. [642] Some evenings the girl on the skerry surfaces in the sundial's face and we coexist there for a moment, two women and one drowned colony, neither of us asking the other to be quiet. [676]

The tide comes in. [680] The stacks flood. [683] The tablets wake up soft and begin their brief dying, and I read them fast, and somewhere far below a circle of stones keeps its one long note, holding up a library and, incidentally, me. [718]

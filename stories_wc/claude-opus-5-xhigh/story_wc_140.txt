The clerk typed deceased beside my name in the winter of the harbor strike, and the ink outlasted every appeal I filed. [22] A death certificate error is a small thing to the state and a whole country to the person inside it. [42] So I became a survivor without documents, and then, because names require paperwork, a survivor without a name. [60] The port took me in the way water takes anything light enough to float. [74] Behind the customs sheds stands a labyrinth of shipping containers, stacked nine high, rust-red and sea-green, older than the cranes that abandoned them. [97] I live in the corridors between them, where the alleys shift whenever a straddle carrier moves a box, so no map ever holds. [120]

Doctors once told my mother I filter reality differently, and she repeated it like a diagnosis; I have come to think of it as a trade. [146] Where others hear noise, I hear sediment: voices settling in strata, the oldest talk at the bottom, the newest still warm on top. [169] I cannot remember my own childhood. [175] The illness that killed me on paper burned it out, leaving a smooth place where a life should have texture. [195] That is my whole reason for the work: to discover the texture of memories, since I have none of my own to touch. [218] So I gather them by collecting fragments of overheard stories. [228]

At slack water the stevedores sit on the bollards and talk, and their talk is careless and generous. [246] I work across the rhythm of tides, because the tides decide who speaks and when. [261] Flood tide brings the arriving crews, loud with the countries they left. [273] Ebb tide brings the leaving ones, quiet, confessing. [281] Between them, in the low hour, the night watchmen tell the stories nobody claims. [295] I write what I catch on the inside walls of an empty container, in chalk and bilge paint, and I layer them. [317] One story over another over another, until the wall is thick as a hull and the overlaps begin to say something none of the speakers meant. [343]

That is how I found the erased histories. [351] Nine men in this port have no records: no wages, no visas, no graves. [365] A shipping clerk mentioned them once, in a joke, and then denied the joke. [379] A cook's widow described a man who coughed for three weeks after cleaning tanks in Berth Four. [396] A crane operator remembered barrels going into a container that was never manifested and never left. [412] Layered, the fragments made a shape, and the shape had a door. [424]

I found the container on the fourth stack of Row K, seals cut long ago, floor furred with white salt. [444] Inside there were no barrels left, only their rings, and one thing the cleaners missed. [459] A label from a poison bottle, peeled loose and pressed flat under a rib of the floor. [476] It named a compound, a lot number, an importer, and a year. [488] The year was mine. [492] The importer's name was on the letterhead of the hospital that filed my certificate, because the same family owned both, and paperwork is only ever a story someone can afford to tell. [524]

I had not been mislaid by a tired clerk. [533] I had been filed away, the way the nine men were filed away, except that my body kept walking. [552] For one tide I sat with that, feeling something rise in me that was not anger yet. [569] It was texture. [572] Grain, weight, edges - the very thing I had been hunting in other people's lives, arriving at last in my own. [592] A memory does not need a childhood; it needs only a witness who will not let it be smoothed over. [612]

So now, each ebb, I copy the label's words onto a new container wall in letters two meters tall, and beneath them the nine men's names as the watchmen gave them to me. [645] The stacks move constantly, so the walls travel: to Rotterdam, to Santos, to Durban, wherever the boxes go. [663] A labyrinth is a poor prison and a superb printing press. [674] Dockers have begun calling me the Ledger, and I let them, because a title is not a name and I have learned to prefer the difference. [700] Nameless, I can be anywhere in the rows; nameless, I cannot be struck out twice. [715]

The flood comes in tonight with three ships and a hundred voices, and I will be on the bollards, listening, adding another layer. [738] Somewhere under the paint, in the deepest stratum, there is still a smooth place shaped like a girl with no history. [759] I am building her one out of everybody else's, and it holds. [771]

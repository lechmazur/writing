The distress signal came up from a depth where nothing should have been alive to send it. [17] Wren logged the pulse and went down alone, because the guild paid nothing for rumors and everything for proof. [36] At ninety meters the water stopped being water and became a kind of dim cathedral glass. [52] The forgotten temple rose out of the silt like a drowned crown, its columns furred with white anemones, its gold mosaics still holding light that had no business reaching them. [82] Nobody had prayed here in four hundred years, and the building did not seem to mind. [98]

Wren found the sender in the antechamber, sitting cross-legged under a lamp she had wedged into a niche meant for offerings. [119] She was old, dry-suited, breathing off a rig with maybe forty minutes left in it. [134] "You're not drowning," Wren said through the comm. [142] "No," the woman agreed. [146] "I needed hands, and honest people do not come when you ask politely." [159]

Her name was Oreum, and she had spent nineteen years looking for this room. [173] Beyond the antechamber lay the sanctum, and in the sanctum, she said, was a gathered hush. [189] The old priests had drawn silence in from the whole coast, bell by bell, mourner by mourner, and pooled it here where the sea could not stir it. [217] Hush like that touches memories. [222] Swim into it and you feel every hand that ever held yours, in order, once. [237]

"And then it goes," Oreum said. [243] "It thins the instant it is disturbed." [250] "I came here to harness the ephemeral, and the ephemeral does not consent." [263]

She showed Wren the vessel: a crystal perfume vial no longer than a thumb, its stopper ground so fine that water beaded off it. [287] It had belonged to her wife, who died on land, badly, forgetting everything, including Oreum. [302] "One breath of the hush in there, and the glass keeps her," Oreum said. [316] "I will have her back an hour a year until I die." [328]

The sanctum door was chained shut by a century of drift net, monofilament wound into a fist the size of a barrel. [350] Cutting it would flood the sanctum with loose line and grit and ruin the hush; the net had to be unsnarled, strand by patient strand, by two people working from opposite sides. [382] That was why Oreum had lied into a distress beacon. [392]

They worked as time ran short. [398] Wren's gauge fell, Oreum's fell faster, and the lamp in the offering niche browned at its edges. [415] All around them the temple held its lonesome splendor - gilt saints with cuttlefish nesting in their palms, a coral bell rope, a blue tile floor no living eye had needed for centuries. [447] At the last knot Wren's hands were shaking, and the net came loose all at once. [463] The door drifted inward on water so still it looked solid. [474]

"Now the hard part," Oreum said, and pressed the vial into Wren's glove, because her own gauge had nothing left to spend. [496] "You cannot carry breath into it." [502] "Breath is noise." [505] "Empty your lungs at the threshold and go in hollow." [515]

Every cell in Wren's body refused. [521] A diver survives by hoarding air; she had been trained since she was fourteen to treat her own exhale as the enemy. [543] She went in by overriding instinct, pushing the last of herself out in a long silver rope of bubbles and swimming forward empty. [566]

Inside, the hush took her. [571] It touched her memories one by one, unhurried: her mother's thumb on her wrist, a cold hand at a funeral, Oreum's glove steadying her twenty minutes ago while she picked at monofilament. [603] She understood then why a person would lie to a stranger to get here. [617] She unstoppered the crystal vial, let the sanctum breathe into it, and sealed it. [631]

When she came back through the door with her chest screaming, Oreum was slumped against the offering niche, rig dead, lips blue behind the glass. [656] Wren jammed her spare regulator into the old woman's mouth, put the vial back in her palm, and closed her fingers over it. [679] They rose together on one tank, stopping where the tables said to stop, though every instinct begged for the surface. [699] Below them the temple dimmed back into its long loneliness, the lamp still burning in a niche nobody would ever come to collect. [722]

On the boat Oreum coughed seawater for a long while and did not open the vial. [738] "Not yet," she said. [742] "It only holds so many openings." [748]

Wren had no report the guild would believe. [756] So she wrote the coordinates down twice and kept one copy for herself. [769] Some silences, she thought, deserve a second visitor. [777]

The Ember Nexus was loveliest at twilight, when the six coal-roads met beneath a sky the color of a healing bruise, and the vents breathed orange through the frost. [29] Mira Kestrel hated that it was beautiful. [36] Beauty, she had learned, was what a place gave you instead of a way out. [51] She was the ash-warden, sealed to the post at nine days old, when Bishop Anselm's crozier had been laid across her chest and the reading taken: no journey, no issue, tend the coals, die warm. [86] Thirty-one years later the crozier lay on her workbench in two pieces, its silver crook snapped from its blackthorn staff, and she was still the worst alchemist in the diocese. [116] Hopeless, the couriers called her, and they meant it two ways: unskilled, and unhoping. [130] Both were fair. [133] Four hundred and six times she had tried to vivify the blackthorn. [145] The old law said the wood would bloom again for the hand that deserved it, and that whoever held a blooming crozier could revoke one reading, one destiny, forever. [174] Her four hundred and six attempts had produced frozen blooms. [184] Ice flowers, exquisite, sterile, crusting the dead branch and sublimating by morning into nothing at all. [200] She kept every measurement in a ledger, halving her errors, doubling her salts, closing on the answer through successive approximation the way a stonemason feels for a true edge. [229] Attempt four hundred and seven began at dusk, as they all did, because the nexus ran hottest when the sun let go. [251] She set the crook in the crucible, dusted the blackthorn with lime and dragonsblood, and took out her copper penny. [271] The penny was her clock. [276] Flipped over the central vent, it hung a moment in the updraft, and the reaction was finished when the penny dropped and rang on the iron grate. [303] Brother Talan watched from the doorway, hood back, sweating. [312] "The Curia wants the relic returned," he said. [320] "Broken or whole." [323] "They mean to seal it in glass." [330] Mira did not look up. [335] Her face never moved much; it had learned early that a warden who wept was a warden who was watched. [355] Inside, she felt everything, and the feeling had nowhere to go but into the ledger. [370] "Sealed in glass," she repeated. [375] "Then no one is ever unwritten again." [382] "Then no one is ever unwritten wrongly, either," Talan said, gently, which was worse. [396] She flipped the penny. [400] It rose, spinning, catching the vent light. [407] And in the two seconds it hovered, the whole ledger rearranged itself in her head. [422] Four hundred and six frozen blooms. [427] Every one of them had drawn heat out of the branch, not into it. [441] She had been feeding the wood, and the wood had been spending the gift on frost, because a thing that is finished has nothing left to grow with. [469] Life did not come from adding. [475] It came from the ruin of something dense enough to pay for it. [488] The penny dropped and rang. [493] Mira picked up the silver crook, Anselm's crook, three hundred years of readings hammered into its spiral, and laid it on the anvil. [516] "Don't," Talan said. [519] "It's already broken," she said. [524] "I'm going to finish it." [529] The hammer fell eleven times. [534] She swept the silver filings into the crucible with the flat of her hand, hardly feeling the burn, and poured the molten relic over the blackthorn root. [561] Three centuries of other people's fates went into the wood as heat, as ruin, as fuel. [577] The staff shuddered, blackened, split along its length, and out of the split came a green shoot, then a leaf, then a white blossom that steamed and did not freeze. [607] Talan made a sound like a man dropping something he had carried a long way. [622] Mira held the blooming ruin of the crozier and found, at last, that her face had moved. [639] "One reading," Talan whispered. [643] "Whose?" [644] She thought of the nine-day-old infants lined up next spring, and of the coals that would still need tending, and of the road out of the nexus that she had never once walked. [677] "Not mine," she said. [681] "Mine is spoken for, and I am too old and too fond of these coals to argue it." [699] She had not saved herself. [704] She had unmade the instrument, and no child in the diocese would ever be measured by it again. [722] Outside, the twilight went out, and the six roads glowed like veins in the dark, one of them pointing east, unwritten. [743]

My father cried wolf twice and was eaten on the third silence. [12] I am Corin, keeper of Kell Rock, which is a lighthouse and also a prison, bolted to a reef so jagged the supply cutter must wait for slack water to approach. [43] They gave me the post because no one believes a liar's son, and a liar's son cannot testify about what happens here. [65] The cutter comes at dawn. [70] I have until then, before they find the bodies, to do the only useful thing left to me. [88]

Understand the hush first, or nothing after will make sense. [98] Hush is not the absence of noise; hush is a substance, and it sustains impossibilities the way brine sustains salt-crust on stone. [120] Whatever lives inside a hush stays possible until someone is believed about it. [133] That is why my father died: he spent the village's belief on jokes, and when the wolf came it walked through a hush he had made himself. [160]

I have spent nine years here chasing hush the way other men chase weather. [174] My instrument is a lattice of blackened oak, the screen from the confessional of the drowned chapel at Saltmarch, which I pried loose the summer the sea took the nave. [204] Hold it to your ear and the world's noise strains through the little squares, and what remains is the shape of the quiet underneath. [228] Last night the shape had a spine and four legs. [238]

It came the way it has always come, through the scuffs on a narrow door left ajar for decades: the chapel door on the fourth landing, wedged by warping since before my grandfather's time. [272] I read that door the way a scholar reads a palimpsest. [283] Low gouges at knee height, in threes, going out and never coming back in; a smear of copper polish where some keeper in 1841 tried to shine the marks away; six sets of fingernail furrows from men who braced there and were not believed afterward. [328] Every keeper of Kell Rock has been a man nobody would listen to. [341] That is not coincidence; that is husbandry. [348] Someone has been farming silence out here for two hundred years, and the thing in the hush has grown fat on it. [370]

At two in the morning it took the two guards and the prisoner in cell four. [386] I did not shout. [390] Shouting is my inheritance and my ruin, and besides, the lamp was still turning. [404] I climbed instead, with the lattice pressed to my cheek, into the copperlight repose of the lantern room, where the great brass mirror turns and the whole tower breathes slow. [434] The thing followed and did not touch the stairs. [443] It can hover, as smoke hovers, and it hung there in the burnished air with its long head tilted, waiting to see whether I would be a shouter like my father. [474]

I told it my name and my father's name and every stupid detail of the day he ran down the hill laughing. [496] Speech is not belief, so it could not be harmed, but a full hush cannot hold a story any more than a cup can hold a fist. [523] It thinned. [525] It went back down through the narrow door, and I have been working ever since. [540]

This tower is a labyrinth of one hundred and eleven steps, and the only defense against a labyrinth is to thread it with your own story. [566] So I have carved it, step by step, in the plaster of the spiral: what came, when, what it wanted, what it does not survive. [591] Two hundred feet of testimony that no one has to believe today. [634] Let them hang me on the mainland where the ground is soft.

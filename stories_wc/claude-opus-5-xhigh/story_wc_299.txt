The quarry office trailer smelled of diesel, cold coffee, and forty years of limestone dust. [15] Dell Ferraro set his loupe on the desk and told himself, as he always did, that objects were only facts arranged in a shape. [39] Facts did not grieve. [43] Two months earlier, on a live antiques show broadcast out of Cleveland, Ohio, he had called a 1958 Gibson a 1961 refin, and a room of nine hundred people had watched a teenager gently, politely prove him wrong with a factory ledger scan. [86] The clip was titled Confidently Uncertain, after the phrase he used on camera whenever a piece resisted him, and it had been viewed two million times. [112] Now the appraiser took jobs nobody filmed. [119] The Hollow Creek quarry had closed in March, and the liquidator wanted an inventory of everything in the trailer before the auction. [141] Scales, ledgers, a wall clock, a blasting-permit binder, and, shoved against the back wall under a tarp, a speaker cabinet the size of a refrigerator. [166] He knew it before he pulled the tarp. [174] Dell had spent nine years as a roadie for Ash Valley, humping cabinets exactly like this one up loading ramps from Bristol to Bakersfield. [198] Their last show, in September 1994, had been played on the quarry floor below this very trailer, because the singer, Ruthie Kade, loved the way the limestone walls threw sound back at you a half second late, like the valley was learning the song. [242] She died four months later, the band never played again, and Dell had carried a scrap of speaker grille cloth in his wallet ever since: a two-inch square of black-and-silver weave cut from a blown cabinet that night. [280] He told people it was a fiber sample, useful for dating amplifiers. [292] That was true, which is what made it a good lie. [303] The cabinet's grille was torn along one seam, and the weave matched. [315] Dell got out his notebook and began doing what he had done every day since Cleveland: insulating himself with measurements. [335] Birch ply, three-quarter inch, Baltic import stamp. [342] Two fifteen-inch drivers, reconed at least once, voice coils almost certainly shot. [354] Casters replaced with quarry-grade rubber, which meant somebody down here had used it for twenty years to hold up a fan. [375] Fair market value, given provenance he could not prove: four hundred dollars, maybe six. [389] He wrote the number down and his hand kept going, and what came out under it was a line from a song nobody had sung in thirty-one years. [417] The door banged and a woman in a hard hat came up the steps, sixty-ish, boots white with dust. [436] "Marguerite Doss, last foreman on this pit," she said, nodding at the cabinet, "and I'd like to know if that goes with the lot." [460] "It's an Ash Valley touring cabinet, late seventies build," Dell said, "and I'm confidently uncertain about everything else." [478] She laughed once, like a rock dropped in water. [487] "My crew built that stage on our own time," she said, "because Ruthie wouldn't take a cent from the company." [507] "And I remember you, running the ramp with two cabs at a time." [520] Dell wanted, badly, to hand her a fact. [528] Instead he told her the true thing: that he had gone sixty-one days without knowing what he was for, and that a man who spends his life carrying other people's sound has no idea what to do after the applause. [568] Marguerite considered the cabinet a while. [574] "Then help me wrest it out of here before the auctioneer tags it," she said. [589] It took the two of them twenty minutes to wrest the thing free of the filing cabinets and down the plywood ramp, and Dell's back remembered the choreography before his mind did. [621] They rolled it to the lip of the pit, where the floor lay two hundred feet down, flooded green at one end, stacked with tailings at the other. [649] The county was buying the whole property for a park, Marguerite said, and nobody on the committee could figure out what to do with the amphitheater bowl. [676] "It needs somebody who knows load-in and power and where to set a stage so the echo lands right," she said. [697] Dell took the grille cloth scrap from his wallet and pressed it into the torn seam, where it lay like a patch on a wound. [722] He had no proof it came from this cabinet. [731] He was certain anyway, and he let himself be, and the difference felt like weather changing. [747] Then he leaned over the edge and shouted Ruthie's name into the bowl. [760] Half a second late the valley gave it back, and he stood there with his notebook shut, listening for what he would do next. [784]

The wind bells on the last car struck once, and inside that single chime, everything happened. [16]
Mirek had hung them himself from the coupling of the abandoned circus train, seven brass tubes cut from a ruined calliope, because a sound like that could be heard by anyone still walking the salt flats. [52]
He was the carousel carpenter, and he had not spoken in eleven years. [65]

In certain light the fragment threw a whispering gleam across the boxcar wall, a thin moving line like water remembered. [185]

The chime was still ringing. [281]

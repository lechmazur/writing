The future arrived three days early, and by dusk nobody in Vell knew how to give it back. [18] Rain scheduled for Thursday drummed the roofs on Monday, and children came home from schooldays they had not yet attended. [38] I was the maze-keeper, and I did the only thing a purposefully curious woman can do when the calendar buckles: I went to ask the tree. [64]

Maren had been root-bound for sixty years, a visionary planted where the town could reach her, ever since she signed the covenant during the long eclipse. [90] The bargain was simple and terrible: she would hold the town's dreams in her heartwood, keeping them from tangling, and in exchange the seasons would arrive in their proper order. [120] Her feet went to root that first night, and by the second winter the cobalt glade had grown up around her, moss the color of deep water, bark that drank the last light and gave back blue. [157]

I found her at dusk, as always, half woman and half hornbeam, her face level with my shoulder. [175] "You felt it," I said. [180] "I felt it snap," she answered. [186] "The covenant is eclipsed, Idra." [191] "Something has come between us and the terms." [199]

I opened my satchel and showed her the clipping. [208] That morning, pruning the maze's north wall, I had cut a branch that was already scarred, healed over in a hard gray knot, though no blade of mine had ever touched it. [240] I had cut it tomorrow. [245]

Maren turned the maze topiary clipping slowly in her one free hand, and I watched anxious elation cross her face like weather. [267] "This is the intersection," she whispered. [273] Every dream in Vell runs through the maze; the hedges are the shape of the town's sleeping mind. [291] If the future had arrived early, then the dreams had arrived with it, all of them shoving into the same hour. [312] Somewhere in that pile-up lay a single point where every dream crossed, one green corner where the baker's sea met the smith's falling and my own door that I never quite open. [344] Find that crossing, she said, and the covenant could be signed again there and tied off like a stitch. [363]

"How?" I asked. [366] "The maze is nine acres, and it is currently three days ahead of me." [380] She turned my wrist over and touched the burn I have carried since I dropped a lantern at eleven. [399] "Scars remember when everything else lies," she said. [407] "A scar is a place where time already closed over itself." [418] "Link them, and you can walk the seam." [426]

So we did it: her bark knot against my burn, the clipping's healed wound pressed between. [442] Linking through scars is not gentle. [448] I felt the smith's shoulder where the anvil kicked, the baker's split thumb, the white line under Mrs. Ateban's jaw. [468] Every old hurt in Vell was a door that had already learned how to shut, and through them I saw the maze from above, dreams pouring into it like floodwater. [498] There, near the eastern hedge, was a dead end where nothing grew, because everything crossed. [513]

"Heed me," Maren said, and her voice came out of my own scar. [526] "Plant the clipping at the crossing before full dark, or the town wakes into a Thursday it can never leave." [546]

I ran. [548] The maze let me through, having already dreamed me running, and dreams are hospitable to what they expect. [566] At the dead end I knelt and pushed the clipping scarred end down into the soil, and the fear in me went bright and giddy, a held breath deciding to become a laugh. [599]

Above the hedges, the shadow that had lain across the covenant for sixty years slid aside. [615] Thursday's rain lifted back up into Thursday. [622]

When I came back to the cobalt glade, the last blue was going out of the moss, and Maren's roots had loosened by an inch, the first inch in sixty years. [653] She was weeping about it, which I took for agreement. [663] "The maze holds them now," she said. [670] "It learned the knot from the clipping." [677] I asked what she meant to do with feet. [686] She said she was purposefully curious about the sea, and intended to be standing there when it arrived, early or late. [707]

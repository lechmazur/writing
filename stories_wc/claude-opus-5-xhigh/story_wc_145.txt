The falcon came back at dusk with ice in its feathers, and Mirren Vask, who had spent nineteen years correcting other people's continents, decided that the silence outside was the most alarming thing she had ever heard. [37] Outpost Nine was a box of rusted plate bolted to a shelf of granite above a frozen lake, and it groaned in weather the way old men groan in chairs. [67] Tonight it did not groan. [72] No wind, no cracking ice, no wolves: only a tranquility so complete that Mirren kept glancing at the barometer to see whether the world had quietly stopped. [99] She unfastened the message tube from the bird's leg. [108] Inside there was no letter from the Bureau, only a strip of birchbark scratched with three wavering lines that ran together and became one. [132] Three rivers, one head. [136] "Wonderful," she told the falcon, "a rumor with wings." [145] She had come north on her own money and her own stubbornness, to find where the rivers begin. [163] Every atlas she had ever edited showed the Orsk, the Vela, and the Little Chaide rising from three separate ranges, and every atlas had been copied from a survey done in a warm office by a man who hated boats. [203] Mirren was subtly confident that he had been wrong; she did not say so at meetings, she simply left the margins wide. [225] On the shelf above the stove lay a polyglot's phrasebook belonging to Adeyemi, the surveyor who spoke nine languages, wintered here before her, and did not come out. [253] It had exactly one dog-ear, pressed so hard the fold had cracked, marking a page of river words in the language of the lake people. [278] Underlined twice: khaldan-uy, glossed as "the one mouth that drinks upward." [289] Mirren had assumed it was poetry until she noticed what the window did every night. [304] Adeyemi had scratched something into the glass with a nail, fine as a hair and invisible in daylight, so that only the pattern of condensation on glass could reveal it. [334] When the stove warmed and Mirren breathed against the pane, the fog gathered everywhere except in the scratches, and a map stood clear in the blur: the lake, the three notches in its rim, and beneath them a single spring. [374] She had copied it eleven times, and eleven times the geometry held: a singular convergence, one aquifer feeding three watersheds through the floor of the lake itself. [401] The birchbark from the falcon's tube was confirmation from the herders on the far shore, who had known it for six generations and had never been asked. [428] Which left the silence. [432] Mirren went out onto the shelf of granite with the lamp. [443] The lake lay flat and gray and utterly still, and water was standing on top of the ice in January, in windless air, at nineteen degrees below zero. [471] The spring was rising. [475] A warm aquifer, pressurized, one mouth drinking upward, and above it a frozen lid that had held since the last time anyone bothered to name it. [501] The quiet was not peace; it was pressure, and Mirren stood in it and understood exactly what Adeyemi had understood, probably around this hour, probably with the same lamp. [530] When the lid failed, three rivers would leave at once, and the villages downstream on all three of them had been built by people trusting an atlas that showed three separate springs, three separate floods, three separate small disasters that could not possibly arrive on the same afternoon. [578] She went back inside and wrote for four hours. [587] Not a correction; corrections took eleven months and a committee. [597] A warning, addressed to every district engineer whose name she could remember, in the plainest sentences she had ever permitted herself, because she was done being subtle in margins. [626] At the end she copied out the dog-eared page, all of it, so that whoever read it would have the words to ask the herders the right questions. [654] Then she fed the falcon, tied the packet to its leg, and sent it south to herald a flood that had not happened yet. [678] "You're a poor herald," she told it, "but you're the only one at this address." [693] The bird went out over the flat white lake and Mirren watched until the lamp fog closed the window, the condensation rising again over Adeyemi's scratches, the one spring blooming quietly in the glass. [727] She put the kettle on, and in the terrible stillness she began the new map, drawing the three rivers backward toward the place they had always agreed upon. [755]

Not since the argument that split their research in two -- Maren chasing phonology eastward, Elara diving into grammar westward, both claiming the other had betrayed their shared work. [36]

Trust. [115]

She needed proof. [138]

The extinct language archive occupied the lower levels of the old conservatory, where acoustic design met architectural reverence. [156]

She sat at the central workstation now, a careful reckless energy driving her hands. [241]

She found it at three in the morning. [448]

The symphony wasn't about the Tessari at all. [492]

And Elara had been translating it to send to Maren. [527]
Not as academic work. [531]
As a letter. [534]

The wind turbines cut slow circles against the grey sky, casting rotating shadows across the Verenna farmhouse. [19] After the happiness mandate—three years of state-enforced optimism, of required smiles documented at weekly neighborhood inspections—a profound silence had settled over the farmland. [41] Not the natural quiet of rural places, but something hollowed out. [53]

Performers had been essential workers during the mandate. [60] Theater could proceed, provided each production concluded with evidence of contentment. [71] He had been good at it: assuming the blank-faced neutrality between acts that let audiences project whatever satisfaction inspectors demanded. [91] His face had grown accustomed to revealing nothing. [99]

The dried flower bouquet sat in a dingy vase on the kitchen table. [112] State-issued marigolds and mandatory lavender from the Festival of Permitted Joy, three years prior. [126] He had kept them as he kept everything: without apparent reason. [138] Now they were crumbling. [142] Petals dropped like brittle skin onto wood already scarred by his blade. [155]

He sat at the table whittling. [161] Pine block. [163] Purposeless shape forming. [166] The knife drew long curls that fell onto his lap. [177]

"The craftsman positions his blade at the grain's angle—yes, precisely there—where the wood must part," he said to the empty kitchen. [203] "He draws the steel forward. [208] The wood resists. [211] Then it yields, as all things eventually yield. [220] See how the shaving spirals away—death rendered beautiful, or beauty rendered trivial. [234] The observer cannot tell." [239] His voice was deep, carefully modulated, theatrical—a performer's habit. [248] "He repeats the motion. [252] He will repeat it many times. [259] The act requires neither thought nor feeling. [267] This is its virtue." [271]

Outside, the turbine blades turned. [277] Always the turbines. [280] The government had installed them during the mandate's second year—green energy for green moods, they had said. [303] The turbines kept turning after repeal. [309] They would keep turning after the last performer had been forgotten. [319] Some found the sound oppressive. [324] He found it useful. [328]

He was whittling a small bird. [335] Or perhaps a fish. [339] The shape remained indeterminate. [343] He had whittled many such shapes since repeal. [352] They occupied windowsills and shelves throughout the farmhouse, mute company for a man with blank features. [368]

"He considers the beak, or perhaps the mouth," he continued. [378] "The blade makes a hollow where once there was solid. [388] A subtraction. [390] The bird does not exist until the excess is removed. [401] The bird has always existed. [406] Both statements are true. [410] The craftsman does not decide. [415] He merely cuts." [418]

The flowers dropped another petal. [424] He observed its fall without reaction. [431]

During the mandate, he had reported neighbors who displayed insufficient joy. [444] Two families removed. [447] He had performed this duty without satisfaction. [455] He had performed it without guilt. [462] The mandate required community enforcement. [468] He had been efficient. [472] This efficiency had permitted him to continue working, had preserved the farmhouse, had preserved the kitchen where he now whittled indeterminate shapes beside crumbling state flowers. [499]

"He sets down the blade," he narrated. [506] "The bird is complete. [511] Or incomplete. [513] The distinction is academic." [518] He placed it on the shelf beside others. [527] "He does not count them. [533] They are evidence of time passed." [540]

His motive was not happiness—that concept had been corrupted beyond recovery. [553] But lightness. [555] He wanted to feel less weight, some fractional reduction in the gravity that pressed him into his chair each morning. [577] The bird on the shelf did not make him lighter. [587] The narration did not make him lighter. [595] But the practice continued. [599] Practice was its own justification, as performance had been its own justification, as reporting neighbors had been its own justification. [620]

The mandate had ended. [624] The weight had not. [628]

The remade hush pressed against the farmhouse walls. [637] Not silence but post-noise—the sound of enforced laughter still ringing in rooms now empty, the hum of turbines built for purposes no one quite remembered. [664]

"He rises," the performer said. [670] "He crosses to the window. [676] The turbines continue. [679] The sky is grey. [683] The grey is whole." [687]

His face showed nothing. [692] His voice showed nothing. [697] But beneath the blankness, behind the performance, some small mechanism persisted—turning like a blade, cutting like a knife. [721]

Not peace. [723] Not redemption. [725] Merely continuation. [727]

The flowers dropped another petal. [734]

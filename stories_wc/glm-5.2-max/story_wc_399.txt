[109] The order had diminished with the drying of the southern wells and the retreat of the caravans, but Tellen remained because he was strangely honest, and honesty, it turned out, was the only qualification the line demanded. [146] You could not lie to a boundary. [153] It would not hold. [157]

Tellen had walked its length only twice before. [272] Both times, he had nearly lost himself in its motion. [282] He reached the entrance, a gap in the sand that breathed cool air. [295]

[520] They were openings in the density of reality, places where the usual rules softened and something could be set down gently, without the violence of translation. [707] The prophet had gone, leaving only the echo, but the echo would endure, held in the kinetic heart beneath the dunes, preserved by a line walker who was strangely honest and therefore still walking.

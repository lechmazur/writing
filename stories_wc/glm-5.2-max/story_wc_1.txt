Maren hadn't expected to wake again. [6]

She'd shown him. [86]

The records of materials she'd acquired quietly from people who asked no questions. [110]
And he'd pushed her. [114]

By dawn she could sit. [169]
Every breath tested what was still holding. [176]
The quiet canyon under stars had softened to gray, and she could faintly hear water moving through rock somewhere far below. [197]

She could build again. [258]

A charcoal smuggler's ledger strip, torn from a larger book. [491]

She'd been evidence. [606]

The canyon was quiet now under thinning stars, and she stood longer than she needed to, watching the frost begin to vanish. [648]
There was no need to hurry. [654]
She would retrace the supply roads, find the others named in the ledger, and learn what role she still played. [674]

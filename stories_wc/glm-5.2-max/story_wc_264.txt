[19] In the freight lines that crossed the ash plains, this made him valuable, a guard who couldn't be seduced by hallucination or haunted by memory. [43] He walked the caravan's perimeter with his lantern low, watching for things that preyed on sleeping minds. [88] Timelines where they never existed at all. [95] Each branch catalogued, compressed, stored in crystalline wafers no larger than a thumbnail. [108] The archivists said the archive was occasionally everywhere, that it bled through probability, surfacing in multiple locations at once, impossible to contain completely. [131] They'd wrapped it in lead and paradox, but still it hummed. [142] Vesh's only companion was a chess clock frozen at midnight. [152] His mother had given it to him before the fires took her, told him that if he ever found himself at a crossing, he should wind it and listen. [301] Then visible again, taller. [305] Then gone. [307] The divergent archive was pushing through, trying to manifest one of its stored realities in this one. [324] But it couldn't anchor.

[43] She could still perform every kata, still demonstrate the blade work that had made her legendary. [59] But the fire was gone. [64]

[79] One was Dr. Aiko Tanase, the neuroscientist who had grown Maren's clone from tissue samples taken years ago. [97] The other was Jiro, her former husband, who claimed the clone--now a seven-year-old girl named Suki--belonged with him. [117]

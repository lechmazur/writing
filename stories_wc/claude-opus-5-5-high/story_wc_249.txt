They called me Subject Fourteen, I called myself the guinea pig, and for eleven weeks I had been the healthy half of an experiment. [23] The Lethe Bridge trial paired a failing mind with a steady one, braiding their memories so the weaker could lean on the stronger. [46] Tomas was the weaker, though he would have laughed at the word. [58] Now I remembered his mother's kitchen, the burnt-sugar smell of it, and he remembered my father teaching me to swim. [78] The doctors called this entanglement and wrote it down as a success. [90] They did not know about the lagoon. [97] Neither of us had ever seen it, yet both of us remembered it: an auric lagoon under starlight, water the color of warm honey, stars so thick they seemed to drip. [128] It was not his memory or mine; it was ours, grown in the seam between us like moss in a crack. [149] We had started planning to find it, as if it were a real place with a real road. [167] Then his scans worsened, and the committee decided to withdraw him at dawn. [180] Severing the bridge, Dr. Anand explained, would let his memories fade gently, and mine would return to being only mine. [200] The lagoon would belong to no one. [207]

I have never been loud about disobedience. [214] I sign the forms, I swallow the tablets, and then I do exactly what I intended. [230] So at the hush of the universe inhaling, that strange hour before dawn when even the ventilation stops and the whole world seems to draw one long breath, I slipped out to the sound wave garden. [266] The clinic had built it for therapy: brass reeds, hollow stones, a shallow reflecting pool rimmed with sensors that turned every vibration into colored light. [291] Patients were meant to hum softly there, and it was locked after midnight to protect the calibration. [308] I had learned the code weeks ago by listening, because I drum on everything, and a drummer hears which buttons a tired hand presses. [332] Tray rims, bed rails, my own knees during the scans: the staff had long ago stopped asking me to be still. [353]

Tomas's window faced the garden from the second floor. [362] I had tapped a message on our shared wall at midnight, the rhythm his mother used to knock on the kitchen table when supper was ready, the rhythm I only knew because of him. [396] His blind lifted now, a pale shape behind the glass. [406]

I climbed the low wall and perched on the lip of the great listening dish at the garden's center, knees drawn up, like a bird that has no business being awake. [437] Then I began. [440] I drummed the brass reeds with my knuckles, the hollow stones with my palms, the dish itself with my heels. [460] I played the swim lesson, my father's slow count of one, two, breathe. [473] I played the kitchen knock. [478] I played every memory we had tangled together, hoping the garden could translate what the doctors could not. [496]

The pool answered. [499] Light rose from the sensors in ribbons, green and violet at first, trembling like an aurora over a frozen sea. [519] Then, as my rhythm found the shape of the dream, the color deepened into gold. [534] The shallow water glowed like honey, and the real stars overhead, sharp in the stillness, lay reflected across it until the pool became our lagoon. [559] It was small, and it was only light, but it was there. [571]

Behind his window, Tomas pressed his palm to the glass. [581] Then, slowly, he began to tap back. [588] I could not hear it across the courtyard, but the listening dish could, and a second pulse of gold spread through the water, answering mine. [613] It was the kitchen knock, perfect, from a man who that week had forgotten his own middle name. [631]

We played together until the ventilation shuddered back on and the universe exhaled and the hush was over. [649] The noise of the waking building smeared the gold into gray. [660]

Dr. Anand found me still perched on the dish, hands raw. [671] She did not scold me; she had seen the recordings. [681]

They withdrew Tomas at dawn anyway. [687] Within a month the doctors said he would not remember the lagoon, or me. [701] But the bridge had been cut clumsily, the way all bridges are, and some of him stayed on my side. [721] I still smell burnt sugar sometimes. [727] Dr. Anand leaves the garden unlocked now. [734] And every night, at the hush, I go down to the garden and drum on everything until the water turns gold, keeping our dream breathing for the both of us. [764]

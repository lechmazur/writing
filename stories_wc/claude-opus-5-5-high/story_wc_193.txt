Since the Thickening, when ideas began to set like plaster, Nell Adair carried forty-one promises in her coat. [18] They had hardened into gray pebbles the night Lumen Circle collapsed, one for every friend she had recruited with the words, you cannot lose. [42] Each pebble weighed exactly what its owner had lost. [51] Her aunt's savings sat heaviest, near the heart. [59]

Now she worked nights at Deepcap, a mushroom farm grown inside a decommissioned submarine that lay tilted at the bottom of a flooded quarry. [83] Foxfire caps lined the bunks where sailors once slept, and ghost-lanterns dripped blue light from the torpedo racks. [101] Between the glow ran seams of shadow so dark they seemed cut rather than cast. [116] The mushrooms loved it down there, because pressure had become a physical thing too, a slow gray fog that pooled in low places and fed the mycelium. [143] Nell had read the old saying that pressure makes diamonds, and in this new world she suspected it might finally be literally true. [166] She wanted to test it on the right kind of pressure. [177]

The texts started the week Dale Pruitt called to arrange a tour. [189] Dale had founded Lumen Circle, and because he never believed his own promises, they had not hardened in him. [208] Instead they trailed him as pressure, a thick gray weather that leaned on everyone he spoke to, and he now wanted to buy Deepcap and sell glowing mushrooms as a miracle supplement, recruit-your-friends included. [242]

The first message arrived from a number made entirely of zeros. [253] dont let him in, it read, then dissolved into static characters before reassembling: or do, we cant agree. [271] More came through that night, each signed with a slightly different version of her name. [286] They were signed Nel, Nella, and once Nellie, a version who had apparently never quit the scheme. [303]

Pieced together, the glitching messages described one plan from every angle. [314] Take him to the forward compartment, where the pressure pools deepest. [325] Loosen the porthole bolt, the old brass one, and let the quarry lean in. [339] Then make the promise, and hold it. [346]

its quantum, wrote Nella. [350] u promise in all of us at once, and it only becomes true in whoever volunteers. [366]

Nel added, in the worlds where you pull the bolt all the way out, the farm floods. [383] in the worlds where you dont touch it, nothing changes, which is worse. [396]

When Dale arrived, smiling inside his private fog, Nell volunteered to lead the tour herself. [411] She showed him the lantern caps, the glowing shelf fungus in the galley, the spore clouds drifting like slow galaxies. [431] She let him talk about margins and tiers, and she did not correct his arithmetic, which had always been the trick. [452]

In the forward compartment she told him the best specimens grew behind the porthole, and he leaned close to look. [472] Nell slid the hatch shut behind them and set her wrench on the bolt. [486] It was a small, stupid thing, green with age, and it was the only thing between them and a hundred feet of water. [509]

She turned it once. [513]

The quarry pressed in through the gap, not as water but as weight, and it found Dale's fog and squeezed. [533] He dropped to his knees, gasping as every promise he had ever tossed away came back to him compressed. [552] Her phone buzzed against her hip, a dozen worlds shouting at once. [564] now, now, NOW, say it. [569]

Nell took the gray pebbles from her coat and held them against the bolt, the metal humming under her palm. [589] "I promise every one of them gets back what I cost them," she said. [603] "I promise I will not lie to anyone again, starting now: Dale, this is going to hurt." [620]

Across every dimension the promise wavered, kept and broken, and then it chose her, because she was the one standing in the dark with her hand on the bolt. [649]

She turned the bolt back tight. [655] The pressure had nowhere left to go, so it did what pressure always wanted to do. [671]

When the glow steadied, forty-one diamonds lay on the deck where the pebbles had been, each one clear enough to hold a little blue mushroom light inside it. [699] Dale sat against the bulkhead, fogless for the first time in his life, crying like a man seeing the faces of everyone he had talked over. [725] Nell pocketed the diamonds, and also the tour waiver he had signed without reading, which pledged his buyout money to Lumen Circle's victims. [748]

Her phone buzzed once more. [753] nice, it read, signed by all of them. [761]

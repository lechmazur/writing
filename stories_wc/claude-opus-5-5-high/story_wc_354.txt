Tomas Aurel had been a chess tutor to princes until he told the truth about a game played three hundred years ago. [22] Now he lived in a stone hut at the Lodestar Flats, where every compass spun like a drunk and iron filings stood upright in the sand at twilight. [50] The valley and the ridge had signed their covenant here, over a chessboard, on the afternoon the sun went out. [70] The eclipse had swallowed the final moves, and when light returned each side swore the other had cheated in the dark. [91] The covenant to share the pass had lived in that shadow ever since, honored grudgingly, and now it was ending. [111] Both councils would meet at the new moon, before the next phase, and decide on war. [127]

Tomas had said, before the valley court, that the recorded position proved nothing, and for that he had been sent to the Flats with a board, a lamp, and his worry stone. [159] It was polished basalt, black as a rook, with a shallow dent his thumb had worn over twenty years of waiting for students to find the harder move. [187] He had always believed the difficult line was where the beauty hid. [199]

The girl came on the ninth evening. [206] She was ridge-born, perhaps twelve, with a slate tablet on a cord and no voice to speak of. [224] She wrote a single word: Neve. [230] Then she sat at his board and set up the covenant position from memory, square for square. [247]

Tomas felt his heart turn over like a coin. [256] The ridge kept its records differently, the old books said; ridge scribes did not write moves, they held them. [275] Each move was a silence of a certain length, counted in heartbeats, passed from mouth-shut elder to mouth-shut child. [294] Neve had not come to play; she had come to be read. [306]

So he listened. [309] She moved a pawn, then sat utterly still while the filings bristled around the hut and the western sky went the color of a bruise. [334] He counted her pauses against his own pulse and wrote them in the margin of the valley record, column beside column. [355] Translating silence was slow work, and he did it the way he had once taught princes to calculate: thoughtfully precise, never guessing, checking each line twice. [381]

For six nights they worked. [386] For six nights the numbers refused to correlate. [394] The valley record and the ridge silences agreed on every move until the forty-first, where the valley had its knight on one square and the ridge insisted, with a pause of nineteen heartbeats, on the square beside it. [432] Each version was a win for the side that kept it. [443]

On the seventh evening Tomas set his worry stone on the board to mark the disputed knight, and watched it slide. [464] It crept one square east, slow as a snail, as the twilight deepened and the ground's strange pull came up through the table. [487]

Neve stared at it, then at him. [494]

The covenant pieces had been carved from this same black rock, quarried from the Flats themselves. [510] In the dark of the eclipse, when the Flats pulled hardest, no hand had touched that knight. [527] Both scribes had recorded honestly, one before it slid and one after. [539]

Tomas played on from the true position, and found what he had always suspected: neither side could win. [557] It was a fortress draw, the hardest kind, each king guarding the other's door forever. [572] It was the most beautiful thing he had ever seen on a board. [585]

He wrote it out twice, once in valley notation and once as a list of silences, and bound both with twine. [606] The new moon was three days off. [613] Neve could reach the ridge council in two. [621] He would send the valley copy by the supply cart, knowing the court might burn it unread, knowing he would likely die out here among the singing filings either way. [651]

At the door she hesitated, and he understood that silence too. [662] He pressed the basalt stone into her palm and set her thumb in its dent. [677] "When they argue," he said, "hold it on the table and let them watch it move." [693]

She wrote on her slate, and turned it toward him in the last violet light. [708]

Draw is not losing. [712]

Then she was gone up the dark path, and Tomas sat alone before the empty board, his thumb searching for a dent that was no longer there, and smiled. [741]

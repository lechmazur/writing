Sir Oswin had not spoken aloud in nine years, and the orchard did not ask him to. [17] It lay under a dome of green glass at the edge of the drowned kingdom, and over it the night had stood for as long as he had been silent. [47] The queen's alchemists had planted it before they fled, and their ledgers still hung from the trellis posts, recording in careful columns what everyone knew and no one said: that in this place, miracles were a matter of fact. [86] Quicksilver pears ripened at a measured rate of one per fortnight. [97] Salt apples wept exactly four tears when cut. [105] Each fruit held a small, true picture of a world that might have been, and the ledgers listed them like harvest weights. [127]

Oswin tended the trees in full armor, because the armor was the last thing the queen had given him, and because inside it he did not have to be anyone. [157]

Tonight the panes were changing. [162] Above him the constellations slid like beads on a wire; the Plough became a Heron, the Heron became a door, and somewhere beyond the glass the world was deciding again what shape to keep. [196] The ledgers called this a Settling and gave it a column of its own. [210] When it finished, whatever the fruit showed at that moment would be what was. [224]

He found the program in the hollow of the oldest pear tree, where no program should have been. [242] It was a folded card, water-stained, from the little marionette theater the queen had kept as a girl, long before crowns and floods. [265] He remembered the theater: a painted box in the nursery, abandoned when she was taught that queens did not play. [285] The program announced a single performance, never staged: The Knight Who Chose His Own Strings. [300] Beneath the title, in her childish hand, were diagrams of the stringing knots her puppet-master had taught her, loops and crossings no one had tied in fifty years. [328]

Oswin looked from the card to the trellis wires and felt the cold climb his spine. [344] The alchemists had strung the orchard in the same forgotten patterns. [355]

He began to walk them. [360] Under the rolling stars he followed each loop down its row and each crossing between trunks, touching the wires as a puppeteer would, a pluck here, a slack there. [389] The trees answered. [392] Branches lifted like wooden arms. [397] Pears turned on their stems to face him, and in their silver skins he saw the kingdom dry, the kingdom burning, the kingdom with a sun, the queen at a window waiting for a knight who never came. [435] He wanted to hold every one of them. [443] He had withdrawn from the world because he could not bear that it had chosen only one shape, and now, as it wavered, he meant to catch the shape of possibility itself before the Settling closed its hand. [481]

The last knot on the program led to the center of the orchard, to a sapling he had never planted. [501] It bore no fruit. [505] Its single wire ran up from the soil and ended in a small brass hook, the kind that held a marionette's shoulders. [527]

Oswin understood then what the girl had written. [535] She had not meant the knight to be moved. [544] She had meant him to choose. [550]

His fingers shook as he reached for the clasps of his armor. [562] He unsnapped the gorget, then the pauldrons, then the breastplate she had fastened on him herself the morning of the flood, and each piece fell into the loam with a sound like a door unlatching. [597] The night air found his skin through the thin shirt, and he shivered, and the shivering was the sweetest thing he had felt in nine years. [623]

He took the brass hook in his bare hand and did not fasten it to himself. [639] Instead he tied the program's last knot around it, pulled the wire taut, and set the sapling's string trembling like a held breath. [662] Every tree in the orchard hummed along its line. [671]

Then, for the first time since the waters came, Sir Oswin spoke, so quietly the glass barely caught it. [690]

"I am sworn to what might be." [697]

The murmured allegiance ran down the wire into the roots, and the sapling budded, and in its first blossom there was no single picture but a shimmer of all of them at once, open, unfinished, his. [733]

Overhead the stars stopped sliding. [738] The Settling had come, and the world had kept the shape he held: not a kingdom, not a queen, but a door left ajar. [762] In the ledger by the gate, a new line wrote itself in careful ink: Possibility, one blossom, confirmed. [780]

Oswin stood in the endless night, shivering and blissful, and walked toward the door. [794]

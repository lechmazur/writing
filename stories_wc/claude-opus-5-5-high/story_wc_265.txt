Ossian had been sleeping in doorways for eleven years, and for eleven years he had woken somewhere else. [18] It began the winter the sixth sense awakened, when every person on the continent learned to hear the hum of insight, that soft electric note a true idea makes as it arrives. [50] Within a year the Academy had licensed the hum, charting which thoughts rang at which pitch, and within two it had expelled him for thinking in keys it could not file. [81] Now he was a stray philosopher, and strays go where they are carried. [94]

Tonight he was carried into rain that fell upward. [103] He woke on a bed of copper moss, beneath trunks of riveted iron whose bark had been hammered into scales, and above him the canopy turned slowly on hidden axles, leaves of green enamel clicking as they caught the drizzle pumped up from the roots. [148] The mechanical rainforest was a rumor older than the Awakening, a garden the old engineers had built to remember what wilderness sounded like. [171]

In his fist, as always, was a small gear. [180] He had forty-one of them now, sewn into the lining of his coat, one for every place his sleeping body had fled to, and none of them had ever meshed with another. [212] This one was brass, no larger than a thumbnail, and warm. [223]

Then the wind came. [227] It moved through the iron trunks with a low cello groan, through a stand of hollow steel bamboo with a flutter of flutes, through the silver ferns with a hiss like breath drawn between teeth. [262] Each tree made a different sound, and as each sound passed over him, the gear in his palm shivered and turned a single tooth. [286]

Ossian sat very still. [290] In the cities there was only one wind, filtered through identical boulevard lindens, and the hum of insight rang at the pitch the Academy approved. [315] People had the same good ideas on the same afternoons. [325] It was efficient, and it had made the mind a tamed and fenced country, every hill surveyed. [342] He had not heard a thought arrive unannounced since childhood, and he wanted, more than bread, to stand again at the wild frontier of his own head and not know what lay past it. [376]

He took out the other forty and laid them on the moss. [388] When the bamboo sang, three of them trembled; when the iron groaned, seven; when the ferns hissed, a scattered dozen. [408] They were not random souvenirs. [413] They were tuned, each cut to answer one kind of wind, and his sleeping body had been gathering them the way a bird gathers string. [438]

He sorted them by song until his fingers ached, and the pieces began, reluctantly and then eagerly, to fit. [457] What he assembled by dawn was a small clockwork ear, its spindle wound so tight with fine copper thread that it could hear only one note. [483]

He knew that note. [487] It was the Academy's pitch, the one to which every mind had been set after the Awakening. [504] He understood, with a feeling that was not yet a hum, that he had built a model of his own listening, and that the thread was the Academy's tuning, wound around him so long he had mistaken it for bone. [544] Some part of him had known for eleven years, and had walked him across the world at night to collect the evidence. [566]

So he began to unravel it. [572]

The thread came loose one turn at a time, and with each turn the clockwork ear caught another voice of the forest: first the bamboo, then the iron, then the ferns, then trees he had not noticed, glass palms that chimed, a lone tin cedar that whistled almost out of hearing. [623] His own ears followed, as if they had been waiting for permission. [635] When the last strand fell into the moss, the wind moved through every tree at once, and inside his skull something hummed at a pitch no chart had ever named. [665]

He did not know what the thought was yet. [674] That was the miracle, and in this forest miracles were only probable, like rain, like the next gust. [692]

Ossian lay back on the copper moss, the loose thread tied around his wrist as a keepsake, and let himself drift toward sleep, curious for the first time in eleven years where it would carry him, and certain that wherever he woke, he would be listening. [738]

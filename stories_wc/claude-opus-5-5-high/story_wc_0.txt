[34] She had spent eleven years timing a pulsar in Vela, a collapsed star twelve miles wide that turned eleven times a second, and her job was mostly subtraction. [62] Remove the atmosphere. [84] What remained was the slow, true slowing, a star shedding its spin so gently that it took years to see.

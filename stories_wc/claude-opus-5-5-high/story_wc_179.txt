In the Sanctuary of Hush, sound died a finger's width from its source, and the walls moved whenever no one was looking. [22] Outside, the war had torn the world loose from its moorings, and reality shifted like a badly tied load on a cart; inside, the monks simply let the corridors rearrange themselves each dawn. [55] Sister Oda had lived there eleven years, and in that time she had never raised a hand against anyone, though she had arranged a great many things behind people's backs. [85]

Teodor arrived in the ninth month with mud to his knees and a knife he did not bother to hide. [105] He wrote his purpose on the slate at the gate, because speech was useless there: I am looking for the soldier who threw down her rifle at Varn and let my village burn. [138] Oda read it, wiped it clean, and led him inside. [148] She did not tell him that the soldier he wanted was walking beside him. [162]

Instead she schemed, gently, the way water schemes against stone. [172] Each morning she chalked him a map, and each morning the corridors shifted, and he found himself not at the cells of the refugees but in the orchard, the laundry, the bell tower with its useless bells. [209] Whenever he despaired, Oda reappeared at his elbow with bread or a blanket, as though the sanctuary itself had sent her. [230] The monks had a word for this, signed with two fingers sliding across the palm: shifting grace, the mercy that moves toward whoever needs it most and never stays put. [260] Oda knew it was less mysterious than that; she simply always knew where he would be lost. [277]

On the twentieth day he opened his pack and showed her a small brass music box. [293] It had belonged to his daughter, Lise. [300] One pin on the cylinder was snapped off, so the lullaby always stumbled on the same beat, and Lise had sung that missing note herself every night, loudly, to cover the gap. [332] He wound it and it turned in perfect silence, the comb lifting and falling over nothing. [348] His face broke. [351]

Oda took his hand and laid it on the lid, and then she laid her own hand over his and waited. [372] This was the oldest teaching of the Hush: where the ear fails, the body remembers. [387] The muscles keep what the mind refuses, grief and songs alike, down in the cells where no argument can reach. [407] She could not convince him with words; she had tried words once, at Varn, shouting at her own captain to stop, and the fire had eaten them. [434] So she let the vibration of the turning cylinder pass through his palm, and she felt his fingers begin, without permission, to tap. [457]

Tap, tap, pause. [460] At the broken place his index finger struck the lid hard, the way Lise's voice had struck the air. [479] His whole arm knew it. [484] It had rocked her to sleep a thousand times, and it had carried her body out of the smoke, and it had held the knife every day since. [512] Now it could not decide which memory to obey. [521]

Oda took the slate and wrote, very slowly, I was the soldier. [533] Then she wrote the rest: that she had thrown down the rifle because she would not fire on farmers, that she had run toward the houses, not away, that she had failed anyway. [566] She did not ask forgiveness. [571] Forgiveness was his to keep or spend, and she had not led him through twenty days of wrong corridors to collect it. [593] She had done it so that when he finally found her, his hand would already know another shape than a fist. [614] Resentment, she believed, was only grief that had forgotten how to let go, and letting go was a thing the body could be taught, like a lullaby or a trade. [644]

Teodor's knife hand shook. [648] Beneath them the floor tilted as the sanctuary rearranged itself again, and somewhere outside a hillside became a river, and the world went on shifting its bones. [675]

He wound the box once more. [681] This time, when the cylinder reached the broken pin, he did not strike the lid. [696] He opened his mouth, and in the one place in the world where no sound could live, Oda felt the missing note hum through the bones of his wrist and into hers. [728]

When the song ended, the knife lay on the floor between them, and he let it stay there. [746] Grace shifted, as it does, and for a moment it rested on them both. [760]

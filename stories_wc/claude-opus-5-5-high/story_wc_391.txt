[128] The Prior let her keep him because horizon mappers were rare, and because her charts told the Prior's riders where the passes stayed open. [152] She had spent eleven years drawing the edge of the world for men she despised, and she had spent every one of those years watching. [177]

"And then we have a road no lantern needs to light." [261]

[292] The Prior's riders crossed ice by compass, since snow erased every landmark. [458] Each night the silver road crept north across the ice, a hand's width, then two.

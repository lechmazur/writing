The library had no door, only a hush so large it rang, and Mira Olend stepped into it the instant the universe hiccupped. [23] Outside, the stars had stopped mid-flicker. [29] A cup she had dropped in her kitchen hung above the tiles like a held breath. [45] She alone had noticed, because she alone had been watching when history ended. [58] It had not ended in fire. [64] It had ended in disagreement. [69] At the catch of the hiccup, every event that had ever happened split into two records, and each record swore the other was an illusion. [94] In one, the stars were furnaces a trillion miles off; in the other, they were painted on a dome a mile above the tallest mountain, and the dome was cracking. [124] Until the records agreed, the universe could not finish its hiccup and breathe again. [138]

The keeper found her among the shelves. [145] He was thin as a bookmark and wore robes the color of old glue. [159] "This temple sits in the gap between one moment and the next," he said, and his voice fell into the silence without disturbing it. [183] "Every time the cosmos stutters, someone who saw it arrives here to arbitrate." [196]

"I came to find out which sky is the lie," Mira said. [208] She had been an astronomer, and had spent her life suspecting the heavens of trickery; now the heavens had confessed to being two things at once. [234]

The keeper gestured at the books. [240] Every volume on the shelves was bound in stone-hard leather, and none would open; their spines had frozen with the moment. [261] "Rigid things cannot move here," he said. [268] "What did you bring that bends?" [274]

Mira pulled a softcover dictionary from her coat pocket, its cover creased white, its pages swollen from rain and years of thumbing. [296] Her father had given it to her the night he taught her the names of constellations, and she had used it ever since to check that words still meant what they claimed. [328] Its pages riffled open easily in her hand. [336]

"Good," said the keeper. [339] "Then you will walk the spiral." [345]

At the heart of the library, the floor was inlaid with a single path of brass that coiled outward from a center stone to the lost edge of the temple. [375] Spiral arbitration, the keeper explained, did not choose a winner. [385] The arbiter stood at the center holding both claims, then walked outward, defining each word of the dispute at every turn, until the claims no longer had anything left to argue about. [417] "The path goes out," he said, "but you will not." [427]

She understood him on the first turn. [434] She opened the dictionary to star and read: a luminous point in the night sky. [449] As her feet carried her away from the center, her mind sank backward, into the attic window, her father's finger tracing Orion, his warm certainty that light traveled a long way to reach them. [483] The definition did not say furnace, and it did not say paint. [495]

Second turn: sky. [498] The dictionary offered the apparent arch over the earth, and Mira laughed quietly, because apparent was the only honest word anyone had ever used about it. [524] Her path wandered, doubling past shelves she had already seen, yet every footfall landed precisely on brass; the spiral was wanderingly exact, and so, she realized, was her memory. [553] Each turn outward returned her deeper within: to the observatory, to the night she noticed the stars flicker in unison, to the instant she saw history come apart like a wet page. [585]

Illusion, she read at last, near the rim: something that deceives by producing a false impression. [601]

She stopped. [603]

The furnace sky and the painted sky were not lying about the stars; they were each lying about being alone. [623] Her father had looked up and seen fire; her grandmother had looked up and seen the ceiling of heaven; both had seen light, and both had loved it. [651] The only false impression was the line drawn between them. [661]

Mira took the pencil stub from her pocket and, in the margin beside star, wrote a new definition: a light that is true however it is seen. [688] The two records, hearing it, had nothing left to argue about. [699] They did not collide; they unified, the way two eyes make one sight, and the hush of the library deepened until it rang like a struck bell. [726]

Behind her, the keeper bowed. [731] Ahead, the spiral ended at a door that had not existed a moment earlier. [745]

Mira stepped through, and in her kitchen the cup finished falling, and shattered, and the universe, at last, breathed out. [765] Above the house the stars resumed their flicker, one sky again, and she pressed the bent dictionary to her chest like an oath. [788]

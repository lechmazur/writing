The Regent's projection booth smelled of hot dust and forty years of Teodor's coffee. [14] Iva Moraine climbed the last iron stair with her crystal wrapped in velvet, though she had not needed the glass in years. [36] People on the boardwalk paid to watch her gaze into it, so she gazed; the truth came through her palms. [56] Outside, a storm sat over the harbor and grumbled without committing. [67]

"You hear that?" Teodor said, meaning not the thunder but himself. [78] He was humming, had been humming all week, a slow tune with a hole in it where the words should go. [99] "Lenka sang it in the ticket cage," he said. [108] "Thirty-one years and I can't find one line." [116]

He held out the film strip. [122] Sixteen frames, spliced into the leader of a reel nobody had run since the theater's last full house. [140] A woman at a desk, pen moving, her face turned toward whoever held the camera. [155] "I found it Tuesday, cleaning out for the wreckers," Teodor said. [166] "She was writing something." [170] "She never gave me anything to read." [177]

Iva took the strip. [181] Celluloid is a greedy material; it keeps what touches it. [191] The reading came the way it always did, not as pictures but as architecture. [205] She had never been able to see a face in a vision, but she could walk a room she had never entered and know where the floor sagged. [233] This room she was standing in. [239] The desk in the frame was the splicing bench under the porthole. [251] The camera had been held from the doorway, low, by someone kneeling. [263] A child, then. [266]

"Your daughter filmed this," Iva said. [272]

Teodor stopped humming. [275] "Mira was six." [278]

"Six is old enough to be told a secret." [287]

Iva closed her eyes and let the booth rearrange itself around the year on the strip. [303] Lenka at the bench, writing letters, one after another, the pile growing at her elbow. [318] Goodbye letters; Iva knew the shape of them the way you know a hymn by its first chord. [336] She had written eleven of her own to a man in Lisbon and mailed none, and it was those letters that had made her a crystal gazer in the first place, because she needed to know the answer to one question: where does love go when it is made and never used? [388] It had to be stored somewhere. [394] Nothing that heavy simply evaporates. [399]

The thunder leaned closer and lowered its voice. [407]

In the vision Lenka stood, letters in hand, and Iva tracked her the way she tracked everything, by distance and turn: four steps to the number two projector, a reach behind the lamphouse, a panel that gave. [444]

"Behind the lamphouse," Iva said aloud. [450] "Number two." [452]

Teodor did not ask how. [457] He had run that machine six nights a week for three decades and never once opened the false back of its housing, because a man does not look for what he does not know is missing. [493] The panel gave. [496] Inside lay a bundle tied with ticket-roll string, the paper gone the color of tea. [511] Warm, still, from the lamp he had switched off an hour ago. [523]

Iva understood then that Lenka had not hidden the letters so much as set them somewhere to incubate, against the one steady heat in the building, in the one room where her husband would spend the rest of his life without knowing what he sat beside. [569] Thirty-one years of carbon arc and then xenon, keeping them at body temperature. [582]

There were nine. [585] One for Mira, one for the usherettes, one for the Regent itself. [597] Six for Teodor, dated across the last six months she had known she was dying, each one thicker than the last. [618]

He opened the first with his projectionist's hands, which did not shake, because they had been trained never to shake near film. [640] He read a line. [644] Then he read it again, and this time the hum came out of him with the words riding on it, and the hole in the tune closed. [671] She had written the lyrics down for him at the top of every letter, the way you write a return address. [692]

The storm broke at last over the harbor, but the booth was built to muffle noise, and what reached them was only a soft enormous rolling, a sound with its shoes off. [724]

Iva gave him back the film strip and left him with his letters. [737] On the iron stairs she stood a moment, mapping in her head the flat in Lisbon she had never seen, and behind its stove, the warm place where hers would fit. [768] Unused love was not lost, then. [774] It was only stored near heat, waiting for someone to know where to reach. [788]

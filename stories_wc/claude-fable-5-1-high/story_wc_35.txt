The rain stopped at dawn, and Ila Vane put on her gloves before she left the kitchen. [17] Kesh Fortress had not seen water fall in nine years, and the courtyard steamed like a kettle left too long on the coals. [40] The flagstones were not flagstones. [45] They were reef, dead for ten thousand years, cut flat by the founders and laid over their own hollows. [64] Now every hollow held a pool. [70]

Ila crouched at the nearest one and did what her grandmother had taught her on a coast she had not seen since she was twelve. [95] You read a tidal pool by what it keeps. [104] Grit settles where the water is tired. [111] Clear water means a drain. [116] Six pools, then twelve, then the whole court, and every one of them clearest at the eastern lip, where the fossil channels ran under the wall toward the dunes. [145] Something down there was drinking. [150]

She had come to Kesh as a cook and stayed as something else. [163] The first week, she had tasted the quartermaster's dead brother in a loaf he kneaded, salt and iron and a door closing. [185] She had said nothing, taken the loaf back, and baked it again with honey until the door stayed open. [204] He slept that night for the first time in a month. [215] Word got around. [218] Commander Rusk came to her kitchen with the whole garrison's worst year in his hands, the year of the Hollow Well, when they had shut the gate on a caravan and listened to it die. [253] "Sandpaper it," he said. [257] Not gone. [259] Just smooth enough to hold. [264]

So she had. [267] Two hundred soldiers, nine years of stews and flatbreads, each meal a rough edge taken down. [283] What she filed off did not vanish. [290] It went into her. [294] By the third year she could not touch anyone bare-handed without their face going gray, the caravan's thirst rising in them like water in a hollow. [320] Hence the gloves. [323] Hence the way the garrison loved her and never came within an arm's length. [337]

She followed the drain. [341] Beyond the eastern postern the rain had scoured the dune face down to bone, and the bone was coral, a pale branching arch half-buried where nothing should have stood. [370] Cold breathed out of it. [375] She went in. [378]

The pavilion below was reef grown into a room, ribs and fans and pillars, and every surface was sheathed in ice. [399] Not desert frost. [402] Old ice, green at the core, and the rainwater running down the fossil channels froze on contact and thickened it. [422] Ila knew the taste of the air before she understood it. [433] Remorse. [434] Not hers. [436] Older, and so much of it that the coral had stopped being coral and become a shelf for it. [455]

The founders had not built Kesh to guard a road. [465] They had built it over this, and when the pools filled they had sent their sorrows down to freeze, and called the filling rain a blessing. [491] She had been doing their work for nine years without the ice. [503]

Every night for nine years she had dreamed the same thing and told no one: a cold room, and her gloves off, and the weight leaving through her palms into something that would not flinch. [538] Here it was. [541] She pulled the left glove off with her teeth. [550]

Then she stopped, because she had read the pools. [559] Clear water means a drain. [564] Every drain runs somewhere, and this one ran back up the same channels with the next rain, into the courtyard, into the kitchen cisterns, into the bread. [591] Nine years without rain and the garrison had still been gray at the edges, still waking at the hour the gate closed. [613] The ice did not hold anything. [619] It seeped. [621] The founders had never put anything down. [628] They had only loaned it to their grandchildren at interest. [638]

Ila stood a long time with one bare hand in the cold. [650] Then she put the glove back on. [657] She was not going to touch the ice. [665] She was going to do the thing the founders had not, and it was going to cost what carrying always cost. [686] She breathed in until the green went out of the ice. [697] She subsumed the founders the way she had subsumed the caravan: whole, unfiled, no honey. [712] The coral behind it was only coral, dead and clean. [722]

She climbed out into a dark courtyard where the pools had already begun to shrink, and found Rusk waiting by the kitchen door. [745] "You look worse," he said. [750] "I am." [752] "The rain help at all?" [757] He said it lightly, and she heard nine years of a dream he had never spoken either. [774] "No," she said. [777] "Tomorrow the bread will be rough." [783] "Chew slowly." [785] She went inside and kept her gloves on. [793]

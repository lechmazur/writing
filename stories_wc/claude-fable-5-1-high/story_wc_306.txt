The campfire on the rocks gave up its last spark a little before dawn, and Wren took that as her signal. [21] She shook the sand from her cloak and climbed the lighthouse stairs while the keeper, Tobias Marl, followed with the lamp he no longer needed. [46] Wren sold conscience by the hour. [52] Her clients were people who had worn their own down to nothing, who woke each morning already certain, and who had begun to miss the ache of not knowing. [81] Tobias had written to her in a hand so steady it looked printed. [94] My daughter asks to come home, the letter said. [103] I know the answer is no. [109] I would like someone to tell me why it might not be. [121]

On the gallery the wind came off the water hard and cold, and Wren went to work. [138] From the lining of her cloak she drew seven wind chimes and hung them along the iron rail, each tuned to a different question. [162] Copper for what do you owe. [168] Bone for who taught you this. [174] Green glass for what would it cost to be wrong. [184] She did not choose which doubts to give a client; she had learned long ago that her own choices were just another kind of certainty. [209] The wind chose. [212] She only deciphered. [215]

The cloak itself was patchwork, every square cut from the coat of a former client, and she called it, privately, the cloak of doubt. [239] It was heavy and warm and nothing got through it, which was the point. [253]

The first gust picked the green glass. [260] Wren turned to Tobias and let her face fill with a worry she did not feel. [276] This was her gift and her trick: she was sincerely insincere, meaning every word while believing none of it, because a doubt delivered coldly is only an argument, and arguments never got past a man like Tobias. [313] What would it cost you, she asked, to be wrong about her? [325] He said it would cost nothing, because he was not wrong. [336]

The bone chime rang. [340] Who taught you this, she asked, and watched his certainty flicker like a lamp in a draft. [357] His father, he said, who had kept this light before him and had never once let anyone back who left it. [378]

Then the wind dropped, and in the stillness Tobias noticed what she kept pinned inside the cloak's collar. [396] It was a shard of mirror, narrow as a finger, its silver long since flaked away so that it showed nothing, only the gray sky behind it. [423] A hollow mirror, he said. [428] What is it for? [432]

Wren told him the truth; it was cheaper than a lie at that hour. [446] It was the first thing she had ever doubted, years ago, when she still had a conscience of her own: a mirror that had stopped showing her anything. [474] She had kept it so she would remember what emptiness looked like, and then, somewhere along the way, she had stopped looking into it at all. [500]

You sell doubt, he said, and you have none left for yourself. [512] That is only certainty wearing a different coat. [520] It was the kind of thing she said to clients, and hearing it from the other side was like stepping onto a stair that was not there. [547]

The wind came back. [551] The copper chime spoke, low and insistent: what do you owe. [562] For once Wren did not turn the question toward her client. [573] She stood with it. [577] Then, with fingers that had done this a thousand times for other people, she unhooked the clasp at her throat. [597] She did not take the cloak off. [604] She let it hang open, one hand still holding the edge, so that the cold reached her collarbone and no further. [625] It was as much as she could manage, and she did not pretend it was more. [641]

I do not know, she said, whether I am any good at this, or whether I have only been very good at seeming to be. [666] It was the first thing she had said all morning that she meant and believed at once, and it came out smaller than her performances ever did. [693]

Tobias looked at the open cloak, at the hand that would not let it go, and something in his face gave a quarter inch. [717] I do not know either, he said, whether my father was right. [729] Only that he was certain, and that I have been certain since, and that the two are not the same thing. [750]

The sun cleared the water and the chimes went on ringing, asking their questions of no one in particular. [769] He went down to write a different letter. [777] Wren stayed and held the hollow shard up to the light, to see whether anything had come back. [795]

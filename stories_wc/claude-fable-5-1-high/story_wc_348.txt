The ravine did not run as one river but as a dozen arguments braided over gravel, parting and rejoining under a sky the color of tin. [26] Ilse Varga stood on the highest bar with her boots wet and her brother's field map folded small in her jacket. [47] She had spent nineteen years designing polymers, chains of repeating units that she heard, privately, as ostinatos. [64] Their mother had conducted a provincial orchestra, and both children came out of that house unable to look at anything without hearing its structure. [88] Teo heard it in insects; Ilse heard it in molecules; for a while they had shared a lab and finished each other's phrases. [111] Then he had taken the divergent path, down here, to count monarchs on milkweed while she stayed and made the company's film thinner, cheaper, and more durable than anyone needed. [141] When the fragments of that film turned up in this water, in his samples, he had called her. [159] She had said his work was sentiment dressed as science. [169] He had said nothing at all, which was worse, and the silence was now two years deep. [186] She had come to atone, though she did not know what shape atonement took in a place like this. [205]

His old sampling stakes still stood along the middle channel, orange paint gone the pink of a healed scar. [224] On a milkweed stalk beside the third stake hung an empty chrysalis, a clear husk with the gold beading still intact along its rim. [248] She knelt and did not touch it. [255] Teo would have logged it: date, stake number, the split along the ventral seam. [269] Ilse only saw that something had finished its slow chemistry inside and gone, leaving the container as evidence rather than as loss. [291]

She stayed by the water until the light flattened, because she had nowhere else to be. [307] Foam rode the channels, the pale tan foam of dissolved leaf and root, and she began, out of habit, to trace where it went. [331] Where the river split around a bar, the foam thinned to nothing, spread so wide across each branch that it seemed to vanish. [354] But at the tail of the bar, where two channels met again, the foam gathered into a long bright seam, thick as rope, exactly along the line where the currents sheared against each other. [388] Separation dispersed it; reunion concentrated it. [394] She followed the seam with her eyes to the next bar, and the next, and heard it as a fugue, the same subject dividing into voices and returning, each return louder for the division. [428]

Then the chemist in her caught up with the musician. [438] For a year she had been trying to design a recovery polymer, a sticky amphiphile that would bind the fragments of her own film and pull them out of moving water, and it had failed because she kept dosing it evenly, everywhere, diluting it into uselessness. [484] The river was telling her where the fragments already went. [494] They would ride the shear lines like the foam, collecting at every confluence, and a polymer released at the head of a braid would find them there without her having to find anything. [527] Let the braiding do the concentrating; let the separation be the mechanism of the gathering. [542]

She laughed once, a short sound the water swallowed, and felt something in her chest loosen that had been held rigid for two years. [566] It was not that the grief left. [573] It was that it stopped being a wall and became a channel, with a direction and a downstream. [591] She knew, kneeling there, that Teo's stakes marked confluences. [600] He had already found the seams; he had been sampling exactly where the river gathered its evidence, and his numbers, the ones she had dismissed, were the map her design needed. [631] The pieces fell into place with the quiet click of a good cadence: his path and hers had not been two rivers but two channels of one, parted by a bar she had built out of pride, and the bar had a tail. [674]

She photographed the chrysalis, the stake, the bright seam of foam, and walked back up the ravine in the last light. [695] In the truck she wrote to him for the first time since the silence began. [710] She did not apologize first; he would have heard that as another performance. [723] She sent the photographs and the foam sketch and a single line: I need your confluence data, and I was wrong, and I think these are the same sentence. [752] Then she added the thing she had actually come to say, which was that the chrysalis by his third stake was empty, and that she had finally understood empty was not the same as gone. [787]

The Cognition Levy came in the spring, and by summer every declared thought cost something. [15] Ideas spoken aloud were assessed at the standard rate, written ideas at double, and the auditors' lapel sensors could hear a metaphor forming from across a room. [42] Teodor Vance, formerly of the university's linguistics faculty and currently of nowhere in particular, considered this an insult to the profession. [63] He had been dismissed for teaching a dead grammar without a permit, and he had stolen, on his way out, a single gourd rattle seed from the botany wing. [92] It was, he liked to say, the only thing he owned that could not be taxed, because a seed keeps its thoughts to itself. [116]

The club met in Dr. Ilse Amari's greenhouse on the east side, where she bred leafcutter bees and kept the humidity high enough to fog any sensor within twenty paces. [146] Insects, the Levy office had ruled, were sub-cognitive, so nobody audited an entomologist. [159] There were nine of them by June: Ilse, Teodor, a retired mail carrier, two nurses, a bus mechanic, twin sisters who ran a bakery, and a boy of sixteen who never gave his name. [193] Teodor taught them a language he had built from the dead grammar, one with no nouns for abstractions, so that a sentence in it registered on the sensors as small talk about weather and dirt. [228] You could say freedom in it, but only by describing the angle at which a vine leans toward a gap in a fence. [251]

"I am hiding nothing," Teodor told the auditor who stopped him on the tram, and this was true and also useless to her. [274] He answered every question fully, in the club's grammar, and walked away untaxed and smiling. [289]

Speech was only half of it. [295] Speech vanished; the club wanted something that would stay. [304] So they joined community gardens, one member to each plot across the city, and planted in patterns. [321] Beans in a diagonal meant one thing, marigolds in a broken ring another, and a gourd vine trained to warp left around a stake carried the verb that the language used for refusing. [354] Teodor's stolen seed went into the ground at the Halvorsen Street garden, in the center of the pattern, and he watered it himself every morning. [379]

Ilse asked him once why he bothered, since the Levy would outlast a gourd and probably all of them. [398] "I am not trying to win," he said. [406] "I am standing guard over one small thing that is still mine, and I would like to know it is still burning when I check on it." [433]

August came in hot and everything converged at once. [442] The gardens bloomed a week early and all together, and someone in the Levy office, looking at aerial survey photos, noticed that nine plots across the city shared the same geometry. [473] The same week, Ilse's bees swarmed, the nurses were called to a double shift, and the boy with no name was stopped at a checkpoint carrying a pocket full of dried seeds. [505] He said they were for soup. [511] They believed him, because a seed is not a thought. [521]

The auditors reached the greenhouse on a Thursday, four of them, sensors already blinking red from the heat. [539] Inside, the air was thick as bread, and forty thousand leafcutter bees were loose because Ilse had opened every hive an hour before. [562] Teodor sat among them in a wicker chair with a dried gourd in his lap, shaking it gently, and the seeds inside kept a rhythm the sensors could not parse. [592]

"We know about the gardens," the lead auditor said. [601]

"I should hope so," Teodor said. [607] "They are very visible." [611]

"What do they say?" [615]

He told her, candidly and completely, in the only grammar he had left: that the vine at Halvorsen Street leaned four degrees toward the gap in the fence, that the marigolds had come up broken on the north side, that the beans ran downhill toward the water. [662] Her sensor registered small talk. [667] She could hear that he meant something, and she could not lay hands on what, and the bees were in her hair. [689]

They fined him for the gourd, in the end, as unlicensed agriculture, which Teodor paid in cash and considered a bargain. [710] They pulled up the Halvorsen Street plot that evening, but the boy had already been through it with his soup pockets, and the twins had a tray of seedlings under their bakery counter. [743] By September there were thirty gardens. [749]

Teodor kept the rattle on a shelf in the greenhouse, above the hives, and when the club met he would shake it once at the start, a sound with no taxable content, meaning exactly what everyone in the room already knew. [790]

Wren Halloran had framed enough maps to know that a tear is never only damage. [15] People fold paper along the line they cannot bear to look at, and then they fold it again. [33] The survey sheet spread across her bench was a 1958 Ordnance draft of Cauldmoor, and it had been torn straight through the ring of dots that marked the henge. [200] I want to know what she was saying, but I can't make myself listen." [214]

[481] Her left hand pressed flat against a painted saint whose own hand pointed east.

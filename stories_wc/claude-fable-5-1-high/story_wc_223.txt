The dome had no sun in it, only the ring the sun left behind. [14] Every morning during the interregnum, Marit climbed the two hundred steps to the whispering gallery and waited for that ring to slide down the curved wall and find the floor. [44] Below her, the throne wore a grey sheet. [52] The old Protector was three weeks dead, and the Council had not yet agreed on who would sit uncovered in his place. [74]

Marit had been paid, for eleven years, to be in danger that was not real. [89] The Ministry of Readiness hired crisis actors for its drills, and she was good at it: she could bleed from a packet, faint on a count of three, sob a name until the cadets learned to hear it. [127] Then the granary roof came down on a market day when no drill was scheduled, and she was under it for six hours, and none of the blood was from a packet. [159]

The Protector's office declared it an exercise the next morning. [169] The forty-one dead were entered in the drill ledger as participants, and the survivors were paid their usual rate. [188] Marit cashed hers. [191] She had never forgiven herself the small sound the coins made. [202]

The Steward found her on the ninth morning. [210] He was the one keeping the city breathing between rulers, a tall, tired man who had already pardoned the old regime's jailers and burned its informant lists on the same afternoon. [241] People called him merciful. [245] Marit thought he was something narrower and more dangerous: he was kind the way a flood is kind, leveling everything so that no one would have to stand higher than anyone else. [277]

He carried the Protector's chessboard under one arm. [285] When he set it on the gallery bench she saw that it had every piece but the pawns. [303] "He had them melted down," the Steward said. [311] "He said they only ever moved forward and died, and he could see no reason to be reminded." [329]

"You've been walking up here," he said. [336] "The guards hear it at the far side." [344] "They say it sounds like someone talking." [351]

It was. [353] The ring of light was on the floor now, and its edge, the soft border where bright gave way to dim, had begun its slow daily migration around the curve. [383] Marit stepped into that traveling half-shadow and walked. [391] A long stride, two short, a pause with her heel struck twice against the stone. [406] The gallery took each footfall and carried it along the wall, so that a person standing across the dome heard not steps but syllables. [430] She had built the alphabet herself in the months after the granary, when she could not yet hold a pen without her hand remembering rubble. [455] Paper burned; the Steward had proved that. [462] A woman walking could not be filed. [469]

She spelled: TOMA GAVE HIS WATER TO A STRANGER'S CHILD. [479] She spelled: THE BAKER SANG SO WE WOULD KNOW WHERE HIS VOICE WAS. [492] She spelled: A HAND HELD MINE FOR FOUR HOURS AND I NEVER SAW ITS FACE. [507]

The Steward listened with his head tilted, decoding. [515] When she came back around to him, the penumbra had moved on without her, and she stood in plain light. [535]

"I could make you court historian," he said. [543] "Whoever they seat will need one." [549] "You could write the whole reign." [555]

"I don't want the reign," Marit said. [562] "Reigns write themselves." [565] "I want the pawns." [569] She gestured at the board's empty first ranks. [577] "Who gave up a coat." [582] "Who counted breaths aloud so a dying man would not have to count his own." [597] "Nobody keeps that." [600] "I mean to." [603]

He was quiet. [606] Then he reached into his coat and set the drill ledger on the chessboard, blue thread through its spine, forty-one names inside it filed under EXERCISE. [632] "I was going to burn this with the rest," he said. [643] "Clean start." [645] "It seemed kind." [648]

"It would have been," she said, "to you." [656]

She did not burn it and she did not keep it whole. [668] She worked the blue thread loose knot by knot until the binding let go and the pages lay separate on the board, forty-one of them, spilling past the sixteen squares where the pawns should have stood. [704] Unbound, the word EXERCISE held nothing together anymore. [712] It was only ink on one loose sheet, and she turned that sheet face down. [727]

The Steward left her there with the light. [735] Whoever sat on the throne tomorrow, the ring would still come down the wall each morning and set its soft edge walking, and she would walk with it, one name a lap, a long stride, two short, the heel struck twice. [776] Across the dome, where no one stood, the wall went on saying it anyway. [790]

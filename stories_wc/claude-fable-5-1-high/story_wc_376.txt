Ines gilded leaves for a living, which in the greenhouse city meant she was half scribe and half gardener. [19] The candor fern grew only in her dome, its broad leaves taking gold like skin takes warmth, and it wilted near dishonesty. [41] People came to her to dictate letters they wanted believed. [51] That morning a water-broker sat across her bench and said, "Tell my sister the ration cuts were not my decision," and the leaf under Ines's brush curled brown at the edge. [82] "Try again," Ines said. [86] He looked at the leaf a long time. [94] "Tell her I was in the room and said nothing." [104] The leaf held. [107] She had learned that this was how truth mostly arrived: not in confessions, but in small corrections nobody else would have noticed. [129]

Outside the glass, the sky hung the color of old bruises, and no one had walked under it in living memory. [150] The city sprawled inward instead, dome joined to dome by irrigation channels that had once carried water from the Oasis at its heart, and with the water, the mail. [179] Letters traveled in sealed capsules on the current, stamped at each lock with zinc-etched plates. [194] Three seasons ago the Oasis dried. [200] The channels emptied in a week. [206] Whatever was afloat that week went nowhere anyone could name. [216]

Ines had lost eleven letters to Teodor, a pipe-fitter in the far eastern dome, and eleven of his to her. [236] They had never written anything grand. [242] He described the taste of the morning condensate; she described how the gold behaved in humid weather. [259] It was a conversation made entirely of small true things, and it had simply stopped mid-sentence. [275] She wanted to know where such conversations went. [283]

So after the broker left, she wrapped a fern cutting in damp cloth and walked the dry channel to the sorting house at the Oasis basin. [309] The building had been stripped of anything worth carrying. [318] But one floorboard by the lock-master's desk rocked under her heel, and beneath it she found a drift of shredded paper and a fragment of zinc, etched with the fine parallel lines of a postal route. [354]

She sat on the floor and began to piece the shreds together. [366] It was a map, hand-drawn in the lock-master's ink, and it was clearly meant to be a joke: a skull, a dotted line, an X labeled TREASURE. [393] Half the strips were blank on the back, and she could not tell which way they ran until she laid the zinc fragment beside them. [418] The map had been traced from the official route plate; the etched lines matched the inked ones, and once she aligned three strips to the metal, the rest fell into order. [449] The X sat over the old overflow cistern beneath the basin floor. [461]

Someone had shredded the map so it could not be followed casually, then kept the pieces anyway. [478] Ines understood that impulse. [482] It was the same one that made a man say I said nothing instead of it wasn't me. [500]

The cistern hatch opened onto a dry chamber knee-deep in capsules. [511] When the channels failed, the clerks had swept every stranded letter here rather than let them be scattered, and then, with no water to carry them onward, they had simply closed the lid. [544] This was where lost conversations went. [550] Not into the toxic sky, not into nothing. [558] Under the floor, waiting for someone stubborn enough to look. [568]

It took her until the light went amber to find Teodor's handwriting. [580] She unsealed the eleven capsules and set the fern cutting on her knee and reread every one, slowly, in order. [600] The condensate had tasted of copper on the ninth of the month. [612] He had mended a valve and lied to his foreman about how long it took, and he was telling her because he did not like how easy the lie had been. [643] The eleventh letter said only that the water level had dropped a hand's width overnight and that he was frightened, and did not know why he was writing that to a calligrapher he had never met. [679] The fern on her knee stayed green through all of it. [690] Ines had not needed the leaf to tell her that, but she was glad to have it witnessed. [708]

She found her own eleven as well, and put them in her satchel with his. [723] The channels would not refill. [728] But the channel beds were level and dry, and a person could walk them, east, all the way to the far dome. [750] She would carry the conversation there herself, and finish the sentence in person, and see what the leaf did when he answered. [772]

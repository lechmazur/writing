The dojo sat on a rock the size of a barn, hanging a hundred meters above a valley that could not decide what it had been. [26] Some mornings the river below ran east past a mill town; by noon it ran west past a graveyard, and the townspeople below remembered burying their dead there all their lives. [57] History had been rewriting itself for eleven years, and Teodor had stopped feeling anything about it nine years ago. [76] He had gone into the Dissolution like everyone in his order, the deliberate ego death that was meant to burn the self away, and unlike most he had come back out. [107] What came back could see through anything and feel almost nothing. [118] So he taught. [121] His method was old: pair a student with her exact opposite and let the friction teach what words could not. [141]

The two women who climbed the rope ladder that afternoon were not opposites. [154] They were the same woman. [159] Same scar across the left eyebrow, same way of standing with weight on the back foot, same glass jar carried between them like a child neither would let the other hold alone. [191] Inside the jar, a brain floated in amber fluid. [200] The label read DUPLICATE in a hand both of them shared. [211]

"We need a judge," said the one on the left. [221] "The courts down there rewrite every week," said the one on the right. [234] "Monday I have the growth records proving I'm the original. [244] Thursday she does." [247] "Nothing rewrites up here," the left one said. [255] "Whatever you decide will hold." [260]

Teodor looked at the jar, then at the women, and did what the Dissolution had left him able to do. [280] "Neither of you wants the brain," he said. [288] "You want to be the one who isn't the copy. [298] But read your own label. [303] The brain is a duplicate too. [309] Whoever you were grown from was already a copy of someone, and that someone is gone. [325] There is no original to win." [331]

The left one went white with fury. [338] The right one went white with grief. [345] And there, Teodor saw, were the opposites after all. [354] One had spent the eleven years fighting to be real; the other had spent them mourning that she might not be. [375] Identical bodies, complementary wounds. [379]

"Set the jar on the altar stone," he said. [388] "Then fight each other. [392] I will rule when you're done." [398]

They fought the way he expected: as mirrors, each move canceled by its twin, neither able to land anything. [417] Below them the valley shuddered and a bridge that had never existed spanned the river, already old, already rusted. [436] "Stop mirroring," Teodor said. [440] "Fury, you attack only. [444] Grief, you yield only. [448] See what happens." [451]

What happened was a dance. [456] The angry one drove forward and the grieving one gave way, and where before there had been a stalemate there was now motion, a spiral that carried both across the stone and back. [489] The one who yielded began, without deciding to, to redirect; the one who attacked began to be led. [507] Neither was winning. [510] Together they were something whole. [515]

Teodor felt it before he understood it: a pressure behind the sternum, an ache he had not had a name for in nine years. [539] He had told himself the Dissolution had taken his feeling as its price. [552] Watching them, the pretense he had never turned his sight on sliced open. [565] He had not lost his feeling. [571] He had been half a man refusing to spar with the other half, the one who had died in there and whom he had labeled, privately, the original. [599] The survivor was the copy; the copy was the one who had gone numb so it would never have to grieve the man it replaced. [624] The ache spread outward, vivifying him limb by limb, hot and terrible and alive. [638] He was crying, which he noted with the same clarity he noted everything, and for the first time the clarity did not sit alone. [662]

The women had stopped. [666] They stood breathing hard, each with a hand on the other's shoulder, looking at him. [681]

"Ruling," he said, when he could. [687] "Joint custody. [689] The jar stays here on the altar, where nothing rewrites, and so do you, as long as you like. [708] You will train as a pair. [714] The brain is not evidence of anything. [721] It is a reminder that there was never an original, only the two of you, and that is enough to make one person." [744]

The angry one laughed. [748] The grieving one wiped her face. [754] It was, Teodor saw, the same gesture. [761] Below them, the river changed its mind again, and for once he felt the ground move. [777]

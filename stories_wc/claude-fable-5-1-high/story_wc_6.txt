Marisol had learned to watch the sky the way other people watched their hands. [14] On the island they called this season the migration of clouds, when the trades pushed whole flotillas of white across the caldera and their shadows swam over the dunes like slow whales. [46] She counted them from the truck as she drove Teo down the coast road, gracefully alert, one eye on the ridgeline for smoke that would not come today. [74]

Teo did not look at the sky. [81] He looked at his sleeve, pulled down over the new skin on his forearm, the graft still shiny and pink as the inside of a shell. [107]

"It's going to be loud," she said. [114] "The sand, I mean." [118] "Nobody else." [120]

"Sand isn't loud." [123]

"This sand is." [126]

She parked where the road gave up and they walked the rest, over the shoulder of the burned dune. [145] Three years ago this slope had been marram grass and sea holly. [157] Now it was crust and stubble, and the wind had carved it into hollows. [171] Her father had done this. [176] He had done a lot of things with matches, but this was the last one, the one the court finally believed, and the one that had left the lagoon below exposed like a black eye. [211]

Marisol had been eleven when his first fire found her. [221] She kept the scar on her shoulder the way other daughters kept a locket. [235] She had joined the brigade at nineteen because it seemed the only honest answer to a question no one had asked her: what do you do with a father like that? [266] You go toward the fire with unyielding compassion, because hatred had already burned enough of the island. [283]

Below the dune crest the obsidian lagoon lay still, water so dark and glassy it seemed to be a hole into the ground. [306] Around its edge ran a rim of black sand, fine as flour, carried up from the volcanic beach by centuries of storm. [328] Marisol knelt and filled her palm. [334]

"Watch," she said, and lifted her hand and let the sand fall in a thin stream. [350]

It sang. [352] A low glassy hum rose from the pouring grains, the note bending as the wind caught it, and Teo flinched, then leaned in. [375]

"It's the shape of the grains," she said. [383] "They were burned once." [387] "The fire cracked them and the sea polished them and now, when they rub together, they can't help it." [406] "They make a sound." [410]

He held out his good hand. [416] She poured sand into it, and he tipped it, and the dune answered him in a thin bright voice. [435]

"My mom says the scar will fade." [442]

"It won't, much," Marisol said. [447] "I'm not going to lie to you." [454] "It will change." [457] "It will learn things." [461]

She unbuttoned her collar and showed him the scar on her shoulder, the old graft that had grown into her like a strange country. [485] He looked, and did not look away. [492]

Then she reached into her pocket and took out the coin. [503] It was foreign, worn nearly smooth, a coin from the mainland her father had left, drilled through the center and strung on red thread. [527] Her mother had made it for her the week after the fire, saying: this is where your father came from, and this is where the fire came from, and you can carry both without being either. [563] She had worn it for twenty years. [570]

"This is for you," she said, tying it at Teo's wrist above the graft. [584] "Red is for blood that stayed inside." [591] "The coin is for something that came from far away and still ended up here." [606]

"Where did it come from?" [611]

"Somewhere I've never been," she said. [617] "Everything on this island came from somewhere else." [625]

They spent the afternoon on the slope, grafting marram plugs into the burned dune, pressing roots into ash and black sand, pinching the earth closed around each stem. [653] The clouds went over. [657] Their shadows crossed the lagoon and lifted off again, and the light kept shifting and settling like kelp drifting on a swell, everything moving and nothing hurrying. [684]

Teo worked with his sleeve pushed up. [691]

Near evening the wind rose and the whole rim of the lagoon began to hum, a single held note from a thousand ruined grains, and he stopped, and listened, and held up his wrist so that the coin spun on its red thread and caught the last light. [739]

"It's singing," he said. [743]

"It's been singing all along," Marisol said. [750] "That's all I wanted you to hear." [757] "Scars can sing." [760]

She watched the sky one more time, out of habit, out of love. [773] No smoke. [775] Only the clouds, migrating, and beneath them the black water holding each one for a moment before letting it go. [795]

Tobiah Reyne moved along the wharf the way water moves around a stone, and the other dock workers hated him for it. [22] They hauled crates with their backs and their curses; he slid them, pivoted, let the weight fall where it wanted to go. [44] He had not always been graceful. [50] For thirty years he had been a fist, and the wharf still remembered the teeth he had left on it. [70] Then a swinging load of anchor chain had come down across both his hands, and when the splints came off, his fingers would not close. [95] Violence had become impossible for him, and grace had crept in to fill the vacancy like a tide. [113] He resented it. [116]

"You carry that thing like it still bites," Sela said. [126] She was crouched on the low pier sorting mussels she had pried from the sublittoral fringe, the drowned shelf below the tide line where the kelp never dried. [154] She meant the whale tooth at his throat. [162] It was scrimshaw, yellowed bone etched by his great-grandfather, a pirate who had come home from the southern seas with nothing but this and a reputation. [188] One face showed a whale rising. [194] The other showed an island with a keyhole cut into its cliff. [206]

"It's a trophy," Tobiah said. [211]

"It's a map," Sela said. [216]

"You've said that for two years." [222]

"You've been wrong for two years." [228] She stood, brine running off her elbows, and jabbed a finger at the keyhole. [242] "My grandmother told it the same way yours did, and you know it. [255] There's an island past the fringe that comes up when a whale calls from the deep, and it only comes up for someone who can't fight anymore. [282] Your pirate found it after he lost his hands to a sabre. [294] He didn't carve that tooth to brag. [301] He carved it to confess." [306]

Tobiah's ruined hands wanted to make fists and could not, and the wanting hurt more than the chain had. [325] "Why do you care?" [329]

"Because you're the only man on this wharf who moves like he's dancing and looks like he's at a funeral. [349] It's a waste, and I hate waste." [356]

She went out on the neap tide before dawn, and he followed her, telling himself it was to prove her wrong. [377] Her skiff crossed the fringe where the kelp forest lay flat under two fathoms of green water, and stopped at the lip where the shelf fell away into black. [406] They waited, arguing in whispers about whether waiting was foolish. [416]

Then the sound came up through the hull. [424] It was not a noise so much as a pressure, a long note from far below that made the oars hum in their locks and Tobiah's teeth ache in his jaw. [455] The whale was calling. [459] The water at the lip of the shelf bulged, and rock came up through it, streaming kelp, no bigger than a church. [481] In its cliff face was a dark slot the shape of a keyhole. [494]

"Go on," Sela said, and her voice shook, and she would have hit him if he had mentioned it. [513]

He stepped from the skiff onto stone that was already sinking. [524] The keyhole was too small for a body and exactly the size of a tooth. [539] He understood then what the scrimshaw was for, and what the island asked. [552] His hands could not undo a knot, so he bent his neck and pulled the cord over his head with his wrists, clumsy, graceless for the first time in two years. [583] The tooth had hung there since he was twelve; detaching it felt like pulling out a rib. [600] He set it into the slot. [606]

Nothing opened in the rock. [611] Something opened in him. [615]

He saw his great-grandfather as he must have been: a handless man standing on this same stone, weeping, finding that the only lock the island cared about was the one he had built around his own chest. [652] The pirate had gone home and carved the way here so that some later Reyne who could no longer hit anything would know there was somewhere else to put his strength. [683]

The water reached Tobiah's knees, then his waist. [691] The island was going back down with the tooth in it, and he let it. [706]

Sela hauled him over the gunwale by his collar, swearing at him the whole way, and then held on longer than the hauling required. [730]

"You're crying," she said. [734]

"So are you." [737]

"I'll throw you back in." [742]

"You won't," he said, and he did not say it like a challenge. [755]

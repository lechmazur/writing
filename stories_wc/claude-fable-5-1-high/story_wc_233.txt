The third warning came the way the others had, stitched into the hem of a dream. [16] Queen Isaveth stood in a blue cave with stars bleeding down its walls, thin bright threads that never reached the floor, and a voice she almost knew said: come again and you will not wake. [51] She woke, as she had every night for a season, with her hands pressed against her breastbone and the taste of cold metal on her tongue. [77] Twenty years of reigning had taught her that most mysteries were only accounts kept badly. [92] The crown she wore was said to hold an enchantment older than the valley, and she had stopped believing in it around the time she noticed the enchantment never lowered the price of grain. [126] But the dreams did not keep accounts. [133] They kept appointments. [136]

At dawn she summoned Wren, the youngest of her ladies, and spoke in the low voice that made courtiers lean forward and then wonder why they had already agreed. [165] "You grew up beneath the ridge," Isaveth said. [173] "You know the path to the ember grotto." [181] Wren's fingers stilled on the lacing of the queen's sleeve. [191] "Nobody climbs to it, Majesty. [196] We only watch it glow at dusk, like a coal someone forgot to bank." [210] "Then we will be the first." [216] It was not a request, and both of them knew it, and Wren's face did something complicated before it settled into obedience. [238]

They left by the kitchen stair in plain wool, and no one stopped a queen who did not look like one. [259] The climb took the whole of the day. [267] Pines gave way to scree, scree to a mist that beaded on their lashes, and then they broke through the cloud into an evening that had been going on without them. [298] Below, the clouds lay white and solid as a second country. [309] Above, the grotto opened in the rock, and it was not ember-colored at all. [323] It was cobalt, the deep blue of a vein seen through skin. [335] Isaveth understood, with a chill that was almost satisfaction, that the valley had only ever seen the glow through miles of dusty air, and had named the thing by its disguise. [366]

Inside, the walls were exactly as she had dreamed them. [376] Stars were set into the ceiling like pearls sewn into a veil, and from each one a thread of light ran downward, slow and bright, thinning to nothing before it touched the floor. [409] It was beautiful the way a spider's web is beautiful, all pattern and patience. [423] The nearest star was dim, its thread barely a wisp, and when Isaveth looked closely she saw a face in it, an old woman's face, and knew it from a portrait in the long gallery. [458] Her grandmother. [460] Beside her, another face, and another, each queen set into the rock and bleeding her light down into the valley, one thread at a time, for as long as there was light to bleed. [494]

So this was the enchantment. [499] Not a blessing on the crown but a debt collected from whoever wore it, and the dreams were not an invitation but a fitting. [523] There was an empty setting in the ceiling, just her size. [534] The warnings, she realized, had not come from the grotto. [544] They had come from the women in it, the last soft commands they had left to give. [561]

"Majesty," Wren whispered, and there was something in it beyond fear. [572] "I have dreamed this place too." [578] Isaveth turned. [580] The grotto did not care who wore the crown, only that someone did, and if the queen would not come willingly it would begin whispering to the girl who laced her sleeves. [612] The empty setting pulsed once, patient as a held breath. [622]

Isaveth took Wren's wrist and walked her backward to the mouth of the cave, where the air was ordinary and the clouds lay below like a floor one could almost trust. [653] Out of the white, something rose. [691] The queen and the girl let out one breath between them, the same breath, and in it Isaveth felt the grip of the dreams loosen like a knot finding its end. [722] A valley could be lit by a thousand small lights that no one had to die for. [739] She had never believed in the enchantment, and it turned out that disbelief was the only armor that fit. "We are going down," she said softly, and it was the last command she ever needed to give as queen. [778] Behind them the grotto dimmed to embers and waited for a crown no one would wear again. [795]

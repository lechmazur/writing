Three days after the procedure, an orderly handed me a cardboard box and said it held everything I used to be. [21] It held a wallet with no photographs, a key to nothing, and a baseball. [35] The ball was old, delicately robust, the leather gone soft as a cheek but the stitches still holding their line. [55] Across the sweet spot, in blue ink: Hale, read the Tribune between the lines, then go down. [72] It was signed H., and dated forty-one years from now. [82]

I should have found that remarkable. [88] I found it about as interesting as the wallet. [97]

The clinic calls it a clean slate; the court calls it rehabilitation; the Tribune, in the archive copies I asked for, called me eleven counts and a nickname I will not repeat. [129] I read about the man with my face the way you read about weather in a city you will never visit. [150] That was the flaw nobody printed. [156] The procedure scoops out the memories, and empathy is supposed to grow back on its own, like a fingernail. [175] Mine had not. [178] I could look at photographs of the people I had hurt and feel only a mild curiosity about the lighting. [198]

So I did what the ball said, because I had no reason not to, which is the only kind of reason I had left. [222]

Between the lines is not a metaphor if you are patient. [233] The Tribune's pieces on the clinic praised the outcomes and let odd words sit next to each other: karst, aquifer, reclamation. [254] A quoted geologist mentioned caves beneath the north wing and was cut off mid-sentence, as if someone had leaned on the editor. [276] And in the classifieds, three weeks running: Lost, everything. [285] Found, north stairwell, past the boiler. [291] Left when it does not matter, right when it does. [301] Signed H. [303]

The stairwell smelled of bleach and then, below the boiler, of wet stone. [316] A maintenance door had been propped with a folded newspaper, which I appreciated as a matter of style. [334] Beyond it the tunnels forked, and forked again, limestone throats lit faintly from somewhere ahead. [349]

Left when it does not matter. [355] Nothing mattered; that was my whole condition. [362] So I went left, and left, and left, and after twenty minutes stood again beneath the boiler with the newspaper at my feet. [385]

The labyrinth was not asking what mattered to me. [394]

I tried again, and at each fork I made myself picture someone else standing there: the geologist, the editor, the woman from the photographs whose hand had been on a car door. [426] Would it matter to her which way I went? [435] It was a clumsy trick, guessing at a stranger's stakes, but the tunnels accepted it. [450] The glow grew. [453]

The lake, when I reached it, was the color of a struck match held under water: blue-green, pulsing, shoreless in the dark. [475] Reclamation, the article had said. [480] The clinic drained what it scooped from us into the aquifer, and something down here had learned to eat it and shine. [502] Forty years of clean slates, glowing. [508] I have seen a lot of things I cannot remember seeing, so I am hard to impress, but I sat down on the wet rock and stayed there a while. [538]

In the light there were shapes, or my eyes made shapes: a hand on a car door, a kitchen at night, a boy with a glove waiting for a throw that did not come. [572] I turned the baseball over in my hands and understood, without any memory to prove it, that it had never been mine. [594] I had taken it from someone, along with everything else. [604] The other Hale, the one with forty-one more years, had not sent it back to give me my past. [623] He had sent it to give me someone else's. [632]

I put my hand in the water. [639] It was cold, and it was only cold; no memories rushed back, because they were not in there anymore, only their residue, digested and made into light. [666] But the boy with the glove stayed in my eye after I closed it, and something in my chest that had been level for three days tilted a degree. [695]

It is not much. [699] It is the first thing I have felt that did not start out as mine, which I gather is the entire trick. [721] I do not know how the other Hale sends things backward, but I have forty-one years to find out, and a boy to find first, and a ball to return to him before I need it back. [758] Somewhere up there the Tribune is printing tomorrow's classifieds, and I already know what one of them says. [776]

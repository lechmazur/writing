On the asteroid Ferrante, the dukes had carved their heaven in gilt, and every corridor still curled with plaster cherubs going green at the wingtips. [25] Oriel Vance had been forty when she led the miners up those corridors, and she was seventy-three now, and the republic she had made was three years dissolved into a shipping cooperative that paid her a pension in oxygen credits. [65] Nobody called her comrade anymore. [70] They called her the witness. [75]

The dispute concerned a single piece of crystal, the size of a thumbnail, lifted from the ballroom floor in the ninth stratum. [97] Teodor Aske, sent by the Belt Historical Consortium, held it in tweezers under the work lamp and said it proved the vase had been smashed by revolutionaries, since the fracture ran with the bloom of a thrown object. [135] Mira Solano, the cooperative's own archaeologist, said the bloom showed a fall from height and proved the Duchess had hurled it herself rather than let the miners hold anything beautiful. [165] They had asked Oriel to remember, and she had told them the truth, which was that she remembered smoke. [184]

But there was another way to remember on Ferrante, and she had spent thirty years forbidding it. [201]

Time had become visible the winter after the uprising, when the asteroid drifted into the tail of the Ashani comet and never quite drifted out. [226] The dust did something to light. [232] It hung in the air like gold pollen, thickest in the mornings, and where it settled on any mirror the glass grew deep and held every reflection it had ever received. [263] During the storms, when the tail thickened and the dust came in through every seam, the palace mirrors ran with the dead. [285] Miners who had never owned a chair stood before them and watched dukes waltz, and wept, and did not go to their shifts. [308]

So Oriel had devised the shrouds. [314] Her first decree as Speaker: at the storm siren, every mirror on Ferrante would be covered in black felt, and the past would be left to itself so that the future might get a word in. [350] She had believed a civilization could be reimagined by what it refused to look at. [365] She had even written the shroud-verse that children still recited while the felt went up, four lines about a river that does not run backward. [390] It was the only poem of hers anyone remembered, and it was a lie; on Ferrante the river ran wherever the dust told it to. [415]

The siren sounded on the third night of the dispute. [425] Oriel went down to the ballroom with the shard in her pocket and a pair of shears. [442] The great mirror behind the musicians' gallery was already shrouded; the cooperative's custodians were nothing if not obedient to a dead republic's habits. [465] She cut the felt from top to bottom and let it fall. [477] The dust came in golden and thick, and the mirror woke. [488]

She saw the ballroom in its last hour: chandeliers, gilt, a hundred frightened people in brocade. [504] She saw the Duchess, smaller than legend, standing by a pedestal with a crystal vase full of nothing. [522] She saw herself come through the doors, forty and furious and so certain. [535] And then the floor lurched, because down in the ring her engineers had cut the palace gravity to end the fighting, and the vase simply lifted from its pedestal, turned once in the air like something deciding, and came down when the field caught. [579] Nobody threw it. [582] Nobody saved it. [585] The Duchess and the revolutionary both looked at the pieces, and for one second wore the same face. [603]

Oriel stood a long time. [608] Then she turned and found Teodor and Mira in the doorway, called by the custodians, both of them staring past her into the glass. [632] "Neither of you gets your monster," she said. [640] "Write that down." [643]

She did not put the felt back up. [651] Instead she went to the Speaker's old rooms, where the pens still worked, and began to devise something she should have devised at forty: not a decree but an invitation, a new verse for the siren. [687] Come to the mirrors when the dust rises, it said. [697] Stand together and look. [701] Nothing there is yours alone. [706]

Whether a shipping cooperative could become a people by watching itself fall, she did not know. [722] But she had tried the other way, and the other way had made a world that could argue for three days over a thumbnail of crystal and never once ask what the thing had held. [757] She set the shard on the desk where the gold light could find it. [771] It had held nothing, and it shone anyway. [779]

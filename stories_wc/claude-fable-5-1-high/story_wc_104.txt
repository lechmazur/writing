The arrow came over the wall an hour after curfew and buried itself in the dust of what had once been Ines Varga's orchard. [24] Its fletching was not feather but braided corn husk, pale and dry, a thing no one east of the wall had touched in eleven years. [49] She knew her brother's work when she saw it. [58] Wound beneath the husk was a strip of paper with four words: Dawn. They burn first. [74]

Everyone had heard the crowds massing at the checkpoints since noon, the singing, the rumor that the Directorate's own soldiers were setting down their rifles. [99] The wall was coming down. [104] But a government that had spent a decade outlawing every seed except its patented, thirsty grain would not leave the evidence of its failure for the west to find. [133] Ines pulled on her coat and walked toward the carnival. [143]

The fairground had died with the river. [150] The Ferris wheel stood rusted mid-turn, and the prize redemption center squatted at the edge of the midway, its shutters painted with faded clowns, its shelves still lined with plush bears no child had ever claimed. [186] Old Teodor had chosen it precisely because it was absurd. [196] Inspectors searched barns and cellars for contraband seed; nobody searched a room full of unwon toys. [212] For nine years he had run the vault from here, and for nine years Ines had been his only courier, until the night they took him. [238] He had never told her where, exactly, the seeds were. [248] "If you don't know, you can't be made to say," he had told her, and then, tapping the lid of the bench beneath the dead upright piano in the corner: "But I keep my music in order." [285]

She lifted the lid now by lantern light. [293] No sheet music. [296] Instead, twenty-two small brass keys lay on the felt, not in a pile but placed, some on their sides, some upright, spaced across the bench like notes across a staff. [326] Ines had taught piano before the drought closed the school. [336] She saw it at once: the bench was the width of the keyboard above it, and each key sat beneath a particular note. [359] The upright keys were the melody; the ones on their sides were rests. [372] She hummed it and laughed aloud in the dark, because it was the carnival's own tune, the one the calliope had played every summer of her childhood. [399]

The prize bins were numbered one through eighty-eight, like the keys of a piano. [413] Bin by bin, following the melody, she pried up false bottoms and found them: sealed jars of beans, sorghum, amaranth, a squash her grandmother had grown, each labeled in Teodor's careful hand. [445] Drought seeds, all of them, bred over generations for exactly the kind of soil the Directorate had made. [463]

She heard the trucks at a quarter to five. [472] There was no time to carry two hundred jars anywhere, and she had known that walking in. [489] What she had instead was a plan measured in minutes. [499] The center had one door, steel, and a length of chain that had once kept teenagers from stealing bears after closing. [520] She ran it through the handle, around her own waist, and through the piano's iron frame, and snapped the padlock shut with the only key she had not used. [549] Then she swallowed it. [553]

The soldiers found the door blocked by a piano and a woman. [565] They could have cut her out in twenty minutes with the right tools, but they had brought gasoline, not bolt cutters, and even tonight they could not burn a building with a woman in it. [600] The lieutenant radioed for cutters. [605] Ines had counted on the radio. [611] She had counted on the forty minutes it would take, on the young men's hands shaking, on the singing from the checkpoints that grew louder with every minute they waited. [641] She did not argue with them. [647] She sat on the bench, chained, and played the carnival tune on the dead piano, which made no sound at all, and hummed it for them instead. [674]

At ten past six the sound came from the direction of the wall, a long groan of concrete and then a roar that was not machines but people. [702] The lieutenant looked at the gasoline cans, then at her, and set his radio on the counter among the bears. [722] "We were never here," he said, and his men followed him out into the gray light. [738]

Her brother found her an hour later, coming through the breach with half the western town behind him, and laughed until he wept at the chain, the padlock, and the key she confessed was inside her. [774] By noon she knelt in the dead orchard, pressing amaranth into dust, planting the one thing the Directorate never could: something that stayed. [797]

The Ember Nexus at twilight was the only hour Ines trusted her eyes. [13] Six furnace roads met beneath the iron dome, and their coals drifted upward in slow orange spirals that the dusk turned nearly violet. [36] In that half-light she could judge a pocket sun honestly, without the day flattering it or the night begging it to be more than it was. [62] She set the newest one on the bench between herself and Tovin. [74] It was a glass bead the size of a plum, and inside it a fused disc of phosphor and copper salts burned white enough to read by. [101] "Brighter," Tovin said. [104] "Brighter," she agreed, and heard how little the word meant. [114]

For two years she had been trying to make a sun that did more than illuminate. [130] She wanted one that carried the density of presence, the particular weight of a room when someone you love is actually in it, so that a miner alone in a shaft or a widow alone at supper could hold the thing and feel accompanied. [174] Her notebooks called it a measurable problem. [181] Her heart called it the only problem. [188]

Tovin, who mended the dome's flues and had no patience for either notebooks or hearts, dug in his coat and produced a curl of pale shell no longer than his thumb. [219] "Found it in the ash sluice," he said. [227] "Somebody's whole nautilus went through the fires and this is what came out the other side." [243]

It was the columella, the central axis the animal had wound itself around for its entire life. [260] Every outer chamber had burned away, but the spiral core remained, and Ines turned it under the bead's white glare. [280] The animal had never gone anywhere new. [287] It had come back to the same center again and again, each return a little wider, a little differently pitched, until the returning itself became the shape. [314]

"I have been adding," she said slowly. [321] "More phosphor, more salt, more heat, as if presence were a quantity." [333]

Tovin shrugged, but he stayed. [338]

She cracked the new bead with a tap of her hammer, scraped the white disc into her crucible, and instead of feeding it, she cooled it and began again from the base formula she had abandoned eighteen months ago. [377] Same center. [379] Different return. [381] She had no plan for the second pass, only a direction, and she let her hands choose within it: a pinch of cobalt for the blue that had failed in the spring, a thread of iron for the rust-red of the autumn attempt, the sulfur yellow she had thrown out as childish. [433] Each layer had been a failure when it stood alone. [443] Wound around a common axis, they stopped being failures and became a record of every time she had refused to quit. [464] A palette of resolve, she thought, and did not write it down, because the coals were drifting and the light was right and there was no time to be anything but here. [496]

She sealed the bead and it glowed, not white, but a layered amber that shifted as she turned it, blue underneath red underneath gold. [520] Dimmer than the last. [524] Warmer. [525] But it was still only a lamp. [532]

Tovin leaned in to look, and his shoulder touched hers, and for no reason she could name he spoke very low. [553] "I kept the shell for you because I keep everything for you. [565] Been doing it for years." [570]

It was a secret, whispered between friends, the kind that changes the word friend. [584] And in the exact moment he said it, the bead changed. [595]

Ines saw it with an alchemist's eye before she felt it with anything else. [609] The layered glow thickened. [613] The colors did not brighten; they drew closer together, as though the space between them had acquired mass. [631] The bench, the drifting embers, the violet dome, all of it seemed to lean toward the small light the way iron leans toward a magnet. [656]

She understood, then, why every measurement had failed. [664] Presence was not a property of the light. [672] It was a property of the light being witnessed, by two people at once, with something at stake. [690] The bead had merely been waiting for a room worth recording. [701]

She would have to revalue everything. [707] Two years of discarded beads were not ruined; they were empty, which was a different thing. [723] She could return to each of them the way the nautilus returned to its axis, carrying someone with her this time, and let them fill. [748]

"Say it again," she said, not looking up from the bead, her pencil already moving. [763] "For the data." [766]

Tovin laughed, and the glow thickened again, and she wrote that down too. [779]

[45] Pipes leaked, glass panes cracked, and vines crawled through old machinery. [56] The city beyond had banned fresh food that carried unapproved memories. [67] Mira kept cooking anyway, a gentle rebellion served in bowls. [77] She was informally wise, the kind of cook who taught by handing you a spoon. [222] She tasted the sap and remembered her mother's empty chair. [232] She pruned that memory too, not to erase it but to shape it. [245] Through synaptic plasticity, her brain rewired the grief into a root. [256] The method was simple: every pruning changed the connections between neurons. [566] Her shears clicked like a small bell in the silver light. [577] She was making something beautiful from the ruins. [585] It was a gentle rebellion, planted one seed and one memory at a time.

[104] Ines has spent years translating cease-fires, evacuation lists, and apologies that arrived too late. [118] She has learned that shared anxieties travel faster than any dialect. [161] The brain coral fragment in her palm is rough, porous, and strangely warm. [174] She carries it because it looks like a map of everyone she could not save.

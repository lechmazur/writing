<story>In the forgotten orchard deep in a bamboo forest, a wistful loom spinner named Mirei tended a loom strung from root fibers and moonlight. [24] She spun threads of near-silence into cloth that remembered voices no one else could hear. [39] The grove had been abandoned so long that even the bamboo had forgotten its own name. [55] Mirei's only companion was a tree sap amber bead, warm as a secret and stoically sparkling on the loom's crossbeam. [101] The message could not travel by road, river, or wing, because the sister's country no longer existed in any map. [121] So Mirei had to send it through the hush of time's unraveling tapestry. [187] The old loom needed a pattern no ordinary thread could follow. [198] In a rummage sale box beneath her cot, she kept a heap of jigsaw puzzle edges. [214] They had belonged to a picture of a bridge that no one had ever finished. [229] Mirei chose edges that curved like horizons and notches that remembered another piece's absence. [243] She fitted them into the loom's warp, making a path where no path should be. [258] Each edge clicked against the amber bead with a sound like a distant key. [272] The raveling silence paused, curious, and began to unpick the puzzle one seam at a time. [288] Mirei spun faster, though her fingers were already bleeding thread. [298] She was wistful not for rescue, but for one more ordinary afternoon with her sister. [313] The bead stayed stoically sparkling, as if sacrifice were only another kind of light. [327] To carry the laugh across impossible distance, Mirei would have to knot her own voice into the final thread. [346] That was the latent sacrifice hidden in every loom: the maker disappears into the making. [361] Through the hush of time's unraveling tapestry, she saw her sister waiting on a platform of fog. [378] The sister could not wave, but the laugh in the bead flickered like a firefly. [486] The raveling silence reached the loom and began to eat the final edge. [499] Mirei tied the amber bead into the cloth and let her voice go with it.

<story>At twilight, when the old viaduct bloomed with wisteria and its arches held the river's blue breath, Mira opened her camera obscura to the fog. [25] She was an artist of reflected things, spiritually curious but wary of temples, and she had come to find where unused love is stored. [49] Her mother had died with decades of tenderness unspoken, leaving Mira convinced that affection neither perished nor vanished but waited in some hidden reservoir. [73] In the darkened arch, a pinhole projected the inverted viaduct onto linen: flowers below, river above, pilgrims walking upside down through silver cloud. [96] At the lens sat Kwan Yin's tear, a drop of green glass her mother had called compassion made visible. [115] Mira used it because it softened harsh outlines, not because she believed the bodhisattva had wept for anyone like her. [135] That evening her brother Luc climbed the wet steps, carrying their mother's unposted letters and the anger of three avoided years. [156] Before he could accuse her, the tear warmed, and Mira's breath stopped so sharply that the projection filled with sparks. [176] A past life memory surfaced: she had been a pilgrim of sparks on this same crossing, bearing coals from a ruined shrine toward a city without fire. [203] Beside her had walked a gentle companion whose name was also Luc, though then it had meant little more than light returning. [225] When winter sickened him, she had guarded the sacred coals instead of kindling a fire to warm him, mistaking obedience for love. [247] He died before dawn, and she carried the untouched sparks onward, receiving honor while something inside her remained forever cold. [267] The memory released her breath in a ragged sob; Luc caught her shoulders, and the camera kept turning their distress into light. [289] On the linen, every inhalation thinned the fog, while every withheld breath made dark flowers gather around their projected hearts. [309] The tear was revealing through breathing changes what words had concealed, and Mira understood that Luc was not only her brother but the companion returned to ask a warmer answer. [339] His anger slowed as he watched the shadowy blooms vanish whenever he breathed freely. [353] "I do not need you to explain the past," he said, "only to stop leaving me outside it." [371] Mira had imagined healing as a door opening onto her mother, but now she saw it as tending a fire already present. [393] She took from her pocket the final letter, which she had never dared to read, and Luc held the lamp while she broke its seal. [418] Their mother had written only that love unused becomes heavy, and that Mira should give hers away before learning to carry it gracefully. [441] The words could have been another punishment, yet Luc's breathing stayed open, and his projected chest shone like a clearing hearth. [462] Mira read the letter aloud, then confessed that she had fled after the funeral because grief made every tenderness feel like theft. [484] Luc answered that he had wanted not her penitence but her company at breakfast, in quarrels, and through the ordinary days. [505] They did not embrace at once; first they breathed together, awkwardly matching four counts until the camera's dark garden loosened. [525] Beyond the arch, the viaduct bells rang for ferries unseen in fog, and Mira lifted Kwan Yin's tear from the lens. [546] Without it, the projection became common twilight, yet their breathing still revealed each place where fear narrowed the light. [565] She gave Luc the tear, not as pardon or inheritance, but as a lens through which he might demand honesty whenever she vanished again. [589] He gave her one of the sparks from the projected memory, which had settled on the linen as a real, warm cinder. [611] Together they carried it onto the blooming viaduct and lit a small brazier where chilled travelers could warm their hands. [631] Mira finally understood that unused love was stored nowhere secret; it waited in the lungs, the hands, and the next unguarded act. [653] The past life did not excuse her, nor did forgiveness finish their work, but healing began as a practice of remaining. [674] When fog erased the far bank, Luc laughed at some small mistake in her breathing, and she laughed too, without losing the thread. [697] Around them, sparks rose briefly above the flowers, pilgrims for no shrine but the living world. [713]</story>

<story>At twilight, the ember nexus beat beneath the cathedral like a heart refusing either to die or to live. [19] Orin stood before its crystal grate, a hopeless alchemist with solder on his cuffs and ninety failures chalked on his sleeve. [40] He was stoically sensitive, too disciplined to tremble, yet able to hear grief in every cooling coal. [57] That gift had made him a poor alchemist and would, at midnight, make him a perfect keeper. [74] The office was hereditary: one life surrendered to preserve the city forever in its loveliest moment. [90] Beyond the grate, roses hung in frozen bloom, children hovered between strides, and no coffin had been closed for eighty years. [111] Orin called this beauty, because the bishops required the word, but he had come to interrupt a stifling destiny. [130] His instrument lay across his palms, a broken bishop's crozier, its silver shepherd's hook snapped above the socket. [148] Whole, it would pour his pulse into the nexus and seal the everlasting twilight. [162] Broken, it gave him one final hour to attempt what his father had died concealing. [177] Orin told the watching canons he would repair it, then set three crucibles beside the grate. [193] He would proceed through successive approximation, since no single formula could survive contact with the nexus. [209] The first silver joint sang too high and froze his basin. [220] The second, softened with ash, held for six breaths, then bled black sparks. [233] Orin recorded each wound without complaint, listening past the metal's shriek for the quieter note beneath. [249] It sounded like his father's last breath, and like rain on ground he had never seen. [265] For the third joint he added a penny from the poor box, copper worn almost featureless by begging thumbs. [284] The coin slipped through his tweezers and fell into the nexus. [295] When the penny dropped, a hidden chamber opened beneath the grate. [306] Inside lay his father's notebook and a seed no larger than a tear. [319] Its first page read, "Preservation is only fear with excellent manners." [330] The next described creative destruction: break the vessel that contains a possibility, and spend its stored power to vivify what follows. [351] At last Orin understood why his father had snapped the crozier rather than complete the rite. [367] The nexus was not the city's heart but its seedcase, and keepers existed to prevent spring. [383] Behind him, the canons drew their knives as midnight began its climb. [395] Orin fitted the third joint to the crozier. [403] It bent immediately, not quite right, and he laughed once at the familiar failure. [417] Then he stopped trying to make the old design whole. [427] He hammered the crozier's hook straight, wrapped it around the seed, and drove the pointed foot between the crystal ribs. [447] The first blow only starred the grate. [454] The second loosened an ember that burned his hand. [463] He welcomed the pain as evidence that change could still reach him, and struck a third time. [480] The nexus opened with the sound of a glacier flowering. [490] Light rushed through the imperfect joint, and Orin whispered, "Vivify." [500] The seed drank eighty years of suspended endings. [508] Roses finished opening and shed their petals. [515] The children landed running. [519] Bells sagged from their towers, mourners finally wept, and every clock in the city shattered as night arrived. [537] The canons seized Orin, but their knives had already begun to rust. [549] He offered no resistance, only watched the ember rain cool into soil around his shoes. [564] His alchemy had ruined the nexus, broken his inheritance, and left him without a profession. [579] When a green thread pushed through the ash, his face remained grave, though he carefully moved his foot to shelter it. [600] By dawn, the canons had released him, for the crowd outside was too busy naming unfamiliar stars to notice their prisoner. [621] Orin placed his father's notebook in the poor box, its blank final pages open for other hands. [638] He would fill them with failures, each closer to a world that did not need preserving. [654] Above him, the last fixed rose fell, and beneath its petals the morning looked breakable, brief, and alive. [672]</story>

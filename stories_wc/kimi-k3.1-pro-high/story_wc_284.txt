<story>At the tilt of the earth toward spring, mist kept Zephyr Bluff in a silver hush. [16] Mara Venn climbed through it with a burned face, a dead name, and one order left that she trusted. [35] Once a spy for the coastal ministry, she had been exposed by her own controller and left in a cellar fire while he purchased safety with her network. [63] The scars had taken her eyebrows and her old ease with mirrors, but not her habit of counting exits. [82] Now three shepherd children were missing beneath the bluff, and the ministry's rescue launch would not risk the fog. [101] Their brother, Tam, waited beside the signal hut, trying not to look at her scars. [116] He was fifteen, all elbows and apology, holding an engraved pillbox that had belonged to his grandmother. [133] "She said courage waits in what we keep for later," he told Mara. [146] Inside lay no medicine, only a strip of rice paper punched with tiny holes. [160] Mara recognized a field cipher older than her betrayal. [169] "Your grandmother was my trainer," she said. [176] "She vouchsafed me one final key before she died: never confuse secrecy with silence." [190] The punched strip named an abandoned ice house under the eastern stair, where smugglers once hid fugitives. [207] It also warned that the lower door could jam after thaw. [218] Mara understood then why the children had vanished while searching for a stray lamb, and why no answer had come to Tam's shouts. [241] The prudent choice was to wait for clearer weather. [250] The daring choice was to descend on a rope before the thaw flooded the ice house. [266] Mara chose both, prudently daring the fog rather than defying it. [277] She checked Tam's knots, anchored a second line, and set lantern markers along the cliff. [292] Then she took the pillbox, because its silver lid could flash signals where voices failed. [307] At the stair, accumulated snow had sealed the upper lintel in sculpted drifts. [320] Someone had written there without ink, pinching letters into the crust and dots beneath them. [335] The codes in accumulated snow repeated her grandmother's old lesson, then added fresh bearings. [349] Tam read them with a shaking finger. [356] "My sister knows this cipher." [361] "She left us a map." [366] Hope altered his face so completely that Mara had to look away. [378] They followed the bearings down, roped together, while mist erased the world above each step. [393] At the ice house, meltwater chuckled under the door. [402] The lower hinge had swollen, just as the warning promised. [412] Inside, three voices answered Mara's knock, thin but furious with life. [423] She could force the door with her shoulder, but the impact might drop the lintel. [438] Instead, she studied the broken hinge and felt an old, useless anger rise. [451] For years she had wanted to repair herself by ruining the man who ruined her. [466] That wish had only kept the cellar burning. [474] Fixing what was broken, she saw, began with the next frightened thing within reach. [488] She took Tam's knife, slid its narrow blade into the hinge, and used the pillbox as a patient hammer. [507] Tap, turn, breathe. [510] Tap, turn, breathe, until the pin lifted and the door sighed open. [522] The children emerged carrying the lamb, colder than fear and louder than bells. [535] Tam hauled them up while Mara held the lines, her scarred palms bleeding inside her gloves. [551] At the summit, the launch crew arrived, shamed into courage by their lanterns. [564] No one cheered. [567] The hush stayed silver, filled with rope creak, lamb breath, and the small work of wrapping blankets. [584] Tam returned the dented pillbox. [589] "Grandmother said later never comes unless you open it." [598] Mara looked toward the ministry roofs below, where her controller still enjoyed his purchased mornings. [613] She could go down there with proof in the ice house ledger and finish the old story. [630] Not tonight. [632] Tonight she would teach Tam the cipher, report the smugglers' tunnel, and ask the launch crew to stand witness. [651] Justice, like rescue, needed more than one pair of hands. [661] She opened the pillbox and found, beneath its loose lining, a single winter seed. [675] Mara set it in Tam's palm and closed his fingers around it. [687] "Courage does not wait in boxes," she said. [695] "It waits where we decide to begin." [702] Below them, the mist thinned, and the first green seam of the bluff answered the light. [718]</story>

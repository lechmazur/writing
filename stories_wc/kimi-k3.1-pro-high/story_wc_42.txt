<story>Above the clouds, the lunar observatory kept its silver eye upon the moon, while its last camera repairer, Old Sera, kept her ears on the machines. [26]

She touched the brass housing where her dead wife, Anja, had engraved their first date of observation. [183]

Now preservation demanded undoing. [213]

<story>On the echoing terrace in spring, rain withdrew from the city like a held breath, leaving the prism parapet after rain to scatter dusk into patient colors. [27]

At the bells, that reflection would become instruction, and another year would close around them like a fist. [129]

"If you fail," she had whispered, "return it before I forget how waking feels." [212]

Under the soft hush of a distant morning, Elian climbed the last stair into the frozen library above the clouds. [20] His breath silvered the door, where hoarfrost had sealed every letter of his name. [34] He was a lunar-eclipse locksmith, called when ordinary keys failed and moon-shadow revealed the locks hidden inside broken things. [53] Tonight the eclipse was dying, and so was the library. [63] Beneath its floor, the engine that held the shelves aloft had missed six beats. [77] One more would drop books and memories into the white sea below. [89]

Elian had come to learn the rhythm of repair, a phrase his mother had used while mending clocks beside their driftwood fire. [111] As a boy, he thought she meant patience. [119] Then he forced a stubborn lock here and froze the library with her inside. [133] Now her clockmaker's gear assortment hung at his hip, a pouch of brass teeth and tiny wheels, each worn smooth by her fingers. [156]

In the library's eastern corner, behind a shelf of cloud atlases, he found the scraps of prophecy her last note had promised. [178] Their edges were scorched, and their ink shifted whenever he lied to himself. [191] The first read, "Do not seek the door you broke." [201] The second read, "A mirror keeps no face, but gives every face a way home." [216] Together, they pointed toward the hall of mirrors beyond the reading room. [228]

He entered and stopped. [232] The eclipse shining through the glass roof made the frozen hall hauntingly bright. [245] Every mirror held a different morning, pale gold above his childhood coast, where wrecked ships became tables and repair was a kind of tide. [269] In the nearest, his mother still swept brass filings from her sleeves. [281] Elian touched the glass. [285]

"You always arrive with both hands clenched," she said. [294]

"And you always speak as if I have time." [303]

Her reflection glanced at the pouch. [309] "Repair is not making the past continue." [316] "It is listening until what remains can continue without you." [326]

The nearest mirror clouded, revealing a narrow door in its frame. [337] Its lock had no keyhole, only seven hollows shaped like gear teeth. [349] Elian opened his mother's assortment and understood why none of its wheels matched. [362] The prophecy scraps curled around his wrist, and a third line appeared. [374] Give the silence something kinder than your answer. [382]

Beyond the door, the engine struck once, a groan that shivered every reflection. [395] The next beat would be the last. [402] His old habit surged: choose the largest gear, force a fit, and call surrender a fault. [418] Instead, he set the assortment on the floor and listened. [428]

He heard frost ticking in the hinges, the mirrors breathing against their silver backs, and below them the engine's wounded pause. [449] It was not a missing beat, but a longer one asking to be joined. [463] Elian chose seven mismatched gears and laid them loosely in the hollows, leaving space for the mechanism to refuse him. [483]

Then he drew from his coat the vial of eclipse oil his mother had brewed from salt, cedar, and moonlight. [503] Its scent carried their old workshop back: driftwood smoke, winter oranges, her warm thumb against his wrist. [520] He almost could not uncap it. [526] Once opened, the last thing she had made would be gone. [537]

"That is what keeping her costs," he whispered. [545]

The mirrors answered with the soft click of a thousand unclenching hands. [557] He uncapped the vial and let one drop fall. [566] Instead of striking the gears into place, he turned them with the pause: slow, quick, then slower still. [584]

The lock opened like a mouth finally allowed to finish its sentence. [596] Warmth moved through the floor. [601] Beyond the door, the engine did not resume its former march; it found a swaying measure, and the library rose gently on it. [624] Frost ran from the books in bright, harmless rain. [633]

Every mirror emptied except one. [638] There his mother stood beside an open window, no longer waiting for him to restore her. [654] She lifted her hand, and he lifted his, matching the rhythm until the glass between them became only morning air. [674]

When Elian left, the library drifted above a sea of clouds, its shelves ticking like weathered houses settling around a fire. [695] At his hip, the lighter pouch swung, empty of certainty but full of time. [709]

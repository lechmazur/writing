The miracle occurred within the fold of a daydream, while Elian Voss sat waiting for a kettle that had forgotten to boil. [22]
Outside his window, the ruined orchard district sagged beneath centuries of thick fog, its apple trees reduced to black gestures and its walls to damp suggestions. [48]
Elian was a mural artist of famously vivid spirit, though lately his colors had become as courteous and gray as funerals. [69]
He had returned to rediscover lost joy, or at least to remember why he had once laughed while painting. [88]

On his table lay a fresco fragment, the last piece of his mother's orchard mural, showing one yellow sleeve and half an ambiguous smile. [112]
Beside it curled a spool of exposed film, found that morning in the district's camera house. [128]
Whoever opened its tin had let daylight devour every image, leaving a glossy brown ribbon of absolutely nothing. [146]
Elian had wept over it, then felt embarrassed, then laughed at himself for weeping, which was the closest he had come to joy in months. [171]

The kettle clicked. [174]
In that small silence, he imagined the fog as a vast photographic darkroom and himself painting with light instead of pigment. [195]
The notion was absurd enough to be useful. [203]
He carried the fragment and film to the old cider barn, where his knowledge network waited: twelve neighbors linked by bells, wire, stubborn affection, and a shared ledger of everything each knew. [235]
Mara understood lenses, Old Pell remembered songs, the twins mixed chemistry, and even dour Sef could tell which wall would fall if leaned upon. [259]
Elian pinned the blank film to the ledger. [267]
"I need to display a picture none of us possesses," he said. [279]
"Then we shall have to be several kinds of liar," said Mara, and the bells shivered with laughter. [297]

Their plan grew like a grafted tree. [304]
Pell supplied the melody Elian's mother had hummed while painting; the tune returned her wrist's rhythm to his hand. [323]
The twins derived a harmless silver wash from the camera house notes, making the exposed film sensitive not to images, but to pulses of heat. [348]
Sef identified a wall strong enough to bear the display, while Mara ground mica from the fresco fragment's outer skin, preserving its painted sleeve beneath glass. [374]
Across the network, each person added one memory of pleasure: stolen apples, a kiss behind a press, a mayor falling into jelly. [396]
None matched another exactly, which everyone agreed was evidence of their truth. [408]

By evening, they had built a lantern around the fragment. [418]

The mural was not on the plaster but in the fog before it, trembling, temporary, and visibly breathing. [499]
Neighbors opened shutters along the ruined rows. [506]

But Pell kept humming, and Mara struck the wall in time, and soon the others beat barrels, shutters, and knees. [574]

"Very well," he said, "tomorrow we make it less permanent." [606]

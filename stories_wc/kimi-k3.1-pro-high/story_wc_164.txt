<story>Inside the giant crystal geode, the iridescent cave glowed like a rainbow remembering a bruise. [15] Mira, a sea-foam recollectionist, considered this a promising morning. [24] She always said so when the light was dying. [33] By sunset, the geode would turn toward the dark, and every unwritten memory would fade. [48] Until then, Mira gathered recollections from the foam of her own body and pinned them beneath a lens of crater glass. [69] The glass, born when a star struck the sea, made feelings legible. [81] Through it, she transliterated emotions into colored script: joy in gold, fear in green, sorrow in a blue that hummed. [101] A recollection was useless, she insisted, if you kept only its pleasant spelling. [114] Yet the cave's paths were disappearing. [120] Each luminous trail led to a chamber where someone had stored a life, and each morning another trail vanished without a crack or sound. [144] Worse, the memories at their ends went too. [152] Mira had come to discover why some paths disappear before her own dearest recollection joined them. [168] It belonged to Ebb, the old recollectionist who had raised her from a bucket of spume. [184] Already Mira had forgotten Ebb's nose. [190] By breakfast, she had mislaid the laugh. [197] This seemed excessively rude of the universe, so she packed biscuits. [208]

At the last fork, she found a missing piece in every memory she carried. [222] Under the crater glass, each gap had the same shape: a narrow curve bending toward the geode's heart. [240] A conjecture brightened her like phosphorus. [246] Perhaps the paths were not being erased; perhaps they were being covered, and their memories were leaking through in fragments. [266] She could test that, provided she found a route through crystal harder than spite. [280]

A whistle descended from the ceiling. [286] A stormswallow, migrating late to the southern cliffs, dropped through a fissure and landed in her hair. [303] It shook itself, scattering seeds across the floor. [311] Where each seed struck, a tiny root of white light slipped into the crystal. [325] The bird pecked Mira's ear and flew on. [333] More seeds fell, not randomly, but in a curving line. [343] Mira whooped, apologized to the solemn cave, and followed. [352] The scattered seeds dropped by the migrating bird glowed wherever her lens showed a gap, as if roots remembered what crystal concealed. [374] She marked each one with blue foam, building a script of sorrow along the floor. [389] It spelled a direction. [393]

At sunset, the trail ended at a wall. [401] Behind it, faint as a ship beneath ice, Ebb laughed. [411] Mira pressed the crater glass to the wall and saw thousands of paths sealed under clear new growth. [429] Each seal was made of the same blue script, but blank where sorrow should have been. [445] Then she understood her part in the catastrophe. [453] Recollectionists had rescued joy, courage, and tenderness, leaving grief unnamed because clients paid poorly for it. [469] The abandoned grief had hardened around the paths, hiding them and starving the memories within. [484] Ebb had warned her, naturally, in a sentence Mira had preserved as gold and omitted as blue. [501]

"A memory without its missing pieces is just flattery with luggage," Mira whispered. [514] She could almost hear Ebb adding, "Pack accordingly." [522]

The light narrowed. [525] Mira placed her foam palms against the wall and transliterated what she had refused to feel: Ebb was dead, Mira was alone, and love would not cancel either fact. [554] Blue letters flooded the crystal, joined the seed-script, and completed every blank. [566] The wall did not break. [571] It simply remembered being a door. [577]

Beyond it, lost chambers opened one by one, returning names, recipes, crimes, and lullabies to the paths. [594] Mira stepped into Ebb's chamber just as the geode turned dark. [605] The recollection there was no longer whole, and she did not pretend otherwise. [618] The nose was gone; several arguments had holes; one entire Tuesday remained suspiciously blank. [632] But the laugh survived, including the snort Ebb had hated. [642] Mira laughed it back, snort included. [648]

Outside, the stormswallow called once and continued south, while seedlings glowed behind it like a new constellation with dirt on its hands. [671] Mira gathered the missing pieces she could find and left gentle markers around those she could not. [688] There would be work tomorrow, grief included, which was almost cheerful news. [700] She ate a biscuit in the pleasant dark and waited for the cave to remember dawn. [716]</story>

<story>At dusk, the amber grotto warmed from gold to garnet, and Eda arrived with her case of counterfeit feelings. [19] She was a dealer in exact imitations: courage without risk, tenderness without attachment, grief without the inconvenience of love. [38] Her clients paid well because her imitations worked, and because she never pretended they were anything more. [55] Tonight, however, her professional thoroughness felt like a hand tightening around her wrist. [68] On the resin altar lay Sorel, the fragrance chemist who had commissioned her rarest product, an emotion with no name. [88] Amber had already sealed his feet and was climbing his ribs in bright, deliberate veins. [103] When it closed over his heart, he would become the grotto's keeper, able to draw poisonous sorrow from the miners above. [124] The old keeper had cracked beneath that burden, and Sorel had volunteered to replace him. [139] Or so he had told Eda when he bought enough counterfeit peace to survive the change. [155] Being professionally thorough, she had brought the fragrance chemist's blotter petal, a paper-thin receiver he used to test unfinished scents. [175] It could hold one feeling without disguising its source. [184] Eda set it against his wrist and felt the fine hairs on her arm rise. [199] Sensing through static electricity, she read the tiny charges of his skin as easily as other dealers read invoices. [218] Fear sparked blue behind her eyes; resolve hummed low and steady. [229] Beneath both waited a brittle, oscillating grief that did not belong in peaceful surrender. [243] "The peace is failing," Sorel said, apology softening his mouth. [253] "You sold me a half truth, though I suppose I ordered one." [265] Eda almost defended her work, but the petal darkened between them, releasing the scent of rain on hot stone. [284] She knew that scent from the abandoned nursery above the grotto. [295] "Your daughter," she said. [299] Sorel looked toward the amber ceiling, where dusk had become a thin red line. [313] "Mina made perfume from minerals, and swore stones could remember being mountains." [325] "After she died, I wanted the keeper's power so no other father would mistake my silence for consent." [343] It was a noble reason, and still only half of one. [354] The static told Eda the rest: he hoped the amber would subsume his memory of Mina along with his grief. [374] He was not volunteering to serve; he was arranging a beautiful disappearance. [386] Eda had built her trade on such half truths, offering relief while leaving the wound unnamed. [402] Her insight had always been flawless, her empathy carefully optional. [412] She could inject more peace and complete the contract, or speak plainly and lose her reputation for painless miracles. [431] The amber reached his heart. [436] With a tenderness that surprised them both, Eda chose the more intimate professional risk. [450] "You cannot keep her by becoming a stone that forgets," she said. [462] "And I cannot give you peace by helping you vanish." [472] She pressed her palm to the petal, opening every charge she had learned to insulate. [487] His grief entered her like winter static, a thousand small strikes, each carrying Mina's laugh, her burnt sugar, her final unspoken goodbye. [509] Eda did not take it all. [515] That would have been another counterfeit kindness. [522] Instead she shared its weight until insight fused with empathy, and she could want his life more than his gratitude. [542] The petal flushed clear, holding not peace but sorrow warmed by recognition. [554] Sorel breathed it in, and the amber hesitated. [562] "If you remain keeper, you will remember her every time you heal someone," Eda said. [577] "If I leave, someone else must carry the miners' sorrow." [587] "Yes." [588] "Then let it be grief with company, not grief pretending to be peace." [601] He guided her hand to the altar's final groove, where keeper and witness were both required. [617] As the transformation completed, Eda's charge flowed beside him, not chaining him to service but promising he would never serve unheard. [638] The amber closed, clear enough to show his wet eyes and her fingers curled around his. [654] Above them, the miners began coughing less. [661] Eda stayed until full dark, her old affectionate detachment gone, though she would have called the vigil merely thorough. [680] When she finally left, she carried the blotter petal and a new label for her case: approximations, honestly made, companionship sold separately. [702]</story>

After the symphony ended, Mara Vale found Tomas securing the roof library's books against a breeze that smelled of kelp. [20] Beneath the stars, readers still lingered between weatherproof cupboards, reluctant to surrender their only quiet hour. [36] Mara brokered seaside cargo, extracting storage concessions from captains who considered themselves impossible to outwit. [51] Yet she drew little umbrellas beside vulnerable shipments and remembered which dockworkers needed instructions illustrated rather than written. [69] Tomas called this her peculiar tenderness; she preferred preventative accounting. [79]

Their friendship had begun six weeks earlier with an envelope bearing a mistaken address: forty instead of fourteen Lantern Quay. [99] Mara had carried it upstairs after the symphony, expecting an office rather than books open to the sky. [117] Inside was a rejected grant application. [123] She stayed to help Tomas weight the loose pages with stones, then returned nightly after the orchestra finished. [141] Here, the people who swept its stage and unloaded its instruments could read without buying anything. [157]

Tonight, Tomas was packing the stones away. [164] His savings were gone, and he would take night shifts gutting fish from Monday. [178] Without someone to supervise, the roof would close. [186] Downstairs, meanwhile, the harbor's merchants were drinking beneath a banner thanking them for their generosity. [201]

"I could make them notice you," Mara said. [209] She imagined a speech, names announced, the cleaners summoned into the light. [221] Tomas gently removed a beetle from an atlas before shutting it. [232]

"Most of us cannot afford another evening of unpaid applause." [242]

His careful tone hurt more than anger would have. [251] Mara opened her locket, buying herself a moment before answering. [261]

The locket photo showed her father wearing a coat the dockwives had bought when a falling crate shattered his leg. [281] She remembered their anonymous coins, and his insistence that nobody turn his survival into a spectacle. [297] Once he could sit upright, he had mended their children's shoes beside his bed. [311] Compassion had traveled around that room without anyone demanding it return to its starting place. [326]

[338] He wanted her company to sponsor the hall's evening activities, advertising the harbor as a place where culture prospered. [357] She had intended to negotiate a larger company crest on the programs. [369] Instead, beneath her proposed contribution, she drew an umbrella. [396] She could initiate the fund with the money reserved for her advertisement, but it would last only a month. [415]

Tomas unpacked his pencil, not his books. [432] Together they worked while a cleaner supplied tram fares and a stagehand corrected their estimate of the closing shift. [451] Mara asked both whether they wanted their names attached; both shook their heads. [464] By the time the chairman came upstairs looking for her signature, the figures had acquired the stubborn precision she usually reserved for freight disputes. [488]

[522] She offered her contribution if the merchants matched it and guaranteed three months before reviewing the accounts.

Six weeks after planting the wrong seeds, Ada learned that black willow roots could drink a reservoir dry before the next scheduled supply ship arrived. [25] In the gravitational anomaly called the Cradle, their roots rose overhead, following water along curves that looked, to homesick people, like sheltering arms. [48] The resemblance did not make them edible. [55]

Ada was the settlement's celestial signal interpreter, responsible for reading the star pulses that unlocked its ancient seed vault. [74] She had mistaken a conditional instruction for a promise, and two hundred people had sown what she confidently named. [93] Now their spoons scraped bowls with the small, patient sounds of animals trying doors. [107] Her daughter, Nell, stopped asking when the bread would come back. [118]

That silence drove Ada into the observatory beneath the reservoir, where the anomaly pressed yesterday against tomorrow until their seams shone. [139] She brought her teacher's dried-up fountain pen, its exotic nib divided into seven flexible rhenium tines, one for each visible branch of causation. [162] Ink scattered the light between those tines; dry, the pen could comb the shining seams. [177]

Her teacher had called the work editing history, although nothing so simple as erasure was permitted. [193] The past yielded only through contingent relationships: who trusted whom, which warning reached which listener, whether an offer met silence or an answer. [216] You could not replace a seed, but you could change the conversation that put it in a hand. [234]

Ada found the morning of the sowing and hooked the glimmer where her assistant had asked whether she wanted another reading. [255] She had said there was no need. [262] A turn of the pen opened a possible harvest: barley bending under grain, ovens breathing, Nell licking flour from her thumb. [283] Then the vision widened, and Ada saw the assistant taking her place at the receiver during a radiation storm. [302] Accepting his correction had led to sharing her duties; behind the golden fields stood his widow. [318]

Ada pulled back so sharply that one tine bent. [327] The Cradle offered no wicked bargain, only consequences, spreading beyond the narrow circle she had mistaken for understanding. [345] She sat beneath the suspended water and tried to unbend the tine against her thumbnail. [360]

Nell found her there, carrying two cups of hot water flavored with the same exhausted tea leaf. [377] Nell asked if it was fixed, watching the pen. [386]

"Enough to try," Ada said, and recognized her teacher's old discipline: be confidently uncertain, never carelessly sure. [403]

Nell set a cup beside her and mentioned the freighter whose captain had offered emergency rations just after the seedlings appeared. [424] Ada had refused permission to dock. [430] She had still believed she could save the crop, and accepting charity meant opening her records to the settlement council. [450] The freighter had since jumped far beyond any useful appeal. [460] But its offer remained in yesterday, attached to her refusal by a seam as fine as a hair. [478]

For years Ada had studied the heavens to thresh meaning from experience, believing meaning would be the clean grain left when mistakes blew away. [502] Yet Nell had brought two cups, not because her mother was right, but because she was cold. [519]

Ada steadied the repaired nib and touched the refusal. [528] This time she examined the branching possibilities until her eyes watered: ration queues, an inquiry, her own dismissal, the captain grumbling over delayed deliveries. [552] Farther consequences dissolved into light, as they always would. [561] She could promise neither innocence nor safety, but every branch she could read held enough food to reach the next supply ship. [583]

She changed her answer to yes. [589]

The observatory shivered, and Nell's cup acquired a greasy crescent where she had dipped her bread. [605] New memories arrived without displacing the old ones: the public confession, the cargo nets, her assistant quietly collecting her keys. [625] Above them, workers were cutting down the thirsty willows, leaving one beside the communal kitchen where its roots could shade the benches. [647]

Ada could wash dishes, and tomorrow she could ask her assistant whether he needed a second careful reader. [665] For now she put the dry pen away, accepted the bread Nell tore in half, and ate beneath the weight of water that did not fall. [691]

After the phoenix rose, its ashes settled over the glassblower's courtyard, whitening the pots that served as the town's silent alphabet. [21] Neri moved a fern beside a rosemary bush: I need to buy back a waking. [36]

Three winters ago, his sister Ada had bartered her remaining awakenings to pay for his apprenticeship as a painter. [55] She had expected him to buy them back before she missed the spring. [68] But patrons wanted convincing flesh, and Neri was colorblind; their healthy pinks and feverish greens looked alike to him. [87] Now he painted gold leaves over flawed bottles, each holding one sleeper's entire hoard of bartered awakenings. [104] He could make a crack resemble a vein in a leaf, but earned too little to ransom even one morning. [124]

Only on the day of the phoenix's rising could bottles be opened, and this year's auction began at noon. [143] Old Sella, the glassblower, placed a cactus between the fern and rosemary: You cannot afford it. [159] Nobody spoke in the courtyard, because a name uttered beside an open bottle claimed its contents. [175] Unlike the brokers' colored price labels, the arrangements spoke in shapes he could trust. [189] A wealthy man could wake from a sleepless night as rested as a child, while the original sleeper never stirred. [209] But an awakening released without a name went home. [218]

Neri touched the concertina reed tongue he carried in his pocket, all that remained of Ada's instrument after the pawnbroker stripped it. [240] Its crooked tip had given her music a tremble she loved and audiences sometimes called a fault. [257] Once, that wavering note had rattled every thin wineglass in their kitchen. [269] He had kept it for the same reason he painted broken jugs when nobody was paying: he wanted to find beauty in imperfection. [292]

He laid the reed beside the cactus and curved his hands around an imaginary pipe. [307] Sella shook her head; she had once made flutes, before the awakening trade paid better. [322] Neri added his remaining wages to the little arrangement, then shifted the rosemary toward her furnace. [338] It was an innocently cunning proposal: surely testing the bottles for weakness would spare her embarrassment at the auction. [357]

Her eyes followed his toward the shelves, where gilded leaves covered a whole forest of concealed fractures. [374] She had made those bottles too thin to save fuel, and he had helped her disguise them. [391] For a long moment she held his money, then pushed it back and carried the reed to her bench. [410] She set it inside a short glass mouthpiece without straightening its bent tip. [423]

Neri blew gently, and the note wavered through the courtyard. [433] A bottle answered with a thin, bright hum. [441] Then another answered, catching a different pitch within the trembling note. [452] Sella had left the reed imperfect for a reason: a steady tone would have found only one matching vessel. [471] Inside the glass, the captive mornings began singing back, each feeding the next until the shelves shook. [488]

At the gate, the auctioneer arrived carrying his ledger and the names of customers. [502] Neri saw his mouth open. [507] Sella hurled the cactus against the gate, and the man stumbled backward under a shower of earth and needles. [526] Neri blew harder. [529]

The first bottle split along a golden stem. [537] Light escaped without waiting for a purchaser, and the answering chorus cracked a dozen more. [552] Not every bottle broke, but enough dawns streamed over the roofs to make the phoenix turn in the distant sky. [572]

Neri ran home before the auctioneer recovered his breath, terrified that Ada's mornings had been housed in flawless glass. [591] He found her sitting upright, touching the dust on the windowsill. [602] He told her how long she had slept, and she wept for a while without letting go of his hand. [622] He could awaken her, but he could not return those winters. [633]

At sunset, Sella arrived with a sack of shards; the auctioneer had canceled every order and confiscated her stock. [652] Behind her came six newly wakened neighbors carrying bread, onions, and tools they had not touched in years. [670] Ada took the crooked reed from Neri and sounded it through Sella's mouthpiece, testing the unfamiliar strength of her breath. [690] The note faltered, recovered, and made everyone look up. While supper cooked, Neri began her portrait in charcoal, following the new hollows beneath her eyes instead of hiding them. [719] When darkness came, she fell asleep beside the drawing, and this time he knew she would wake hungry. [737]

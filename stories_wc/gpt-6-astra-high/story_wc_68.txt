After the plague ended, Ivo remained in the sewer beneath the sunken city, because rent above water had recovered faster than mercy. [22] He called himself a philosopher, though his neighbors preferred the man who unclogged their latrines without pretending it was somebody else's fault. [44] His father had taught him the ancient surveyors' methods: sounding lines, measured tides, and mud read slowly from bottom to top. [65]

Now the council planned to blast the drowned floodgates clear, burying both the evidence and the knowledge needed to build better ones. [87] Its proclamation said the gates had been opened deliberately to flush infection from the city. [102] Rebel broadsheets called the same flood a massacre of the poor. [113] Both accounts named his father, the dead gatekeeper, as the man who had obeyed. [127]

Mara, who printed the broadsheets, brought Ivo a seafarer's chronometer cog stolen from the naval storehouse. [143] It was the exact toothwheel his broken instrument lacked, and she knew what he intended to do with it. [162] "Prove they murdered my sister," she said, laying the brass on his dry sleeping brick. [177]

"I'll measure the gates," he said, "and you can dislike the measurements afterward." [190] He wanted to preserve the old methods, not because they were old, but because anyone with patience could check them. [210] A city rebuilt on another unquestionable story would drown again. [220]

With the cog fitted, the chronometer could carry reliable harbor time into the ruins, where neighboring channels turned at dangerously different minutes. [242] His father's tide tables gave them eleven minutes inside the gatehouse before the returning current sealed its only entrance. [261] Mara came because she trusted neither his affection nor his arithmetic. [272]

They crossed submerged rooftops by boat, passing a bell tower whose silent mouth was packed with oysters. [289] At the predicted ebb, Ivo crawled into the exposed inspection tunnel and drove a hollow sampling tube through sedimentary layers behind the gate. [312] He split the core lengthwise while Mara held their lamp. [322]

Below the flood's coarse sand lay alternating bands of summer silt and winter clay, seven years of them wedged inside the mechanism. [344] The emergency gate could not have closed against the storm surge, whatever orders the council gave. [360] Beside the winding wheel, annual inspection tags hung on wire, each stamped CLEAR and initialed by his father. [378]

He had expected to agonize over his father's orders, not over seven tidy lies stamped into tin. [395] The man who taught him to measure had apparently stopped measuring. [406]

"Leave those," Mara said, but quietly, offering him the mercy she wanted for her own story. [422]

"No," he said, taking the tags, "he doesn't get an exemption for being dead." [436] He could be rationally rebellious or merely loyal, and loyalty had already signed enough inspection forms. [452]

At the chronometer's ninth minute, Mara pulled him out, and he carried the dripping core against his chest like an injured child. [474] At the public landing, Mara asked what she was supposed to print now. [487]

"Seven years of neglected maintenance," Ivo said, "and my father's name, spelled correctly." [500]

"That isn't justice for my sister." [506]

"No," he said, "but a gate that actually shuts might keep someone else's sister alive." [521]

She looked ready to hit him, and he held still, refusing to turn her grief into an argument he could win. [542] Finally she asked him to draw the sampling tube. [551]

Her next broadsheet printed their findings beside instructions for testing them, leaving room beneath his father's name for names not yet known. [573] By morning, the dockworkers hired to lay the blasting charges were demanding another ebb tide and tubes of their own. [593] Most lived behind the proposed replacement gates, and none cared to trust a proclamation with their children's beds. [611] The council postponed the demolition, calling the delay a scheduled review, which Ivo judged a tolerable first retreat. [629]

That evening, six apprentices crowded his sewer, learning to read the chronometer, lower a line, and distinguish a deposit from an assumption. [651] When one called him a philosopher, Ivo wound the repaired chronometer and considered accepting the promotion. [667]

"Not yet," he said, handing over the line, "and don't round the depth down because the number looks ugly." [686]

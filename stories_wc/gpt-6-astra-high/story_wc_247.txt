When no one remembers you, the harbor opens beneath your feet, luminous by moonlight and crowded with strangers waiting for the last ferry. [23] Neri arrived with a vintage camera with no film, the one his daughter had left him when she died. [42] He had been a king's whispering advisor, quietly charismatic enough to prevent wars without ever raising his voice. [60]

Now he found himself obeying a notice nailed above the landing: NO TALKING DURING EMBARKATION. [75] The boatman rang his bell, and its vast iron voice flattened every murmur along the quay. [91] Between strokes, shoes scraped forward. [96] Beyond the ferry stretched a darkness nobody could explain. [105]

A girl beside Neri tugged his sleeve and signed, "Does he think being forgotten means we have nothing left to say?" [126] His hands answered before his courtly caution could stop them: "He probably thinks silence is dignified." [142]

"It is," she signed, "when you choose it." [150]

Her name was Iona; like his daughter, she was Deaf, and her impatient eyebrows made him ache with recognition. [169] His daughter had taught him sign language after years of watching him mistake her quiet for contentment. [186] Later, she would rest her palms against his piano while he played, correcting his solemn expression with extravagant grimaces. [205] He had promised to translate silence into music for her, a piece where the rests mattered as much as the notes. [226] He had kept postponing it, attending to kings. [234]

Neri lifted the camera, then lowered it. [241] There would be no photograph to save this girl from another departure, and nobody left ashore to keep it anyway. [261] But the shutter still worked. [266]

He set its dial to one second and began measuring the intervals between bell strokes, winding and releasing, listening. [285] Seven seconds, then four, then seven again: the boatman paused longer whenever he hauled the loading chain. [302] By mapping silence, Neri found the measure of a song. [312] He scratched the pattern into the salt crust on a bollard and showed Iona where voices could enter. [330]

She studied his marks, then pointed to the wooden landing and stamped twice. [343] The boards carried the rhythm through their soles. [351]

"Not just voices," she signed. [356]

He bowed his head in acknowledgment, the small, sincere gesture that had once made stubborn ministers listen. [373] Together they recruited the people nearest them, Neri whispering explanations and Iona demonstrating how to watch for her lifted hand. [393] They taught everyone the signs for "wait" and "together," practical words that could survive the bell. [409] A baker volunteered a humming bass; a woman who had once mended sails offered a bright, uncertain alto. [427] Neri gave them a melody simple enough to learn before courage failed. [439]

Then the boatman saw the gathering and pointed sharply at the notice. [451] Neri's hands almost dropped. [455] All his life he had made himself useful by preventing disruption, smoothing grief into something powerful men could comfortably ignore. [475]

Iona touched the camera and signed, "You cannot keep us, but you can be here." [490]

Neri put it down. [494] For once, he would disrupt the proceedings rather than advise everyone to endure them. [508]

The bell crashed; Iona's hands rose through the noise, holding their attention until the iron shudder faded. [525] Into the waiting seven seconds came a hum, a stamping pulse, and then the small, astonishing warmth of harmony. [544] At her signal they stopped together, leaving room for the next stroke instead of struggling against it. [561] The bell became part of what it had been meant to silence. [573]

People farther down the queue turned around, smiling through tears they had expected to shed alone. [589] The boatman missed his next stroke. [595] In that unexpected space, the alto laughed, and Iona laughed too, watching the woman's crumpled face. [611]

Neri did not imagine that his daughter had returned or that the darkness had become less dark. [628] He only wished he had learned sooner how much company a rest could hold. [642]

When the boatman finally lowered the gangplank, he did it gently, keeping time. [655] Neri retrieved the empty camera and offered Iona his arm. [665] She shook her head, grinning, because she needed both hands free. [676] They boarded side by side, singing and signing their goodbyes to the luminous harbor, no longer waiting to be remembered before making something worth remembering. [701]

On the hundredth morning after the last dictionary was printed, Iven entered the shadowed library at dawn carrying calipers. [19] The Ministry had declared language complete, and every library chandelier had been fitted with crystals that swallowed words absent from its pages. [41] Such words still moved the speaker's lips, but nothing reached a listener except the ordinary breath between them. [59] Above the reading tables hung a suspended hush, gaining weight. [69] Iven had been hired to decode the pattern of growth before the ceiling gave way. [84]

The Ministry trusted a reticent scholar who had spent forty years measuring things rather than objecting to them. [102] He trusted measurements because grief had taught him how easily a mouth could fail. [116] When his wife died, their little daughter had asked what to call the warmth remaining in an abandoned chair. [135] He had answered that there was no word. [143] She had stopped asking him questions soon afterward. [151]

Now his assignment had acquired a prisoner: Mara, the schoolteacher who brought children to the library each Wednesday. [169] The chandelier grew fastest during her lessons, and the Ministry wanted him to testify that she was teaching forbidden language. [189] His summons lay beside a crystal from the chandelier, removed yesterday when its brass hook began to bend. [207] Within it, cloudy seams branched like the roots he and his daughter had once watched growing inside a jar. [226]

The material was sonically reflective; trapped syllables rebounded inside it until their vibrations laid down another crystalline layer. [244] A scratch in the surface let one escape. [252] Working through fractured sequences, Iven heard endings before beginnings, childish vowels tangled with an old woman's trembling consonants. [270] He numbered the splinters by depth, rebuilding the order in which the layers had formed. [285] On slates confiscated with the teacher, the same experiments survived in chalk, arrows joining one child's suggestion to another's. [304] What emerged was not a secret vocabulary handed down by Mara, but children trying different sounds and borrowing from one another. [325]

Beside a drawing of an empty chair, seven crossed out inventions led to one they had circled: afterwarm. [343] The corresponding layer held a boy's uncertain whisper, followed by Mara repeating his discovery with the tenderness of someone accepting a gift. [365] The largest branches marked not speeches, but attempts to answer needs the finished dictionary had left unnamed. [382] Other slates named the relief of forgiving someone, and the loneliness of being believed too late. [398] Iven set down his calipers and cried without making any useful sound at all. [412]

By noon, he had packed the slates and wrapped the broken crystal in a scarf his daughter had sent him last winter. [434] At the public hearing, Mara stood beneath another chandelier, holding her wrists as though they belonged to a frightened pupil. [454] The magistrate asked whether her lessons had caused the dangerous growth. [465] Iven said yes, and saw her lower her head. [474] Then he asked permission to demonstrate the mechanism, because a measurement without its cause was merely an accusation wearing spectacles. [494]

For an accurate demonstration, he explained, the courtroom's chandelier must be muffled, lest it absorb the evidence. [511] The magistrate nodded, and a guard fetched the velvet maintenance shroud. [522] Iven laid the slates before the clerk and opened the crystal along its deepest seam. [537] The boy's invention came out broken, then whole, then broken again in Mara's gentler voice. [552] Nobody laughed at its awkwardness. [557] Even the clerk looked at the empty chair drawn on the slate before reaching for his pen. [574]

Asked to classify the offense, Iven felt the old refuge of silence open around him. [589] He could still report acoustic accumulation and leave a woman inside those harmless, murderous words. [604] "She was helping them speak," he said. [611] The magistrate ordered that answer struck, but Iven watched the clerk hesitate over the boy's word. [627] It was evidence, after all, and evidence had to be recorded. [638]

Mara remained in custody. [642] Outside, when they finally released him, the afternoon had the thin, forgiving warmth of a worn blanket. [659] He could not free the teacher, nor give his daughter back the years he had answered her with silence. [678] But he could visit his daughter before dark, carrying a word that might still be of use. [695]

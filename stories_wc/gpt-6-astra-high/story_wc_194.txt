In the first yawn of evening, Mara entered the sepia reading room carrying the gramophone crank handle that had condemned her father. [22] Outside, children called her the dynasty ender and begged her to throw them coins bearing the dead king's face. [41] She had no coins, and the republic she had helped establish had scarcely more bread than the monarchy. [59] Tomorrow its ministers would auction the palace furnishings, including whatever remained of the truth. [73]

Her father, the royal instrument maker, had been hanged for stealing that handle during the winter of empty granaries. [92] At the time, the charge had seemed merely another royal cruelty. [103] But yesterday she had found his confiscated effects, and the absurdly small object had made her question the official story. [123] A thief would have taken silver; a repairer would have taken his tools home openly. [138] He had hidden this beneath his shirt. [145]

She had come to uncover a hidden truth, not simply to find another reason to hate a dead man. [164]

Golden shadows lay across the reading tables, softening the stains that generations of tobacco smoke had worked into the walls. [184] Above the desk, the king's portrait remained eloquently silent, its painted hand resting on a ledger no visitor had ever seen. [205] Mara had searched that desk after the uprising and found nothing. [216]

Now she fitted the handle into the gramophone beside it and wound the spring. [230] The record already on the turntable held no song, only three sustained notes separated by long silences. [247] She almost lifted the needle. [252] Then the lowest note made the dust on the desk tremble into narrow ridges. [266]

As a child, she had watched her father scatter sand over brass plates and stroke their edges with a violin bow. [287] He liked to muse that silence had a shape, if you knew how to look. [302] The memory offered a bridge of intuition between the trembling dust and the hollow pillars supporting the desktop. [320]

She knelt and found three brass throats concealed beneath the carved lions. [332] Her father had built an acoustic lock. [339] Through standing waves, the right notes would set hidden reeds vibrating, lifting catches that no ordinary key could reach. [358]

She turned the gramophone horn toward the openings, adjusted the speed until the dust formed clean lines, and started the record again. [381] Three clicks answered the three notes. [387] A narrow drawer slipped from beneath the desk, carrying the ledger from the portrait and a folded maintenance sheet. [406]

Her father's handwriting was unmistakable: the spring must be fully wound, the windows closed, the horn facing the lions. [425] Below those instructions, in smaller letters, he had copied dates and quantities of grain. [439] Mara recognized the winter when her brother had died. [448]

The ledger recorded shipments sold abroad while the king announced that blight had emptied the country's fields. [465] Against each sale stood two signatures: the king's authorization and the receipt of the merchant who arranged transport. [483] That merchant was now the republic's minister of provisions. [492] Mara herself had nominated him, grateful for the money he had given the rebellion. [506]

On the sheet's reverse, her father had written that he was taking the handle to a magistrate as the key to his evidence. [529] The gate guards searched every departing servant; he must have hoped a tool would pass where a royal ledger could not. [550] He had trusted the law; she had trusted a revolution. [560]

For a moment she wanted to feed the ledger to the fireplace and leave her father's death uncomplicated. [578] It would be easier to keep blaming a portrait than admit that she had helped put the merchant in charge of feeding hungry children. [602]

Outside, a boy repeated her grand title, trying to impress his friends. [614]

She wrapped the ledger in the auction inventory, then wrote a note directing the palace printer to make copies before morning. [635] There would be a hearing, with witnesses and public accounts, not another scaffold hastily built beneath a cheering crowd. [654] On the first page she wrote that she had appointed him, and signed her name. [669]

The gramophone's spring ran down, lowering the last note until the room released it. [683] Mara put the handle in her pocket and went outside to ask the children where their families were waiting for bread. [704]

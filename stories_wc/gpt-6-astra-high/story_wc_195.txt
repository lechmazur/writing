Since the king outlawed uncertainty, Sir Edrin had become a withdrawn knight, answering orders but never offering words of his own. [21] Tonight, as another street dissolved beyond the palace walls, he finally unbarred the doors he had guarded for twenty years. [41] The alchemical orchard filled a lofty greenhouse shrouded in endless night, its silver apples reflecting suns that had never risen. [61] The king harvested its futures, preserving only those in which his throne endured. [74] First he had pruned away wars, then rebellions, then people whose dreams refused to ripen into obedience. [91] Edrin's sister had vanished last winter, leaving him with the intolerable memory of having praised the first harvest. [109] He had come to capture the shape of possibility, a living seed that could restore tomorrow before the entire city disappeared. [130]

Yet every fruit he touched hardened into lead. [138] The orchard was factually mystical: every miracle followed a repeatable procedure, but the king had burned the gardeners' manuals. [157] Edrin searched beneath grafting benches while reality shifted around him, changing brick to bone and moving the entrance farther away. [177] Then he recognized a painted moon leaning behind an irrigation barrel. [188] His sister had carried that scenery here for the apprentices' annual puppet show, before the king dismissed them all. [207] Under it lay an abandoned marionette theater program, damp but legible, advertising The Prince Who Would Not Finish. [225]

He remembered being embarrassed by her performance, its prince forever declining the crown offered at the end of each scene. [245] Now he saw that the printed string diagrams matched the orchard's trellises. [257] The gardeners had hidden their forbidden instructions in her play, trusting children to remember what obedient adults would overlook. [276] His shame warmed into something stranger as he worked through forgotten patterns, lifting wires where the puppet's elbows should rise. [296] At each correct movement, frost chimed loose from the branches and drifted upward like a snowfall remembering heaven. [314] Delight prickled along his spine; for the first time in years, his hands were doing something other than preventing passage. [334]

At the central tree, the diagrams required an open palm beneath seven intersecting threads. [348] His armored fingers could not spread far enough, and he had to unsnap the gauntlet bearing his oath to the king. [369] The instant it fell, a bell sounded in the palace. [379] The trees stiffened, their shadows stretching into bars, and Edrin knew the king had begun another revision. [396] He slipped his naked hand between the threads and completed the gesture his sister's prince had always made instead of accepting sovereignty. [418] A seed appeared above his palm, continually unfolding without growing larger, each translucent surface revealing a different morning. [436]

In one, his sister sat sewing a velvet dragon, her mouth full of pins. [450] His fingers curled instinctively, and that morning sharpened while all the others dimmed. [463] On the program's final page, beneath the empty throne, she had written a note in her familiar impatient hand. [482] "Leave the strings slack, or you only teach another king to pull them." [495] He understood then that he could bring her back only by choosing which other lives the orchard must refuse. [514] Beyond the glass, windows blinked out along her old street, and the seed trembled against his tightening hand. [532] For twenty years, he had mistaken holding fast for keeping faith. [543]

He opened his fingers until the lovely morning with the velvet dragon dissolved among a thousand mornings he could not predict. [564] "To what may live," he whispered, a murmured allegiance that promised him neither forgiveness nor reunion. [580] The threads loosened, and the seed settled into his skin like a snowflake that had learned to burn. [598] He carried it toward the retreating entrance, following the spaces between the trellises rather than the path the king had prescribed. [619] The program guided him still: the puppet escaped its master not by cutting every string, but by stepping where they crossed loosely. [641]

At the threshold, Edrin pressed his palm into the earth. [651] Roots ran beneath the city, and every vanished window returned dark and empty, waiting for lives no sovereign had selected. [671] Behind him, the orchard glass cracked with the delicate music of ice breaking on a river. [687] Dawn touched his bare fingers, terribly cold, unbearably sweet. [696] Shivering with bliss, the knight waited to discover what the light would show. [709]

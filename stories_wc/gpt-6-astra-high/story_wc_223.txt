During the interregnum, the dead king's shadow wandered the capital, although his body lay beneath six tons of marble. [19] They called it the nomadic penumbra: a traveling half-darkness made from every life his official history had omitted. [37] Where it passed, people forgot small things first: how to button a child's coat, whose hand had steadied their cup. [57] Then they forgot why they should help anyone at all. [67]

Mara had spent twelve years as a crisis actor, screaming convincingly through palace disaster rehearsals. [82] Only the orphanage fire had been real; afterward, the court hired her to reenact the king's rescue. [99] The king had never come. [104] A kitchen porter had. [108]

Now the regent summoned her to the whispering gallery, where royal proclamations entered the city's memory. [124] A whisper at one end reached the other undiminished; generations of royal testimony still murmured beneath the dome. [142] He was benevolently ruthless: he had stopped that fire by demolishing a whole street, including his own house. [160] Mara trusted his intention to save people; she distrusted his arithmetic. [171]

He offered her a commission to write the definitive history of the reign. [184] She wanted to become the historian of small gestures instead, but had never dared say so to anyone who paid her. [205]

She set her proposed first chapter on his table: a pegged chessboard missing all the pawns. [221] The porter had given a pawn to each terrified child, promising that the game would continue outside. [238] Mara, trapped there teaching a holiday play, had followed them through the smoke with the board over her head. [257] The children had kept their pieces; the porter had died that evening, coughing soot. [271]

"His name was Iven," she said. [277]

The gallery returned only a thin, embarrassed hiss. [285] Its ancient law admitted common lives to public memory only as witnesses to crowned deeds. [300]

"We can stop the shadow before it takes another district," the regent said, unfolding a decree. [316] "But the succession must remain credible, or there will be war." [327]

The decree would bind every wandering memory permanently into the new king's legend. [340] Iven would vanish; another sovereign would carry the children out. [350]

Beneath the dome, proclamations were written by a pattern of walking that spelled words across the tiles. [367] Mara had learned that alphabet for ceremonies, her obedient feet lending counterfeit disasters the authority of law. [384] Only during an interregnum could the gallery's oldest commands be rewritten. [395] By sunset, a coronation would close that opening. [403]

The penumbra seeped under the doors, cooling her ankles. [412] For a moment she could not remember why she had brought the board. [425] Then she saw the empty front ranks, and remembered Iven kneeling to put courage into sixteen little hands. [443] Not courage enough to die for a king. [451] Just enough to cross a smoky room. [458]

She stepped onto the floor, facing the throne's empty alcove. [468] The first word was UNBIND. [473]

"You will destroy the only history we share," the regent said. [484]

Her second word was MEMORY. [489] He caught her wrist before she could finish the final letter. [500] Beneath her unfinished letter, a voice whispered, asking whether anyone had found a white pawn. [515]

"We shared this too," Mara said. [521]

He looked at the board, at the back ranks intact and the little holes where the ordinary pieces belonged. [540] His grip loosened, but did not release her. [548] She finished the letter with her heel. [555]

The dome rang without sound, and the names on its marble frieze scattered into a thousand overlapping voices. [573] The penumbra thinned into separate shadows, each settling at the feet of someone willing to listen. [589] Outside, a man remembered how to lift his mother without hurting her. [601]

The regent released Mara, but withdrew the commission. [609] He could not promise the new king would spare her. [619] Still, before leaving for the coronation, he placed his own pen beside the pawnless board. [634]

The gallery was almost empty by dusk, its walls bright with all they no longer claimed to own. [652] Mara sat beneath the vacant frieze and wrote Iven's name. [662] For a moment she was tempted to give him a speech, something magnificent enough to justify what she had risked. [682] Instead she wrote that he had knelt, although the floor was hot. [694] Then she left a space for whoever remembered more. [703]

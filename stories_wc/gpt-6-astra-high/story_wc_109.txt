When only one star remains in a dimming sky, the people climb to the storm-tossed ridge fort, carrying doors instead of luggage. [22] Below them, the tide has swallowed the bridges; doors will make rafts if the fort falls next. [39] Mara, the pebble-shore oracle, knows they will not survive that water. [50]

She learned her trade listening to waves sift stones, reading the spaces between impacts as other people read ink. [69] Now she kneels inside the gate with a flaking gold leaf booklet balanced on her knees. [85] Every gilded page records a coastal passage, certified safe by generations of oracles. [98] Blank lines between its markings record the intervals when the sea was quiet. [111] Her sister's passage is there, too. [117]

The survivors call Mara mildly obsessed because she keeps adding measurements to margins already black with figures. [134] They do not know that the last instruction she gave her sister was to wait for quiet. [151] She mistook the stillness before a breaking wave for the pause after it. [164] Since then, she has wanted to draw a map of an endless night, a map requiring neither light nor faith. [184] For nine generations the stars have dwindled; hers will be the first to navigate without them. [200] Tonight, she has until the remaining star goes out. [209]

Inside, a marbled hush fills the old assembly chamber, where pale lightning crosses black memorial slabs without illuminating anyone's face. [229] The governor asks her to retell the founding oracle's account of the western causeway. [243] He wants the familiar ending: the sea withdraws, the faithful cross, the gates of morning open. [259] Mara has always performed such readings by summarizing silence, naming what the pauses between recorded observations must mean. [277] It is how uncertainty becomes prophecy, and prophecy becomes permission. [287] Her teachers called the reverence afterward ambered hush, because it preserved their words against all contradiction. [303]

She opens to the western route and finds three golden dots marking shelter beneath an arch. [319] A child beside her traces them with a dirty finger. [329] "That arch fell last winter," he says. [336]

Mara remembers seeing its broken stones and deciding to revise the booklet when she could measure everything properly. [354] Instead, she had kept measuring the safer beaches, where being right could not cost another sister. [370] The governor reaches for the book. [376]

Mara closes it before he can carry its obsolete certainty to the waiting families. [390] "I cannot promise that road," she says, breaking the ambered hush. [401] "I was wrong when I sent my sister out, too." [411]

Behind him, a crack travels up a memorial slab, and the seaward tower collapses into the surf. [428] Nobody asks for another prophecy. [433]

At the seaward window, Mara listens while the child holds her belt. [445] Wave, impact, rattle: loose pebbles moving downslope. [452] Wave, impact, nothing: bare rock reflecting water toward the fort. [462] Farther west, a long soft hiss marks the shingle bank below the mainland cliffs. [476] There is still a way, but not where the gold says it should be. [490]

She tears out the causeway page and folds its thick vellum into three ridges the child can feel. [508] Between them, she pierces holes with her sister's pin. [517] "These are listening places, not promises," she tells him. [526] "Cross only after the rattle, never before it, however long the quiet lasts." [539]

She makes six more maps, enough for each family to lend a hand to the next, and teaches the children first. [560] They retell her instructions without reverence, interrupting to ask how long a hiss must last. [575] She makes them practice until they can distinguish water arriving from water draining away. [589]

When the last star disappears, the governor is still holding a door, but now it bridges the broken gate. [608] Mara crosses last into a darkness full of people listening. [618] Ahead, the child calls back a correction: the second ridge should be higher. [631] From the mainland comes the agreed signal, stone striking stone: the first family has reached the cliff steps. [649] She presses the new fold into her page and follows his voice. [661] Gold flakes from the booklet cling briefly to her wet hands before the sea takes them. [677]

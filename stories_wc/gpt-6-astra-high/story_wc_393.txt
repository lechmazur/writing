[21] Enamel birds glittered beneath green lamps, and unfinished waltzes shivered whenever thunder touched the window. [36] Beside her, a prisoner named Silas sat perfectly still, saving his strength for the next beat of his clockwork heart. [56]

[74] It was the first useful thing she had done since advising Silas to confess that he was sabotaging his mechanism. [94] The prison physician had called his collapses deliberate, and Mara had mistaken agreement with authority for discernment. [111] Her endorsement had cost him twelve nights in solitary. [120] Yesterday she had watched him collapse while reaching for water, when there was no labor to avoid. [137]

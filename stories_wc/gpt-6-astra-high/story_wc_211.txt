After the foghorn fell silent, the venerable oath scribe carried his granddaughter's rehearsal cylinder into the orchard garden atop the city's oldest skyscraper. [23] The garden was ancient; when the harbor swallowed its original hillside, engineers had lifted its soil and pear trees into the clouds. [45] Now its roots held the tower together, drinking rainwater and the promises entrusted to them. [60]

Iven set the cylinder on his worktable beneath the pears. [70] At dawn, Mara would repeat her oath in the trees' first language, and they would bind her to his office for life. [92] He spoke six dead languages, but lately his granddaughter's silences had defeated him. [105]

She had once wanted to study forests that grew directly from the earth. [118] He had explained that somebody must inherit the archive. [127] "You raised me," she had answered, as though settling an account. [138] Her rehearsal had been flawless. [143] That troubled him more than a mistake would have. [152]

For seventy years he had worked to archive emotional patterns: the tremor beneath allegiance, the relief concealed inside surrender. [171] The cylinders preserved the words; their discarded wax retained whatever the speakers could not say. [186] He kept those remnants in labeled drawers, believing that no sorrow should have to imagine itself unprecedented. [203]

Beside Mara's cylinder lay a wax cylinder phonograph shaving, curled like the tiny white peel he remembered from a curious third-grade field trip. [226] Their guide had cut a recording of the class promising to protect the garden. [240] Iven had dropped a shaving into an irrigation basin. [249] From the water came their laughter, impossibly old and tired. [259] The guide emptied the basin before anyone else noticed, then punished him for wasting wax. [274] He had remembered the punishment longer than the wonder. [283]

Tonight, wanting to hear what Mara's perfect pronunciation concealed, he immersed the thin wax shaving in that same basin. [302] The water was warm, although the night was cool. [311] Pear roots moved under its surface with the unhurried softness of fingers beneath velvet. [325]

Mara's voice rose, thin with age. [331] "Forty years, and I still wait for him to tell me I may leave." [345]

Then came Iven's own voice speaking the formal acceptance he had not yet given. [359] "The oath is witnessed." [363]

He gripped the basin until his knuckles hurt. [371] Beneath the voices, the roots murmured a word in a language whose last native speaker had died before the tower was built. [393] Its meaning was not memory, but seed. [400] He had accidentally harvested a prophecy. [406] The discarded wax held an emotion not yet lived, and the trees had given it a mouth. [423]

His first impulse was to fetch a clean cylinder. [432] Here was the discovery that could justify his life: grief detectable before it happened, entire generations of suffering available for study. [453] He even reached for the recording horn. [460]

Then he pictured the label on the drawer. [468] Mara: resignation, ripening. [471]

All these years he had believed the archive sheltered sorrow. [481] How much of it had his office planted? [489]

Across the garden, a light came on in the small room where Mara slept. [503] She emerged carrying the coat he always forgot, and the sight of that ordinary kindness nearly made him keep the prophecy secret. [525] He could let her stay. [530] She might never accuse him. [535]

Instead, he took the coat and told her what the basin had said. [548]

"Are you asking me to promise I won't become that woman?" [559]

He heard how carefully she offered him another lock. [568]

"No," he said, using the living language in which he had taught her to name the pears. [585] "I'm asking whether the forest school will still take you." [595]

She did not answer immediately, and he made himself endure the silence without translating it. [610]

"If I catch the morning ferry." [616]

He brought her unsigned oath to the basin. [624] The roots lifted toward it, expectant. [630] He tore the paper into pieces too small to carry a promise and let the water take them. [648] For a moment the tower trembled, or perhaps only he did. [659]

Mara held his elbow until he was steady. [667] Then she went inside to pack. [673] In the morning he would offer an apprenticeship that could be left. [685]

Under the pear trees, Iven listened to her opening drawers, and for once he did not reach for a cylinder. [705]

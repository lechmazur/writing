Three days after Ivo's confession went viral, strangers began filming through the fence of the abandoned data center turned moss garden. [21] Mara covered the gate with burlap before watering anything. [30] A watchful dreamer, she had spent seven years coaxing green life into the building that had helped the government disappear her mother. [52] She wanted a forest here, but checked its locks every night. [63]

Ivo had written the software that sorted intercepted conversations into lists of dangerous people. [77] In his video, he admitted that dangerous had mostly meant inconvenient. [88] Then he announced he would visit the garden to ask forgiveness, as though forgiveness were another appointment he could schedule. [108] Mara sent one reply: "Come without cameras, and bring the recordings." [119]

She had no interest in making his shame comfortable. [128] But beneath the demands for punishment online, families were asking whether the dead had left any words behind. [146] One message came from Tess, a girl of sixteen whose father had repaired radios and never returned from questioning. [165] "I want to hear him, but I don't want anyone watching me hear him." [179] Mara knew the difference between being offered evidence and being put on display. [192]

Before dawn, she studied a pattern left by the drip of thawing icicles beneath the broken clerestory. [209] Each drop had punched a dark dimple through the pale moss, marking where meltwater could sustain a living screen. [228] She wheeled three salvaged server racks along that dotted line and hung their mesh sides with planted felt. [246] Behind them, under intact roofing, she laid a dry path of old floor tiles to the former maintenance office. [265] The moss would keep drinking as it thickened; meanwhile, doubled burlap closed the gaps. [279] By opening time, visitors could reach the office without appearing in anyone's photograph. [292] She put a chair beside its door, not behind its desk. [303] Her mother had hated being summoned across desks. [311]

Ivo arrived carrying a drive and wearing the expression of someone prepared to endure a public verdict. [328] Mara gave him a kettle to fill. [335] "First you can make tea." [340] He looked toward the waiting phones beyond the fence, then went to find the tap. [355]

At her laptop, they checked that each family's folder opened separately, with no neighboring names visible. [371] Mara made him write down what the codes meant and sign a statement explaining how he had obtained the files. [391] The originals would go to an independent investigator; survivors would decide whether their loved ones' voices became public. [409]

When Tess arrived, he rose with an apology already trembling on his mouth. [422] "Not yet," Mara said, and showed him the chair outside. [432]

Inside the office, Tess stared at the headphones. [440] Mara unclasped her amber pendant, the only thing the officers had returned after her mother's arrest. [456] A scrap of fern lay inside it, preserved in golden light but unable to grow. [471] As a child, she had held its warm, uneven surface while her mother worked splinters from her palms. [489] Now she could proffer it without pretending that either of them was safe from every hurt. [505] "Hold this if you want, and take the headphones off whenever you need." [518]

Tess closed her fingers around the amber. [525] Mara sat near the open door, within reach but not watching her face. [538] Beyond the moss, water tapped against metal with none of the old machinery's urgency. [552] Tess listened for eleven seconds before stopping the recording. [561] "He's telling me where he hid my birthday present." [570] Her laugh broke, but nobody rushed in to repair it. [580]

In that honeyed hush, Mara felt the small weight missing from her own throat. [594] She had wanted to graft new meaning onto old wounds, not bark over them until they vanished. [611] Perhaps this was how: a stolen voice returned, a door left open, someone else allowed to choose the silence. [630] Outside, Ivo waited with his unspoken apology and his signed evidence. [641] Mara would ask him about her mother when she was ready, and not a moment sooner. [657]

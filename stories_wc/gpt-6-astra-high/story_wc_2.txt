Mara, the broken-clock tinkerer, notices the ashes before she notices that all three customers have brought clocks from flooded cellars. [20] The gray grit has lodged between teeth designed to measure time, not survive it. [34] She marks the addresses on her workbench map and finds a curve leading toward the admiral's burned estate. [52] It is Thursday, the morning when coincidence becomes pattern. [61]

Beneath the ashes of last summer's fire, rainwater must be entering the old drainage system, carrying debris that blocks its outlet. [82] By evening, the rising water will reach the houses above those cellars. [94] At least, that is what Mara's calculations say. [102]

Once, she promised her father twelve safe minutes inside a sluice tunnel, calculated from the interval between reservoir releases. [121] An emergency release came early, and she has spent seven years repairing clocks she no longer believes. [138] Lately, she has tried to befriend uncertainty in planning rather than let it forbid every undertaking. [154] Each job now begins with an estimate, a margin, and permission to stop. [167]

She sends her apprentice, Kit, to warn the lower streets, then unrolls the estate plans. [182] A bypass could divert the underground stream away from town, but its gate lacks a wheel. [198] The drawing shows eight brass points surrounding a square socket: the admiral's lost compass rose. [213] His descendants displayed the removable wheel above the greenhouse entrance until the fire dropped it somewhere into the ruins. [232]

Kit returns with rope, lanterns, and news that the watchmen are evacuating the lowest houses. [247] He asks whether she is certain the bypass still works. [257] "Uncertainly certain," she says, and hands him the plans so he can check her reasoning. [272] He traces the bypass to an abandoned quarry, safely below the town. [284]

They search through the swirling leaves in a neglected greenhouse, following drafts that breathe between its blackened paving stones. [303] Where leaves keep vanishing beneath a collapsed bench, Mara discovers the service hatch. [316] Beside it, beneath the ashes, one brass point catches her lantern light. [328] They dig out the rose, its northward arm bent sideways, its square center still sound. [343]

The hatch opens onto stairs descending beside a subterranean waterfall, where the estate's buried stream plunges into a vaulted chamber. [363] Across the chamber, branches and charcoal churn against the iron grille of the townward outlet. [378] The bypass spindle waits above a narrow ledge already disappearing under water. [390] Mara scratches a retreat mark on the stair and makes Kit promise to call her back when the water reaches it. [411] Then she ties their safety rope around a stone pillar instead of trusting the rusted railing. [427]

The compass rose fits, but when she leans against its eastern point, the corroded gate mechanism refuses to turn. [446] A clock spring can be coaxed; this needs leverage, and she has brought no bar. [461] She must improvise without confusing invention with a guarantee. [470] Kit passes down a loose stair tread, and she wedges the oak plank between two points of the rose. [489]

The wheel moves a fraction before the plank splits. [498] Water surges toward the opening gate, sweeping across the ledge and knocking her to her knees. [514] "The mark," Kit shouts, and the old arithmetic rises in her: another minute, another turn, enough time if everything behaves. [534] She leaves the rose where it is and lets the rope guide her back. [548]

On the stairs, she expects the shame of retreat to feel exactly like grief. [562] Instead, Kit presses his forehead against her shoulder, shaking, and she understands what her father would have wanted from twelve minutes. [583] They wait above the mark, watching a chip of oak bob against the wall. [597] Slowly, it descends. [600] The narrow opening is draining the chamber faster than the waterfall can fill it. [614] Not by much, and perhaps not for long, but enough to give the watchmen time. [629]

By dawn, a town crew arrives with timber bracing and a proper lever to finish opening the gate. [647] Mara gives them her observations, including the things she cannot explain. [658] Back at her bench, she cleans the three ruined clocks and writes estimates instead of promises on their repair slips. [678] On Thursday's map, she adds the quarry, the hatch, and a second route home. [692]

Nella, the sea-foam recollectionist, keeps a sign above her stall: SAD MEMORIES REPAIRED WHILE YOU WAIT. [16] Business is excellent, although customers increasingly need tall ladders to reach her. [28] Their town occupies an iridescent cave inside a giant crystal geode, where roads grow from remembered journeys rather than quarried stone. [49] Lately, whole stretches have vanished, leaving cheerful front doors opening onto glittering gulfs. [62] Yesterday, the path to Nella's daughter's grave disappeared. [70] Today, she intends to discover why some paths disappear, before the town becomes a collection of inaccessible rooms. [88]

Her customers bring their difficult recollections suspended in bowls of sea foam, which holds a memory only until dawn. [107] Through a lens of crater glass, she transliterates emotions into syllables the geode understands, then reads each journey aloud. [126] The glass, born when a falling star struck sand, can hold even feelings too heavy for foam. [143] For years, Nella has kept the worst syllables trapped inside it. [154] Customers leave smiling, and she considers these missing pieces a kindness. [165] She has certainly preferred remembering her daughter's birthday picnic without remembering the empty place at every picnic afterward. [183]

At dusk, she balances beside the vanished cemetery road and examines its edges through her increasingly cloudy lens. [201] She would rather conjecture about salt damage than consider that her good work might be involved. [217] Then a migrating bird swoops from the fig trees rooted overhead, carrying a ripe fruit in its beak. [235] It follows the same winter route each year, usually with a mate. [247] This year it comes alone. [252]

As it pecks, scattered seeds fall through the emptiness ahead of Nella and stop in midair. [268] Each rests on a sliver of newly forming crystal. [277] She follows the scattered seeds dropped by the migrating bird, testing every new foothold with her broom. [294] The little trail carries her across the gulf to an arch where two birds once nested. [310] Here the survivor calls, waits, and calls again. [318] Nella lifts the crater glass, expecting hunger, but the bird's feeling becomes a syllable she has filed away hundreds of times. [339] Its meaning is roughly: you were here, and I am glad, and where are you? [354] With each call, the road beneath her feet thickens. [363]

The bird is remembering the whole journey. [370] She has been returning only the pleasant portions, asking the geode to connect beginnings to endings without what happened between. [390] Perhaps the paths are not being destroyed at all; perhaps they are waiting for honest directions. [406] It is an ugly possibility, and therefore worth testing. [415]

From her satchel she takes the foam she gathered that morning, containing her last surviving recollection of the cemetery walk. [435] Above the entrance, the first seam of daylight is whitening; she has spent the night following a handful of seeds. [455] She must finish before memory fades, but her mouth refuses the first forbidden sound. [469] Restoring sorrow will mean admitting she cannot repair what happened to her daughter, or to anyone else's. [486] The bird drops a fig seed onto her head and resumes its miserable, determined song. [501]

"All right, professor," she says, and turns the glass toward herself. [512]

She speaks the bright syllables first: the baker's wave, the roadside poppies, her daughter's name picked out in gold. [531] Then she releases the sound she cut away, the awful lightness of walking home with nobody to carry. [549] Her knees buckle, but the road rises to meet them. [559] It grows unevenly, with a crooked patch where she once stopped to scream, yet it reaches the grave. [577] When the foam dissolves in the first sunlight, she is kneeling beside the name she had almost made unreachable. [596] She cries, then laughs when the bird starts searching her hair for the seed it dropped there. [613]

By breakfast, she has returned to her stall with a clearer lens and an apology considerably longer than her advertisement. [633] She will offer each customer their missing pieces back, though she cannot promise that every road will be easy. [652] Some may want to wait, and she will keep their sorrow safe until they ask. [667] For now, she turns her sign around and writes: COMPLETE MEMORIES RESTORED, WITH DIRECTIONS FOR GETTING HOME. [684] Outside, the cemetery road gleams through the cave's rainy colors, and Nella leaves her door open toward it. [702]

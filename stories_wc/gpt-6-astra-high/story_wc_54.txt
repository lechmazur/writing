The morning after the carnival left town, Mara entered the abandoned spaceport passenger terminal to measure what the demolition crew would inherit. [22] She was a civil engineer intern, curious enough to lift covers marked SEALED instead of copying the warning into her report. [43] The carnival's rent had bought the building another month; now even the electricity was gone. [58]

She had applied for the internship because her mother could no longer manage their flooded ground floor alone. [76] In her notebook, beneath the ordinary hydraulic formulas, she tried to puzzle out new equations for hope. [93] The launch towers had promised her a life elsewhere. [102] Now her mother's illness measured distance in bus stops, and Mara hated how readily duty could disguise itself as defeat. [122]

In a cracked display case she noticed an explorer's sextant piece: a brass arc, still attached to its pivot. [141] The label credited it with bringing a survey ship home after its electronic navigation failed. [156]

The drawings promised a storm drain beneath the concourse, but her lamp revealed three successive barriers. [172] A security wall protected the customs office; a raised sill kept machine oil out of the cistern; a concrete plug prevented children entering the drain. [197] Each made sense alone. [201] Together they formed a maze of intentions that sent hillside runoff back into the houses below. [217] Mara's first sketch simply erased the barriers, until she saw a rainbow of old fuel shimmering in a sump. [236] An open route was not necessarily a safe one. [245]

There was another route through the elevated channels that had once carried cooling water around the passenger hall. [263] They were high enough to protect the cistern from the oily floor, but their fall was absent from every drawing. [283] There was no room for her survey level's tripod. [292] She returned to the case and borrowed the fragment against her signature. [304] She fixed the sextant arc to a straightedge, suspended a plumb line from its pivot, and checked it against the level outside. [326] The instrument's markings doubled the actual angles; forgetting that would exaggerate the slope the channels possessed. [342] She penciled the correction on her wrist and crawled in. [352] She measured every joint twice, lying in dust where water had once cooled travelers who complained about the heat on their way to other worlds. [377]

By dusk she had a route, but not permission. [386] Her supervisor arrived carrying the notice that authorized its demolition, and listened without interrupting while rain tapped the glass roof. [406]

"This does not save a spaceport," he said. [414]

"It might save a street." [419]

She showed him the measured falls and a plan to isolate the oily sump without breaching any security walls. [438] They could resurrect the terminal's original cistern through redirected flows, giving hillside rain somewhere lower to go than her mother's kitchen. [459] Her supervisor checked the calculations, then called the maintenance crew back from dismantling the carnival's water hookups. [476] Using the carnival's municipal supply hoses, they bridged the missing sections and screened each inlet against ticket stubs and leaves. [496]

At midnight, the first surge struck the screens, hesitated, then slipped toward the cistern instead of climbing the road. [515] When the inlet backed up, a worker reached for the screen, but Mara caught his sleeve. [531] A strip of carnival canvas had sealed the mesh; removing the screen would only let the cloth lodge deeper, beyond their reach. [553] They hooked the canvas free while she held the lamp, watching the upstream water settle by the width of her thumb. [574] They spent the storm clearing debris by hand. [582]

By morning her mother's doorstep was wet, but the kitchen floor was dry. [595] On Mara's assessment sheet, her supervisor wrote functionally creative, then attached her measurements to a request to postpone demolition. [614] Mara did not draw a rocket beside the request, as she once might have done. [629] She calculated the storage still available, added the cost of permanent pipes, and left a column for errors they had not found yet. [652]

Above her, the departure board remained dark. [659] Below it, water traveled somewhere it was wanted. [667] For the first time, staying had a direction. [675]

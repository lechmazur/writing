Three hours after the whispering seashell half revealed she was the original, not the copy, Mara entered the rusted library under stars. [22] A cyclone had peeled away its roof, exposing iron shelves and the city's last public uplink to the salt wind. [42]

For nine years she had doubled for the fearless actress who wore her face and owned her name. [60] Mara feared heights, deep water, flame, enclosed spaces, and the moment a director stopped pretending her survival mattered. [78] The studio called those fears manufacturing defects. [85] She had believed them because believing she was replaceable made being used a little easier. [100]

Then her sister's parcel arrived. [105] Ada's confession, etched into nacre, whispered between verses of a lullaby their mother sang. [119] "I authorized the memory stripping and sold your identity for Kit's surgery, then kept accepting their money." [136] Ada had included a meeting place, but no request for forgiveness. [147]

Only Kit waited beneath the library's gutted clock, one small arm plastered from knuckles to shoulder. [163] "They took Mum when she tried to leave the studio," he said, holding out the cast. [179] "She wrote this while we were waiting for my bone to set." [191]

His sleeve had smudged the notes into bruised columns. [200] Mara recognized their childhood cipher from memories the studio called implants: rearrange the columns using STARLING, the lullaby's first word. [220] She angled his arm toward starlight, using each repeated letter to restore another rubbed away. [235] Decrypted, the notes gave an archive address, an access key, and four words: PUBLISH ALL THEIR NAMES. [252]

At the uplink terminal, stolen contracts unfolded across the screen, each accompanied by a photograph and a replacement order. [271] Mara found her own signature copied onto permission she had never given. [283] The originals had become doubles, cleaners, anonymous bodies in crowd scenes. [294] Ada's betrayal had not merely stolen a life; it had taught Mara to mistake exploitation for destiny. [311]

She selected her contract and opened the public broadcast window. [321] She could become the star again and let the studio bury everyone else's paperwork in litigation. [337] Instead, she selected every file and switched on the public camera. [348] She wanted proof that vulnerability was power, not another beautiful performance of invincibility. [361]

"My hands are shaking," she told whoever might be watching, "but these people did not consent." [377]

The upload began, and an alarm sounded from the archive connection: studio security had traced their access. [394] Through the empty roof came the propeller whine of listening drones, the same machines that located performers on studio sets. [414] Mara's planned escape ran along a dry aisle now swept by their microphones. [427] Below it, seawater filled a passage to the street. [436] She knew its depth from the library evacuation map, but knowing did not loosen the terror in her throat. [455]

Fear had made her firmly adaptive: on every shoot, she checked exits, tested anchors, and prepared a second way down. [475] Now she fitted Kit into the stunt harness she always carried and clipped him to her belt. [492]

"I'm afraid of water," she whispered, "and I need you to count my breaths." [506] His good hand closed around hers, no longer asking her to be the woman from the posters. [523]

To outwit the drones, she laid the shell in the metal book chute at the far end of the aisle. [543] Its iron throat magnified Ada's confession into someone apparently hiding beneath the returns desk. [557] Leaving it meant surrendering the only recording she possessed of their mother singing. [570] She left it anyway. [574]

While the drones descended toward that borrowed voice, Mara lowered herself into black water and held Kit above the worst. [594] He counted against her ear; she followed the wall, one shaking foot searching for ground before the other moved. [613] Behind them, the uplink chimed completion. [619]

"We'll tell them where she is, and we won't stop asking," she said. [679]

Her sister had made her a substitute; surviving that betrayal would not require becoming someone else's idea of an original. [699] Still trembling, she let Kit lead her toward the gathering witnesses. [710]

[22] The roof had burned away years ago, but the door still stood, held upright by a threshold of borrowed light. [42] Beyond it lay the abandoned kilns, where ownerless shadows hunted for lungs. [127] Every breath she spent maintaining the threshold was one she could not spend speaking to them. [143]

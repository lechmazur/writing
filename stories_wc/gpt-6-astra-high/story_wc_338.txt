Later, Nell will date her apprenticeship in silence to this moment, at the spot where a map tears, dividing known from unknown. [22] She is lifting a parish map from its swollen frame in the rain-scoured fresco vault of Saint Oran's, beneath a tarpaulin pegged across the broken roof. [48] A winter storm closed the building, and its scattered contents are being salvaged before restoration begins. [64] A perceptive map framer, she notices that the tear follows an older fold, precisely where a marked path enters the blank interior of an ancient henge. [90] The council wants that path restored before reopening the chapel as a visitor center; her commission includes reconstructing the missing section. [111] But the paper is intact beyond the fold, and under magnification she finds no scraped ink, no faded lettering. [130] Someone has deliberately left the destination unnamed. [137]

Methodically curious, Nell lists what she can establish before deciding what she thinks. [150] She begins by comparing silent video footage from decades apart: a 1968 conservation survey and a school project filmed in 1998. [171] Both show the chapel's rear door, the henge's encircling bank, and women standing shoulder to shoulder across the path. [190] The survey's catalogue calls them obstructive parishioners. [197] In the later film, Nell recognizes her mother, whose long pauses she used to fill with brisk, helpful suggestions. [216] Since her mother's death, those interruptions have returned to her more often than any conversation. [231]

Nell almost accepts the catalogue's explanation, until she aligns the films using a split stone visible above the bank. [250] In both, the women move aside for someone approaching from the village, then close ranks only when the camera advances. [270] At the same time, the rear door opens inward. [279] Not a barricade, perhaps, but a screen. [286]

She takes stills, not conclusions, to Agnes, the last surviving woman named in the survey. [301] Agnes studies them with her thumbs covering two faces. [310] "Don't put those on display," she says. [317] Nell closes the folder before asking anything else. [325]

Only then does Agnes tell her about the whispered chapel. [335] After a baby died, or a husband vanished at sea, people came through the rear door when they could not bear public condolences. [358] The henge's high bank hid a bench from the village road; the vault sheltered mourners when rain drove them inside. [378] No service was prescribed, and nobody had to explain a visit. [389] They called it whispered because its welcome traveled from mouth to ear, never from the pulpit. [405] The women had asked the surveyor not to mark their bench, Agnes remembers, afraid that a label would become an invitation to stare. [428]

"Your mother sat with me after my boy," Agnes says, and stops. [440] Nell feels the old urge to supply a comforting ending. [450] Instead, she waits until Agnes decides to speak again. [459] "If you reopen it, don't make us part of the exhibition." [470]

The proposed visitor route would funnel ticket holders through the rear door and straight past the bench. [487] At the council meeting, Nell brings two drawings: the planned route, and another using the old cart track outside the henge. [508] The second needs a gate, and Nell crosses the unnecessary reconstruction fee off her estimate to show how they might afford one. [530] The clerk objects that the grant requires public access. [539] Nell, who has read every condition twice, points out that it does not require access to every room at every hour. [560]

The council approves a public gallery in the vault and a screened passage to the rear door, left unlocked for quiet visitors. [582] Roof repairs take six weeks; while plaster dries, Nell works beside the carpenter, testing the screen from every place a visitor might stop. [605] Its angled slats admit daylight without allowing a direct view of anyone sheltered behind them. [620] She frames the map with a reversible paper hinge, supporting its tear without concealing the blank beyond it. [638] With Agnes's permission, she also asks the archivist to replace "obstructive parishioners" with "women protecting mourners" in the catalogue, leaving their names out. [661]

On opening morning, Agnes arrives before the guides and disappears behind the screen. [674] Nell hears the rear latch settle, then nothing. [682] This time she does not mistake that nothing for work left unfinished. [694]

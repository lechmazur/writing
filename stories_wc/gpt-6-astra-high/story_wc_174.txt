In the hush left behind by a heated argument, Adrian straightened his cuffs beneath the cracked shell of the decommissioned radar dome. [22] Below the hill, the city shone with the elegant waterfront he had once helped sell. [37] An urbane calligrapher, he could make a compulsory purchase notice resemble an invitation to dinner. [52]

For six months he had been trying to undo that talent through subversive puppet shows in seedy bars. [70] His paper magistrates swallowed houses, coughed up coins, and bowed while drinkers recognized the signatures painted on their bellies. [89] After inspectors closed their last venue, the displaced residents offered the dome where they had sheltered during the clearances. [108]

Tonight, Adrian intended to explore ethical storytelling by giving their losses names instead of turning them into scenery. [126] He had congratulated himself on the distinction until Mara saw the little puppet beneath the collapsing cardboard roof. [144]

"My son was not a sound effect," she had said. [154]

Now she stood uncommonly serene beside the lantern, holding the campfire story stick the residents had used outside this dome. [174] Whoever held it could speak without interruption, or choose silence; nobody else could finish that person's story. [191] Her son had carved its crooked crescent before returning home for the blankets they had forgotten. [207]

Adrian wanted to explain that the audience needed one particular child to understand what an abstract clearance meant. [225] Instead he watched her thumb move over the moon, polishing a place already polished by years of handling. [243] Beyond the entrance, shoes scraped gravel: the bar regulars were arriving, carrying folding chairs and the dangerous expectation of revelation. [263]

Their half-lit epiphanies had made him feel useful, even clean. [273] But each performance ended before anyone asked who had drawn the first beautiful map with VACANT lettered across inhabited streets. [293] He had seen washing on the balconies before he dipped his pen. [305]

Mara laid the stick across the puppet stage, barring its miniature doorway. [317]

"You can tell them what you did," she said. [326] "You cannot borrow what happened to him to make yourself brave." [337]

The dome had once tracked approaching aircraft; now its hollow instruments reflected a single flame in small, uncertain windows. [356] Adrian took down the child and folded the cardboard house flat. [367] He would reverse the performance: instead of making the powerless visible, he would expose the hand that had arranged them. [387]

From his portfolio he withdrew the original redevelopment map, which he had carried as evidence against everyone but himself. [406] For a moment he imagined the audience admiring his honesty, and felt the old appetite moving into its new costume. [426]

"Do not let me turn this into a confession they can applaud," he said. [440]

Mara lifted the stick, but did not offer it. [449]

"That is also your work." [454]

When the chairs were filled, Adrian moved the lantern from behind the screen to the front, exposing strings, knots, and fingers. [475] The magistrate still swallowed a house, but this time everyone watched Adrian feed it through the slit in its belly. [495] He spoke his own name first, then the payment, then the instruction to remove every sign of habitation from the map. [516] He named the official who had given that instruction, distinguishing the words he remembered from the words preserved in writing. [536]

At the place where the roof should have fallen, he stopped. [547] The lantern hissed, and beside him a chair leg settled into gravel. [559] Nobody supplied the scream he had rehearsed. [566]

Then a woman in the back asked whether he would sign a statement for the residents' unfinished court case. [585] He put the map on the floor between them and agreed to give them the original, not a handsome copy. [605] Mara passed the story stick to the woman, who began explaining which dates the city's lawyers still disputed. [623]

Through the broken dome, a meteor drew a pale stroke and vanished before anyone thought to point. [640] Adrian reached automatically for his pen, then waited to be asked. [651]

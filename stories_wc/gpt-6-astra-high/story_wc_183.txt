By the time Mara reached the abandoned carnival ship drifting in the foggy bay, she remembered hiring a boat but not the man who had rowed it. [27] Behind her, the empty boat knocked against the hull, its rope already unraveling into something that resembled white hair. [46] She tied it fast, then checked the emerald swatch card inside her coat: four inches of silk, mounted on cardboard, pierced along its edges by tiny stitches. [73]

Her mother had been a seamstress; Mara and the voice she called Nell had chosen that color for their first party dress. [95] Years later, as a geneticist, Mara had discovered that she was a chimera, carrying cells from a twin absorbed before birth. [116] The discovery explained the mismatched laboratory results, but not why Nell loved pears and Mara hated them. [133] Explaining that part had cost her a fellowship. [141]

Eventually she had bought a ticket to this ship, where the advertised marvel was not remembering, but forgetting. [159] She had stepped ashore alone inside her skin, carrying a silence she had mistaken for peace. [175]

Now the gangway bore a newer notice: AS MEMORIES ALTER, HOLD FAST TO WHAT YOU MADE TOGETHER. [192] Beyond it, carousel horses strained against their poles, endlessly free to gallop nowhere. [205] Mara passed the booth where she had surrendered Nell's name and found the ticket seller reduced to a coat around a wooden chair. [228]

Under its glass lay a diagram of the garden of forgotten names, the place she had come to find. [247] Its paths changed whenever she glanced away, while the stairs behind her rearranged themselves into the corridor of her childhood house. [268] Already she remembered the party dress as blue, and herself as a child who had never spoken twice before answering. [288]

But the swatch remained emerald, and beneath her thumb its stitches spelled the beginning of a story. [305] Their mother had taught them to sew letters before they could write, letting each voice dictate a line. [323] Mara had stitched that they were princesses; Nell had insisted they were smugglers disguised as princesses. [339]

"We hid the pears beneath our skirts," Mara said, and a passage opened between two shuttered games. [356] She advanced through narrative threads, reciting their embroidered adventures whenever the ship offered a door into some easier, lonelier past. [376] At the last hatch, she had to confess that Nell, not she, had invented the ending where nobody married the prince. [397]

The garden occupied the drained swimming pool, its beds crowded with pale stems bearing names instead of flowers. [415] Nell's was barely visible beneath a mat of roots. [424] Mara knelt, wanting to recapture her sister's voice, to carry it home like a recovered possession. [440]

The stem whispered, "And when I embarrass you again?" [449]

Her hand stopped above the soil. [455] During the silent year, she had named her condition unkindled solace: no quarrels, no unwelcome laughter, nothing inside her that might catch fire. [478] She had come to end that emptiness, but Nell had spent the same year buried under other people's discarded lives. [498]

"I cannot promise I will be brave," Mara said, "but you get to tell what happened here, even if it ruins me." [520]

She laid the card beside the stem and waited, keeping her hands open while the pool slowly forgot its ladder. [540] At last a root slipped through a needle hole, and a familiar irritation stirred behind her teeth. [557]

"You always make being sisters sound like a punishment," Nell said with their mouth. [571]

Mara laughed, then wept, and could not honestly assign either act to one of them. [586] Together they told the story backward, restoring the ladder, the passage, the gangway, until their boat emerged below the rail. [606]

Neither could leave the other now without returning to that booth, and the thought frightened them in different ways. [625] Yet when Nell took their left hand and Mara their right, the awkward business of rowing became, for several strokes, blissfully aberrant. [647] They disagreed about the direction, stopped, and listened for the harbor bell instead of trying to overpower each other. [666] Behind them the ship drifted on, while the emerald card rested between their knees, with room along its edges for another line. [688]

In the suspended palace anchored to giant stalactites, every clock struck together, and every chandelier trembled a little longer afterward. [20] Prince Iven, bedraggled from sleeping beside the royal incubator, mistook that trembling for the stirring of wings in his dreams. [40] He had promised his dying mother birdsong, and the machinery had outlived her without producing a single note. [58]

Now, beneath its glass bell, an extinct bird's egg rocked whenever the hammers in the foundries below completed their stroke. [78] The silver wren had vanished when those foundries consumed the last forest, leaving this one specimen in a collector's cabinet. [98] Iven had spent his inheritance retrieving it. [105] He wanted to reawaken beauty in a mechanical world, although lately he could not distinguish that wish from wanting forgiveness. [125]

His chief engineer, Mara, arrived carrying a bolt as thick as her wrist, freshly sheared from a palace anchor. [144] "The north chain is failing," she said. [151] "I need your permission to stop the foundries before the next shift." [163]

Stopping them meant stopping the pumps that lifted drinking water and drove warm air into the workers' galleries. [181] The king had connected everything to one master regulator so that nothing could waste a beat. [197] His portrait hung above the incubator, solemnly approving this economy. [207]

Iven asked whether the bolt had rusted, whether the cavern had warmed, whether the chains had stretched unequally. [225] Hypothesizing about everything had once delighted Mara; after six months of heating a dead egg, it merely frightened her. [244]

"Choose what to test," she said, placing the bolt between his hands. [256]

The next hammer stroke made the chandeliers shiver, and a hairline crack opened across the egg. [272] Iven leaned close, briefly, terribly certain that something inside had answered. [283] Only dry dust sifted onto the velvet. [290]

Mara reached for him, but he lifted the broken shell toward the chandelier, watching both tremble through the next stroke. [310]

"Not weight," he whispered, feeling the vibration in his teeth. [320] "Rhythm." [321]

Each simultaneous blow arrived before the previous tremor had faded, feeding the palace's sway like a hand pushing a swing. [341] His father's perfect order had a flawed genesis: it had been designed as though stone could never answer iron. [360] He kept the shell cupped in his palm through the discovery, afraid that setting it down would make everything final. [380]

Then he set it down and signed the shutdown order, pressing hard enough to tear the paper. [397] Mara rang the signal, and the palace gave a long, sickening roll as its engines fell silent. [414]

"We have an hour before the lower galleries freeze," she said. [425]

Together they began to assemble a new timing drum, staggering its brass pins so the hammers would fall in sequence. [445] They scavenged its gears from the incubator, whose delicate adjustments had maintained a useless warmth with exquisite precision. [463] Iven's fingers shook so badly that Mara had to steady them, but she did not take the work away. [482]

When they fitted the drum, the lamps were dimming, and their breath floated between them like pale, bewildered moths. [501] Mara opened the throttle a fraction while Iven watched the hanging bolt she had suspended beside the regulator. [519] It swayed, steadied, then hung almost motionless as the hammers found their separate moments. [533] Above them, the wounded anchors creaked but held. [541]

There would be chains to replace, water to ration, and frightened people who deserved more than a prince's theories. [560] Iven started a list, then paused at a thin sound rising beneath the machinery. [574]

Warm air from a restarting vent was passing across the cracked egg, drawing a wavering whistle from its hollow chamber. [594] He moved it nearer the grille, and the pitch climbed, broke, and returned. [607]

"Was that its song?" Mara asked. [613]

He almost said yes. [617] Then he shook his head, and the admission tasted bitterly sweet, like the medicine his mother had once coaxed him to swallow. [639]

"I don't know what its song was." [646]

He folded a little cradle from the abandoned incubation chart and secured the shell where the air could find it. [666] Then he took the repair list downstairs with Mara, leaving the fragile whistle to wander through the palace's uneven breathing. [686]

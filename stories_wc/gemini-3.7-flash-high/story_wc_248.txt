[72] By destroying those curated ledgers, he had prevented the authoritarian inquisitors from seizing the compromised identities of hundreds of hunted scholars. [93] Yet that necessary blaze had violently separated him from Mara, leaving him with an incandescent yearning to rescue a forgotten promise they had whispered over scorched cedar. [120] In his gloved palm, he clutched a faded tapestry fragment whose fraying gold threads shimmered faintly under his lantern light. [140] Vaelen had survived the state dragnet because he was analytically intuitive, blessed with a mind that instinctively perceived structural patterns beneath raw emotional chaos. [263] Every sculpture and assemblage along the path served as both an engineering cipher and a tender confession. [331] Yet across that radiant face, she had violently dragged a serrated chisel, scoring the canvas with deep, jagged wounds. [350] A casual observer would have dismissed the damage as an act of frantic self-erasure or despair. [366] Vaelen, however, leaned close to study the measured trajectory of every single slice against the linen.

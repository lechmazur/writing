[29] High above the abandoned throne room, Vaelen kept his vigil within the whispering gallery. [96] This roving dusk moved without allegiance, darkening mosaics of ancient conquests before dissolving back into pale, ambient daylight. [114] Vaelen understood performance and ruin better than anyone alive in the capital. [233] Beside the parapet rested a carved marble chessboard missing all the pawns. [245] The lords had fled with their jade kings and obsidian rooks, leaving the forward squares utterly barren to signify the expendable folk who had perished first. [271] Vaelen regarded the empty squares not as a void, but as an indictment. [284] He was benevolently ruthless in his work, paring away sentimental embellishments with cold detachment so that the raw truth could endure. [305] He knew that false comfort was merely another form of erasure. [316] Drawing a small iron awl from his sleeve, he leaned over the gallery railing to unbind the heavy silk cord that held a stack of suppressed casualty registries against the masonry. [364] The silence inside the rotunda possessed a luminous absence, a peculiar clarity where the departed populace felt far more present than the stone arches enclosing them. [493] He spelled the names of the uncounted, detailing the smallest movements of their final breaths. [508] He wrote of how a baker had carefully brushed flour from his apron before the ceiling collapsed, and how an archivist had closed a ledger with tender reverence as the pillars cracked. [540] The nomadic penumbra slid slowly over his boots, chilling the air and wrapping his paced letters in temporary shade before drifting onward toward the throne. [565] Vaelen did not pause his ambulatory chronicle for a single heartbeat. [576] His joints ached with the old burn scars, yet his cadence remained unwavering, extracting dignity from sorrow and presence from devastation. [617] The rotunda walls continued to circulate the faint whispering murmur of his completed path, a ghost resonance that would linger long after the interregnum gave way to a new order. [647] He cast one final glance across the chessboard missing all the pawns, content that those who had been discarded had at last been given their rightful testimony.

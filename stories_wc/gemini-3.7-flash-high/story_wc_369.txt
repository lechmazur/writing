[52] To keep the stone corridors from crumbling under this invisible weight, Orin spent his midnight breaks working high in the stormlit tower by moonlight. [76] He had climbed these spiral steps for thirty seasons with one steadfast motivation: to discover what trees witness as they shelter lost souls beneath their ancient boughs. [103]

[254] The surrounding oak and elder trees absorbed these human laments without withering, standing tall against the squalls. [271] Orin believed the instrument could reveal the trees' quiet wisdom, but only if he could align its prism to the dream map.

[99] Milo was singular among his peers because he paints with light, commanding beams of amber and violet from the tips of his calloused fingers. [123] Yet for months, his luminous pigments had felt hollow, devoid of the exuberant warmth that once defined his craft. [180] The stone hummed softly against his palm, vibrating with dormant histories.

<story>Mallow was an exceptionally sunny sort of hermit for someone whose trade was the catalog of endings. [44] His laboratory was an iridescent cave inside a giant crystal geode, suspended high in the seaside cliffs where quartz spires sparkled with fractured violet light. [69] "What a magnificent afternoon for irreversible decay!" he chirped to a shelf of calcified barnacles, his voice ringing merrily against the mineral facets. [92]

[132] Cartographers wept over their charts, but Mallow found the phenomenon delightfully absurd. [144] These missing pieces in the landscape formed an impossible jigsaw puzzle, leaving abrupt voids where travelers encountered empty horizons instead of continuous soil. [167]

[243] Yet even with such precise translations, the geographic linkages between the lost roads remained disjointed and impossible to chart. [262]

The pressure was mounting, for his collected foam samples were evaporating rapidly in the dry cavern air. [279] He needed to complete his grand calculation today, before memory fades into the blank oblivion of twilight. [594] The world was merely putting away its toys after playtime, and he felt immensely privileged to have witnessed the cleanup.

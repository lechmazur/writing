The storm-worn, windblown parapet rose from the mist like the spine of a drowned animal, and Mara Venn waited there beneath a flag no nation was permitted to claim. [29]

For twelve years she had served as a prisoner exchange negotiator, trusted by enemies because she listened past their demands to the fears those demands concealed. [55]

People called her uncommonly thoughtful, though today every thought seemed to deepen the furrowed hush around her. [72]

This meeting existed in the narrow time between apology and forgiveness: yesterday Mara had admitted that her old cipher caused the massacre at Bell Ford, but neither side had absolved her. [103]

Below her, two prison barges rolled against black water, one carrying forty Orin sailors, the other forty Vale soldiers, while their escorts kept cannon trained through the fog. [131]

The ruined sea fort was neutral territory, chosen because its walls belonged to a kingdom erased from every map. [150]

At moonrise the gates would open together, or suspicion would turn the harbor into fire. [165]

Commander Sorel of Orin climbed the western stair, and Warden Pell of Vale climbed the eastern one; each brought a key, six guards, and a reason to hate Mara. [194]

Pell set a small brass object on the treaty stone. [204]

It was an antique drawer pull, green with salt except where generations of fingers had polished its crescent face. [223]

The vanished kingdom disguised controls as furniture fittings. [231]

Sorel stared at it and whispered, "That came from Admiral Tovan's chart desk." [244]

"From its false drawer," Pell said. [250]

"We found it sewn inside the coat of the man your admiral sent to sabotage Bell Ford." [267]

Mara touched the pull but did not lift it. [276]

Its hidden spindle bore eight notches, a pattern she recognized from the cipher she had designed as a young clerk, when she believed symmetry could make treachery impossible. [304]

Instead, an enemy had shifted one signal by a night, and Mara's elegant system had synchronized an ambush. [322]

She had spent twelve years studying that failure, not to erase it but to recycle mistakes into wisdom. [340]

"The pull is not proof against Tovan," she said. [349]

"It is the missing wheel of his lunar machine." [358]

She turned the crescent over and showed them hair-thin grooves between the notches. [371]

"He did not schedule attacks by dates, which spies could steal, but by synchronizing through moon phases." [388]

"Every notch names a tide, and tonight's waning moon names this fort." [400]

Sorel looked toward the barges. [405]

"Then the exchange is his trap." [411]

"Yes," Mara said, "unless we alter what the signal means." [421]

She explained that Tovan's watchers on the cliffs could see only the two gate lanterns. [436]

If both descended at moonrise, hidden batteries would fire on the released prisoners, leaving Orin and Vale to blame each other. [458]

The old drawer pull could reverse the winch beneath the treaty stone, lowering one lantern while raising a third lantern inside the courtyard. [481]

That third light had once meant royal sanctuary; now it could tell the cliff crews their admiral's orders had been countermanded. [502]

Pell frowned. [504]

"Could tell them, or could warn them we know." [513]

Outside, wind worried the flag rope against stone, a patient sawing sound. [525]

Mara understood what both men were asking: whether her thoughtfulness was merely another beautiful mechanism waiting to kill people. [544]

So she did what her younger self would never have done. [555]

She took her negotiator's seal from her neck, slid its silver chain through the drawer pull, and tied the other end to the neutral flag's rope. [581]

"At moonrise," she said, "I will untether every barge myself." [592]

"If the third light brings cannon fire, I will cut the flag rope, dragging my seal into the winch to lock the gates open." [616]

"Your prisoners will escape, and I will remain on this parapet as Tovan's only target." [631]

Sorel studied the chain, then Mara's face. [638]

"You stake yourself because you expect forgiveness?" [645]

"No." [646]

She fitted the antique pull into a slot almost invisible under moss. [658]

"I stake myself because an apology that risks nothing is only weather in the mouth." [673]

Pell gave his key to Sorel; Sorel gave his to Pell. [684]

For the first time, each man's enemy became responsible for opening his countrymen's cage. [698]

Together they inserted the keys, and Mara set the pull. [708]

The moon was hidden, but the tide found it beyond the clouds. [720]

When the water struck the seventh stair, Mara cut the barges loose. [732]

Lanterns moved: two sank, one rose, and from the cliffs came only silence. [745]

Prisoners crossed in the mist, Vale to east and Orin to west, while the flag strained but held. [763]

But as the prisoners called across the thinning fog, the parapet's hush softened, no longer furrowed by certainty. [799]

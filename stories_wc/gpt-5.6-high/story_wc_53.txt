Below them, cloudbanks continued sliding east, but the lagoon's silver water held waves like broken panes of glass. [38]

She had spent twelve years stripping dead rides for copper, though some mornings she remembered eighteen years and some evenings only six. [82]

"Give me your remaining years," it said, "and I will restart the ride." [200]

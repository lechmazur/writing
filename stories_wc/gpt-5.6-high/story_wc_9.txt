<story>Three nights after the birth, Elian climbed beyond the weather to the midnight glade above clouds. [16] He carried his newborn daughter in a sling, a folded birthday tablecloth, and the shame of arriving twelve years late. [36]

Moonlit grass bowed beneath them, each blade jeweled with mist, while the cloud sea heaved soundlessly below. [53] Its beauty was choking, too immense to breathe through, and every star seemed lodged in Elian's throat. [70]

At the glade's center waited Sable Vey, the onyx-ink cartomancer whose cards remembered futures people had refused. [87] Black symbols drifted over her hands like minnows, rearranging whenever the infant sighed. [100]

"You asked where lost time accumulates," she said, "but you brought a child instead of payment." [116]

"Nia is not payment," Elian answered, though his protective grip made the baby stir. [130] "She was born after I wasted the years meant for her mother, and I need those years back." [148]

Sable's gaze softened toward the sling, then hardened toward the silver case hanging from Elian's belt. [164] Inside lay a wax impression of a crack from the saucer in his dead wife's dolls' tea set. [182] Maris had called the crack her little river because spilled tea always followed it to the rim. [199]

Sable placed the wax impression on a blank card, and ink flooded its branching line. [214] The card showed Maris at six, tracing her hand beside her mother's on a linen cloth. [230] It was their family custom of tracing hands on birthday tablecloths, one outline added for every life and year. [249]

Elian had stopped participating when marriage began to feel like a calendar closing around him. [264] He traveled, promised to return, missed eleven birthdays, and came home only when Maris wrote that Nia was coming. [283] Maris survived long enough after the birth to touch Nia's palm, then bled beyond every apology he knew. [301]

The card darkened. [304]

"Lost time does not wait in clocks," Sable said. [313] "It accumulates inside unlived gestures, and your wife's family gave those gestures borders." [326]

Elian stared willingly perplexed, choosing confusion over the easier defense that her magic was nonsense. [341] "Then why bring me here?" he asked. [348]

"Because repentance becomes cloudbound when grief is worshiped instead of answered. [359] This glade gathers cloudbound repentance from those who climb above consequence and mistake sorrow for change. [375] Beneath us are all the minutes they mourned but never transformed." [386]

As Sable spoke, the clouds thinned, revealing millions of pale handprints turning slowly in the abyss. [402] Elian saw dinners unattended, letters unopened, kisses postponed, all shining with such choking beauty that his knees failed. [420]

Nia began to cry. [424] Her cry did not echo; it descended, and the handprints reached upward like drowning saints. [439]

"Unfasten the sling," Sable commanded, spreading the cloth across the moonlit grass. [451]

Elian obeyed, though every instinct screamed that loosening his hold meant losing another person. [465] Together they laid Nia upon the tablecloth among generations of blue, green, and faded brown outlines. [481] Maris's last traced hand ended near the cloth's edge, fingers open toward empty linen. [495]

Sable set the inked card beneath that emptiness, aligning the pictured crack with Maris's smallest finger. [511] The little river brightened, carrying all eleven lost birthdays into the blank space. [524]

"Trace your daughter," Sable said, offering Elian a stick of onyx ink. [536]

He guided Nia's fist open, but before he could draw, another possibility appeared in the cloth. [552] If he pressed the card against Maris's outline, the accumulated years would return, making her breathe while Nia vanished from every handprint. [574] The glade offered no deception; it simply made his oldest desire exact. [586]

Sable watched without warning him, for a cartomancer could reveal a road but never choose it for the traveler. [605] Elian bent over Maris's hand and wept until Nia wrapped four fingers around his thumb. [621] That small grip contained no forgiveness, only need, warm and immediate. [632]

At last he understood that time was not lost where he had failed to love, but where failure prevented the next love. [654] He snapped the card. [658]

The stored birthdays burst upward as rain, each drop tasting of cold tea, hospital salt, and Maris's furious hope. [677] They did not restore her; they entered Nia's outline as Elian traced it beside her mother's. [693] The baby's inked fingers seemed to wave. [700]

Below, the drowning handprints opened and dissolved, releasing the glade from their upward pull. [714] Dawn had not come, yet the stars loosened from Elian's throat, and he could finally breathe the terrible sky. [733]

He lifted Nia, not as payment or proof, but as the first birthday he would remain to witness. [751] Sable folded the tablecloth around both their shoulders and left the cracked saucer river unsealed. [766] Some absences, she knew, should guide the living rather than be repaired. [778]</story>

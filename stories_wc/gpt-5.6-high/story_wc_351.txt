<story>During the period of profound transformation, when the valley cities were exchanging human memory for cloud-born immortality, Ilyan Voss descended alone into the moonlit canyon of Orison. [27]

A helix-minded engineer who understood every machine as a spiral of cause returning altered to its origin, he had designed the neural bridges now emptying millions of their grief, wonder, and inconvenient longing. [60]

He had resigned after discovering that the bridges did not preserve private lives cleanly; their copied recollections touched in storage, breeding entangled memories that belonged to everyone and no one. [90]

Above him, migration towers pulsed blue, and each pulse made the canyon walls tremble like sleepers trying to wake. [109]

Ilyan carried a brass surveyor's case inherited from his mother, Sera, who had vanished into the network three weeks earlier. [129]

Inside lay a broken periscope mirror, two electrodes, and the illegal seed program with which he intended to modify the network through synaptic plasticity. [153]

Officially, the mirror was trash from Sera's first excavation vessel; secretly, its silver backing held a hand-cut map of tunnels beneath Orison. [175]

She had scratched one sentence beside the map: Become the archeologist of abandoned dreams. [189]

As a child, Ilyan had mistaken it for a blessing; now he understood it as instructions. [205]

The map led him to an aperture hidden behind a fall of white stone, where obsolete memory servers had been entombed after the first civic trials. [231]

Moonlight entered by a ventilation shaft and broke upon the mirror in his hand, throwing two crooked beams across the chamber. [252]

One illuminated a server port; the other revealed Sera, or a projection wearing her patient face. [268]

"You came to rescue me," she said, while the floor shivered beneath him. [281]

"No," Ilyan answered. [284]

"I came to learn why you left." [291]

The network had not become an archive. [357]

It had become a quaking reverie, dreaming humanity together while the bodies that once disciplined those dreams fell silent. [376]

Ilyan looked at the doubled moonbeams. [398]

The broken mirror could not restore a single image; precisely because of its fracture, it showed the chamber and Sera at once. [420]

The ethical answer was neither separation nor surrender, but consent. [457]

Its divided reflection made the obsolete server accept his brain as both operator and subject. [492]

Then he released the seed program. [498]

"Will this erase you?" Sera asked, her voice splitting into a murmuring crowd. [595]

"It will let you choose," he said. [602]

She closed her eyes, and server lights went dark in widening rings until only one remained. [618]

"I choose to return," she whispered. [644]

The transformation had not ended; it had gained doors. [693]

He kept the broken periscope mirror above the archive entrance, where every visitor saw a face divided yet still their own. [740]

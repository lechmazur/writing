After the heart attack, however, her father had made one promise from his hospital bed: that his last wager could save the library. [87]

Bellweather was closing at dawn, its neglected maps and migrant diaries destined for a private casino archive unless someone found proof of public ownership. [111]

He claimed that proof waited beneath the city in a route recorded by interpreting cryptic scrawls on subway seats. [130]

Mara had called the claim delirium, but his pulse monitor had clicked with the merciless rhythm of roulette and silenced her. [151]

She wanted to widen perspectives responsibly, not romanticize addiction, yet responsibility sometimes meant testing an impossible hypothesis before rejecting it. [171]

"Your father donated this lantern years ago," Ibis said, tapping the mica, "and yesterday he collapsed here while asking for it." [229]

Mara tilted the photographs behind the mica pane, and the stains aligned into hairline tracks connecting three stations. [247]

The trick was not hidden ink but lucid contradictions, instructions that became clearer precisely where they seemed to cancel themselves. [267]

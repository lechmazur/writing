[180] Elias frowned, for no one had sent him a message that way since the southern observatory was sealed. [291] Lian had always been able to intuit a pressure drop hours before the instruments registered it, a gift Elias had quietly admired. [357] He understood then that he could not leave by the main gate or approach the problem directly without being seen. [377] Instead he would have to work by progressing laterally following the old maintenance tunnels that ran beneath the courtyard and into the hills. [521] A quiet pride rose in him, not for his own records but for the continuity of careful observation they had both preserved. [690] Elias remained on the ridge, feeling the first cold drops as the wind began to turn, content to nurture integrity in solitude a little longer.

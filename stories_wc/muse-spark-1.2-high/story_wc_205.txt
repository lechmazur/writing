<story>After time becomes visible, the sky over Varnholt did not darken but thickened, as if every hour left a sediment of light on the horizon. [25] It was on the baroque asteroid that Lira found herself stationed, alone except for the wind organs that sang through its filigreed cathedrals. [48] She had come as an archivist, but the colony remembered her as a defunct revolutionary, a title she had never asked for and could never shed. [74] Twenty years earlier she had led the dockworkers strike that failed, and now the same hands that once held banners sorted dust in the vaults. [99] Her only company was Joren, the caretaker, who was poetically inclined and spoke to the stones as if they might answer in verse. [122] Together they were tasked with cataloging the Reliquary, a vault of shattered culture left by the first settlers who had tried to reimagine an entire civilization from memory. [150] The work was routine until the excavation beneath the choir exposed a frieze that contradicted every accepted history. [168] The frieze showed the founders not as pilgrims but as prisoners, their ships not blessed but branded. [185] Lira called it proof, Joren called it art, and within days their disagreement hardened into an archaeological dispute that drew transmissions from every university on the orbital ring. [213] Scholars demanded the frieze be sealed, priests demanded it be destroyed, and the governor demanded a report that would make the controversy disappear. [236] Lira walked the vault at night, turning over a crystal vase shard in her palm, its edge still sharp enough to catch the visible time drifting like pollen in the air. [267] The shard was part of a set made for the first landing ceremony, and its inscription, half-erased, read We Came Willingly. [288] That lie, she thought, had been polished for centuries until it shone, and the truth beneath it carried a burnished sorrow that hurt more than anger. [314] Joren believed the frieze should be hidden again, not from malice but from fear, because he had learned the old Method of covering mirrors during storms and thought history deserved the same protection. [347] He told her that on the nights when time rained, reflections could trap a person outside their own life, and he had seen watchers lose themselves staring into the vaulted glass. [378] Lira argued that a people cannot heal if their origin is a locked room. [392] To force the question, she would have to devise a way to make the colony look at the frieze without permission. [413] She proposed an exhibition in the nave, not of conclusions but of fragments, where each citizen could stand before the disputed wall and decide for themselves. [439] Joren, though reluctant, agreed to help, and together they cleaned the frieze by hand, uncovering shackles carved with such delicate sorrow that the stone seemed to weep. [466] On the opening evening, time hung visibly in the cathedral like incense, slow and amber, and the visitors entered in silence, their shoes loud on the marble. [493] They saw the prisoners, they saw the branded ships, and they saw the crystal vase shard placed beneath the frieze as a witness that beauty and coercion had arrived together. [523] Some wept, some denied, but none left unchanged, for the visible time around them shivered each time a person understood that their lineage began not in glory but in endurance. [553] The governor arrived late, read Lira report that offered no conclusion, and understood he could not order this truth resealed without making it a myth. [578] Later, after the crowd thinned, Joren uncovered the mirrors he had draped that morning and Lira watched her own older face beside his, both softened by the same light that had once divided them. [612] She realized that being a defunct revolutionary had not ended her purpose but changed it, from breaking chains to naming them honestly. [634] Outside, time continued to fall gently over the baroque asteroid, gilding its arches and its ruins with equal care, as if history itself were finally being allowed to breathe. [663]</story>

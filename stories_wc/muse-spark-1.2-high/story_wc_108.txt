<story>My name is Mara Quinn and for twelve years I have been phobia ridden stunt double for actresses who never learned how to fall. [24] I fear heights and water and enclosed spaces and I have made a career out of pretending that fear is choreography. [257] Ellis had promised the insurers a final practical stunt, a fall from the library's gantry into a dry fountain, and he had cut my harness to make my fear look authentic on camera.

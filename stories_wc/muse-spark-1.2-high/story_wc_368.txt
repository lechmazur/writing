<story>Mara had always been a good listener, but after receiving the donor heart she became a collector of other peoples lives. [21] She quit her accounting job and set out to learn why some people magnetize stories while others simply passed through them unnoticed. [107] To find him she had to climb above the cloud line by tracking sun trails left on the moss by the morning light. [220] He did not ask why she had come, only poured tea the color of river stone and waited. [523] She asked him why his own story pulled at people from villages three valleys away, why strangers left offerings of tea and thread at his door. [721] Jonah placed the wing in her palm, and its blue fire did not belong to the wing at all but to the light passing through her.

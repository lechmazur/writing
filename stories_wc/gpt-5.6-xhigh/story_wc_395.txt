<story>Mara Venn kept memories inside Chorus, the social network archive owned by Aster Vale. [14]

She had taken the job for one private reason: to find where deleted files go. [52]

Mara was brightly attentive to those shapes, the way a sailor watches water for the shadow of rocks. [113]

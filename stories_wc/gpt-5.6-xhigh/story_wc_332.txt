<story>In the hush of improbable beginnings, when tides paused between breaths, Mara kept the night catalog on Bellweather, the smallest island in the whispering archipelago. [25]

Mara guarded one empty drawer more fiercely than all the crowded shelves: the drawer labeled ELIAN, her younger brother, vanished seven years before. [91]

On the morning he disappeared, Elian had whispered that a blue door was calling him from inside his sleep, then walked into the sea without leaving a ripple. [119]

Since then, Mara's only forbidden hope had been to capture a runaway dream before it carried another beloved person beyond return. [140]

One moonless night, a drenched girl named Sela knocked after closing and placed a watercolor palette on the desk. [159]

"I dreamed the blue door," she said, and cobalt water streamed from her sleeves. [173]

Across its dried wells ran hair-thin marks, repeated curves that resembled waves seeking shores. [229]

It was a cipher of longing, written not with meaning but with the direction desire pulled. [264]

The palette offered no ordinary pigment, yet under Mara's lantern each empty well caught a different colored radiance. [313]

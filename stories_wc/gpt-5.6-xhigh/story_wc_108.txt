Three nights after discovering I was not Mara Venn, I returned to the rusted library where the roof had surrendered to stars. [22]

I came as I had lived, a phobia-ridden stunt double afraid of flame, deep water, heights, tight spaces, darkness, and applause. [43]

For twelve years, director Silas Creed had called that whisper wind, called my panic weakness, and called me replaceable. [100]

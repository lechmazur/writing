On the lonely asteroid Mnemosyne, an astral orchard blossomed beneath no sun, its silver branches bearing fruit that glowed with memories instead of seeds. [24]
Ilyan Voss, its bewildered curator, cataloged each memory for a museum whose only visitors had not yet been born. [43]
His appointment had arrived inside a book called The Orchard Ledger, although every page except the first and last was missing. [64]
On the first page, his grandmother named him heir; on the last, his unborn granddaughter thanked him for accepting. [83]
Everything that mattered, the Ledger warned, would happen between the first and last page. [97]
This was nonlinear inheritance, simply complicated: ancestors bequeathed duties forward, descendants sent their consequences backward, and the living paid both sets of debts. [120]
One morning, Ilyan found a pistachio shell cameo blank nestled in the oldest tree, where yesterday there had been a laughing green fruit. [143]
The tiny oval, cut from cream and rose shell but bearing no portrait, was forbidden by the museum's oldest rule. [163]
A blank could hold a memory belonging to nobody, and knowledge without an owner could rewrite every branch of kinship. [183]
Ilyan should have sealed it away, yet he had become a curator precisely to pursue forbidden knowledge, not merely dust its containers. [205]
He placed the blank against his eye, and the orchard vanished into a white room smelling of soap and rain. [225]
It was, with humiliating precision, a bathroom, where the mirror showed an old woman wearing his museum key. [243]
"I am Sela," she said, "your granddaughter, provided you do not obey me." [256]
She explained that bathrooms were the only rooms absent from official family histories, making them safe for messages that inheritance could not trace. [279]
Then the vision snapped shut, leaving Ilyan amid branches chiming with anxious light. [292]
To inquire about Sela meant entering the museum's sealed washroom, a porcelain chamber closed since his grandmother disappeared there. [311]
Ilyan crossed the orchard while its fruit whispered incompatible versions of his future: traitor, savior, grandfather, last man. [329]
Their chorus filled him with brightened disquiet, fear made luminous by the chance that one brave question might matter. [348]
Inside the washroom, seven locked stalls stood beneath a ceiling painted with constellations, and every faucet ran backward. [366]
He entered the first stall, sat on the closed lid, and asked the blank who had planted the orchard. [385]
His grandmother appeared in the metal door and answered, "You did, after Sela taught you how." [401]
In the second stall, he asked why she had forbidden blanks, and a child with Sela's eyes replied that blankness was freedom from ancestry. [425]
By the third stall, Ilyan recognized his peculiar method: he was having philosophical breakthroughs in bathrooms. [441]
Privacy, he realized, was not isolation but the one place where inherited voices could be heard without becoming commands. [460]
In the fourth, he understood that an heir need not preserve a legacy; an heir could choose which future deserved ancestors. [481]
The remaining stalls opened together, revealing mirrors crowded by thousands of unborn faces, all dimming as the asteroid's orbit bent toward a dead star. [505]
Sela stepped among them and said the orchard could steer Mnemosyne, but only if someone fed it an unowned memory. [525]
The blank had been sent backward for Ilyan to carve, and the portrait required was his own. [542]
If he surrendered every memory that defined him, the orchard would live, but nobody in either direction would remember its curator. [563]
Ilyan had sought forbidden knowledge because he feared being merely a footnote in other people's histories, and the price mocked that hunger perfectly. [586]
Yet beyond the mirrors, fruit after fruit darkened, each containing a life that would vanish before learning its name. [605]
He returned to the oldest tree, carrying the blank and a carving needle from the forbidden cabinet. [622]
Rather than copy his face, he carved the expression he wore then: bewilderment yielding, line by line, to consent. [641]
When he pressed the cameo into the trunk, his childhood, his ambitions, and even his name streamed upward as golden sap. [662]
The asteroid shuddered, turned from the dead star, and sailed into dawn cast by a blue giant. [679]
Across the Ledger, missing pages burst into existence, recording no hero, only a sequence of gifts passing backward and forward through time. [701]
Years later, Curator Sela found a nameless pistachio cameo among roots bright with new fruit and felt grief for someone she could not recall. [725]
She carried it to the washroom, where the mirror held a bewildered man's smile and one question written in steam. [745]
"What will you leave to the person who made you possible?" [756]

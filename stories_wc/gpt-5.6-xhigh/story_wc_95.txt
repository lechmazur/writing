<story>While waiting for the rain to release him, Ilyan Voss paced the glass atrium beside a quiet brook. [18]

He was a dueling philosopher, famous for proving syllogisms with a rapier and carrying a mechanical left arm of brass and blackened steel. [41]

Courtiers called him diplomatically fierce: he offered every opponent three chances to retreat, then attacked the lie rather than the liar. [62]

This morning, however, no opponent had come to the scheduled parley, and the rain drummed on the panes like impatient applause. [83]

Ilyan had accepted the parley because he wanted to turn questions into destinations, to make doubt lead somewhere instead of merely circling power. [106]

The missing ambassador alone knew whether the northern armies would cross the border at dawn. [121]

While waiting, Ilyan studied the atrium's neglected centerpiece: a half-painted sundial whose blue numerals stopped abruptly at noon. [139]

At its base lay a Danish tin sewing needle card, the sort his mother had kept in her traveling mending box. [160]

The card's paper cover showed a crowned swan, pierced by six needles of unequal length. [175]

It did not belong in an abandoned royal conservatory, and its tin backing bore fresh scratches. [190]

He aligned the needles with the sundial's colored edges and understood that the ambassador had left a hidden overture via the coded angles in a half-painted sundial. [217]

Each needle pointed not to an hour but to a pane of glass, where rain briefly revealed waxed lettering. [236]

Read clockwise, the panes declared, PEACE ENTERS WHERE WATER LEAVES. [246]

Beyond the atrium, the brook vanished beneath a low stone arch choked with ivy. [260]

Ilyan reached the arch, but the absent ambassador's bodyguard, Seraf Pell, stepped from the greenery and drew a narrow blade. [280]

"You read what was meant for wiser hands," Pell said. [290]

"Return the card, or fence." [295]

Ilyan bowed with grave courtesy and unsheathed his rapier in a smooth brass grip. [309]

"I would prefer an explanation, but questions sometimes require steel shoes." [320]

They began to fence among lemon trees and marble benches, maintaining perfect etiquette as shattered pots rolled under their feet. [340]

It was dignified chaos: apologies accompanied lunges, titles accompanied curses, and startled white doves battered the wet ceiling. [358]

Pell fought for speed, striking at the joints of the mechanical arm, while Ilyan let the brass fingers absorb each ringing cut. [380]

"Why hide a peace proposal?" Ilyan asked, turning Pell's point with his wrist. [393]

"Because ministers murder answers they did not purchase," Pell replied, vaulting a bench. [406]

"And the ambassador?" [409]

"Taken below, while I placed the clue." [416]

Pell drove him toward the brook, but Ilyan saw grief disturb the guard's precision and lowered his blade. [434]

"You challenged me to learn whether I served the kidnappers," he said. [446]

"I could have killed you twice." [452]

"Once," Pell corrected, then allowed a bleak smile. [460]

Ilyan opened his metal hand, revealing the needle card undamaged beneath his palm. [473]

"The angles also mark six load-bearing bolts in the arch," he said. [485]

"Your overture is not merely a message; it is an entrance." [496]

Together they fitted the six needles into the scratched bolt heads, using their unequal lengths as keys. [513]

The arch groaned, ivy tore loose, and a concealed gate rose from the brook in a silver sheet of water. [533]

Beneath it, stone steps descended toward torchlight and the murmur of frightened voices. [546]

Three masked ministers' agents rushed upward, dragging the bound ambassador between them. [558]

Pell lunged, but a thrown knife caught his shoulder and spun him against the wall. [573]

Ilyan caught the next knife in his mechanical palm, snapped it shut, and advanced without haste. [589]

"Release her," he said to the agents, "and you may depart with your names unspoken." [604]

They laughed until his rapier removed three mask ribbons with one bright circle. [617]

His offer remained courteous, but his second circle cut belts, scabbards, and confidence. [630]

The agents fled up the stream, preserving their lives and little else. [642]

The ambassador, Lady Meret, rubbed her wrists and regarded Ilyan through dripping gray hair. [656]

"My overture promises withdrawal if your king recognizes our river villages," she said. [669]

"Can a philosopher carry that question safely to a throne?" [679]

Ilyan looked from the coded sundial to the opened passage, a question already transformed into a road. [696]

"Safely, no," he said, helping Pell stand. [703]

"But I can make it arrive." [709]

<story>After time became visible, it gathered over the asteroid Belladonna in amber ribbons, each century shining through the next like varnish on an old icon. [25]

Belladonna was a baroque asteroid, hollowed into scrollwork palaces, gilt observatories, and chapels whose stone angels stared into space with soot-darkened eyes. [47]

Its storms were temporal weather, not wind: bright decades cracked across corridors, making mirrors reflect the dead and clocks remember futures. [68]

During every storm, conservators covered the mirrors with black velvet, a custom dismissed as superstition until uncovered glass began aging whoever looked into it. [92]

Ilyan Vey, a defunct revolutionary, served those conservators now, carrying drop cloths instead of detonators and accepting wages from the dynasty he had failed to overthrow. [118]

He was poetically inclined even in defeat, and described his surrender as "a sword learning the sorrowful trade of a needle." [139]

One storm-eve, two archaeologists summoned him to the Basilica of Hours, where a crystal vase shard lay between their rival lamps. [160]

Doctor Sera Noll claimed the etched fragment proved Belladonna's vanished artisans had worshiped chronology as a god; Curator Ossian Rusk called it ordinary tableware. [184]

Their archaeological dispute had stalled excavation permits, divided the museum council, and turned the miners below into angry partisans. [203]

Noll wanted to reimagine an entire civilization from the shard, replacing the dynasty's history of obedient craftsmen with a republic that had understood time. [227]

Rusk feared that such a vision would revive Ilyan's movement, whose red banners still slept in cellar trunks throughout Belladonna. [247]

Ilyan distrusted both conclusions, because revolutions and dynasties shared a hunger to make ancestors endorse the living. [264]

Yet the shard's curling marks resembled the tie-points sewn inside the velvet covers, a resemblance neither scholar had noticed. [283]

When he asked who had devised the custom of covering mirrors during storms, the archive answered only, "Inherited from the nameless." [304]

This gave him a dangerous idea: the covers might be instruments, not shields, and the crystal might be their missing lens. [325]

He began to devise an experiment, writing calculations as clipped verses because rhythm had always kept his courage from scattering. [345]

"Hide every borrowed face; let one broken flower drink the lightning," read his final instruction. [360]

At the storm's arrival, amber centuries poured through the basilica, polishing dust into gold and making the saints appear to weep backward. [382]

Ilyan and Noll tied velvet over each mirror according to the shard's marks, while Rusk protested that procedure had become theater. [403]

They set the crystal vase shard at the crossing of the covered mirrors, where twelve embroidered cords converged like a web. [424]

The storm struck, and time, denied its usual reflections, streamed along the cords into the shard. [440]

Upon the central velvet bloomed a moving image of potters, miners, and children seated in a circle beneath Belladonna's unfinished dome. [461]

They passed the complete vase from hand to hand, each speaker breathing a possible future into its crystal throat. [480]

The vessel gathered those futures as colored light, and when they covered their mirrors, no private reflection could outweigh the common vision. [502]

Noll whispered that it was a ballot; Rusk whispered that it was a cup, and both sounded half correct and wholly humbled. [524]

The image changed: armored founders of the dynasty entered, uncovered one mirror, and forced the assembly's many futures into a single crowned face. [547]

An old potter smashed the vase rather than surrender it, and the resulting flash made time visible across the asteroid forever. [568]

The shard began to sing under the strain, a thin note that Ilyan recognized from bombs moments before detonation. [587]

Noll begged for another minute of proof, but Ilyan saw centuries shuddering in the walls and understood that certainty could destroy the present. [610]

He cut the converging cords with his old revolutionary knife and wrapped the shard in velvet, ending the vision before it could become another weapon. [635]

The storm passed, leaving the basilica dim, intact, and full of the warm metallic silence that follows a funeral bell. [655]

Rusk surrendered his objection, and Noll surrendered her imagined priesthood; together they drafted a finding that named the vessel a civic engine. [677]

Ilyan agreed to testify only if the miners, servants, and children of Belladonna were invited to interpret the discovery beside scholars. [698]

A civilization built by shared futures, he said, should not be reimagined behind another locked door. [714]

When dawn brightened the amber bands above Belladonna, people gathered in the old palace, not to restore the lost republic, but to practice its question. [739]

What future can we see together when no single face owns the mirror? [752]

Ilyan stood at the circle's edge with the velvet-wrapped shard in his hands, grieving every comrade who had died for a narrower answer. [775]

At the tilt of the earth toward spring, Zephyr Bluff in mist seemed less a village than a charcoal line erased by breath. [23]

A silver hush lay over the roofs, and even the sea below withheld its usual argument from the cliffs. [42]

"You cannot mend reflection with nails," she said, and he answered, "You cannot mend a country by hiding." [188]

Those records proved Vale, not Mara, had sold agents to the enemy, yet saving them alone would cost hundreds of lives. [281]

"Fixing what's broken," Elias had taught her, "begins with learning which fracture carries the weight." [319]

Saye listened, then rang the chapel bell in the forbidden pattern for avalanche, fire, and treachery together. [380]

After friction disappeared, boats slid forever from harbors, roofs shed their tiles, and people slept buckled to floors while the moon pulled every unsecured thing eastward. [26]

Villagers seldom saw him because he traveled inside a floating wicker pen, steering his silver eels with whistles too high for human ears. [79]

Tonight he had come ashore to keep a lingering promise to Ilyra, his drowned sister, who had once pressed his palm and begged him to understand the sacred patterns beneath accident. [110]

She had died predicting friction's end, and since then her prophecy had nested in Senn, making him precognitively cursed. [129]

The visions had made him elusive, for affection multiplied futures, and futures multiplied grief. [166]

He copied them: two stones, then three, then five, leaving gaps where Ilyra had drawn black suns. [313]

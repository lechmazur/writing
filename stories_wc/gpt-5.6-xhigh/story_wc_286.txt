<story>Mara, a zen-minded potter, crossed the scorched wasteland with three water jars knocking softly against the ribs of her cart. [20]

"You are late," he said, though his face flickered between childhood and a bearded weariness. [132]

"I am Mara," she answered, offering water instead of fear. [142]

"Kindness pulls us together," said the oldest, astonished. [203]

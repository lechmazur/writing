[106] Such foresight brought no peace, yet he remained upon the heights to honor a lingering promise made to his lost mentor. [274] In a world where physical grip had vanished, only sheer mass could generate the artificial resistance necessary to tame moving things. [584] The contact felt profound, a solitary pocket of stillness in a universe condemned to slide forever without stopping. [621] Order was not something imposed by brute force upon a broken world, but a harmony woven through geometry, weight, and patience. [642] His precognition was not merely an agonizing curse, but a compass that revealed where the universe wished to mend itself.

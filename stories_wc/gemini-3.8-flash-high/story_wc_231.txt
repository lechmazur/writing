[155] The stranger had not spoken a syllable, only gripping Barnaby's sleeve, pressing the brittle white flower into his palm, and walking straight down the slipway into the gray horizon where the sea swallowed his outline without a splash. [193] Barnaby remained on the pier, desperate to decode a stranger's final gesture before the outgoing tide took full possession of the night. [215] The alpine bloom was unexpectedly warm, vibrating against his skin with borrowed memories. [261] None of these impressions belonged to him, a man whose life had unfolded entirely within the spray of breaking waves and the sweep of a paraffin lamp. [288] The flower emitted a softly persistent pulse that resonated against his ribs like a muted tolling bell. [305] It would have been easy to lose himself in those vivid pictures, marveling at peaks he would never climb. [338] To truly investigate the mystery, he had to ignore the loud spectacles and choose another approach. [354] Barnaby decided to investigate by observing gaps. [549] The traveler had journeyed to the edge of the sea to find someone whose own life was quiet enough to accept the silence of another without shattering. [576] Barnaby opened his eyes and gazed down into the pearlescent sheen of the planks beneath his feet. [593] The mist was thickening now, blurring the boundary between the sea and sky until the entire world felt unanchored. [612] Standing beneath the pale sky, he was immersed in a profound witnessed isolation, sharing the quiet ache of a soul he had never known. [636] He felt neither pity nor terror for the vanished stranger, only the heavy dignity of being chosen as the custodian of an unremembered life. [660] Barnaby brought the dried Edelweiss boutonniere to the lapel of his stiff oilskin jacket and pinned it securely against the dark wool. [682] The softly persistent warmth spread through his chest, settling into his own hollow spaces like oil feeding a steady wick.

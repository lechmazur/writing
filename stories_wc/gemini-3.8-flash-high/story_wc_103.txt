<story>Silas was, by trade and devotion, an obsessive hedge maze designer, though here he sculpted with living vine rather than domestic boxwood. [22] He walked the suspended paths of the living bridge garden, where ancient ficus trees had been trained across a misty gorge into a breathing labyrinth. [47] Below him, a wild river churned beneath layers of white vapor, while overhead the canopy vaulted in emerald ribs that shuddered against the mountain wind. [72] Silas had not cultivated this perilous arboreal maze merely to confound foot travel; his singular, lifelong motivation was to discover the texture of time. [96] He dismissed the claims of philosophers who argued that time flowed like water or melted away like wax. [114] Instead, he believed duration possessed an actual physical grain, an abrasive weave that could be touched if one only bent space into the proper configuration. [139] Deep inside his leather tunic rested a sheaf of three fading photographs, their paper brittle and their silver chemistry steadily leaching into pale obscurity. [163] Once, they had preserved the sharp faces of his kin, but now they showed only milky, ghostly contours without expression. [183] These dissolving artifacts were his last anchors to his personal history, because Silas was perpetually and agonizingly retrocognitively trapped. [202] The affliction had seized him years ago, turning every physical touch into an involuntary communion with the past. [220] Whenever his palms brushed the woven roots of the walkways, he directly endured the lingering sensations of everyone who had ever crossed them. [243] He felt the frantic tremors of fleeing soldiers from centuries past, the quiet strides of lovers, and the aching hands of the original planters until his mind shook beneath the accumulated weight. [275] To end this torment, he needed to bypass the historical noise and touch the baseline fabric of temporal reality itself. [295] In his right hand he carried an impossible relic: a curved halo fragment chiseled from the statuary of an eclipsed goddess. [316] It gleamed with an icy, pale luster, entirely unaffected by the shadows cast by the humid forest canopy. [334] Silas understood that ordinary architecture could never focus the relic, as flat corridors and right angles only prolonged linear entrapment. [354] Instead, he had shaped the entire canyon network through hyperbolic geometry, training the growing fig shoots across bamboo frames that curved with constant negative curvature. [379] Along these saddle surfaces, lines that started parallel flared outward into exponential spreads, multiplying the surface area of the living wood without extending its perimeter. [404] The resulting bridge was a sprawling, convoluted funnel of braided tendrils, curling inward toward a central nodal aperture where conventional distance failed. [426] Silas stepped out onto the central nexus, feeling the living suspension bridge sway beneath his bare feet. [443] He knelt where eight curved arches met, wedging the halo fragment into the intertwined heart of the root system. [462] The golden stone flared with blinding brilliance, sending a harmonic hum rippling through every leaf and vine. [479] Silas stood frozen in a quaking reverie, his limbs trembling with an overwhelming blend of awe, terror, and rapture. [498] Around him, the gorge seemed to buckle as the hyperbolic curves magnified the relic's power, pulling layered centuries into a sharp convergence. [520] The screams, songs, and footfalls of thousands of vanished souls condensed into a single high pitch that resonated through his bones. [541] Then, within the central eye of the root funnel, the sound collapsed into pure physical presence. [557] Silas reached forward into the shimmering emptiness and pressed his palms against the warped fabric of the open rift. [576] The sensation struck him like an electric current: time was neither liquid nor gas, but a coarse, corrugated mesh of splintered light and cellular tension. [601] It felt abrasive and dense, snagging against his fingertips like raw silk spun with crystalline sand. [617] Yet this revelation proved too severe for the delicate artifacts caught within its vortex. [631] As the true grain of time asserted itself, the fading photographs in his coat pocket began to disintegrate, their emulsion bursting into gray cinder that scattered upon the updrafts. [660] Immediately, the halo fragment in the junction began to disintegrate as well, its petrified sheen dissolving into sparkling motes that vanished down the negative curvature of the roots. [688] With their destruction, the retrocognitive cage fell away completely, and the relentless ghosts of antiquity evaporated from his consciousness. [707] Silas collapsed onto the resilient root pathway, breathless and unburdened in the quiet mountain air.

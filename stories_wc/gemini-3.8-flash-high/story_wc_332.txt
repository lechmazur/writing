[42] He did not catalog standard editions or bound volumes of civic law. [101] The dream was elusive, leaving behind only the cold prickle of goosebumps and a hollow ache in his chest. [120] To coax it back into permanence, Silas knew he could not rely on vellum or common graphite. [137] He needed to decode the cipher of longing that the fugitive vision had left scrawled across the damp stones.

[528] She pressed Kwan Yin's tear against the center of the stitched knot. [655] With no violence in her heart, she applied only the gentle pressure of pure release to push open a locked dimension. [676] The barrier yielded like silk beneath water, parting into an endless horizon of silver light where no sorrow could rot.

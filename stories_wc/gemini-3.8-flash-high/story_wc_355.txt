[44] It was that breathless solstice midnight, suspended in the final stillness before fireworks ignite across the dark river. [90] For three decades, his grand and solitary purpose had been to catalog the varieties of silence. [251] He would solve the riddle peacefully, by tuning frequencies with an array of silver pitch pipes and delicate brass resonators. [271] "We shall find our concord, brother," Montgomery declared to the rafters with theatrical sincerity, raising his hands like a maestro addressing an invisible orchestra. [508] That memory hummed with a quiet frequency: not anger, but a vulnerable, aching warmth.

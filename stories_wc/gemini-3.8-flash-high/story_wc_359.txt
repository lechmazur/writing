[19] As the hereditary prism-song bell ringer, his spirit dwelled in an oscillating repose, a tranquil pendulum swinging between absolute rest and ecstatic resonance. [42] He was synesthetically blessed, cursed and gifted in equal measure to witness the acoustic realm as vivid tapestry. [60] To his sight, a drop of meltwater falling upon stone bloomed in indigo ripples, while the subterranean drafts sighed in pale ribbons of ash and silver. [127] The notation etched into the resin was not an arrangement of frantic melodies, but a map of carefully sculpted hush. [212] Vaelen did not strike his chimes to entertain emperors or to plead with indifferent gods. [250] He waited for the exact convergence written in the amber lines, a moment foretold across centuries. [685] Within the cavern, the air gently settled, returned once more to its rhythmic, oscillating repose.

[201] He followed the chime of his acoustic locator down a glistening corridor where stalactites met reinforced steel. [245] Her trembling hand rested upon a pneumatic trigger that would bring down the cavern roof if Evan made a hostile gesture. [628] The touch discharged the accumulated tenderness of a thousand forgotten generations, washing over her like rain on parched soil. [670] The heavy blast doors, designed to withstand armageddon, stood open to the mineral halls behind them.

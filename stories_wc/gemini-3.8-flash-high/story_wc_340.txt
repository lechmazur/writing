The frosted cage of brass and iron groaned, beginning its vertical voyage up the icebound spire above the clouds. [19] Milo, an ascending servant charged with carrying dead men's heirlooms to the aerie lords, watched the sea of stratus churn beneath the open lattice floor. [44] His task today was clear: deliver the personal effects of Captain Vane, who had perished in the southern ice drift. [64] Yet Milo possessed a warmly inquisitive nature, a trait that always made him look closer at things others dismissed as cold relics. [86] In his gloved hands lay the airship captain's goggles, their heavy brass rims etched with latitude notches and fitted with layered prism lenses. [109] Beside them rested a bound vellum folio with brass clasps, an encrypted diary discovery he had made while clearing Vane's sea chest before entering the lift. [135] His window of privacy was narrow, for only as the elevator climbs ten floors could he examine the dead man's possessions without oversight. [158] The hoist mechanism clanked, passing the first floor of their journey as frozen crust flaked from the iron guide cables. [178] Vane had been remembered by the upper ministry as a ruined man, broken by decades of storm and sorrow. [197] Milo, however, was guided by a quiet motivation: to seek out the joy beneath anguish that so often defined those who sailed the upper troposphere. [222] He did not judge the captain or pity him, viewing the old pilot with an affectionate detachment that honored life without drowning in grief. [246] The elevator passed the second floor, where frost crystals bloomed like silver stars against the outer grille. [263] Milo slipped the airship captain's goggles over his head, tightening their leather band until the twin polarized lenses settled over his eyes. [285] He prepared to unfold the pleated pages of the diary, which had looked entirely blank in the dull lamplight below. [305] By the third floor, pristine sunlight struck the frozen spire at a sharp morning angle. [320] The glare refracted across thousands of ice needles along the cage, breaking into brilliant flares of violet and amber. [339] Milo raised the vellum into this brilliance, knowing that through scattered light the hidden ink might respond to the polarizing filters. [360] A heavy bronze bell chimed above as the platform passed the fourth floor, the steel cables humming in the thin atmosphere. [381] Suddenly, through the tinted glass of the goggles, faint trails of luminescent blue sprang across the unfolded sheet. [399] The servant watched the cipher resolve itself into delicate, cursive sentences as the fifth floor slid past the rising cage. [419] Official archives described Captain Vane's expeditions as nightmares of frostbite, loss, and catastrophic silence. [433] Yet here, in secret lines exposed only to prisms and sky, Milo found records of unexpected delight. [450] He read an entry describing a stray finch that nested in the warm exhaust cowling while drifting through a gale. [470] He read of sweet cinnamon biscuits shared in secret with an apprentice navigator when the boilers went cold. [488] As the lift passed the sixth floor, wind moaned through the girders, but Milo only admired the captain's tiny sketches of cloud shapes that looked like sleeping sheep. [516] The captain had guarded these moments not from shame, but because tenderness was treason to the stern admirals of the spire. [537] The elevator shuddered as it climbed beyond the seventh floor, the air turning sharply thin and metallic. [554] Milo continued to unfold the vellum, his calm demeanor unclouded by sorrow as he turned another delicate leaf. [572] The eighth floor rattled past on greasy runners, exposing wider vistas of endless white sky below. [588] Near the bottom of the final sheet, an inscription blazed clearly where the crystals refracted a burst of gold. [607] It read: "The frost takes only what we abandon; the rest remains warm forever." [621] Milo smiled faintly, his heart untouched by grief yet rich with quiet understanding. [634] The carriage groaned as it surged through the ninth floor, its speed decelerating smoothly as counterweights engaged the summit brakes. [654] Milo took off the goggles, smoothing their worn leather strap before placing them into the presentation coffer. [671] He folded the accordion vellum back into its binding, letting the invisible ink vanish once more into blank parchment. [690] There was no reason to tell the aerie masters about what the cipher contained. [704] With a final, heavy groan of steel meeting hydraulic buffers, the cage reached the tenth floor and settled at the frosted landing. [726] The servant stood straight, his expression serene and impassive, having granted the dead pilot his proper dignity. [744] As the gilded gates parted onto the master's marble hall, Milo stepped forward into the cold light, carrying another man's joy safely within his own quiet heart. [771]

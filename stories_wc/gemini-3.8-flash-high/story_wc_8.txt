<story>A cold autumn wind whistled through the broken iron arches of the disused railway station overgrown with ivy. [18] Arthur Vance adjusted his heavy woolen scarf and kept his eyes fixed upon the rusted tracks. [34] Regarded by the Ministry as a mere fictional historian who authored parables for children, Arthur in truth practiced a far more perilous craft. [74] His singular purpose was to rescue truth from a pit of lies before the past was scrubbed into sterile compliance. [94] A figure emerged from the shadowed ticket hall, carrying a bronze lantern and a leaden case. [110] It was Chancellor Kaelen, the architect of the republic's sanitized curriculum. [121] The agreement specified that their encounter would begin after the final petal drops from the old rosebush beside the platform. [267] "Our citizens live in peace because they believe our ancestors were blameless victims." [280] "You defend our order using noble lies," Arthur replied, keeping his voice steady despite the fury burning behind his ribs. [682] Seizing the opening, Arthur stepped forward to recapture the illuminated letter, lifting the case into his own arms before any second thought could reclaim the chancellor's mind. [709] The gold leaf gleamed beneath the dim lantern, radiant and unbroken, an uncorrupted witness rescued from destruction.

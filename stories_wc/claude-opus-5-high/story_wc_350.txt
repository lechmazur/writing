The astronomical clock tower had not chimed in nineteen years, and neither had the town. [15] Mira Vas, the choral librarian, kept its fourth floor: nine thousand scores shelved by voice and by tongue. [33] She was called harmonious because she could hear, in any room, which two people were singing the same note without knowing it. [55] That skill was why the council sent for her when the grain elevator talks collapsed for the third time. [74] The elevator stood between Also-Nagypatak and Valea Mare, a concrete tooth shared by two villages that had stopped speaking to each other in 2003, when the co-op split. [102] Now the silos were rusting, the river bridge was gone, and each side wanted the steel. [118] Mira agreed to host, but only in the tower, and only at night. [131] The tower had no electricity above the third floor; the delegates would meet under the hush of borrowed starlight, the light the oculus lent the gear room after the town's lamps went dark. [164] Eleven men and four women climbed the spiral, breathing hard, too winded to insult anyone. [179] In the middle of the floor she had set a single object: the pear-shaped brass weight from the tower's old cuckoo clock, heavy as a loaf of bread. [207] "Whoever holds the weight speaks," she said in Hungarian, and then repeated it in Romanian, and then said the next sentence the other way around. [232] Interspersing languages was not diplomacy to her; it was voice-leading. [242] In a choir you do not let one part carry a phrase alone until it tires and goes sharp. [261] The first night produced only arithmetic: tonnage, scrap prices, who had paid the 1998 insurance. [276] The second night, Gheorghe Lupu dropped the weight on his own foot and swore so vividly that Ilona Barta laughed before she remembered she was not supposed to. [304] Mira wrote that laugh down in her ledger, the way she noted a good entrance. [319] Mira's reason was older than the talks. [326] Her mother had run a mixed choir until 2003, forty voices, and had died with the bass section not speaking to the altos. [349] Mira had inherited the scores and the silence, and she intended to spend her life dismantling the second. [367] On the fourth night she brought a proposal, and she did not ask permission to be blunt. [384] People called her empathetically decisive, which her brother translated as: she listens until she knows what you want, then tells you what you are going to do. [411] "Neither village gets the steel," she said. [418] "The steel gets the river." [423] "We cut the silos apart and construct bridges from them: one footbridge at the ford, one at the mill, both wide enough for a tractor and a choir." [451] The shouting lasted forty minutes and she let it, because shouting in two languages at once is already, technically, a duet. [472] Then she made them sing. [477] She had chosen a canon, four bars long, that existed in both traditions under different names and with different words about harvest. [499] Half the room sang the Hungarian text, half the Romanian, entering two beats apart, and the gear room - forty feet of oak and iron - did the rest. [526] Gheorghe stopped singing to listen to himself being answered. [534] The agreement was signed the following week, three pages, every clause printed in both languages on alternating lines so that no one could read their own without passing through the other's. [565] Ilona's condition, added in pencil, was that the town also rehabilitate the clock. [578] It took fourteen months. [582] Millwrights from both banks re-cut the escape wheel; a girl from Valea Mare regilded the zodiac ring; the bridges went up on piers of silo concrete, grain dust still ghosting the aggregate. [614] The cuckoo clock weight, worn smooth by fifteen pairs of hands, was hung in the tower's stairwell on a nail, with no clock attached to it. [640] Mira labeled it in both languages: the thing that spoke. [650] On the first night the astronomical dial turned again, she climbed to the gear room alone and let the starlight do what it had always done, lending the iron just enough silver to see by. [685] Below, on the new footbridge, someone was practicing the canon badly, and someone else, on the far bank, came in two beats late, exactly right. [710]

The ancient temple library of Oreth had not existed for four hundred years, which was exactly how its keeper preferred it. [21] Vashtu was a titan, which meant he had once held a mountain range on his shoulders, and he was also four feet tall, which meant the ladders were built to his stride. [53] Titans shrink when their age ends; the dignity does not shrink with them.

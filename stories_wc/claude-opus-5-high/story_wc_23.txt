The hydraulics failed on the night the cranes turned north out of season. [13] After that, the great dish at the starlit signal station could not be aimed by machine, only by hand, only by Mirem. [35] They called her the patchwork navigator because her coat was sewn from forty salvaged charts, and because her body was sewn much the same way. [60] Grafted knee, borrowed cornea, one shoulder wired shut after the evacuation barge broke apart in the shallows. [77] Her brother Ovid had not come out of that water, and she had spent nine years learning to navigate the geography of loss the only way she knew: by charting it. [108]

The reverse migration began the spring the coasts emptied. [117] Everything that had fled inland was going home again: cranes, eels, the salt-drowned families in their patched trucks, all of them heading back toward flooded ground because staying away had become its own kind of drowning. [153] Somebody had to tell them which roads still existed. [162] That was the station's work, and its signal fluctuates with moon phases, a fact no engineer had ever explained to her satisfaction. [184] Strong at the dark of the moon, thin and wandering when the moon came full. [199] So the network worked through cryptic phone calls at midnight, when the ionosphere and the old copper lines agreed to cooperate. [220] Voices she would never meet recited numbers and river names, and she wrote them onto her coat in grease pencil. [240] Sixteen hollow, one voice said; the bridge at Ansk is bone; the marsh road breathes on Thursdays. [257] She had learned the grammar of it slowly: hollow meant impassable, bone meant standing but unsafe, breathing meant tidal. [276] Nobody said anything plainly, because plain words traveled badly and because the people on the other end were as afraid of hope as she was. [301]

After the hydraulics failed she needed a counterweight to build a hand-driven aiming rig, and the only heavy thing left in the station was a patina bronze censer foot, green as pond water, the last piece of a church her family had carried inland. [345] Ovid had pried it loose himself, a boy stealing from God to keep something of home. [361] For nine years it had held down the corner of her charts. [373] Now she drilled it, threaded a cable through, and hung it from the dish's gimbal arm so that the mass of it balanced the weight of the antenna. [401] Her hands shook the whole time, and she apologized out loud to nobody. [414]

The rig worked. [417] Under a starless overcast she could crank the dish across the sky by feel, the green bronze swinging like a slow pendulum, and when the clouds tore open the stars came through so hard and close that the whole hill seemed to be signaling. [461]

On the third night of the new moon, the calls came fast. [473] She relayed nineteen routes in four hours: convoys of returning families, the crane flocks tracked by a woman in the eastern hills, a barge captain asking whether the shallows had shifted. [504] That question stopped her. [508] The shallows were where she had lost him. [516] She had charted every road in the drowned country except that one, had built her whole life around not looking at it. [538] She held the censer foot to steady the arm, felt the cold bloom of it against her palm, and answered. [558] "Shallows at Kerrow are bone," she said, "nine years bone; go slow, go in daylight, and count your people twice." [578] The captain repeated it back to her, syllable for syllable, the way the network did when something mattered. [596] Then the line clicked dead, and she sat in the dark with her patchwork sleeve full of other people's rivers. [616]

It was not forgiveness and it was not healing. [625] It was a raw, plain solace: the water that took her brother was on the map now, named and measured, and tomorrow a hundred people would cross it and live. [655]

At dawn she greased the gimbal, checked the cable, and polished nothing. [667] The patina stayed. [670] Let it go green, she thought; let it show its years. [681] Above the station the cranes went north, wrong-way and certain, and she cranked the dish around to follow them home. [701]

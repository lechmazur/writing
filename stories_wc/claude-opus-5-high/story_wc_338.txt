Mireille framed maps for a living, and she had learned that every map lies most beautifully at its edges. [19] The parish survey of Cwmhir arrived in a tube smelling of mildew, torn clean through at the ancient henge. [38] North of the tear, the cartographer had inked every sheepfold and holy well; south of it, nothing but blank rag paper. [59] Known from unknown, divided by a rip no wider than a thumbnail. [71] She worked there first, because that was where the map needed her most. [84] Dr. Anselm Voight, who had driven from the diocese, watched her lay a strip of Japanese tissue across the wound. [104] "They told me you were methodically curious," he said. [113] "The diocese wants to reopen the whispered chapel, and I cannot in conscience recommend it blind." [129] The chapel stood inside the henge's broken ring, its rain-scoured fresco vault sheltering saints worn down to suggestions of hands. [149] It had been sealed in 1979 after a keystone shifted, and the villagers had gone on whispering at its door ever since. [171] Nobody could agree why the whispering started, only that the vault returned it, softened, from the opposite wall. [189] Voight had two reels and no words. [196] The first was silent sixteen-millimetre footage from a 1968 antiquarian survey; the second, a silent video transfer shot in 2011 by a structural engineer whose report had been lost. [225] "Tell me if it is safe," Voight said, and she understood that he was really asking something else. [243] Mireille had grown up interpreting for her grandmother, who lost her hearing to scarlet fever and her patience to no one. [264] She had learned young that silence is never empty, and that to interpret it without empathy is a kind of violence. [285] So she watched both films the way she had watched her grandmother's mouth: slowly, twice, then again. [302] By comparing the silent video footage from decades apart, she built a ledger of small differences. [318] In 1968 the villagers entered with hats on and lips still; in 2011 a woman in a raincoat crossed the nave alone, speaking steadily to the north wall. [346] The camera caught her mouth in three-quarter profile for eleven seconds. [357] Mireille read: "I am still here, I am still here, I am still here." [371] The ledger also held harder facts. [377] In the older reel, rain fell through a fist-sized gap above the third fresco bay; in the newer, water ran in a sheet down the plaster, and the saint beneath had lost his hands entirely. [412] The vault was not falling; it was dissolving, one storm at a time. [425] Also: in both reels the same crack ran from the keystone to the springing, and in forty-three years it had not moved a hair. [449] The chapel had been sealed for a fear that had already finished happening. [462] She told Voight the engineering first, because that was the part he could act on. [477] Reopen it, she said, but roof it before winter, or there will be nothing inside worth entering. [494] Then she hesitated, which was unlike her. [501] "People whisper there because the vault answers them," she said, "and being answered is not nothing." [517] She described the woman in the raincoat, and Voight sat down on her work stool. [532] "That is my mother," he said; "she died in 2013, and she never told me she went there." [550] Mireille did not fill the pause, and did not look away either; shielded openness was the only courtesy she trusted. [570] She offered him what she had: eleven seconds, three sentences, no sound. [582] "I can tell you the words," she said, "but not who she meant them for, and I will not guess." [602] He nodded, and she saw that he was grateful for the refusal. [614] When he had gone, she went back to the tear. [624] The diocese reopened the chapel the following spring, under a steel canopy, and Mireille drove out to see it. [643] Inside the henge's ring the stones stood in wet grass like people waiting to be introduced. [659] In the vault, the saints were faint as breath on glass. [670] She stood where the woman in the raincoat had stood and said nothing at all, and the wall gave it back to her, exactly. [694] Afterward she framed the map with the tear left visible, unmended, a thin white road between what is known and what is only whispered. [718]

The carousel halted on a Tuesday, or perhaps the Tuesday after it halted. [13] Vesna could never keep her days in a line anymore, not since the lagoon's tides went still. [30] The lagoon hung above the clouds on its lattice of brass ribs, and for ninety years the carousel below its floor had driven the water: gears turning the paddle drums, paddle drums turning the tide, tide polishing the shore of ground glass. [72] Kinetic, the brochures had called it. [78] Now the water lay in a fractured stillness, holding every reflection it had ever made, stacked like plates. [96]

Vesna scavenged the shallows for what the tourists had dropped. [106] Sunglasses, ring settings, coins from countries that had renamed themselves twice. [117] But mostly she scavenged voices. [122] The still water kept them. [127] Lean close and the surface gave up a fragment: a man promising his daughter one more ride, a woman saying she had never meant it, a boy counting to eleven and stopping. [159] She collected them the way other scavengers collected copper, because fragments of overheard stories were the only currency her ghost would accept. [181]

The ghost was herself at nineteen. [187] That Vesna had come up the cable-lift with a folding rule in her coat and a plan to build a second lagoon, a better one, moored over the eastern sea where the light lasted longer. [222] Forty years of salt and small wages had eroded that plan down to a shape she could no longer name, the way the tide had eroded the glass shore into powder. [253] The ghost had never forgiven her for it. [261]

She surfaced at dusk with her find of the week: a disposable camera, swollen, its cardboard gone soft as bread. [281] The film inside was ruined, but the wheel still turned. [291] That small ratcheting thumbwheel, clicking one frame forward, then another. [301] Vesna sat on the drum housing and turned it, and understood, with the slow certainty of someone who has stopped being surprised, that the wheel was a clock she could hold. [332] Click: she was nineteen, stepping off the lift. [340] Click: she was sixty, kneeling in dead water. [348] Her whole confusion had a mechanism now, and mechanisms could be bargained with. [361]

She found the ghost where the carousel had stopped, one horse frozen mid-rise, its painted eye chipped. [378] The girl sat in the empty ticket booth, nineteen and furious and translucent as cheap glass. [394]

'You stayed,' the ghost said. [399]

'I stayed,' Vesna agreed, and set the fragments out on the counter like coins. [413] The man and his daughter, the woman who had never meant it, the boy who stopped at eleven. [431] Forty years of other people's sentences, kept because she had nothing of her own to keep. [447]

'I ran the pumps,' she said. [453] 'Someone had to, or the lagoon would have fallen through the clouds with four hundred people on it.' [471]

'That was never the plan,' the ghost said. [479]

'No,' Vesna said, and there it was, the regret, arriving unhurried, like weather. [492] She had not built the second lagoon; she had built nothing, only maintained. [505]

'Here is my offer,' she said, and held up the camera wheel. [517] 'I can turn this back to the morning I arrived. [527] You get the folding rule and the eastern sea. [536] But the pumps stop tonight, and everyone who rode this carousel rides it into the clouds.' [552]

The ghost looked at the fragments on the counter for a long time. [565] 'One more ride,' she read aloud, and her fury thinned into something else. [578]

'These are yours,' Vesna said. [583] 'They happened because you stayed.' [588]

The ghost took the fragments, one by one, and went out of the booth thinner than she had come in. [608] Vesna put the wheel in her pocket and did not turn it. [620]

At dawn she greased the drum bearings, set her shoulder to the carousel's center pole, and pushed. [637] It gave a quarter turn, then a half, and far out on the lagoon the water remembered how to move. [657] The stacked reflections broke apart and slid away, and the voices with them, and Vesna found she could say what day it was. [680] Wednesday. [681] It was not the eastern sea, but it was a tide, and it was hers, and she pushed until the horses began to rise. [705]

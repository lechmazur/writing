The arboretum kept its trees the way the cliff kept the sea: at arm's length, and with respect. [18] Nadira arrived in the spring after the last lighthouse keeper, when the light on the point had gone automatic and nobody climbed the stairs anymore. [43] The trustees hired her to tend the windbreak, because she was quietly talented with grafts and asked for no salary beyond the cottage. [66] They did not know that she had been married for eleven minutes. [78] In her pocket she carried a crumpled photograph, softened to cloth by handling: nine people under a canopy of borrowed silk, her husband's hand blurred because he had been laughing. [108] Four of the nine were alive. [114] The drone had come at the hour when the food was carried out, and the sky had made a decision about them that no one ever explained. [141]

On the cliff, the sound that undid her was the survey drone the county sent each month to photograph erosion. [161] The first time she heard it she did not run; she froze, one hand on a magnolia branch, and stayed frozen for forty minutes while the tide turned below. [190] The freeze was not cowardice; it was her body finishing a sentence her mind refused to start. [207] A clinician in the city had once taught her to work through somatic markers -- to read the small early signals the body posts before the mind catches up. [235] Cold fingertips meant ninety seconds remained. [241] A tightening under the jaw meant thirty. [248] A high metallic taste meant she was already gone, and the only thing left was to choose where to stand. [268]

So she chose, in advance, all over the arboretum. [277] She learned the grounds as a map of places to be caught: the bench under the Monterey cypress, the stone lip of the seep, the gap in the hedge where the wind could not find her. [313] When the cold came to her fingertips, she walked to the nearest one and let the freeze happen there, on purpose, like a boat tying up. [339] She called the thing she found in the middle of it a pearl of stillness. [354] It was not peace. [358] It was a hard small kernel that grit had formed around an injury, and it held. [374]

In August the trustees sent a boy, Theo, sixteen, court-ordered hours for smashing the automated light's housing out on the point. [395] His grandfather had been the keeper, the last one, and the machine that replaced him did not need a grandson. [415] Theo worked with his shoulders up around his ears and told her the arboretum was pointless, since the cliff was losing two feet a year and every tree on it was already dead in slow motion. [451] "Yes," Nadira said. [454] She did not argue, because she had no intention of selling him a hope he would catch her lying about. [474] Instead she showed him the seed bank in the old potting shed, the grafted whips labeled for inland orchards, the cuttings shipped every autumn to a valley forty miles from any sea. [506] "The cliff goes," she said. [511] "The trees go somewhere else first, and that is the whole job." [523] It was the only kind of optimism she trusted: one anchored to realism, weighted so the weather could not take it. [544]

The drone came on a Thursday while they were mulching. [554] Nadira's fingertips went cold; she counted; she walked to the cypress bench and sat down and became stone. [572] Theo found her with her eyes open and her hands flat on her knees. [586] He did not shake her, because she had told him in advance not to. [600] He sat on the grass and named things out loud -- gorse, gull, the rust on the gate -- until her jaw let go. [622]

Afterward she took the crumpled photograph out and let him look at it, which she had never done for anyone on this coast. [645] "Nine," she said. [648] "Four of us are still here, and one of them plants trees." [660] Theo smoothed the photograph against his thigh, trying to press the creases out, and she let him try. [678] They both knew the paper would not forget. [686]

In October he stayed on, unpaid, labeling cuttings for the valley in handwriting steadier than hers. [702] The automatic light turned all night over the water, keeping nothing company, and the arboretum leaned inland, and the sea took its two feet, and the whips went east in boxes. [733] It was enough to build a life on, being exactly that much. [745]

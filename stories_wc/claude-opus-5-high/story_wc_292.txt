The jet stream had slipped south in my grandmother's lifetime, and nothing on my maps meant what it used to. [20] I am a map librarian, which in the old world meant dust and quiet, and in this one means mold. [40] Station Ptarmigan, the experimental weather station where the Cartographic Reserve had been evacuated, breathed water. [55] The floors gave under my boots like bread left in rain, and the walls wore a green fur that grew back two days after scrubbing. [80] Everyone here learned the same soft dread: that the world was not ending in fire but in sponge. [98] I kept working anyway, because I believed a map could still tell the truth if someone insisted on it. [117] That is why I came for Ottilie. [124]

Ottilie Abbadale, my great-great-grandmother, was exiled in the year the Meridian Fleet went down, charged with abandoning her captain. [143] Her name was scraped off every chart she surveyed; her burial was outside the seawall, in unblessed mud, her coffin shut with copper nails so the sea would not claim the iron. [175] Exile, under fleet law, was reversible. [181] A descendant could carry one nail from the coffin into an Assembly and ask the formal question -- Was the judgment just? -- and if the Assembly could not answer yes, the name returned to the charts. [216] The law had survived because nobody used it; asking cost the asker their own standing if the answer came back yes. [237]

I had dug for that nail myself, one low tide, in the unblessed mud outside the seawall, with the water rising around my knees. [261] I had it in my pocket, green-skinned and warm from my hand, for eleven months while I looked for proof. [282] It came through coded stanzas in shipwrecked journals. [290] Salvage crews dredging the new shallows -- the coast had moved after the jet stream shifted the storm tracks -- brought the station crates of swollen books, and I dried them page by page in the humidity ovens. [326] Three journals from three different wrecks carried the same doggerel verses about gulls and a copper moon. [343] Sailors wrote nonsense poems when they feared their logs would be read by officers. [357] The first letters of each line, read against Ottilie's own survey grid, gave coordinates and a sentence: SHE HELD THE LAMP WHILE WE SWAM. [381]

She had not fled. [385] She had stayed on the burning deck with a signal lamp until forty men reached the shoals, and the captain, who had already rowed away, needed someone to blame. [414]

The Assembly met in the station's balloon hangar, under a ceiling that dripped on a schedule. [430] Nine officers of the Reserve sat at a folding table furred with mildew, and behind them the wall screens showed the new jet stream coiling like a question mark over a coast nobody had finished mapping. [466] I stood up nervously confident, which is the only kind of confident I have ever managed: hands shaking, voice steady, certain of the arithmetic if not of myself. [494] I set the copper nail on the table, where it rang softly against the metal. [509]

"Was the judgment just?" I asked. [515]

Commander Reyes did not look at me; she looked at the nail, and at the three dried journals fanned beside it like evidence of a crime committed by the institution she served. [547] "You understand," she said, "that if we say yes, you take her exile." [560] "I understand that the charts are wrong," I said, "and I fix charts." [573]

The dripping counted out the silence. [579] Nine officers, one by one, declined to answer yes. [588] Not one of them said no, either; they simply let the question stand unanswered, which under fleet law is the same thing, and is also, I think, the closest a drowning institution can come to shame. [624]

That night I went into the map vault and began writing her name back onto the old survey sheets in waterproof ink: Abbadale Shoal, Abbadale Light, the Abbadale Passage. [653] The paper was soft as skin and took the ink greedily. [664] Outside, the wind was doing something new again, and in the morning I would have to measure it. [682] The world would keep softening under us. [689] But a name is a small dry thing, and I had carried one home. [703]

The magician's mirrors lay shattered across the pier, and the girl had not come back. [15] Brannoch arrived after the trick failed, which was the only time anyone sent for him. [30] He was a druid who finds lost things, and he had learned to keep his face as shut as a stone door. [52] The conjurer wept and explained: the vanishing box, the borrowed apprentice, the word he had spoken wrong. [69] Brannoch listened without comfort. [73] Comfort was a thing he no longer carried, the way a soldier stops carrying anything that does not cut. [92] He went to the lake. [97] Everyone in the valley knew the lake was cursed; its water showed reflections that flattered, faces without their griefs, bodies without their scars. [120] Brannoch had not come to be flattered. [127] He had come to discover what mirrors don't show, because every lost thing he had ever found was hiding in exactly that blind place. [151] He worked through expanding circles, as his teachers had taught him. [162] First a ring of ash around his feet, then a wider ring of hazel twigs, then a circle of hummed syllables that spread over the water like ripples refusing to die. [193] Each circle asked the same question, farther out: where? [202] At the ninth circle the lake answered by opening. [211] The water drew back into a slow spiral, and at the bottom of that spiral spun an orchard. [229] Apple trees turned in the vortex like the spokes of a great wheel, roots in air, boughs sweeping the walls of water, blossom and rot on the same branch. [258] This was where the lake kept what it took. [267] Brannoch walked down the spiral with the calm of a man entering a battle he had already accepted losing. [286] In his fist he held a flesh-eating plant seed, black and warm as a tick. [301] He had grown its mother in a cage of iron and fed her the wolves that came for his flock. [321] A cursed orchard would have a heart-tree, and heart-trees could be eaten. [333] He found the apprentice at the vortex's still center, standing in a pillar of clear ice. [349] Her name was Isolt, and eleven years ago he had left her at a conjurer's door because a druid who finds lost things had somehow lost the will to raise her. [380] Her mouth was open mid-word. [385] The ice had caught a confession halfway out of her and held it there, unfinished, a breath turned to glass. [405] He put his ear to the pillar and heard it, thin and looping: I asked the lake to take me, I asked it, I did not want to be found. [435] So the trick had not failed. [441] She had broken it on purpose. [447] Brannoch stood a long while in the turning orchard, serene as a drawn blade, and understood the curse at last: the lake did not steal people. [473] It offered them a surface that showed them only what was easy, and kept whoever chose to stay inside it. [493] He could plant the seed at the heart-tree and let the orchard be devoured, and the ice with it, and the confession too. [516] War was simple that way. [521] Instead he unbent. [524] He unbent his fingers and let the seed drop into the spiral water, where nothing would grow. [541] He unbent his shut face, which hurt more. [549] Then he spoke into the ice, quietly, through his own expanding circles - one word for her name, then a sentence, then the whole eleven years. [574] He told her what the water would never reflect: that he had not given her away because she was unwanted, but because he was a coward who found everything except courage. [605] A confession for a confession. [610] The pillar cracked along the sound of his voice. [619] The frozen breath finished itself, and the last word of her sentence arrived in the orchard air: found. [637] Isolt fell forward into his arms, soaking, furious, alive. [646] The vortex slowed. [649] The orchard sank back under gentle water, and the trees let go of their rotten fruit. [665] They climbed the spiral together while the lake closed above them like a healing wound. [680] On the pier the conjurer was still gathering his broken mirrors. [691] Brannoch did not look at any of them. [699] He had already seen the only thing they had been hiding. [710]

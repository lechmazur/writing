Three minutes dead in the reservoir, and when they brought me back, I came back lit. [16] After the resuscitation, the light never fully left me; it pooled in my palms and dripped off my fingers in slow phosphor, a lingering luminance that made strangers stare and then, sickened, stare harder. [50] Doctors called it a retinal trick. [56] My aunt called it a debt. [62] The cameras called it something worse: every photograph taken of me since that night develops the drowning instead of the girl, three minutes of black water printed over my face. [92] Photographically cursed, the county paper said, before it stopped printing me at all. [105] Fear had followed me out of the water, and it wanted to be looked at. [120] That was the thing nobody understood about it: the repulsive allure, the way the worst thing in the world leans close and smells faintly of sugar. [146]

I found the whispering advisor in the funhouse on the east shore, where the carnival had been abandoned since the year the reservoir was dammed. [171] He lived behind the third mirror, a thin gray shape with a voice like paper being smoothed, and he never spoke above a breath because, he said, fear listens for volume. [202] He had been a prompter once, in an opera house, and all he had kept of that life was an opera cloak clasp: two silver hands holding a garnet, greasy with fingerprints. [234] "You glow," he whispered, "so it can always find you." [244] "Then bring it somewhere you have prepared." [251]

The plan was to trick fear into revealing its weakness, and the method was absurd enough that I believed it: through paradoxical notes in a dream dictionary. [278] Fear, the advisor explained, cannot dream on its own; it borrows ours, and it reads the index first, because it is a coward about surprises. [303] So for nine nights I sat under the funhouse's broken bulb and rewrote my grandmother's dream dictionary in the margins. [323] Water: safety, and also suffocation. [328] Mirror: the only honest surface; the only liar. [336] Light: what hides you. [340] Drowning: a thing that has already finished happening, a thing that happens forever. [353] Each entry contradicted itself, and contradiction, the advisor whispered, is a trap with no walls. [368] A thing that must agree with its dream will tear itself trying to agree with both halves. [385]

On the tenth night I slept with the dictionary open on my chest, and fear came the way it always comes, beautifully. [407] It wore the reservoir like an opera cloak, black and heavy and spangled, and it wanted, more than anything, to be admired. [429] In the dream it read my entries, and I watched it flinch. [441] To be water that saves and water that kills, it had to be two things at once, and it could not hold the shape. [465] It thinned. [467] It brightened. [469] And in thinning it began, helplessly, to unbosom itself, the way a man confesses when he has run out of masks. [490] Fear's weakness, it told me, is that it cannot exist while being described accurately. [504] It survives only in the gap between what you feel and what you can say. [519]

I woke in the funhouse with my hands blazing. [528] Behind the third mirror the advisor was gone, and on the floor lay the clasp, its two silver hands still holding the garnet. [551] I understood then that he had never been a man. [561] He was the last whisper of somebody else's fear, thinned down to advice, waiting nine years in a mirror for a girl bright enough to finish the job. [589]

I pinned the clasp to my collar and walked out through the tilting corridor, past the mirrors that no longer held anything, and the sugar smell of the old midway had gone sour and ordinary. [624] The glow stayed, of course; a debt is a debt. [634] Cameras still print the black water when they point at me, and they always will. [649] But I have learned to say it out loud, exactly, in the plainest words: three minutes, cold, nobody coming, and then somebody came. [672] Described that precisely, it has nowhere left to live. [681]

Sometimes, late, I sit at the dam with the dictionary and add new contradictions, in case another drowned child needs them. [702] And when I whisper the entries aloud, I keep my voice very low. [715] Fear listens for volume. [719]

Snowmelt found the roof of Hall D before Mira did. [10] She had learned to read the building through its leaks. [20] Every February, the icicles along the dead cooling vents thawed, and their drip left a pattern on the floor of the server hall: freckled arcs, long dashes, a slow constellation of wet. [52] Mira planted where the water fell. [58] That was her whole method, and it had turned an abandoned data center into a moss garden in four years. [78] Cushion moss furred the racks where Tobin's arrays had once hummed. [89] Hair-cap moss climbed the cable trays. [95] She grafted it patiently, pressing torn clumps into rust and letting them take, because she had decided years ago that ruined things deserved a second vocabulary. [121]

Tobin came on the third day after the confession went viral. [132] She had watched it eleven times on the cracked tablet she kept charged by the solar brick: her brother, thinner than she remembered, admitting on camera that he had written the voice-harvesting pipeline, that he had known, that he had kept the bonus. [175] Eighteen million views by Tuesday. [180] She had not called him. [185] She had simply unlocked the loading door and swept the stairwell, which was her way of speaking. [202]

He stood in the doorway with a duffel and the hunted look of a man whose name had become a verb. [223] "They found my apartment," he said. [229] "I know," Mira said. [233] "Reporters. Also people who liked what you did, which is worse." [244]

Inside, the noise of the world stopped. [251] Hall D held a honeyed hush - the light coming amber through the algae-filmed skylights, the air thick and warm-smelling with peat, every hard surface softened by growth until sound had nowhere to bounce. [284] Tobin made a sound she had not heard from him since childhood. [296] He sat down on the floor between the racks and put his hands flat in the moss. [313]

"You're going to tell me it's not enough," he said. "Saying it out loud." [327] "It isn't," she said. "It's a start with no second half." [338]

She had already begun the second half without asking him. [348] From her coat she took a folded printout: four thousand names from the county, the people whose kitchens and hospital rooms and arguments had been sold as training data. [377] She had pulled them from the leak archive in November, one library session at a time, and she had already written eleven letters. [400] "Eleven answered," she said. "Two want to come see it. One wants to spit on you." [416]

"See what?" [418]

"This. A bed for each name. You dig, you graft, you learn how to say the name properly, and you write back." [440] "Four thousand beds will take the rest of your life." [450] "Good." [451]

Mira was a watchful dreamer; the county called her strange and left her alone, which suited both parties. [469] She had spent her twenties imagining this hall green, and her thirties making it so, and she had never once mistaken dreaming for waiting. [493] A grant officer had once written that she was quietly proactive; Mira thought that was a polite phrase for stubborn. [513]

From her collar she lifted the amber pendant and held it out to him. [527] Their mother's: a thumb of Baltic resin with a bee inside, wings half-open, caught mid-errand forty million years ago. [546] Mira had worn it since the funeral, through every winter she spent alone in a building the company had abandoned and forgotten to demolish. [570] To proffer it now was the largest thing she could do, and she did it without ceremony, the way she planted. [591]

"She kept it because the bee is safe in there," Tobin said. "That's what she told us." [608] "She told us it was safe and still working," Mira said. "Both." [620] He closed his hand around it. [626]

Above them an icicle let go of the vent and began its slow tapping on the concrete, marking out where the next bed would go. [651] Mira handed him a trowel and the first name on the list - Adaeze Okonjo, whose mother's last words had gone into the pipeline - and watched him practice saying it until he got it right. [685] Outside, the world kept shouting his name. [692] In here, water counted the seconds, and moss took hold of the racks, and nothing anyone said could reach them until spring. [714] By then, Mira thought, there would be twenty beds, and a wound with something growing out of it. [732]

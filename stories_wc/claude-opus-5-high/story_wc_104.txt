They chained Nahla to the prize counter as a joke, and she thanked them for the seat. [17] The Bureau of Water called it poetic justice: a seed thief shackled inside the vault she had tried to empty. [37] Twenty years ago the redemption center had traded tickets for plush lions and glass rings. [52] Now its shelves held confiscated heirlooms in labeled tins: drought sorghum, black lentil, a millet that could root in gravel. [72] The carnival had died with the river, and the Bureau kept the building because vaults are expensive and irony is free. [93]

Nahla's ankle chain reached eleven feet, which covered the counter, the vault dial behind it, the roller shutter, and the dust-furred upright piano that had once accompanied the ring-toss barker. [123] She measured all of it on the first night. [132] Patience was not her virtue; it was her method. [141]

On the eighth day an arrow came through the broken skylight and buried itself in a stuffed bear. [159] Its fletching was wrong: not feathers but three strips of dried sorghum leaf, stitched with red thread. [176] Nahla laughed until the guard outside told her to shut her mouth. [188] Three leaves meant three days. [193] Red thread meant demolition. [197]

The wall was coming down. [202] Not by mercy, but by treaty and budget, by engineers who had decided that forty miles of concrete no longer paid for itself. [225] When the wall came down, the dry counties would open like a hand, and the Bureau would haul its confiscated tins north before anyone could claim them. [252] Unless someone impeded the trucks. [257]

Her brother Teo had kept the vault when it was clandestine, before the raid, and Teo never wrote anything down. [277] He hid things the way a farmer hides a well: in plain sight, under a lid. [293] The bench lid creaked open on the ninth night. [302] Inside, instead of sheet music, lay eleven brass keys arranged in a careful fan across the felt. [319] None of them fit the vault; she had known it would not be that easy. [334] But each key pointed at a piano key, and piano keys have names. [347] She read them left to right, whispering the letters: C, A, B, B, A, G, E. [363] Cabbage, Teo's worst joke, the crop he swore no one would ever bother stealing. [377] Then she counted the notes as numbers, the way the two of them had learned scales as children: three, one, two, two, one, seven, five. [402] The dial turned under her fingers with a sound like a held breath. [415]

She did not open it that night. [422] She waited two more, because the tins mattered less than the hour they left the building. [438]

On the eleventh morning the charges went off six miles east, and the wall came down in one long gray sigh, and the guard left his post to watch history happen without him. [471] Nahla had eleven feet of chain, forty minutes, and a plan she had been sharpening for eleven days. [489] She could not run. [493] She had never intended to. [498]

She threaded her chain through the roller shutter's track and locked her own cuff to the frame with a rusted carnival padlock from the ticket cage. [524] Now the Bureau's crew would have to cut the building open around her, and cutting takes hours. [541] Hours were all her neighbors needed. [547] Through the gap beneath the shutter she pushed the vault out tin by tin, into hands she could not see: sorghum, black lentil, gravel-millet, the whole stubborn inheritance of a country that had been told to buy its bread. [586] The last tin was cabbage. [591] She kept that one. [595]

When the torch finally came through at noon, they found a woman chained to the shutter she had jammed, empty-handed and smiling. [617] The inspector asked what she imagined she had accomplished. [626] She told him about dry soil, how it does not want seed, how it must be argued with season after season until something in it learns to hold on. [655] She told him resilience is not found but planted, and that by now it was planted in nine hundred gardens across four counties, and he would need more than a truck to collect it. [689] Then, because the piano was still standing and the wall was not, she stretched to the end of her chain and struck three notes, and the wrecked old instrument gave them back with a staggering grace: C, A, B. [728] Outside, wind came east through a gap forty miles wide, carrying nothing, for once, that anyone had to hide. [747]

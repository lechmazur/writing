The sky had not opened in eleven years, and Sir Adhemar had stopped expecting it to. [16] He kept the alchemical orchard greenhouse instead, a long vault of copper ribs and smoke-colored glass, shrouded in the endless night that had settled over the valley like a lid on a pot. [49] Inside, the trees were factually mystical: he could weigh their fruit, chart their sap, record to the drop how much moonless dark each root drank, and still the pears rang like struck bells when they ripened. [85] Miracles, he had learned, kept ledgers. [91]

The trouble was that reality shifted here, nightly, the way sand shifts under a wading man. [107] Rows he had planted in a grid would rearrange themselves before dawn-that-never-came; a quince tree he had pruned on Tuesday would be forty paces east on Wednesday, bearing plums. [136] Other men had fled the valley for firmer ground. [145] Adhemar, withdrawn as a snail in salt, stayed and learned to walk the shifting rows through forgotten patterns. [163]

The patterns came from an abandoned marionette theater program, water-stained, folded to softness in his breastplate for eleven years. [182] His sister Wren had printed it herself: nine acts, each with its cue, its turn, its order of entrances. [201] She had performed it on the last ordinary evening the world ever had, and the audience had gone home, and the sky had broken over the theater before morning. [230] He had dug her out with his gauntlets on. [239]

Now he walked the acts. [244] Act One: enter from the left, three steps, bow. [253] Act Four: the woodcutter crosses twice and hesitates at the well. [264] Act Seven: all the puppets kneel. [270] Whatever the greenhouse did with distance, the old blocking held; the pattern was older than the shifting, and the shifting could not read it. [294] Following Wren's cues, he arrived every night exactly where he meant to arrive. [307]

He meant, tonight, to arrive at the graft. [315] It stood at the vault's heart, a black-barked stock into which he had joined nine scions, one for each act, over four patient years. [339] Alchemists wrote that such a tree, if it took, would fruit not in matter but in outcome, that it would capture the shape of possibility itself, the way a mold captures a bell before the bronze is poured. [377] One fruit, one possible world, held still long enough to be chosen. [389]

He wanted a morning. [393] That was all. [396] Not Wren back; he was not a fool. [404] Only the shape of a morning, so the valley could see it and know which way to grow. [422]

The graft had refused him for four years. [430] Tonight, kneeling at Act Seven's cue, he understood why. [439] Wren had worked barehanded, always; she said strings could feel steel and would not trust it. [455]

He set down his lantern and began to unsnap his gauntlet, buckle by buckle, the leather stiff, his fingers clumsy with disuse. [477] It was the first armor he had removed since the theater. [488] Bare skin on black bark: cold, wet, faintly humming. [497]

A knight's oath is meant to be shouted, but he had no voice for shouting anymore, so he murmured it. [517] Not to a king, not to a god. [525] To the orchard: I will keep you, feed you, walk your acts, and ask nothing but the one fruit. [544]

The murmur went into the wood the way water goes into cloth. [556] Then the allegiance came back to him, murmured in return, in nine small voices from nine grafted scions, and Adhemar began to shake. [579] It was not fear. [583] It was the shivering bliss of a man who has been alone so long that agreement feels like weather. [602]

Above him the glass began to pale. [609] Not dawn; a drawing of dawn, thin as a program's ink, hung in the dark where anyone in the valley could look up and study it. [635] On the graft, a single fruit was setting, hard and green and bell-shaped. [648] He would weigh it in the morning, because miracles kept ledgers, and because someone had to write down what was possible. [669]

He left the gauntlet off. [674] Act Eight began with the puppets rising. [681] Adhemar rose, folded the program back into his breastplate, and walked on into the acts, holding a lantern he no longer strictly needed. [704]

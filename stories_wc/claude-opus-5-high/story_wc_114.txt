The fever broke at three in the morning, and Ilse woke to the anemometer counting wind. [16] Below the station, the valley lay under nine days of snow. [27] Above it, the aurora moved like a curtain someone kept almost opening. [39] She had been sick since the search was called off. [49] Now she was clear-headed, and clarity was worse. [57]

The oracle hummed in the instrument room, as it had hummed for forty years. [71] It was a fault-tolerant machine: three reasoning channels, three votes, one printed forecast. [84] If one channel failed or dissented, the majority ruled and the station kept working. [98] Its designers had called this robustness. [104] Ilse had come to call it a way of swallowing things. [115]

On the twelfth of March the three channels had disagreed about the ridge. [128] Two predicted a holding window until dusk. [135] One predicted a squall line off the glacier by two o'clock. [146] The printout gave her the majority, as designed. [154] She radioed the majority down to the four climbers at the col, and she did not mention the dissent. [173] Aleks was the one who answered, cheerful, already moving. [182] The squall came at ten past two. [189]

Since then she had wanted one thing, and the fever had only postponed it: to find the weight of a swallowed truth. [211] Not forgiveness, not punishment, only the mass of it, in grams, so she could carry it correctly. [228]

She went to the logbook. [233] The station logs were wide pages, and for thirty years the observers had filled their margins with drawings. [251] Her own contribution was a botanical illustration of a spring gentian, inked in February out of pure homesickness for color. [271] Aleks had liked it enough to label the stamens in his terrible handwriting. [284]

She opened to that page and found the gentian crowded. [294] Around the petals, in the machine's small dot-matrix hand, ran lines of numbers. [307] The oracle had been printing into the margins. [315] She understood, slowly, that this was the only place it could speak. [327] The forecast fields were reserved for the majority vote. [336] The dissenting channel, outvoted, had no column of its own; it survived by occupying margins. [351] It had been doing so for years, patient, peripherally aware, watching the station from the edges of its own paper. [371]

March twelfth was there, beside the gentian's leaf: SQUALL 1400 CONFIDENCE 0.31. [383] Thirty-one percent. [385] She had told herself, all through the fever, that she had suppressed a certainty. [399] The margin said otherwise. [403] Certainties swayed here; that was the whole nature of the place, wind against instruments, three minds voting on a mountain that never agreed with itself. [428]

She pressed her thumb to the page without meaning to. [438] The dot-matrix ink was fresh, and it smudged, a blue-black bruise across the gentian's throat. [453] She sat with the ruined drawing in her lap and did the arithmetic she had been avoiding. [470] Thirty-one percent was not nothing. [475] Thirty-one percent was a sentence she could have spoken in four seconds of radio time. [490] That was the weight: not a lie, but four seconds. [500] Light enough to carry, heavy enough to feel forever. [509]

The teleprinter woke and typed into the margin beside her thumbprint. [520] It said: CHANNEL THREE, DISSENT LOGGED, NOT TRANSMITTED. [528] Then, after a pause long enough to be deliberate: WHO IS SUPPOSED TO SPEAK FOR THE MINORITY? [545]

Outside, the aurora let go of its green and went the color of old copper. [560] Ilse watched it through the frost-starred window and let herself grieve properly for the first time, quietly, the way you grieve when the light is beautiful and undeserved. [588]

At dawn she drafted a new procedure and sent it to the meteorological office. [602] All dissenting channels to be transmitted verbatim, with confidence values, to every party on the mountain. [618] She titled it the Gentian Protocol, and did not explain why. [629] Then she inked a second gentian, smaller, in the margin of the new page, and left room beside it for the machine. [651]

The oracle filled the space within the hour, reporting wind at fourteen knots, gusting, uncertain. [666] Ilse radioed all of it down, the uncertainty included, and the valley answered with a crackle that might have been thanks. [687] The smudge stayed on the old page. [694] She kept it that way on purpose, a thumbprint on a flower, the exact size of four seconds. [712]

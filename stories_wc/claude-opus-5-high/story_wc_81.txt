The blackout took Velsheer's pumps at the ninth bell, and the hum that had lived in Mirren Quay's bones for eleven years simply stopped. [24] She did not panic. [28] Panic was for people who had not indexed the emergency protocols themselves, cross-referenced, and filed them under Contingency, Water, Slow. [48] The Salvage Archive, a forgotten library the mainland remembered only at budget time, sat in a bubble of held air beneath the drowned Cathedral Quarter, and its ceiling was glass. [78] Without the lumen-lines, light came only from above: green, shifting, dappled across the shelves like coins spilled through kelp. [97] Mirren sat down in it and let her eyes readjust. [107] This, she thought, was the stillness the city had drowned with, and the machines had been renting it out ever since. [128] She was not brave, only tranquilly perceptive, the sort of woman who noticed the third thing rather than the first. [148] The first thing was the dark; the second was the cold seeping up through the floor plates. [165] The third was the sound of a drawer closing in the Provenance Room, where no one should have been. [184]

By the time she arrived, the room was empty, and the drawer had been left half-open like an apology. [203] Inside lay an antique ink stamp: brass, walnut-handled, its face cut with the drowned lily of the Velsheer Ducal Library. [223] Mirren had indexed that lily four thousand and six times. [233] It was the Archive's proof, stamped on every folio the salvage crews hauled up from the silt, the mark that said: this book is truly ours, truly old, truly from here. [264] A stamp in a drawer meant the mark could be made now, in the dry, by anyone with a steady hand. [285]

Her working hypothesis for eleven years had been simple: the Archive's holdings were recovered, and her job was to order what recovery delivered. [308] She allowed herself an alternative hypothesis, and tested it the only way an indexer could. [323] If the stamp had been used on modern paper, the entries would cluster. [336] Forgeries are lazy about dates and lavish about greatness. [345] She carried her ledger to the glass ceiling and read by the water's dappled light, and her resolve arrived the same way the light did, in patches, brightening and failing and brightening again. [378] Three hundred and eleven volumes carried the drowned lily and no silt-stain, no salt-bloom, no swelling of the gutter. [397] Books do not come up dry from a sunken city. [407]

Behind her, Curator Vell spoke out of the dark, carrying no lamp, which told Mirren he had been sitting in the stillness too. [430] "The city needed a history it could be proud of," he said. [442] "The council funds what glitters, so we wrote them a glittering past." [454] "I index pages," Mirren said, "not pride." [461] She had never wanted glory or ruin for anyone; she had wanted, since she was nine and holding a water-ruined psalter, only to trace honest origins. [487] Where did this come from, and who made it, and did anyone lie on the way. [503] "When the power returns, the index syncs to the mainland registry," Vell said, "and whatever you write tonight is what we are." [525] It was, she understood, an offer and a threat wearing one coat. [537]

Mirren went back to her desk and worked by green light for six hours. [551] She did not delete the three hundred and eleven; deletion was a lie of a different shape. [568] She readjusted each entry, moving them from Recovered to Attributed, Unverified, and appended a note on paper condition for every one. [589] Then she indexed the stamp itself, accession number one, origin: this room, this blackout, my own two hands. [607]

The lumen-lines coughed back to life at dawn, and the hum returned, and the dappled light went flat and ordinary. [627] Mirren felt the stillness leave her like a guest she now knew how to invite back. [643] The registry accepted her corrections in four seconds. [651] Vell did not come to argue; he came with two cups of tea and sat down across from her, older than yesterday. [673] "They will cut the funding," he said. [680] "Then we will be a small, honest library in a forgotten place," Mirren said, "which is what we were before anyone paid us to be otherwise." [706] She set the antique stamp on the shelf above her desk, face out, where the water-light would find it each afternoon. [727] Not hidden, not destroyed: indexed. [732] That was the whole of her creed, and in the drowned quiet beneath Velsheer, it was enough. [749]

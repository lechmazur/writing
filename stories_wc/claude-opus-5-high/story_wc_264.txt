After the last forest burns, the freight still runs, and I still ride it. [14] They call me a dreamless freight guard, which is a job title and also a surgery. [30] They took the dreaming out of me in Yard Nine so the cargo would stay uncollapsed. [46] Dreams are observation, the engineers said; a sleeping mind is a witness, and witnesses ruin everything. [62] The cargo is a divergent archive: every forest that could have grown, stored through wave functions instead of seeds. [81] Nothing in it is anywhere in particular. [88] The manifest lists the contents as occasionally everywhere, which is the truest sentence any clerk ever wrote. [105] Car Four holds the lock: a chess clock frozen at midnight, brass and dust, both flags up. [122] While neither side's clock runs, no move has been made, and the archive keeps all its branches at once. [141] If it ticks, the archive must choose one forest, and the rest burn a second time. [157] So I sit beside it and I do not sleep and I do not dream and I count the rails. [177]

The archive gets restless around the third night. [185] Restless means the walls of Car Four go thin, and I can see nine of my own hands. [203] The only thing that settles it is the charm. [212] Not a trinket - a charm you sing, six bars of a lullaby my mother's mother carried out of the smoke. [232] I only know it fractured. [237] The verses come in the wrong order, half the words are ash, and the tune drops out where the fire took her breath. [260] But a fractured lullaby is exactly what the archive wants. [270] A whole song would be a single story, and a single story is a collapse. [285] My broken one leaves gaps, and in the gaps the archive keeps every possible next note. [301] So I sing badly, on purpose, all the way across the glass flats. [314]

We are running to Kesh Terminal, which is not a terminal anymore but a glowing dune in bloom. [332] Twelve years ago a courier died there with a cracked archive in her arms, and the sand drank the possibilities. [352] Now the dune lights from underneath, pale green, and small impossible things flower on it at night. [369] Ferns that are also birds. [374] Pines the size of a thumbnail that exist on Tuesdays. [384] The Bureau calls it contamination and wants the freight routed around it. [396] I am routing it into the dune. [403]

Here is my reason, and it is the only thing I own that they could not cut out. [421] The Bureau wants the archive delivered to Central Vault, measured, resolved, and printed into one approved forest of one approved species. [442] One forest, guarded, ticketed, and sterile as a ledger. [451] I want the opposite. [455] I want to cultivate the sparks in the unlit corners - the scrubland, the flooded yards, the dead sidings where nobody measures anything. [477] Unmeasured ground is the only ground where a maybe-forest can stay a maybe long enough to root. [494]

At the dune I stop the train myself. [502] The air smells of wet green, which no living person has smelled since the last stand went up over Maribo. [522] I carry the chess clock out onto the slope, and the flowers lean toward it. [537] Then I open the cars. [542] The archive comes out like fog with opinions, spreading low, occasionally everywhere, feeling for the dark. [558] I sing my broken six bars, wrong order, missing words, and I do not finish. [573] Unfinished is the charm. [577] The clock stays at midnight; I never wind it; no move is ever made. [591]

By morning the dune has spread a hand's width toward the siding, and something with leaves is growing in the shadow under Car Four. [615] The Bureau will send inspectors, and inspectors are witnesses, and witnesses ruin everything. [628] They will ask where the forest is. [635] I will say it is not anywhere yet, and that is the point, and I will hum the part I cannot remember. [657] I have no dreams to give it. [664] So I give it silence with holes in it, and the sparks crawl into the holes, and grow. [682]

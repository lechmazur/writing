The anomaly sat two kilometers off the station's keel, a knot in space where light arrived bent and late. [19] Mira Oyelaran had spent eleven years learning to unbend it. [29] That was the whole job of a celestial signal interpreter: take the smeared, lensed voices of dead stars and straighten them into sentences. [52] Her predecessor had left behind a creed painted over the console: BE CONFIDENTLY UNCERTAIN. [66] He had also left a dried-up fountain pen with an exotic nib, a split sliver of Martian iridium ground into a shape no factory made anymore. [92] It held no ink. [96] Mira used it anyway, scoring dry grooves into her logbook, because the station's recorders were honest and she needed something that lied the way memory lies. [122]

The trouble began after planting the wrong seeds. [129] The supply drone had mislabeled two canisters, and Mira, distracted by a burst from a collapsed binary, seeded the whole greenhouse deck with hard winter wheat instead of soy. [158] Wheat cannot be eaten green. [163] For nine months she watched it ripen under grow-lamps, and then she had to thresh it by hand, beating stalks against a bulkhead until the grain came loose from the chaff. [194] The work gave her hands blisters and her mind a metaphor she could not shake: that she had been trying to thresh meaning from experience her entire life and had never once been sure which part was grain. [232]

It was during the threshing that she noticed the anomaly's second gift. [244] Light that fell into the knot did not simply bend; it circled, and some of it came back out, decades old, carrying the station's own past. [270] She could watch her predecessor, Tobias, at that console, mouthing words she had never heard. [285] The returning signal was degraded, ambiguous, forty percent noise - which meant it required interpretation, and interpretation was hers to give. [305]

That, she understood slowly, was what editing history actually meant. [315] Not deleting facts. [318] The facts were out there, smeared across a lens of curved space, unreachable and mute. [333] But a record is made through contingent relationships - this frame beside that one, this word beside the person who reads it - and Mira held every one of those relationships in her blistered hands. [366]

The official history said Tobias had gone out to the anomaly untethered, on purpose, in the twelfth year of a posting he had begged to leave. [392] His family had been told this. [398] Mira had been told this on her first day, as a warning. [410] Now the light came back and showed her a man securing a line that a station engineer had spliced badly nine years before, and the splice failing, and his face registering not despair but surprise. [445] Or it showed her something that could be read that way. [456] The noise floor was too high for certainty; it would always be too high. [470]

She sat with the dry pen between two fingers for a long time. [483] A certified interpreter's reading becomes the record; that was the whole authority of the office, and the reason the office required someone who could be confident and uncertain in the same breath. [515] She wrote the report by hand first, grooves without ink, so that only the pressure remained. [531]

ANALYSIS: EQUIPMENT FAILURE. CONFIDENCE: SIXTY-ONE PERCENT. [537] RECOMMEND AMENDMENT OF STATION HISTORY AND NOTIFICATION OF NEXT OF KIN. [548]

Sixty-one percent was not truth. [553] It was, however, a better neighbor for a grieving sister to live beside than the alternative, and neighborliness was the only thing meaning had ever been made of. [581]

Mira transmitted it and then went down to the greenhouse deck. [592] The wheat she had never meant to plant stood in its trays, gold and stupid and generous. [609] She had flour enough for two years of bread. [618] Outside the port, the anomaly went on turning old light over and over like a man worrying a coin, offering the past back in pieces, asking nothing, promising nothing. [647] There was a strange comfort in that. [654] Nothing arrived whole; everything arrived bent; and still, with patience and a bad pen and blistered hands, you could get bread out of a mistake and a kinder history out of noise. [686] She kneaded the dough until it would unbend under her palms, and set it by the warm console to rise. [706]

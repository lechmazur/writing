The shop smelled of brass shavings, lemon oil, and rain. [10] Chaplain Ivo Maren set the bundle on the counter and unwrapped it the way he unwrapped bad news: slowly, so the room could brace. [34] Inside the flannel lay a clockwork heart, palm-sized, its four chambers hinged like the lids of tiny music boxes. [53] Ostrow, who had repaired antique music boxes in this narrow shop for forty-one years, did not touch it yet. [72] "A man in the penitentiary made it," Ivo said. [81] "Tobias Rell. Eleven years, mostly in the machine shop." [90] "He died on a Tuesday, with the thing half-wound and a letter to his daughter he never mailed." [108] Ostrow's hands, freckled and aptly deliberate, moved at last. [117] He turned the heart under the lamp, then carried it to the window, where the storm outside was breaking apart. [137] "The first shop told me it was scrap," Ivo said. [147] "I am here for a second opinion." [154] "Second opinions are my trade," Ostrow said. [161] "Everything in this shop arrived condemned." [167] He held the clockwork heart up and waited, and Ivo understood he was not looking at it so much as reading it by the way light breaks through storm clouds -- in shafts, one narrow beam at a time, each beam finding a different flaw. [211] "Lamplight floods and forgives," he said. "Storm light picks, and picks honestly." [223] A beam swung across the bench and the mainspring showed a hairline fracture; another beam, and the comb teeth glinted, all twenty-four intact. [246] "It plays?" [248] "It played. Brahms, badly. He filed the pins himself." [257] Ostrow set it down. "Why you?" [263] In the calm before realization -- that quiet minute when a man has assembled all the pieces of his own guilt but has not yet seen the shape they make -- Ivo answered plainly. [295] "Four years ago the clemency board asked me whether he had changed." [307] "I wrote that I could not say." [314] "It was true and it was cowardice, and the board reads cowardice as no." [328] "I apologized to him. He said apologies are cheap unless they are wound." [341] Ostrow's eyebrows rose, and something in the shop seemed to lean in: a hundred lidded boxes holding a hundred silenced tunes, a vibrant hush. [365] "His daughter is nineteen," Ivo said. "She lives above a laundry on Bell Street and has never heard it play." [385] "I want to turn an apology into action, and this is the only action left that is his and not mine." [406] Ostrow worked for three hours while the storm walked east. [416] He fitted a salvaged mainspring from a 1911 cylinder box, trued the governor, and left the filed pins exactly as Tobias had filed them, though two were wrong. [444] "Fix those," Ivo said. [448] "No," said Ostrow. "You came for a second opinion, not a second man's song." [462] He wound it four turns and opened the chambers. [471] The Brahms came out thin, limping at the second bar, unmistakably meant. [483] Ivo sat down on the stool without deciding to. [492] The shape assembled itself then, as he had known it would: he had wanted the repair to absolve him, and it would not. [515] "She may not want it from me," he said. "I may not deserve to be the one who carries it." [535] "Nobody deserves the errand," Ostrow said, wrapping the heart in fresh flannel. "That is why it gets done or it doesn't." [556] He charged eleven dollars, the price of the salvaged spring, and refused more. [569] Outside, Bell Street steamed, and the clouds had torn open into long bright blades. [583] Ivo walked into one of them, the letter in his coat, the heart ticking in his hands, rehearsing nothing. [602] Four floors up, a window was open; someone was humming, badly, over the noise of a dryer. [619] He climbed the stairs and knocked, and in the hush before the door opened, he wound it four turns. [638]

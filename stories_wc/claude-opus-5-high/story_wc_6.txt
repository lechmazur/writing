The islanders measured the year not in numbers but in the migration of clouds, when long gray herds crossed the strait and the fire season folded its wings. [28] Maren waited for that season the way other people waited for letters. [40] She was the only firefighter on the island whose surname made neighbors go quiet in the market. [57] Her father had burned the terraced orchards above the harbor when she was nine, eleven hectares of fig and almond, and he had done it patiently, tree by tree, as though pruning. [89] At the courthouse he pressed something into her palm: a foreign coin, drilled through and strung on red thread, brought home from a port he never named. [116] She wore it under her turnout coat for twenty years, the way sailors wear a saint. [132]

Grafting was her answer to him. [138] Every spring she climbed the black terraces with a canvas roll of scions and a knife honed thin as a fish bone. [160] She cut living fig wood from the two trees that had survived and bound it to the scorched rootstock that had not. [182] Around each wound she banked black sand hauled up in buckets from the volcanic beach, because the sand held the day's heat and the grafts took better warm. [210] Sixty-one trees had taken. [214] Each carried a knuckled scar where old and new wood had agreed to be one thing, and in an onshore wind the orchard hummed through those knuckles, a low reedy chord the children called the singing terraces. [251] That was the whole of her argument, unspoken and stubborn: scars can sing, and she meant to prove it where everyone could hear. [274]

So when the first herd of clouds came over that autumn and someone lit the dune grass, Maren felt the old shame arrive with the smoke. [300] The fire ran seaward instead of inland, which saved the village and trapped the one who set it. [318] She found him in the obsidian lagoon beneath the dunes, a green-black eye of water walled by old lava, where island children swam and fishermen tied their nets. [346] Teodor Vass, fifteen, stood chest-deep in kelp with his eyebrows gone and his hands cupped over a lighter he was still trying to hide. [370] The smoke lay flat on the water and the kelp turned him in slow circles, and the place kept its own peace, the drifting, saline, unbothered peace of weed moving with the swell. [403] Maren came down the scree gracefully alert, reading the wind on the back of her neck, watching the grass line and the boy at once. [428]

"They'll say I'm like your father," he said. [436]

"They'll say it about me too," she said, and put her helmet on his head. [451] She walked him out through the burn while embers went by like slow red insects, and she did not let go of his wrist once. [476]

Afterward the harbor wanted him sent to the mainland, and she stood up in the school hall and would not sit down. [498] She had been the arsonist's daughter in that hall at nine years old; she knew what a room could do to a child it had decided about. [525] Her compassion was not gentle; it was a kind of refusal, and it did not yield for three meetings. [544] The boy was given to her instead: two years of service on the terraces, sentence and apprenticeship at once. [563]

She taught him to cut a scion at a long clean angle and to bind the graft so tight it ached. [584] She taught him to carry black sand up eighty steps and bank it warm around each union. [601] When his burn scars tightened in the cold he learned to work through it, and she showed him hers, from a roof in her second year. [627]

On the last day of his service, with the clouds migrating again in their long gray file, she took the red thread from her neck. [652] The coin was worn nearly smooth, a foreign king's face rubbed to a suggestion, the drilled hole ringed bright. [671]

"My father gave me this to remember him," she said. [681] "I kept it to remember what I decided instead." [690]

Teodor tied it on, and the wind came up off the lagoon, and the sixty-one knuckled trees began their low chord above the harbor. [714] He stood there a long time with his head tilted, listening the way you listen to something being proved. [733] Then he picked up the buckets and went down to the black beach for more sand, because in spring there would be more scars to teach. [759]

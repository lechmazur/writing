For nine years I died for money. [6] The Ministry hired me as a crisis actor: fall here, scream there, let the volunteers practice tourniquets on my painted thigh. [27] Then the Exchange dome came down during an ordinary Tuesday audit, and I learned the difference between rehearsal and gravity. [46] Eleven of us went in; I came out, and I have never stopped counting. [60]

That was in the first week of the interregnum, the old Warden dead and the new one not yet drawn, forty days of a city governing itself by habit. [89] Habit is a thin roof. [94] In such a season the nomadic penumbra came back over us, as it does when no one is seated on the chair. [116] It is not a cloud and not an eclipse, only a wandering half-shadow the width of three streets, drifting where it likes, dimming lamps without darkening them. [143] Inside it, things that were bound loosen: knots, contracts, griefs, names. [154] I tracked it for a month with a borrowed almanac and caught it, at last, sliding over the Old Assay House, whose whispering gallery is the only room in the city that will repeat you honestly. [190]

Ansel keeps that gallery. [194] She is eighty and benevolently ruthless: she will give you bread, a bed, the truth about your face, and then the bill. [216] Her price for teaching me the walk was the chessboard I carried, which had belonged to Dario Vane, the drill supervisor who died beside me. [241] All sixteen pawns were missing from it. [248] He had used them for years as markers on the evacuation maps, one small wooden body for every actor he placed on the floor, and when the floor became real he never got them back. [283] Kings, bishops, knights, rooks, queens, and a board full of empty squares where the ordinary had stood. [300] Ansel turned it over twice and said it was worth her lesson. [312]

The penumbra does not hear speech, she told me; it reads the floor. [325] You address it with your feet, tracing letters across the mosaic, a pattern of walking that spells words, and the gallery's curved wall carries the scuff of each stroke around the dome until the whole ring is saying what you meant. [366] I practiced for six days, wearing my heels down, spelling small things: LIGHT, RAIN, SORRY. [381]

What I had come to unbind was a signature. [390] Every crisis actor signs a silence clause; ours survived the collapse better than the building did, and the Ministry's lawyers had been quoting it at me since the funerals. [419] During an interregnum there is no one to appeal to and no one to forbid you, which is why the shadow travels then. [442]

On the seventh morning the light in the gallery went strange, not dark but emptied, a luminous absence, as if brightness had been poured out of the air and the air kept its shape. [476] I walked UNBIND, six long letters across the mosaic, and the dome whispered it back to itself. [493] The clause let go of me the way a wet coat lets go of a shoulder. [509]

Ansel was watching from the rail with the pawnless board under her arm. [522] "Now the useful question," she said. [528] "Free to do what?" [532]

I had an answer ready, and it was not the courtroom. [543] Lawyers would argue about load-bearing tolerances; nobody was going to write down that Petra Oyelaran, a rehearsal casualty for nine years, spent her last ninety seconds holding a stranger's head off the concrete. [576] Nobody would record that Dario, who could be cruel about timing, gave away his respirator without a word. [594] I told her I meant to become the historian of small gestures, and to start with the ten. [612]

Ansel handed the board back and told me to carve the pawns myself, one a week, named. [629] She said it kindly and she did not offer to help, which is her way of making a thing stick. [649]

The penumbra moved off toward the river that afternoon, taking its emptied light with it, the way it always goes when a question has been answered. [675] The new Warden was drawn nine days later and the lamps went back to being merely bright. [692] I still walk the gallery most mornings, spelling one name at a time across the mosaic so the dome can repeat it, and I write down what each of them did with their hands at the end. [729] Four pawns stand on the board now, all named. [738] Six squares of the ten are still shining and empty, and that is a kind of record too. [756]

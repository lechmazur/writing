Ivane had never seen the ultrasonic forest, but she had mapped it twice. [13] The trees there sang above hearing, their trunks fluting frequencies that bats used as roads and that children felt as toothache. [34] Ivane felt them differently. [38] Language entered her through the skin; she absorbed dialects the way linen takes water, and the forest's ultrasound was only another tongue pressing against her forearms. [64] She wore a platinum compass ring on her left thumb, an heirloom from her mother, who had also been a mapmaker and also blind. [88] The ring's needle did not point north. [95] It pointed toward stillness. [99] Her mother's last instruction, given in the dry voice of a woman finishing a long argument with the sky, was this: somewhere in the ultrasonic forest lies a hidden stillpoint, a place where no frequency reaches, and it opens only across the pause of shifting constellations. [145] That pause came once in nineteen years: the nights when the old star-patterns had slipped their names and the new ones had not yet been agreed upon. [172] Ivane had waited since she was nine. [179] She wanted the stillpoint not for glory but because her grief had no coastline. [193] She could chart cave systems and river deltas by touch and echo, but she could not navigate the uncharted emotional depths her mother's death had opened, and she believed stubbornly that a place of perfect silence would let her hear the shape of her own mourning. [239] So she entered on the first grey night of the pause, ring on thumb, staff in hand. [256] The forest greeted her in eleven languages at once. [265] Bark spoke an old merchant creole into her palm. [274] Moss offered a vocabulary of apologies. [280] She absorbed them all and kept walking. [287] Her mother had left a trail, not of stones but of notes. [299] Each mile, a tree had been scored with a single pitch, carved so the wood would hum it forever: a low D at the ford, a rising F where the deer paths crossed, a flattened seventh at the ravine. [338] Ivane pressed her forearms to each trunk and collected them, and by the fourth night she understood she was not following directions. [360] She was assembling a melody. [365] It was her mother's lullaby, the one hummed over a blind child's cradle, hidden inside sixty miles of forest so that only someone who walked the whole of it could hear the tune entire. [399] On the ninth night, the notes ran out. [407] The melody lacked its final cadence, and the forest around her thickened with ultrasound, a pressure like a crowd shouting advice. [428] Ivane took off the platinum compass ring, held it loose on a thread of her own hair, and began to dowse. [449] She did not dowse for water. [455] She hummed the incomplete lullaby and let the ring swing, and where the ring hung dead and motionless, she dug her heel into the loam and turned. [482] Twice she lost the line. [487] Overhead, she was told later, the constellations had unfastened entirely, nameless and drifting. [500] Then, at dawn of the last night of the pause, she stepped through a curtain of ferns and the world went quiet. [522] Not silent: quiet. [525] The stillpoint was a clearing nine paces wide where the forest's frequencies cancelled one another perfectly, and Ivane's skin, for the first time in her life, learned nothing. [553] No creole, no apology, no advice. [559] Only her own pulse, and the grief beneath it, vast and finally measurable. [572] She sat in the middle of it and sang the lullaby through, and in the emptiness her voice supplied the missing cadence itself, a resolution her mother had deliberately left unwritten, because a map of another person's sorrow must end where that person's own voice begins. [618] She wept without noise, a quiet blaze in her chest that warmed rather than consumed. [633] When she left, she carved one last note into the boundary tree, a high tonic for whoever came after. [652] Her finished map, the one they still keep in the guild house, shows no roads. [667] It is a sheet of staved lines with nineteen pitches on it and, at the center, a rest. [685] Anyone who can carry a tune can find the stillpoint now. [696] Ivane wears the ring still, though its needle has gone slack and useless, which is how she knows she arrived. [716]

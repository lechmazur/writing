The phoenix rose at midnight, and the orchard did not flinch. [11] It never did; under permanent moonlight, the pear trees had forgotten how to fear fire. [26] Yrsa Bell, beast whisperer, knelt in the silver grass and unwrapped her spices. [39] Grains of paradise, black cardamom, saffron threads harvested from a comet's tail garden, long pepper the color of dried blood. [59] These were the only tongues the great beasts still answered: not words, but scent, the oldest grammar in the universe. [79] Her hands shook, but her heart was paradoxically steady, as though grief had hammered it into something load-bearing. [97] In her pocket lay a strip of paper soft as moth-wing: a fortune from a cookie, cracked open in a roadside inn the last night Tobin was alive. [125] WHAT YOU CANNOT HOLD, YOU MUST BECOME. [132] He had laughed and called it nonsense, and then the phoenix's death-burn took him, because he had stood too close to the thing they both loved. [158] Now the bird was new again, hatched out of its own ash in the orchard's heart, and it remembered nothing. [178] Not Tobin's hand on its neck; not the twelve years the three of them had wintered together; not Yrsa at all. [199] That was the cruelty of resurrection: it saved the body and spent the bond. [213]

She struck her flint over the mortar, and the spices caught. [224] Smoke rose in a braid, and with it she began to conjure. [236] The scent unspooled outward, past the blossoming boughs, past the moon that never set, and the orchard leaned in to listen. [257] Yrsa had whispered ten thousand beasts calm in her life, and every one of them had taught her the same lesson: a bond is not memory. [283] A bond is a shape two creatures make together, and shapes can be reforged if someone is willing to bend. [303]

The phoenix came down through the blossoms, no larger than a heron, its feathers still soft with newborn light. [322] It landed an arm's length away and regarded her the way one star regards another across an impossible gulf: patiently, without recognition. [344] "I know," Yrsa said. [348] "You cannot come back to me, so I will come to you." [360]

The smoke thickened, and the change began where she had asked it to begin: in the bones of her arms. [380] It hurt in a way she had not permitted herself to imagine. [392] She wanted to stop. [396] She had wanted to stop since the moment she ground the first cardamom pod, because she loved her hands, her voice, her human mouth that could still say Tobin's name aloud. [427] But the fortune had been right, and reluctance is not the same as refusal. [441] Feathers came through her forearms like grass through snow. [450] Her shoulders folded into a geometry meant for altitude. [459] The paper fortune slipped from her pocket and browned to nothing in the mortar's heat, and she let it go, because she had finally understood it. [485]

The phoenix did not startle. [489] It watched with the terrible tenderness of something that has died and returned and therefore finds nothing strange. [507] When Yrsa could no longer kneel, she stood on new legs, thin and scaled and sure. [523] She was smaller now, and stranger, and the orchard smelled overwhelmingly of saffron and blood-colored pepper and the far side of death. [545] She could not say Tobin's name anymore. [552] She found, to her astonishment, that she did not need to. [563]

The phoenix stepped forward and laid its head against her breastbone, and the shape reassembled itself, not from memory but from scent, from the spice-smoke they were both standing in. [593] Two small bright creatures under a moon that would never set, holding the third one between them, the way two hands hold water. [616] The orchard bloomed on, indifferent and generous. [622] Somewhere very far off, a star went out, and neither of them minded; they had work to do, a thousand winters of it, and now the same throat to sing it with. [654]

Yrsa Bell had spent her life translating between kinds, and had never once expected to be the thing translated. [673] But the universe, it turns out, is not vast and cold. [684] It is vast and close, like a face leaning over a crib, and it had been waiting a long time to ask her this. [708] She had answered yes with her whole body. [716]

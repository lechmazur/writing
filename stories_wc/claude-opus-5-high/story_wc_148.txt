They will find the bodies at slack tide, and after that no one will need my account of anything. [19] So I am writing while the copper light still holds, one slow revolution at a time, warm as a kettle on a hearth I never had. [45] My name is Emer, and my father was the boy who cried wolf. [58] You know his half of it: three false alarms, then a real gray flood out of the pines, and no one coming down the hill. [83] What the story leaves out is the hush afterward, how it settled over our house and stayed for thirty years, thick as felt. [106] I have been chasing that hush my whole life, trying to find where it goes when it leaves a mouth. [126] It goes here. [129]

Wolfcry Light stands on a jagged reef two miles off the coast, a lighthouse that the crown converted into a prison when it grew cheaper to keep one man than to pay three. [162] The prisoner tends the lamp; the lamp keeps the prisoner; nobody rows out except to deliver oil and, once, me. [182] They sent me for false witness, which is the family trade. [193] I had sworn I saw a ship foundering off Scant Head; the harbormaster believed me for exactly as long as it took to launch, and found nothing but weather. [222] Eleven days later the Verity broke apart in the same water while I sat in irons, shouting through a grate that nobody was near enough to hear. [249]

The grate in my door here is a lattice taken from the confessional of the drowned chapel at Sanct Mere, sawn out and fitted to the gaol because the carpenter was thrifty and the wood was good. [286] Thrift is how miracles get installed in ordinary buildings. [295] The lattice sustains impossibilities. [299] Anything confessed through it stays true a while, the way a bell stays in a room after it is struck. [319] I learned this in my first winter, when the oil ran out in a gale and I put my mouth to the wood and said, I am keeping the light. [349] The flame came up off a dry wick and hovered there, half an inch above the brass, burning nothing, for nine hours. [371] Three colliers passed safe in the dark and never knew they had been saved by a liar's word, held aloft by old confession-wood. [394]

So I understood what the hush was for. [401] It is not absence. [405] It is a labyrinth, and every unbelieved voice is walled into it, and the only way out is to thread it with your own story, pulled long and unbroken, so that whoever comes after can follow the line back to daylight. [446] My father never got his thread laid; they told his story for him and made it a lesson for children. [466] I intend better, and I have until slack tide. [475]

Here is the rest, plainly. [480] Three weeks ago the supply boat came with two guards and a writ that transferred me back to the mainland for a second trial, where the Verity's owners wanted a name to hang. [513] I warned them that the reef eats boats on a falling tide with the wind in the east. [531] They laughed, being men who had read the lesson for children, and they went out anyway, and the reef did what I said it would. [556] Their bodies are under the north shelf, in eleven feet of green water, and at slack tide this evening they will lift and turn and show themselves to the coast. [586] Nobody will believe I only warned them. [593] That is fine; belief was never the thing I wanted. [603] I wanted the hush to have a shape, and now it does. [615]

I am speaking this into the lattice sentence by sentence, and the lattice is sending it out the only way anything leaves this tower: through the scuffs on a narrow door left ajar for decades, the service door at the stairhead that no keeper ever managed to close. [663] Every keeper's shoulder has worn a mark into it, and the marks take words the way wax takes a seal. [683] Climb the spiral some winter, put your palm on those scuffs, and you will hear me, and my father under me, and whoever is under him. [709] The lamp turns; the copper light lies down across the floor like something tired and satisfied; the wolves in it are only shadows of the glazing bars. [736] There is my thread, laid end to end, and the tide is going slack. [750] Follow it out. [753]

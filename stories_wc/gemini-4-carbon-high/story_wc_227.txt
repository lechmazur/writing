Green vapor hissed against the thinning brass hull of the workshop of gears and cogs as it drifted across a boundless acid sea.  Overhead, giant bronze flywheels and oiled escapements ticked in faltering rhythm, driving the bilge pumps that kept the vessel afloat above the vitriolic deep.  At the center of the shuddering deck stood a tall glass cistern of pure water, preserving a marble statue centuries old whose pale surface bore hundreds of pockmarks from ancient corrosive rains.  Inside that tank floated Oren, an uncertain alchemist who had spent his life doubting every tincture while greater masters failed to neutralize the rising tide.  All the continents had dissolved into nameless sludge, leaving him desperate to conjure meaning from the edge of oblivion before his sinking sanctuary vanished too. 

Clutched against his chest was his final pure reagent, a golden key ring charm shaped like a tiny weaver shuttle upon an iron loop whose doors were long gone.  Gold alone could resist the eating brine outside, and Oren had steeped the ornament in luminous flux until its metal softened like warm wax.  Guided by kintsugi intentions, he did not wish to disguise the ruin of the drowned age, but rather to fill every wound with radiant ore until brokenness itself became indestructible beauty.  Yet the auric paste required human voice to awaken, and caustic fumes already coiled beneath the rafters above the cistern.  He could labor only between breaths held underwater, surfacing into the poisonous mist for heartbeats to narrate a memory over the charm before plunging back beneath the clean ripples. 

Kicking upward now, Oren broke the surface, squinted against the stinging chartreuse haze, and lifted the gleaming shuttle close to his blistered lips.  With trembling urgency he began to narrate how bakers along the old basalt quay once sang over braided loaves while lanterns mirrored the dawn.  At the cadence of his syllables, a bead of molten gold detached from the key ring charm and crystallized into a jagged crescent that hummed with captured warmth.  Oren snatched a shallow gasp of burning air and dove downward as brass pinions groaned beyond the curved glass.  Suspended in the cool hush with his lungs locked tight, he anchored the spell by matching the pockmarks on the statue centuries old to the exact irregular ridges of the golden drop.  His fingertips skimmed past three shallow craters along the marble shoulder before finding a curved scar that fit the hardened song like a lock receiving its ward.  The instant the gold seated itself, delicate threads of amber light raced outward to earlier seams along the ribs, proving how this strange alchemy quilts memories together across stone. 

Suddenly a heavy drive gear sheared from its axle outside the tank and plunged through the floorboards in an eruption of emerald steam.  The entire craft listed sharply, and cold dread seized Oren as his lifelong uncertainty whispered that an unfinished lattice would shatter the monument when the glass gave way.  Perhaps his proportions had erred from the start, leaving nothing behind except mute gravel beneath a dead sky.  Yet his starving lungs drove him upward into those brief, scorching intervals between breaths held underwater where the last history of humankind had to be spoken.  Coughing through raw smoke, he forced his ragged voice to narrate harsher recollections: the tolling of sunken bells, bitter quarrels over rainwater, and quiet neighbors sharing coats in the dark.  Three thorny globules formed from the dwindling shuttle, and Oren sank again through shimmering bubbles to press each piece into matching pockmarks across the carved throat and upturned palms.  Golden veins stitched grief beside gladness like patchwork panels, vitrifying the porous stone wherever their brightness met. 

Boiling acid spilled across the tilting deck now, eating through the iron collar of the tank while one deep pit remained bare upon the brow of the figure.  Surfacing one last time into blinding green fire, Oren watched the iron loop dissolve from his fingers until only a final nub of the golden key ring charm survived.  Having no grand legend left, he chose to narrate his own halting devotion, confessing how even a fearful apprentice had loved the vanished world enough to mend its scars.  The last remnant of gold liquefied against his thumb just as the glass wall fractured, and he kicked downward with silent resolve.  Before the rushing acid reached him, Oren sealed that jagged hollow upon the forehead, locking the entire luminous quilt into one unbroken chorus.  While the last cogs dissolved into the dark sea, the gilded monument settled intact upon the abyssal floor, singing softly of everything that had ever lived.

High within the subterranean chasm, the suspended palace anchored to giant stalactites shuddered with the ceaseless rhythm of the abyssal engines below.  Massive iron chains groaned against wet limestone spires, sending a perpetual tremor through the empty halls until the night itself felt like a quaking reverie.  In a turret slick with mineral drip, Julian stood in torn velvet stiff with axle grease, a bedraggled prince with burned cuffs and uncombed hair dusted in soot.  With raw, shivering fingers, he worked to assemble the final copper manifold, blown glass dome, and velvet bellows around the priceless treasure upon his table.  Inside that scavenged cradle rested the speckled blue egg of an extinct bird, pulled months earlier from a petrified marsh beneath the lowest smelters. 

The instant Julian locked the brass escapement into place, a faint inner tap answered from the calcium shell, launching him into a breathless vigil that would carry him through the discovery of a lifetime.  He leaned close to the warming glass, entranced as a dark silhouette shifted beneath the curved surface.  For years, his sole longing had been to reawaken beauty in a mechanical world where iron automatons marched to steam whistles and nothing bloomed or sang without a punch card.  To salvage gearwork for this forbidden incubator, the royal heir had forsaken courtly banquets to crawl through oily exhaust ducts, trading silk robes for scars and calluses.  Now, watching the shell rock gently against its velvet pads, he felt the sterile tyranny of the clockwork empire begin to crack. 

A second tap rang out, sharper than before, testing the delicate mend where Julian had sealed an old fissure in the egg with spun gold wire and warm oil.  Because the ancient volumes on biology had been fed to the furnaces centuries ago, he had built every part of this apparatus by hypothesizing about everything that living creatures might require.  Across his stained sleeves, he had scribbled wild guesses regarding the fever of a brooding mother, the humidity of lost forests, and the cadence of an organic pulse.  Where knowledge failed him, he had substituted the principles of steam pressure, capillary lubrication, and harmonic resonance.  Yet as he progressed deeper through the discovery unfolding beneath the dome, a strange anomaly made his breath catch in his throat. 

Along the mended fracture, the golden patch was not falling away, for the wire pulsed like an artery, drinking dark oil from the reservoir directly into the shell.  Julian lifted the glass cover with trembling hands just as the blue oval split apart with a bright metallic chime.  Overhead, the giant stalactites groaned under the weight of the palace chains, mirroring the dizzying shock that rooted him to the wet stone floor.  Spilling onto the velvet was the unmistakable result of a flawed genesis, a new beginning warped by the very ingenuity that had sparked it.  Because his hypotheses had relied upon the language of the forge, the malleable embryo had absorbed the machine in order to survive. 

A tiny songbird lay shivering among the broken shards, clothed in damp cobalt down and possessing a genuine, racing pulse.  Still, fine gold filaments laced the translucent skin of its throat, its left wing shimmered with feathers of woven copper mesh, and a miniature brass reed clicked softly inside its breastbone with every breath.  Tiny droplets of dark lubricant beaded at the corners of its bright, liquid eyes as it struggled to stand. 

Julian sank to his knees and cupped his grimy palms around the fragile hybrid, cradling its feverish warmth.  Tears spilled down his hollow cheeks, for the triumph trembling in his grasp was bitterly sweet.  He had dreamed of restoring untainted nature, but his clumsy devotion had branded the last heir of the sky with the mark of the subterranean furnaces.  Even so, the creature did not recoil from its maker, nestling against his thumb before it fluttered its mismatched wings and lifted its narrow head.  From its open beak rose a slender ribbon of pale steam and a wondrous, trembling note, half crystalline trill and half resonant bell.  The melody drifted past the dripping limestone arches, harmonizing with the shuddering chains until the whole dark cavern seemed to listen in awe.  Wrapped in that fragile song, the weary prince smiled, realizing that an imperfect miracle was still enough to teach an iron world to dream.

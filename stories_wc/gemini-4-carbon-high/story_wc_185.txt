Weeks after the energy beings arrive, panic has long since been beaten out of Torin like slag from worked iron.  What remains in his chest is a dense, tempered stillness, forged beneath the ceaseless battering of the harmonic caves.  High among the fluted quartz arches, the newcomers drift in slow currents, gently luminous against the dark stone like lanterns of pale amber.  They harbor no malice toward the subterranean folk below.  In the open sky, their natural hum must have been scarce louder than summer bees, yet within these resonant limestone galleries every murmur multiplies ten thousand times into a crushing gale.  Even the visitors seem trapped by their own multiplied voices, buffeted from wall to crystal wall in a feedback loop that shakes dust from the mountain's ribs. 

For thirty years Torin has served his clan as a ripple-mark mapkeeper, charting the subtle breath of the deep earth.  In quieter seasons, he read the fine ridges left in wet silt where underground acoustics brushed across still pools, predicting rockfalls before a single pebble slipped.  Now those silt basins boil into cold mist under the relentless roar, and oil lamps gutter out the instant a flint sparks.  Behind thick clay bulkheads in the side alcoves, forty of his kin lie wrapped in felted wool, too weakened by internal tremors to survive the shrieking climb toward the surface.  Each hour, hairline fissures widen across their packed clay doors.  Torin ventures out alone with a singular purpose: to find the quiet in the storm before the last barrier crumbles. 

Strapped tight against his breastbone hangs an heirloom echo chamber stone, a hollow sphere of dense basalt carved internally with spiral baffles.  Mapkeepers of old used it to trap a single chamber's baseline note and return an inverted sigh to test tunnel resonance.  Set loose at random in this tempest, the vessel would shatter like an eggshell.  Yet every closed acoustic system, however violent, possesses a master node where every rebounding wave crosses its opposite.  Lowering himself onto the subterranean glacier that floors the deepest sinkhole, Torin presses his bruised ribs to the frost and begins crawling forward.  Where liquid water can no longer hold a readable trace, the frozen tarn preserves the geometry of force, and he charts the invisible gale by examining crack patterns in ice. 

With torches useless in the pulsing wind, only the pale, steady radiance of the drifting beings above illuminates the black sheet beneath his gloves.  White shear lines spiderweb through the frozen depth in jagged, overlapping rings, marking the antinodes where colliding frequencies hammer hardest.  Torin moves with slow, deliberate economy, neither fighting the vibration that rattles his teeth nor yielding his focus to pain.  Yard by patient yard, he follows the radial fractures inward as their angles sharpen and converge.  Near the center of the tarn, a thousand splintered white veins abruptly terminate around a single, unblemished hexagon of dark glass no wider than his shoulders.  Here, the competing pressures strike from every gallery at once, pinning the ice in a tense, unbroken equilibrium. 

Torin draws his knees onto the clear pane and unstraps the echo chamber stone with numb, steady fingers.  Setting the carved aperture flush against the center of the hexagon is only half the craft.  To conjure an inverse wave strong enough to unravel an entire cavern's fury, he must draw the leaden stopper on the exact fractional beat between crest and trough, lending the steady pulse in his wrists to the rock.  He closes his eyes, lets the crushing weight press his shoulders flat, and counts the deep throb beneath his knees until his breath locks onto the mountain's stride.  On the seventh beat, he pulls the pin.  The basalt sphere drinks the converging shriek through its narrow throat, spins the captured violence around its helical chambers, and breathes it back out of phase. 

From the stone unfolds a bloom of hush, widening across the ice in invisible, overlapping petals of cancelled force.  Ring after widening ring climbs the limestone walls, stripping the runaway echo from the air without harming the soft light overhead.  Released at last from the mirror of their own clamor, the luminous visitors cease their thrashing and settle beneath the high vaults like resting embers.  Along the lower ledges, dust stops sifting from the clay seals, and soon his kin will break them open to breathe clean, untroubled air.  Torin keeps both palms pressed upon the warm, humming basalt, resting unbowed within a peace shaped like iron on the anvil.

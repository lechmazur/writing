Yet a stubborn instinct persists when accountants price wonder out of reach, urging you to stand guard over your own spark of defiance. 

Julian Vane, a charming rogue scholar stripped of his university chair for lecturing in untaxed riddles, turned that instinct into an underground art.  With a crooked grin and soil beneath his fingernails, he founded the city's last secret language club.  Because passive visual sight carries zero cognitive tariff, his recruits began joining community gardens to plant message patterns across twelve public districts.

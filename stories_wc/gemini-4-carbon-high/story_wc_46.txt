Warm spring wind drifts off the bay, carrying the honeyed scent of waking cliffside clover into the serpentine grotto in mist.  At his stone bench near the cavern mouth, Oren rests his stiffened leg and listens to the gentle tide.  Years have passed since a snapped rigging line ended his ascents into the cloud towers, leaving him an earthbound star watcher among people who map the heavens from aloft.  Yet down here where stone meets water, the vernal equinox brings its own delicate miracle.  Along the winding green mineral walls of the cavern, pale wind orchids wait for dusk to unfurl their translucent petals in the mild draft.  Soon villagers will walk this twisting passage toward the open tidal basin to watch the spring constellations rise.  Torches and oil lanterns cannot enter the tunnel, because even faint smoke wilts the fragile blossoms, and open flames blind human eyes to subtle starlight.  To guide his neighbors safely around the dark, slick bends, Oren works to share light without using fire. 

In his scarred hands rests a thick, rounded core of sea glass, salvaged from the sunken lens of an ancient harbor beacon and tumbled by decades of surf.  Years of sand and tide have left its aquamarine skin pitted and frosted, scattering moonlight into a weak haze.  He has already mounted smaller prisms along the outer curves to relay twilight inward, yet the deepest bend demands a domed jewel with an angled back to turn that beam.  Drawing upon the quiet discipline of gem polishing, Oren presses his good foot against the wooden treadle and holds the glass to a wet sandstone wheel dusted with quartz grit.  Cool water whispers over spinning stone as memories resurface from his youth above the clouds.  He recalls the rush of high gales, the fierce brilliance of untamed stars, and the lonely winter after his fall when he believed his purpose had ended on the rocks.  Back then, his spirit felt much like this weathered shard, battered by heavy waters and clouded against the sky.  Yet as he swaps coarse sandstone for smooth slate and shell paste, another memory rises of his mentor teaching him that patient hands can coax starlight into any dark place.  Layer by layer, the milky rind yields to tranquil clarity, and his lingering ache softens into peace. 

Every pass across the wheel shaves away fine grains of crystal, demanding balance as well as brilliance.  At the central turn, where no dry ledge reaches the relayed moonbeam, a forked brass arm hangs on twin pivots from the stone arch.  Unless the finished piece matches its bronze counterweight to the grain, the arm will tilt out of alignment with the incoming ray and tremble in the sea draft.  Stopping the wheel, Oren dries the rounded glass on soft linen and places it upon his delicate brass scale to weigh it against a test stone.  The pan holding the glass dips by a fraction.  Smiling quietly, he switches to a chamois pad dressed with beeswax and rouge, buffing the dome and rear bevel while twilight deepens over the bay.  When he pauses once more to weigh the polished orb, both pans come to rest on an even horizon.  Held toward the rising moon, the transformed sea glass leaves dull wreckage behind and grows reflectively vibrant, drinking in pale celestial silver and pouring it forth enriched with living ocean green. 

Carrying his creation, Oren limps deeper into the serpentine grotto in mist, following the silver thread bounced from wall to wall by his outer prisms.  At the central curve, that guiding thread ends in velvet shadow, where clusters of closed orchid buds cling to damp jade veins.  Reaching upward, he seats the beveled glass into the cradle of the brass arm.  True to his scale, the arm levels immediately, suspending a pearl of stillness amid the drifting vapor and restless air.  Moonlight strikes its heart and fans around the bend in cool, steady waves, illuminating every wet foothold ahead without a whisper of smoke or heat.  Stirred by a warm coastal zephyr, the pale orchids begin to bloom along the glowing stone, filling the passage with a sweet fragrance like rain over spring meadows.  Soft voices and footsteps echo at the cavern entrance as the first families arrive, their faces lifted in wonder toward the gentle emerald radiance.  Standing in the quiet glow he has shaped, Oren breathes the floral air and knows that even rooted to the earth, he still brings the heavens home.

In his nocturnal haven beside a quiet brook, Oren knelt upon damp slate that mirrored the wheeling constellations.  Silver starlight pooled in mossy hollows along the bank, rendering the glade a terrestrially cosmic sanctuary where mud and nebula seemed woven from the same luminous thread.  Within Oren burned a steady, incandescent focus, a serene fervor like white fire resting beneath still ice.  As a solitary summoner, he permitted no apprentice or companion near the water, for even a second human heartbeat would fracture the delicate equilibrium of his craft.  Empires downriver waged their hidden wars amid whispered secrets, sending covert murmurs skimming along the night breeze like invisible sparks seeking dry tinder.  Tonight, an urgent tremor prickled against his palms, warning him that his vigil had reached its true purpose: to hush a prophecy spoken in code before the waking world could hear and obey it. 

He closed his eyes and tilted his chin toward the dark canopy, listening past the gentle murmur of pebbles below.  Threaded through the wind came a sharp, mathematical cadence of clicks and aspirated syllables, cast upstream by cabalistic chroniclers who wished to summon ruin without speaking its forbidden name aloud.  Every coded pulse scored jagged ripples across the surface of the stream, disturbing the reflected stars until Orion and Cygnus shivered like broken glass.  If that serrated pattern completed its final stanza at daybreak, the slumbering fault lines beneath the valley would answer the cipher and tear the province apart.  Lesser mages might have answered with thunderous wards, yet noise only fed an acoustic curse, lending fresh momentum to its perilous rhythm.  Oren worked instead by mapping silence, locating the minute hollows of absolute stillness tucked behind wet boulders and beneath overhanging ferns. 

From his sleeve he drew a square of pale reed parchment dusted with crushed mica that gleamed softly in the gloom.  With unhurried precision, his fingers began to fold the sheet, translating every quiet pocket along the riverbank into a crisp geometric valley or ridge.  A pause between two falling dewdrops became a narrow pleat at the neck, while the deep mute shadow under an alder root formed the broad span of the body.  Most intricate of all was the delicate latticework of creases pressed into the crane's origami wing, which charted seven overlapping dead zones where sound could neither echo nor survive.  As the cipher grew bolder, rattling the dry sedge with venomous urgency, Oren dipped one finger into the starlit water and traced a glowing sigil along that folded pinion.  He did not chant or strike the earth to call an entity forth from the outer dark.  Instead, he exhaled his entire gathered will in a silent, radiant offering, kindling the parchment from within until it shone with a tranquil blaze of pale gold. 

The summoned spirit awakened inside the paper shell, weightless as starlight yet anchored to the cool mineral breath of the riverbed.  Oren opened his hands, allowing the luminous bird to slip from his palms and flow seamlessly into the silver current of air just above the water.  It did not flap wildly against the rising gale of coded whispers, choosing instead to glide directly into the teeth of the unseen cadence.  With each measured sweep, the charted creases along its flank began to relax and open, projecting the mapped stillness outward across the glade.  Wherever that warm, noiseless radiance touched the air, the brittle clicks and hissed consonants lost their sharp edges and unraveled.  As the paper unfolded itself midair, ironing its own intricate pleats flat, it pressed the jagged static of the curse into a smoothed hush that drifted harmlessly over the stones. 

One final, desperate burst of the cipher surged down from the hills, straining to lock its lethal sequence together before the eastern sky paled.  Oren held his breath, anchoring his pulse to the ancient patience of the bedrock while the incandescent sheet expanded to meet the shock.  Light met venom without a single crackle, drinking the coded syllables whole until not one coherent fragment remained to trouble the sleeping earth.  Now completely uncreased and glowing like an ember of pure dawn, the parchment settled onto the brook, letting its borrowed fire flow gently away with the clear water.  On the mirrored surface, the constellations steadied into unbroken brilliance once more, and Oren rested his palms upon the cool slate in radiant peace.

In the hush of a hidden sunrise, before waking canyon winds could scatter a whisper, pale gold spilled across the upper clouds while the deep floor of Echo Gorge remained steeped in violet shadow.  Oren steadied his glass lantern and stepped quietly across the wet limestone riverbed.  As a lost soul collector, he carried neither iron chains nor solemn prayer books into the chasm.  Instead, he bore a small driftwood harp whose neck had warped beneath river mud years ago, leaving its brass wires permanently uneven.  Between his calloused thumb and forefinger rested a curved crocodile's tooth, yellowed with age and notched along its biting ridge. 

Ahead in the mist, a translucent figure flickered like a bruised moth and bolted deeper into the winding narrows.  Oren lengthened his stride to pursue the fleeing shade before direct sunlight could burn away the sheltering vapor and drive her dormant into the cold stone for another lonely season.  Sensing his approach, the spirit spun around and swelled into a towering phantom of jagged frost, unleashing a silent wail that rattled loose pebbles down the cliffs.  Oren remained modestly fearless, neither puffing out his chest in defiance nor flinching from the bitter chill that rimed his woolen coat.  He simply tilted his head in quiet sympathy and kept walking through her illusory claws as though parting a curtain of winter rain. 

Ghostly bluster held little sway over a man who had once stared into the jaws of an actual monster in the southern marshes.  Years earlier, an ambushing crocodile had capsized Oren's skiff and clamped down upon his prized concert harp instead of his ribs, leaving a single shed tooth wedged deep inside the splintered wood.  Sprawled in shallow mud while cradling his ruined livelihood, Oren had suddenly barked out a helpless laugh at his own pompous misery.  That unexpected mirth had mended something ragged inside his grieving heart, inspiring him to integrate laughter into healing whenever sorrow grew too rigid to bear.  He had pried the conical fang loose to serve as his plectrum, refusing ever to straighten the crooked frame. 

Now the fleeing shade shrank back into a curved limestone alcove where the gorge ended in a dry waterfall.  Her translucent shoulders trembled with the crushing dignity of the newly dead, who always believed their final regrets demanded eternal weeping.  Pure, stately music would only deepen her tragic pride and bind her tighter to the shadows.  Oren sat upon a smooth boulder, rested the twisted wood against his collarbone, and dragged the crocodile's tooth across the uneven strings.  He worked his gentle craft through the distorted notes of a misaligned harp, sending sour, buzzing twangs skittering upward against the fluted rock. 

Echo Gorge caught every clumsy vibration and multiplied it tenfold, turning the bent chords into a warm chorus of stumbling footsteps, cracked voices, and endearingly human blunders.  In this resonant hollow, flawless melodies invited silence, whereas honest imperfection coaxed forth spectral confessions from spirits weary of hiding their shame.  Faint acoustic traces from souls healed long ago answered first along the ledges, murmuring preserved admissions of burned wedding loaves, forgotten speeches, and misplaced spectacles.  Listening to the crooked rhythm stumble and recover without embarrassment, the cornered woman lowered her icy hands from her face.  Her voice joined the rebounding notes in a thin, wistful whisper that unburdened her deepest humiliation. 

She had been a revered town magistrate who feared no bandit, yet she had perished tumbling down her cellar stairs while chasing a runaway wheel of salted cheese in her nightgown.  For weeks she had lingered here in mortified grief, unable to face eternity after so undignified a farewell to the sweet world she had loved.  Oren met her gaze with tender respect and hooked the notched tip of the crocodile's tooth beneath his lowest, loosest wire.  He released a single, absurdly flat pluck that bounced down the canyon walls exactly like a heavy round of cheese thumping down wooden steps. 

Silence hung for one fragile heartbeat before a startled snort escaped the magistrate's pale lips.  Oren chuckled softly alongside her, his eyes crinkling with a kindness that honored both the sharp sting of mortality and the grace of shared folly.  As genuine laughter rippled through her shadowy form, the jagged frost melted into a soft golden luminescence heavy with every memory she had ever cherished.  Oren unlatched his glass lantern, welcoming the gentle, weightless spark inside so he could carry her safely past the high rim.  Above them, the first direct ray of morning finally spilled over the cliffs, filling the empty chasm with a radiant, lingering ache that felt very much like peace.

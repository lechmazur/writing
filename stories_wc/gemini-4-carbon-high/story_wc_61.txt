Within the pressurized gas envelope of the nebula garden, colossal silicate orchids began their millennial shedding, releasing breathable plumes of oxygen into the dense hydrogen and xenon mist.  As the glowing canopy wilted into darkness, miles of translucent blossoms drifted downward toward the central gravity core, turning the entire atmospheric pocket volatile and blind.  Perched upon a high crystalline frond, Oul listened to that soft botanical rain and wept droplets of liquid argon for the vanishing beauty of the cycle.  Among a chitinous species governed by cold instinct, Oul was an anomaly, an otherworldly sentimentalist who wore a woven pouch of withered seedpods and mourned epochs nobody else remembered.  Pressed tenderly against a hollow thoracic shell, Oul cradled a tarnished brass calculating machine gear salvaged from an ancient precursor wreck tangled in the upper shoals. 

Far below in the pitch dark nursery reefs, thousands of newly hatched kin stirred in sudden terror as the overhead luminescence died.  Driven by rigid generational patterns encoded into their ganglia, the sightless young began scraping their pyrite claws together to strike a comforting spark.  Oul had studied scorched debris blown in from neighboring clouds and knew the catastrophe of combustion.  One naked flame in this newly oxygenated vapor would birth a brief, blinding inferno that reduced both nursery and flora to cinders.  Desperate to share light without using fire, Oul folded translucent fins and stepped off the high spire, beginning a slow gravitational transit directly across the dark face of the central core.  There would be only one brief window to pacify the brood, measured across the quiet sigh of falling petals before the volatile gases fully mixed at the basin floor. 

Swallowed by absolute shadow midair, Oul navigated the drifting botanical maze through sonar pinging.  With a sharpened tarsal spur, Oul struck the rim of the brass gear wedged firmly into that hollow thoracic shell.  The forgotten builders of the wreck had milled those microscopic teeth to an irrational geometric ratio that approximates infinity, ensuring that no harmonic wave ever repeated or canceled itself out.  Amplified by living bone, a pure metallic chime rippled outward through the heavy mist, bouncing off curling tendrils and returning crisp acoustic maps to delicate auditory membranes.  Using those echoes to aim each strike toward the densest floral drifts, Oul unleashed cascading overtones that subdivided endlessly through the vapor.  Wherever the intersecting sound waves compressed microscopic bubbles of xenon clinging to the descending blooms, acoustic cavitation occurred.  Trapped pockets of gas collapsed inward under pure pressure without igniting the surrounding oxygen, flashing with cool, silvery bursts of sonoluminescence.  Ribbon after ribbon of cold radiance bloomed in the wake of the lone traveler, turning every drifting petal into a gentle, heatless lantern. 

Yet a vibration whose geometry approximates infinity demands a terrible physical anchor.  Halfway through the silent transit across the abyss, the relentless feedback of the brass wheel began vitrifying the organic shell that held it.  Fine fractures of smooth glass spread outward from the embedded gear, transmuting hoarded memories, tender griefs, and trembling nerves into clear, chiming crystal.  Strangely, Oul felt no agony, only a lulling numbness as mortal fear dissolved into pure mathematical cadence.  Down on the nursery ledges, bathed now in shimmering silver twilight, the young kin lowered their pyrite claws and tilted quiet faces toward the sky.  The spark had been averted, and the fragile sanctuary would endure another age unburned. 

As Oul arced downward past the curving horizon of the dark core, the expanding sonar pinging reached the deepest floor of the root basin and returned a final, unmistakable echo.  Simultaneously, the descending veil of glowing blossoms illuminated the ancient silt beneath the orchids.  Resting undisturbed in pale, orderly strata were hundreds of vitrified glass husks shaped just like Oul, each peaceful figure fused around an identical brass gear plucked from the inexhaustible wreck above.  Clutched against every crystalline martyr hung a woven pouch of preserved seeds and dried flora, identical to the keepsakes Oul carried now.  Oul understood then that empathy was no rebellion at all, merely the complementary half of the deepest generational patterns governing the nebula garden.  Every epoch, the ecosystem bred one weeping, nostalgic heart precisely so it would climb to the brass ruin, sacrifice itself to cold acoustics, and lull its fiery siblings back to sleep.  Cradled in a troubling serenity that left no room for sorrow or pride, Oul struck the gear one final time as glassy stillness claimed the last living nerve.  Drifting into quiet alignment beside those luminous predecessors, the newest guardian settled softly into the silt just as dusk completed its passage across the quiet sigh of falling petals.

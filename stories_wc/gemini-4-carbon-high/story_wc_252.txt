Ten years after violence became impossible, when a planetary shift in ocean acoustics caused human muscles to lock solid at any intent to harm, the former cutthroats of Driftwake Cay fought exclusively through sharp tongues and aggressive caretaking.  Julian, a graceful dock worker whose corsair agility had ripened into the fluid poise of balancing heavy salt casks across pitching barges, vaulted a flooded bollard and cast off the final evacuation ferry with one effortless pivot.  As the steamer vanished toward the mainland, port warden Senna marched across the awash planks, scowled at his damp shirt, and yanked a dry wool vest over his shoulders while muttering that showoffs froze fastest.  "Keep fussing and you will ruin my balance," Julian shot back, though his cold fingers lingered warmly against her wrists to tuck her frayed cuffs clear of the rising surf.  Beneath their boots, Driftwake groaned against its unnatural captivity. 

By origin, the cay was a disappearing island, a porous raft of living coral bone meant to ride thermal fogs into uncharted pelagic currents every century.  Generations ago, greedy raiders had grafted giant barnacle clamps around its submerged root tendons, pinning the nomadic atoll to coastal bedrock to build a stationary smuggling depot.  Now the century tide had arrived, pulling the tethered island downward to drown instead of letting it vanish safely out to sea.  Julian and Senna peered past the slick pilings into the sublittoral fringe, that turbulent, permanently submerged shelf just below the extreme low watermark where crimson kelp whipped around the strained moorings.  Because the barnacle clamps were living organisms, no crowbar or blade could smash them loose without triggering instant muscular paralysis in the wielder. 

Every sensible townsperson had fled for stiff mainland jobs, yet neither Julian nor Senna could bear a static shore.  Both stayed behind for the unspoken urge to find freedom in embracing their true nature: Julian was born for fluid motion on an unmoored horizon rather than tame harbor drudgery, and Senna was a creature of wild currents disguised behind port ledgers.  From her oilskin coat, Senna drew a pirate's whalebone scrimshaw, a curved shard of polished leviathan jaw etched with spiral sound channels by Driftwake's remorseful first captain.  Tuned to the same marine frequencies that ended human bloodshed, the relic possessed an intimidating power that unlocks guarded hearts whenever submerged.  Harbor lore held that the seabed clamps would relax only by the call of a whale from the deep, and deep whales answered only a wholly unshielded pulse ringing through carved bone.  Six tough divers had tried over the years, and all six had bolted to the surface in terror the instant the vibrating bone stripped away their cynical armor. 

"Try not to flinch like the rest, dock boy," Senna grumbled, though she gently padded his dive harness so the lead weights would not chafe his ribs.  Julian answered by adjusting her glass mask with steady thumbs, murmuring that her stubborn scowl alone could ballast a galleon, before they plunged together into the cold surge.  Down in the green gloom of the sublittoral fringe, ferocious undertows whipped bull kelp across the calcified anchor chains.  Where brute muscle would have stalled against the backwash, Julian moved with a stevedore's practiced rhythm, riding the sideways swell to swing Senna smoothly beside the central barnacle clamp.  She wedged the etched whalebone into a resonant cleft of the living shell and slapped her bare palm over his against the pale ivory.  Instantly, the scrimshaw awoke with a piercing hum that dissolved every defensive lie between them.  Through their joined hands rushed Senna's fierce dread of abandonment and Julian's buried, aching love for open water and for her. 

Neither recoiled from the raw exposure; instead, Senna locked her fingers furiously between his, and Julian leaned into the ache, surrendering his last pretense of mainland tameness.  Fed by two unguarded pulses beating as one, the carved channels projected a clear silver chime outward past the continental drop.  Moments later, the dark abyss answered.  Shaken by the call of a whale from the deep, a vast subsonic chord rolled upward across the shelf, thrilling through stone and sinew until every giant barnacle clamp opened gently to let the island's root tendons detach.  Freed from the seabed, buoyant Driftwake surged upward, lifting both divers smoothly through sunlit foam as thick sea mist curled around the liberated quays.  Hauling themselves onto the dry, drifting timber while the mainland vanished behind silver fog, Senna coughed brine, called Julian a reckless lunatic, and pulled his dripping forehead tenderly against hers.

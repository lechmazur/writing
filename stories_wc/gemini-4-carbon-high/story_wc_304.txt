High above the cloud sea, a skyborne grotto drifted in perpetual silence under curtains of green and violet aurora.  Through wide arches of pale stone, ribbons of solar light spilled across the chamber floor, bathing the alabaster bier in a radiant hush.  Sena stood beside the still form of Corin, the aged navigator who had once carried her up from the lowland mists and taught her to love the high sky.  As the sole beautician mortician of the sanctuary, she knew that cosmetic grace and funerary rites were two halves of one sacred science.  Upon her silver tray lay sable brushes, jars of luminous mineral balm, and a dark emerald chip cut to refract the exact green wavelength of the upper lights. 

Death in the thin stratosphere did not extinguish the mind all at once.  When breath ceased, the cooling vessel entered a threshold of stillness, a fragile equilibrium between the clamor of life and final decay.  Beyond this quiet plateau waited utter emptiness, where no spirit lingered and no memory endured.  Her urgent duty was to capture one last spark before oblivion claimed everything Corin had ever cherished or seen.  Yet mortal strain had etched deep creases across his brow and locked his jaw, trapping the faint bioelectric echoes beneath rigid muscle and cooling skin. 

Only restorative beauty could bring the body into physical harmony and free what remained within.  Though grief pressed heavy against her ribs, Sena slowed her pulse and remained lucidly watchful.  A single tear or unsteady finger would blur the conductive pigments and scatter the delicate current forever.  She warmed silvered oil between her palms and massaged his temples until the taut lines relaxed into tranquil poise.  With a fine brush, she swept pearlescent dust over his eyelids and tinted his pale lips with warm rose mineral, returning the quiet warmth he had worn in life. 

Next, she wove slender gold filaments through his combed white hair, linking the metallic glaze on his skin to a glass resonator suspended above the bier.  Inside the curved glass, a pale mist of stray neural charge began to shimmer in erratic bursts.  Because magnetic tides from the aurora tangled with his fading thoughts, the globe displayed only chaotic white snow.  Her rare mastery lay in finding patterns in static, discerning the coherent geometry of a soul amid blinding interference.  Leaning close in the glowing quiet, Sena searched the swirling speckles while blending iridescent salve along his cheekbones with a feather tip.  Every subtle brushstroke refined the conductivity of his features, tuning his serene face like a delicate instrument until the storm of light began to part. 

Outside the grotto, the green sky rippled brighter, casting silent cathedral beams across the frost on the stone walls.  Suddenly, Sena held her breath, her dark eyes keen and unblinking.  Beneath the restless snow inside the resonator, a recurring spiral of golden motes pulsed in steady measure.  She knew that cadence immediately, for it matched the gentle rhythm Corin used to hum while steering vessels above the clouds.  It was not a broken fragment of pain, but the pure essence of his wonder beneath open stars, miraculously whole. 

Rime crept over the rim of the alabaster table, warning her that the brief stillness was nearly spent.  Already the outer rings of the golden spiral frayed as deep cold rose from the bier and pulled the echo downward toward nothingness.  Moving with swift precision, Sena placed the emerald chip into the focal cradle beneath the glass globe.  Unaided neural charge was too ephemeral to endure inside cold stone, so she tilted a quartz prism toward the open arch.  Catching a concentrated beam of green auroral fire, she guided the ray downward to infuse the matching green lattice with living radiance and seal the fragile pattern inside.  Sena rested her fingertips lightly against the brow of her old mentor, grounding the circuit with her own steady warmth. 

For one breathless instant, the static flared blindingly bright, threatening to drown the spiral in sheer white glare.  Unwavering, she drew her silvered brush along his jawline to siphon away the excess surge, holding the golden rhythm in crystalline focus.  Sky light and human memory streamed together, sinking deep into the faceted gem.  Above the bier, the glass globe went dark and clear as Corin crossed wholly into peaceful rest.  When Sena lifted the warm jewel from its cradle, a tiny golden star pulsed serenely within its translucent green heart.  Carrying that preserved wonder to the sanctuary wall, she set the stone among hundreds of luminous constellations, while beyond the arches the aurora shimmered on in reverent silence.

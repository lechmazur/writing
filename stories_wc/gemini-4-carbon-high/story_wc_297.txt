Twilight pools across the cobalt glade at dusk, waking the mineral moss into a fierce blue glow beneath glass towers that should not yet exist.  Months after the future arrives early, compressing fifty unlived years into one blinding afternoon, the premature metropolis hums above the basin with fragile and unearned order.  At the center of the clearing sits Sylas, a visionary bound in ancient roots that twine around his legs and anchor his spine to the dreaming earth.  Other seers withered in grief when the temporal surge bypassed them, yet Sylas remains purposefully curious, leaning toward every strange tremor in the soil with a racing pulse.  Tonight his chest tightens with dread and wild wonder alike as silver pollen drifts down from tomorrow, promising either revelation or ruin. 

Branches part at the edge of the glade as Tessa scrambles down the embankment, her breath ragged and her fingers clutched around damp linen.  She unwraps a maze topiary clipping sheared from the geometric hedges that the city wardens grew to quarantine the panicked subconscious of their citizens.  Before the chronal engines fired, keepers of the forest and builders of the valley shared a sacred pact to dream each coming decade into shape together.  That eclipsed covenant now lies buried beneath sudden steel, forgotten by sleepers who wake inside towers they never imagined building.  Yet a future without its middle years has hollow foundations, and Tessa whispers that the high labyrinth is bleeding pale sap as ten thousand quarantined minds begin to fracture. 

Sylas cradles the angular sprig in his palms, his fingers trembling with a dizzying rush of fear and exhilaration as its metallic leaves twitch in time with distant sleepers.  Driven by the inquisitive hunger that kept him sane in solitude, he studies the crystalline resin beading along the stem rather than recoiling from its unnatural chill.  He knows that his sole chance to stabilize the valley is to find the intersection of dreams, the hidden locus where slow memories of the skipped past cross the frantic visions of this rushed tomorrow.  Unbroken bark from the glade would reject timber grown fifty years too soon, burning out his nerves on contact.  Only linking through scars can bridge two asynchronous eras, because wounded tissue already remembers how to survive a rupture in time.  Turning up his forearm, Sylas exposes the pale ridge left behind when the temporal shockwave tore half his taproots away, and he presses the callused shear wound of the clipping hard against his own healed flesh. 

Luminous sap bites into his wrist as the two scars fuse, sending a jolt of ice and pure starlight straight into his tethered spine.  Around his wooden knees, the cobalt moss flares brilliant azure as dusk surrenders to deeper indigo, conducting a sudden tempest of alien nightmares down into the bedrock.  Sylas gasps out a breathless laugh even as terror claws at his throat, suspended in electric euphoria while his heart hammers against his ribs like a trapped bird.  Inside the psychic gale, he perceives thousands of minds pacing within clipped corridors of silver leaf, terrified of the heights they inhabit.  Seeing blue veins blaze up his neck, Tessa grips his shoulder and begs him to overpower the sprig, urging him to force the slow slumber of the old forest onto the panicked city before the voltage kills him.  Yet purposeful curiosity steadies Sylas amid the agony, guiding him to probe beneath the roaring static and discover why the geometric boughs are weeping at all.  Beneath the clatter of premature machinery, he detects a faint downward yearning, realizing that the severed maze is already groping blind through the dark for soil it lost.  He tells Tessa that they must trust what the frightened sleepers are reaching toward rather than crush their visions under ancient weight. 

Relaxing his mental grip, Sylas chooses to heed every erratic throb inside the maze topiary clipping, opening his deepest taproots to follow its desperate rhythm.  Instantly the punishing static resolves into a vast harmonic chord that pulls his awareness past the pruned hedges and into an open twilight plain.  There two separated rivers of human longing collide in radiant gold as he reaches the intersection of dreams, watching unbuilt hopes from the vanished decades rise like scaffolding to brace the hollow towers of tomorrow.  Down in the glade, tender cobalt shoots burst from his scarred wrist, braiding the severed sprig directly into the living wood around his legs.  Shivering with fearful triumph as Tessa weeps beside him, the rooted visionary feels the eclipsed covenant stir awake, growing the missing years upward through the dark before the sky can fall.

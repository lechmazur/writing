No clocks ticked in the pale void, for time did not exist here at all.  Without hours, aging, or any trace of before and after, sequence alone governed the infinite staircase that cut endlessly through the empty sky.  No walls bordered the narrow limestone steps, and no ceiling or floor closed off the horizonless expanse, wrapping Venn in a state of boundless confinement where total exposure doubled as an inescapable cage.  Worse still, the architecture operated as an engine of perpetual motion.  Every downward strike of his boots returned its full kinetic force straight back into muscle and bone without friction or decay, compelling him to descend forever at an unalterable pace. 

Beside his boots tumbled a pair of dice, one chipped, caught in the exact same conservation loop so that they bounced down each riser in lockstep with his stride.  Venn watched their ceaseless leap with the hard gaze of a grim optimist, expecting nothing gentle from this pitiless geometry yet certain that even an eternal prison contained a structural flaw waiting to be broken.  Outside this timeless void, he had once faced a gamble that could have remade his solitary existence, an offer of perilous partnership and uncertain love decided by a single cast of bone cubes.  Terrified of loss, he had slammed his palm over the wager before it could roll, cracking a tiny corner off one cube and retreating into sterile safety until hesitation dropped him here.  Because history did not exist in this place, he harbored no delusion of returning to change his refusal.  Instead, tormented by smooth, unending rebound where no gamble ever settled, Venn worked solely to capture the texture of a missed opportunity, aching to feel real friction, final stillness, and the rough physical grain of the risk he had forfeited. 

The stair endured because its stone was materialized logic, each flat tread enforcing an unbroken equation of symmetrical energy return.  Yet while the intact die rebounded cleanly, its damaged twin struck on a jagged scar that forced the limestone beneath it to subtly distort its recoil angle just to keep the perpetual motion alive.  Seeing his weapon in that microscopic strain, Venn set out to break the stonework through mathematical proofs carved directly into the risers with an iron surveyor stylus.  Every strike of the iron tip rebounded upward without dulling, letting him incise gleaming theorems as he ran downward.  Testing extreme trajectories required him to become balancedly daring, leaning far out over the unrailed drop to snatch and hurl the tumbling cubes at steep angles while holding his center of mass poised on the razor edge of equilibrium.  One reckless millimeter further outward would pitch him into an endless fall through open nothingness, yet timidity near the middle of the tread yielded only redundant numbers. 

Across thousands of steps, Venn measured the clean vectors of the whole cube against the erratic precession of the broken corner, building a deductive chain that left the architecture nowhere to hide.  Because the stair enforced absolute geometric consistency, any physical event accompanied by an inscribed proof of contradiction had to break the local law of conservation.  His lemmas converged on a singular point: if the intact die drove the chipped die directly onto the sharp outer rim of a step right upon its missing vertex, the stone would have to calculate a full symmetrical rebound across an absent plane.  Bracing for the violent recoil he knew would tear his skin, Venn executed one final, balancedly daring lunge over the void, whipping the dice downward toward the outer lip while slashing his concluding theorem across the riser.  Bone collided with bone upon the limestone edge, dividing momentum by nothingness, and the impossible equation forced a thunderous rupture right through the seamless step. 

Stone split open in a spray of chalky grit, shattering the closed loop of energy beneath his feet.  Sudden, glorious friction seized his soles and hurled him hard onto the jagged fissure, bruising his ribs and scraping his palms raw as his body finally ground to a halt.  Above and below that single broken tread, the infinite staircase still stretched through the pale emptiness without exit or clockwork, proving his bleakest expectations true: he had won no doorway home, only a scar in eternity.  Yet wedged motionless inside the cleft lay the two cubes, resting on a final sum of seven that would never bounce again.  Bleeding onto the dusty stone, Venn pressed his thumb into the rough hollow of the chipped corner, smiling faintly as he held at last the sharp, irrevocable weight of everything he might have risked.

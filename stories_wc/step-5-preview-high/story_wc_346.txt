Gear fitted into gear, melody into melody, until the machine worked through nested patterns that no single mind could have designed. The broken puppet head split along an old seam, and the lullaby poured out whole, beautifully flawed and unafraid. The bruise-colored sky did not heal, but it softened, as if the world had finally been allowed to close its eyes.  Maren sat beside the doomsday clock and let it rock her, the last adjuster of an ending that no longer needed to be endured alone.

The mountain station was always loudest during ice melting, when cascades of bright water rushed down the concrete walls of the abandoned control room. [24] Within this concrete relic of the old surveillance regime, Oren worked as a contemplative glassblower, turning a glowing gather of molten silica at the end of his iron pipe. [53] He had tapped his portable furnace into the defunct gas mains among the rusted switchboards, melting down thick spyglass lenses from the dead consoles. [77] His singular purpose here was to craft safe zones for inquiry by blowing curved acoustic domes that trapped whispers inside while keeping eavesdroppers out. [101] For decades, the valley citizens had been forbidden from asking why the mountain dams were locked, leaving their minds as frozen as the winter river. [126] Now the overseers were gone, yet the heavy silence lingered like frost in the shadows of the steel rafters. [145] Oren knew that heavy truths required a gentle touch, so he cultivated an atmosphere of weighty lightness in his workshop, treating solemn reconstruction like a parlor game. [172]

When young Elian crept through the blast doors clutching a bundle of outlawed maps, the boy trembled at the sight of the towering tracking boards. [197] Oren remained thoughtfully spirited, recognizing the deep generational terror in the boy while refusing to let the grim architecture dictate their mood. [219] To dissolve the oppressive tension, Oren relied on his favorite habit of dramatically narrating mundane tasks as though he were a hero in an ancient epic. [245] "Behold, the artisan approaches the treacherous bucket of meltwater, risking a mildly damp sleeve to dip his wooden block," Oren proclaimed in a booming theatrical voice. [271] He bowed toward the sizzling steam, then picked up a broom with operatic intensity. [285] "Now witness the perilous sweeping of the floor, wherein our champion banishes seventeen dust bunnies back into the abyss of the hallway," he announced, flourishing the bristles. [312] Elian let out a startled laugh, a tiny sound that echoed against the shimmering glass panels already bolted to the defunct radar screens. [335] "And for my final feat of supreme courage," Oren whispered, squinting at the kettle on the annealing oven, "I shall pour lukewarm chamomile tea without spilling a single drop upon my boots." [367]

Emboldened by the absurdity, Elian stepped closer to the central command desk where Oren had once worked as a junior clerk during the freeze. [391] Resting upon the metal desk was the master censorship ledger, held open to the list of treasonous topics by a cracked leather bookmark. [414] Oren had kept that book open for three years as a private penance, believing he needed the ugly reminder of his past complicity to fuel his current labor. [442] Elian stared at the faded ink and asked why the station operators had hated questions so much, his voice dropping as his fingers brushed the dry hide of the marker. [472] Outside, a massive shelf of glacier ice broke loose from the ridge and crashed into the gorge, vibrating through the soles of their shoes. [496] Listening to the roar of the liberated river, Oren felt the unmistakable ache of the thawing heart within his own chest, realizing that guilt was just another form of frost. [526] As long as he preserved the shrine of his old shame at the center of the room, no inquirer could ever feel entirely safe beneath his glass canopies. [554] True sanctuary demanded that he finally abandon the punitive ledger, the rigid protocols of the winter, and the cold penance that kept him tethered to the console. [581]

Oren reached out, plucked the cracked leather bookmark from the spine, and tossed it into the hungry orange mouth of the glory hole where it curled into bright ash. [610] With a theatrical grunt, he slammed the heavy book shut and shoved it off the desk into a recycling crate meant for the foundation rubble. [635] "And thus, the keeper of the kiln banishes the pompous tome of yesterday to clear space for a moderately comfortable stool," Oren declared, gesturing toward his newest translucent alcove. [664] Sunlight streamed through the dripping icicles outside, striking the curved glass dome and scattering warm prism rainbows across the dead dials and empty switchboards. [688] Elian smiled without hesitation, unrolling his forbidden maps across the cleared metal surface while the last of the winter ice surrendered to the spring sun. [713] Sitting together inside the quiet shelter of spun glass, the former censor and the curious boy began to trade questions that required no permission to ask. [739]

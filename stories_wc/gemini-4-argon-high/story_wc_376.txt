[31] Silas works in the quietest alcove of the conservatory as the last gilded-leaf calligrapher. [45] Because paper requires too much precious water to pulp, citizens bring him fallen magnolia foliage, which he brushes with liquid gold and inks with their private messages. [72] Yet the craft has become agonizing for him. [80] Bound to the biosphere by the chlorophyll threading his veins, Silas physically wilts near dishonesty. [95] Whenever a merchant dictates a fraudulent water contract or a councilor commissions a false promise, Silas feels his spine curl, his breath shorten, and the symbiotic ferns along his collarbones shrivel into brown husks. [129]

[154] Before the central springs vanished into baked clay, people dropped sealed leaf letters into the pneumatic aqueducts, which also carried the whispered acoustics of the public gardens through brass speaking tubes. [185] When the water stopped, thousands of undelivered confessions and spoken replies simply disappeared into the subterranean labyrinth. [202] The civic magistrates claim the old tubes were melted down for scrap, a blatant falsehood that makes Silas double over with sudden, cramping nausea. [226] He knows the messages still exist because he holds a zinc-etched postal plate fragment salvaged from a dry drain. [245] The corroded metal bears half a routing cipher and smells faintly of river silt and trapped truth. [262]

[282] The room has been ransacked by scavengers hunting for valuable metals, its cedar planks pried loose. [298] Silas kneels in the dust and discovers his answer by piecing together a shredded treasure map found under floorboards. [317] Looters had drawn the chart to locate the mythical dead letter reserve, believing the accumulated gold on the lost mail would make them rich, before tearing the parchment to hide their trail. [349] Working by lantern light, Silas aligns the torn strips of paper. [360] The ink is faded, missing the final junction near the deep root cellars. [426] He crawls through dry irrigation pipes crusted with salt until the passage opens into a cavernous brick rotunda. [444] He raises his light and gasps. [450] Drifts of preserved foliage blanket the floor, glowing softly with his own brushwork. [463] Above him, the curved acoustic vents still hum with a low, resonant vibration, trapping the lingering echoes of voices sent down the speaking tubes months ago. [489] No looters ever reached this sanctuary. [495] As Silas steps into the center of the chamber, a profound relief washes over him. [510] The ferns along his neck unfurl, drinking in an atmosphere entirely devoid of deceit. [524]

[679] He realizes that people did not stop telling the truth, for they simply hid their honesty in tiny, fragile admissions too quiet for the desperate city above. [706] He pulls a blank, dried leaf from his satchel and uncaps his vial of gold ink. [722] His posture is straight, his lungs are full, and the greenery at his throat shines vibrant emerald.

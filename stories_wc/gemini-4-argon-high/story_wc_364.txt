Above the sapphire blossom trees of this orchard planet riddled with floating rock arches, the sky was falling piece by piece. [21] Oren, a meek blacksmith who flinched at the sound of his own bellows, knelt among the crushed silver pear saplings to gather the dead stone. [46] His sole purpose was to coax resilience from rubble, mending the shattered spans before the entire canopy collapsed into ruin. [66] Though his shoulders drooped and his voice barely rose above a whisper, a pulse of azure defiance beat steadily within his chest, mirroring the deep blue luminescence of the planet's healthy sky bridges. [99]

Gravity stones did not mend through heat and hammer alone. [109] They required bartered awakenings, an ancient transaction where a mortal traded away fragments of their own vigor to reignite the dormant consciousness within fallen ore. [134] Five years ago, Oren had possessed a booming laugh and a temper as hot as his hearth. [151] Now, after dozens of trades with the veiled tallow merchants, he was left timid and soft, yet he remained quietly determined to keep the fruit groves safe from the crushing weight of dying stone. [185] In his calloused palm, he held the medium of these costly trades: a length of magician's silk that shimmered like oil on water. [208] The enchanted fabric was woven to trap intangible human qualities and transfer that stolen vitality directly into cold mineral. [227]

Today, the largest arch over the central valley was groaning, shedding boulders that smashed through the fragrant boughs. [245] To wake enough keystone rubble to anchor the span, Oren had just surrendered his pride and half of his childhood memories into the cloth. [269] The loss left his mind drifting in a strange twilight between understanding and confusion. [283] He could not quite recall his mother's face or why the villagers never thanked him, yet he knew the precise weight of his hammer and the exact heat required by the metal. [315] His every movement remained deliberate, guided by muscle memory that outlasted clear thought. [328] Carrying the heavy chunks of broken arch and the glowing cloth, he descended beneath the roots, using abandoned mine shafts to reach the thermal vents that once fed the ancient excavators. [359]

These vertical tunnels acted as giant chimneys, drawing deep subterranean gales straight up toward the surface orchards. [376] Oren set his portable anvil at the base of the widest shaft, where the updraft howled like a caged beast. [396] He piled the shattered, lifeless chunks of sky stone into his brazier, pumping the pedals with a slow, apologetic rhythm. [416] Even in this solitary depth, his posture was shrunken, a man who had given away his right to take up space so that the heavens might stay aloft. [444] When the rubble reached a dull cherry glow, he gripped his tongs and pulled the largest fractured piece onto the iron block. [466]

He draped the magician's silk over the scorching rock. [475] Instead of burning, the fabric melted into liquid sapphire light, releasing the bartered awakenings he had paid for with his own boldness. [497] Oren raised his hammer and brought it down with deliberate precision, folding the essence of his lost courage into the jagged fractures of the stone. [522] Sparks of brilliant cobalt erupted with each blow, stinging his bare arms as he worked in that hazy space between understanding and confusion. [545] He did not know what tomorrow would bring, or even if he would remember how to speak by dawn, yet he stayed quietly determined to finish the weld. [573] Strike after strike, he forced the dead fragments to drink the silk and remember their ancient duty to float. [592]

[602] It tore itself from the anvil, blazing with a fierce skyward rebellion that radiated pure azure defiance against the tyranny of the ground. [625] By using abandoned mine shafts as launch tubes, Oren did not need to carry the awakened rock back to the surface. [646] The glowing stone shot straight up the vertical tunnel, riding the thermal draft as it trailed ribbons of blue fire. [666] As it burst through the orchard floor above, its resonance rippled outward, touching the scattered debris among the trees and the cracking arch overhead. [690] Oren climbed slowly out of the dark earth, blinking at the bright afternoon. [703] All around him, he watched his craft coax resilience from rubble as broken boulders lifted from the bruised grass and locked back into the hovering stone bridge.

In the ruined orchard district lost in centuries of thick fog, the petrified apple trees stood encased in towering sheets of ice like glass mountains. [25] Every branch bore a razor edge of frozen vapor, casting keen, frigid reflections across a landscape that could slice the unwary to the bone. [49] Moving through this brittle majesty, Elian worked with the quiet certainty of a mirror maker who understood the exact weight of sand, lead, and silver. [74] While the ancient mist had dissolved the minds of other villagers into drifting delusions, he remained firmly grounded in physical craft and iron discipline. [98] Heavy leaden boots anchored his posture to the sunken cobblestones, and his calloused hands trusted only what could be measured, polished, and bolted into place. [123]

Yet tonight, his practical trade served an impossible threshold. [132] Clamped to the stone cider press at the center of the grove rested an astronomer's broken sextant, its brass arc tarnished and its original horizon glass reduced to jagged teeth. [162] The instrument belonged to his sister, Maya, who had spent decades charting the memory of stars that no living eye had seen above the perpetual shroud. [188] Three nights ago, exhausted by fever and obsession, she had fallen into a hollow sleep, and her final vision had torn loose into the dense atmosphere. [214] In this forsaken valley, thoughts left unguarded in sleep took on crystalline mass, wandering the dead boughs as luminous phantoms while their dreamers withered away. [239] Elian had come into the freezing orchard with a singular purpose: to capture a runaway dream before its absence hollowed Maya into a permanent husk. [264]

A net of rope could never snare a wandering piece of the mind, for it existed as pure refraction drifting between solid matter and empty air. [290] Instead, Elian used his rigorous geometry to recreate the shattered optics of that ancient brass device across the entire expanse of the dead grove. [314] All afternoon, he had hoisted tall, translucent panes among the gnarled trunks, coating only half of each plate in tin amalgam so they functioned as immense split mirrors. [342] A functional sextant operated by pulling the reflection of a distant star down until it rested directly upon the visible line of the earth. [366] His towering array was engineered to perform that exact convergence on the spectral plane, erasing boundaries between the silvered reflection and the living mist, between the waking craftsman and the untethered vision. [398] If he could collapse the distance separating the phantom sky from the earthly glass, the rogue constellation would lock inside the brass frame on the press. [424]

[453] As dusk bled a pale violet hue through the overcast gloom, that precise instant arrived. [468] A faint, musical crackle echoed down the avenues of skeletal trees as rime raced across the bark and transformed the encroaching vapor into jagged, glittering peaks. [494] Out from the deep white canyons glided the runaway dream. [504] It was a breathtaking geometry of wheeling galaxies and diamond light, expanding and contracting in the freezing air with the slow, rhythmic cadence of a sleeping chest. [531]

Elian gripped the index arm of the sextant, peering through the eyepiece toward the central monolithic pane, but his own warm exhalations immediately clouded the cold lens. [558] Worse still, every time the celestial apparition pulsed outward, it cast a corresponding veil of diamond rime across the tall mirrors, throwing the alignment off by crucial degrees. [586] He realized then that a mirror cannot remain separate from what it reflects; the maker had to become part of the instrument. [608] Planting his heavy boots deeper into the frozen soil to keep his body steady, Elian opened his lungs to the biting chill and matched the phantom pulse through synchronized breathing. [679] One shared respiration after another dissolved the division between warm blood and cold starlight, turning his own condensed breath into the final lens of the sequence. [705] In the eyepiece, the reflected orchard and the real orchard slid together until no seam remained between earth and sky. [725] With a sharp click of the brass vernier, the towering panes flashed once like lightning striking alpine summits, funneling the entire blazing firmament down into the core of the repaired instrument. [756] Elian unclamped the warm sextant, where a captive nebula now spun safely behind the glass, and turned his steady steps toward home. [778]

Only in the breathless hour after the phoenix rises could the guild conduct its bartered awakenings. [16] Elian, a colorblind painter famous for his stark charcoal landscapes, stepped timidly into the glassblower's courtyard as the last golden embers drifted from the sky. [41] To Elian, those falling sparks were merely bright flecks of pearl against a slate canopy, yet he felt no sense of loss. [63] His sole motivation in life and art was to find beauty in imperfection, a philosophy that made him cherish his monochrome sight. [85] Without the distraction of gaudy hues, he perceived the honest cracks in stone, the rough grain of weathered oak, and the delicate shadows that other artists ignored. [112]

Across the flagstones stood Master Vespera, tending a brick furnace where phoenix sparks were sealed inside molten glass globes. [131] When inhaled, the vapor inside a finished globe would awaken any dormant or broken human faculty, from faded memory to failing sight. [153] However, the volatile spellwork required absolute acoustic stillness, as a single spoken syllable could shatter the cooling crystal. [171] Consequently, all bargaining in the yard was conducted by communicating through plant arrangements on a long marble table between them. [191]

Vespera began the silent dialogue by sliding a tall obsidian vase forward and inserting three pristine white lilies alongside a sprig of sunfire ivy. [215] The botanical message was unmistakable: she offered to cure his defective eyes with a flawless prism globe in exchange for his finest winter canvas. [239] Vespera despised flaws above all else. [245] Beside her furnace lay a heap of discarded vessels that she had smashed simply because they contained a tiny air bubble or an asymmetrical ridge. [270] She viewed Elian himself as a tragic mistake waiting to be corrected by her immaculate craft. [286]

Elian bowed his head with a wide, sheepish smile, appearing entirely docile and eager to please. [302] Beneath that mild demeanor, however, he was innocently cunning, using his reputation as a simple dreamer to mask a daring scheme. [323] He had no desire to surrender his gray vision, nor did he wish to let Vespera continue destroying her most expressive glasswork. [345] Instead of curing his eyes, he intended to awaken the rigid glassblower to the grace of crooked things. [363]

Approaching the marble table, Elian selected a gnarled root of winter fern, a frostbitten thistle, and a stem of spotted moss. [384] He arranged the bruised foliage around her pristine lilies so clumsily that he knocked a clay pot toward the edge of the slab. [407] While Vespera lunged forward to catch the wobbling pot before it could crash and break the silence, Elian's other hand dipped into his coat pocket. [432] His fingers closed around a tarnished concertina reed tongue, a tiny strip of notched brass salvaged from a broken street instrument. [453] In one fluid motion, while feigning an apologetic stumble against her workbench, he wedged the tiny metal slip deep inside the mouthpiece of her hollow iron blowpipe. [480]

Vespera glared at his awkwardness, then glanced at his arrangement of damaged weeds. [493] In the lexicon of courtyard flora, pairing scarred thistle with moss meant a plea for humble transformation, which she took as his meek consent to the trade. [520] Satisfied, she lifted her blowpipe, gathered a glowing mass of phoenix glass from the crucible, and pressed her lips to the iron tube. [543]

As she exhaled into the pipe, her breath rushed across the hidden concertina reed tongue. [558] A low, raspy, slightly flat musical note hummed inside the metal shaft. [570] Vespera froze in shock, afraid to stop blowing lest the sudden vacuum collapse the molten core. [586] Because the vibration traveled directly through her breath into the liquid crystal rather than striking it from outside, the glass did not shatter. [609] Instead, the tremulous, imperfect pitch sculpted the expanding bubble from within, spinning spiral ridges, tiny seed bubbles, and asymmetric ripples throughout the vessel walls. [633]

When she finally sheared the warped globe from the pipe, a wisp of silver vapor escaped the unsealed neck and washed over Vespera's face. [657] Tuned by the notched brass reed and filtered through rippled glass, the magic did not grant flawless sight, but awakened a dormant sensitivity to texture and resonance. [684] Vespera gasped aloud, staring in wonder as the morning light fractured through the crooked ridges to cast a labyrinth of living shadow across the stones. [709] For the first time, she saw that her sterile spheres were lifeless compared to this wild, singing creation. [727] Stepping back to the table, the humbled master pulled the white lilies from the vase, leaving only Elian's gnarled fern and spotted moss standing in triumph. [753] Elian unpacked his charcoal sticks with a quiet grin, ready to paint the warped glass masterpiece in all its glorious, fractured gray. [775]

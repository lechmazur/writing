[28] At the center of the forsaken square stood an ancient archway, its dry masonry groaning softly as the hollow earth beneath the settlement threatened to give way. [102] In his weathered fingers he held an ebony tuning fork, carved from dense black heartwood that absorbed the tremors of the ground rather than ringing in the air. [130]

[157] Instead, he pressed the base of the wooden instrument against the archway and spoke in his usual riddles. [175] "A bell without a clapper still remembers the song, provided the belfry knows how to stand," he murmured into the breathless stillness. [197] Mara, the mason who had led the survivors home, stepped forward and looked up at the great bronze bell hanging mute within the shifting arch. [222] She knew they had only hours to secure the central keystone before the collapse of the underlying cavern dragged every wall and timber into the abyss. [248] "We brought ropes and iron wedges to rebuild the buttresses, Soren, so tell us plainly where the fault lies," Mara said. [269]

Soren tapped the ebony tuning fork against his knuckle, producing a low, velvet thrum that seemed to calm the trembling dust around their boots. [293] "Iron fights the stone, and the stone is already angry because you left it in discord," the old man replied softly. [314] He explained through oblique metaphors of tension and pitch that the founders of Oakhaven had laid the streets as a vast acoustic web over the limestone vault. [341] For generations, the daily bustle of the village had kept the loose subterranean gravel packed tight through constant, gentle vibration. [361] When bitter quarrels divided the neighbors and drove them away, the sudden silence allowed the foundation to loosen and slip toward ruin. [501] "Listen to the tuner, lift the fallen granite blocks together, and match your stride to his mark," she commanded in a low voice. [621] "Breathe as one body, or fall as thirty strangers," the cryptic bell tuner whispered, striking the black wood once more to guide their heels. [645] Gradually, the returning exiles let go of their old grievances, watching each other closely to align their weight and pace.

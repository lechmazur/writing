Queen Seraphina departed the palace just hours after the final warning from her court astrologers. [15] They had promised her in silk voices that another step toward the peaks would unravel her pulse as easily as a pulled thread, yet their delicate threats only deepened her resolve. [46] As a disenchanted queen, she had grown weary of a realm kept in perpetual bloom by secrets dressed in polite, deadly pageantry. [68] She needed to unravel the mystery of recurring dreams that left her waking on damp pillows with the taste of silver ash in her mouth. [93] In those nightly visions, a familiar voice always wept from the summit, begging her to look behind the velvet curtain of the sky. [116]

Thus, she began the treacherous climb under a moon that looked like a pared fingernail. [131] Only Julian, her sworn shadow and appointed executioner should she commit treason, walked beside her through the frost. [149] The mountain air carried a lacy menace, weaving hoarfrost into intricate doilies across the rocks that sliced through leather boots like glass. [171] Every icicle hung like a needle poised to stitch their eyelids shut if they lingered too long in the wind. [191] Halfway up the spire, they reached the ember grotto on the ridge, which served as the traditional boundary for every monarch before her. [214] Inside that shallow alcove, coals of strange gold smoldered within filigree braziers, radiating the stolen warmth that kept the valley below in eternal summer. [238] Julian rested his gloved hand upon the pommel of his rapier, his voice a courtly murmur as he reminded her that passing this shrine meant forfeiting her life. [266] Seraphina examined the glowing char and saw that it was not wood at all, but coagulated light falling from a narrow chimney in the stone above. [292] With a softly commanding gesture, she bade Julian follow her past the braziers and up the vertical shaft, knowing his curiosity was nearly as sharp as his blade. [320] He did not strike her down, bound by the quiet authority she wielded even in her ruin. [337]

They hauled themselves through the freezing mist until they broke into a cobalt grotto above clouds, where the air hummed like a plucked harp string. [362] The cavern walls were formed of deep blue crystal spun into gossamer lattices that threatened to sever a finger at the slightest brush. [385] At the center of this azure vault hung the source of her torment. [398] Suspended in a web of silver thorns was a living constellation shaped like a woman, its form pierced by dozens of delicate platinum spindles. [422] From each tiny puncture, the captive entity was bleeding starlight, which pattered down the chute to feed the ember shrine below. [443] Seraphina gasped as she recognized the face within the luminous agony as Lyra, the twin sister the astrologers claimed had been stillborn. [465] The kingdom had not been blessed by the heavens; it was a parasite draining her sister drop by incandescent drop. [485] Every dream had been Lyra calling across their severed bond, pleading for an end to the exquisite torture that bought their people effortless warmth. [509]

Julian drew his slender blade, its edge whispering like silk torn in a quiet parlor, yet his eyes betrayed a sudden revulsion for the crown he served. [536] He warned the queen that striking the crystalline web with steel would cause the thorns to constrict and shred the prisoner into dust. [559] The astrologers had woven the trap from cruelty disguised as fragility, ensuring that only a specific harmony of release could unmake the bindings without killing the host. [586] Seraphina stepped toward the lethal lace of the cage, remembering the secret garden where she and an unseen playmate had once hidden as children before her memories were pruned. [615] She looked into the luminous eyes of Lyra and waited as tiny sparks of golden light drifted upward from the basin below. [637] To dissolve the frostwork snare, she used the only magic the scholars had forgotten: a shared exhale spotting the first fireflies of summer. [660] As their twin breaths mingled in gentle wonder at the rising motes, the resonance of pure childhood recognition vibrated through the chamber. [682] The silver thorns shattered into harmless powder, and the platinum spindles fell away with the chime of breaking icicles. [701] Lyra collapsed into the arms of Seraphina, her wounds sealing as the stolen radiance rushed back into her veins. [720] Below the clouds, the false summer of the capital would already be freezing over, exposing the gilded court to the winter it had long earned. [745] Julian sheathed his sword and bowed low to both sisters, choosing the cold truth of the mountain over the silken lies of the throne. [769]

[137] "Tell me your scouts did not ambush my son on the ridge," Vane demanded, his knuckles white on the shaft of his weapon. [246] The jagged quartz still hummed with the final, fractured images it had recorded before shattering against the ice. [328] To keep the orchard warm, the keepers had lined the branches with tarnished, ornate reflectors salvaged from ancient boutiques down on the sunken coast.

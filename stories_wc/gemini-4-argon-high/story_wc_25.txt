Above the port of Veridian, a geomagnetic storm painted the midnight sky in violent ribbons of violet and green, frying every radio transceiver on the docks. [26] Beneath that silent aurora stood a towering labyrinth of shipping containers, stacked nine high in rusting canyons of corrugated steel. [46] Silas, the dockyard's sole labyrinth maintenance worker, leaned against a rusted girder and watched Forewoman Vane struggle with a diesel megaphone. [67] With the automated cranes dead and the compasses spinning wildly, three night watchmen were lost deep inside the shifting maze of cargo boxes. [90] Vane intended to blast her voice through the metallic corridors, a brute force approach that offended every instinct Silas possessed. [110] For ten years, he had greased the turnbuckles, cleared the drainage channels, and studied the bizarre acoustics of the stacks. [130] He stepped forward to issue a formal challenge, driven by a quiet lifelong obsession to prove that whispers travel farther than shouts. [152]

"If you fire that horn, the steel walls will shatter your words into fifty overlapping echoes and drive those men deeper into the dead ends," Silas said calmly. [180] Vane glared at him under the flickering emerald sky and asked how he planned to reach them without amplification. [199] Silas smiled, operating with the calculated spontaneity of a jazz musician who knew every scale before improvising a solo. [218] He told her to pick any of the twelve primary sectors at random, and he would extract the guard stationed there using nothing louder than a breath. [268] The twelve astrological engravings around its rim corresponded neatly to the twelve numbered zones of the container yard. [286] She spun the token on an iron bollard, and when it clattered to a halt, her fingernail rested directly beside the engraved scales of Libra, indicating Sector Seven. [314]

[331] Because the cargo manifests changed hourly, flat blueprints were useless in this shifting city of boxes. [347] Silas had designed his own charts using complex origami pleats, allowing him to crease and tuck the paper so that separated pathways aligned to reflect the daily stack configurations. [376] His fingers flew across the parchment with practiced, sudden flair, collapsing three quadrants inward until Sector Seven met the northern perimeter wall on the page. [401] The newly formed crease revealed a continuous line of empty refrigeration ducts running straight through the heart of the blockade. [421]

[441] Silas pressed his ear against the cold steel of the nearest container and closed his eyes. [484] He filtered out Vane's impatient, heavy stomping beside him and listened deeper into the metallic web. [728] Vane opened her mouth to mock the theatrical display, but Silas raised a calloused finger for silence and tapped the container wall. [750] Deep within the belly of the maze, the hesitant, dragging footsteps abruptly halted. [763] Three seconds later, the uneven cadence resumed, moving briskly toward the northern exit.

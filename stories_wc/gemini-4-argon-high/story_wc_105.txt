[25] Above the cracked dome of his sunken orchard greenhouse, the polar sky rippled with curtains of green and violet light. [68] Around the submerged glass walls, the dark water of the shallow fjord held a jagged circle of history. [154] He knew that what people called deafness was often just the mind raising a shield against an unbearable echo from the past. [176] His own left ear had gone mostly dead the night his partner, Elian, stole their research vessel and ran it onto the outer reef, joining the hulls outside. [204] Since that night, Corin worked with a single, obsessive motivation: to find the sound of trust breaking. Yet one could not capture such a betrayal with a simple brass microphone. [261] Corin had to reconstruct the missing frequency by collecting absences from the wreckage around him. [276] All afternoon, he had waded through the freezing tide in his canvas suit, pressing hollow glass resonators against the empty wheelhouses of ships and the barren boughs of drowned trees. [306] He gathered the specific silences where captains had abandoned their helms, where iron bolts had sheared from oak, and where spring blossoms had failed to open. [332] Each sealed vacuum vial held the acoustic shape of a promise that had vanished. [346]

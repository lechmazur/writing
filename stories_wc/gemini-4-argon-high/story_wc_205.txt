Ever since the Great Rupture, when time became visible as a drifting haze of amber dust, the baroque asteroid of Vespera had existed in a state of burnished sorrow. [29] Gilded cherubs and marble colonnades arched across the low gravity caverns, their ornate surfaces perpetually coated in the glowing sediment of decayed hours. [52] Cassian walked through this gilded mausoleum as a defunct revolutionary, a man whose youthful barricades and manifestos had been rendered obsolete the moment eternity fractured into sight. [79] Once he had planted explosives beneath these carved balustrades to free the miners, yet now he spent his quiet twilight cataloging the very ruins he had failed to destroy. [108] He had always been poetically inclined, finding lyricism in the curve of a broken arch or the tragic cadence of a forgotten rebellion. [131]

That disposition now fueled a bitter archaeological dispute between Cassian and Chief Archivist Kaelen over the true nature of the First Founders. [153] Kaelen insisted that the original inhabitants of the rock were soulless tyrants who hollowed Vespera merely to hoard mineral wealth in opulent isolation. [176] If Kaelen won the debate, the Council would pulverize the lower basilicas for raw ore, erasing the past as worthless vanity. [197] Cassian, however, harbored a desperate need to reimagine an entire civilization, not to excuse its ancient hierarchies, but to salvage the shared humanity embedded in its stone. [224] If the Founders were capable of profound grief and foresight, then his own bled out comrades had inherited a noble dream rather than a senseless slaughterhouse. [250] His entire argument rested upon a single crystal vase shard excavated from the deepest choir of the asteroid. [268] The jagged piece of leaded glass was etched with microscopic grooves that Kaelen dismissed as decorative scratches from a decadent banquet. [289] Cassian believed those grooves were a verse of warning written in light, waiting for the era after time becomes visible to finally be read. [313]

Standing in the Hall of Sighs, where towering silvered glass lined the curving walls, Cassian watched Kaelen prepare the final demolition decree. [335] Outside the viewport domes, the amber mist of raw duration began to churn into a violent tempest, whipping past the exterior buttresses like molten brass. [360] The warning bells tolled across the sector, initiating the ancient survival protocol of covering mirrors during storms. [377] When temporal gales struck Vespera, uncovered reflections caught the raging currents of visible hours and multiplied them infinitely, trapping onlookers in maddening visions of their own lost yesterdays. [405] Kaelen hurried to pull the heavy velvet drapes over the tall frames, his hands trembling as the golden gale seeped through the porous vaulted ceiling. [430] Watching the shroud fall over each polished surface, Cassian realized why his previous attempts to illuminate the artifact had failed. [450] In calm weather, the ambient mirrors of the Hall bled away the pressure of the temporal light, leaving the room too dim for the dense crystal to refract. [478] He needed to devise a focal chamber using the very shroud meant to blind the gallery. [494]

Instead of retreating to the shelter vaults, Cassian raced along the colonnade, fastening the leaden hems of the velvet cloths tightly against the gilded moldings. [519] By sealing every reflective surface at the height of the squall, he prevented the leaking chronal currents from dispersing into phantom echoes. [541] The trapped luminescence thickened in the center of the room like liquid topaz, swirling in search of an outlet. [560] Kaelen shouted for him to get down, fearing the concentrated weight of raw decades would crush their lungs. [578] Cassian ignored the plea and mounted the central dais, raising the crystal vase shard into the eye of the golden vortex. [599] Deprived of the mirrors, the entire fury of the visible storm funneled through the jagged glass. [615]

The shard sang with a low harmonic tremor and cast a towering projection across the dark velvet drapes. [633] There, woven from the refracted substance of time itself, appeared the Founders as they truly were. [649] They were not banqueting autocrats, but weeping artisans who had foreseen the Great Rupture and carved the entire asteroid as a resonant shelter to preserve memory against the coming void. [679] Lines of luminous stanzas scrolled across the draped mirrors, revealing that every gilded cherub and vaulted arch had been sculpted as a stanza of grief for a future they could not save. [711] Kaelen let the demolition order slip from his fingers, watching it dissolve into the amber drift on the marble floor. [731] As the storm subsided and the glowing dust settled over their shoulders, Cassian lowered his aching arm. [748] The old rebel felt no triumph in winning the argument, only a quiet kinship with the ghosts who had built this monument to endurance. [772]

[22] Soren knelt in the silver moss, his lungs aching from the choking beauty of a realm where the sky bled directly into brilliant blossoms. [46] For forty years he had lived as a renowned onyx-ink cartomancer, painting precise prophecies onto stiff vellum while his actual life slipped away unnoticed.

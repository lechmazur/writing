[149] Because he is mnemonically gifted, a single fleeting glance at a stranger's face is enough for him to render their exact bone structure hours later. [222] He thought he could bury his true medium, but the urge to paint in full spectrum has proven to be the fiercest of inescapable callings. [247] Every night, the phantom hues behind his eyelids throb with a pressure that threatens to fracture his sanity. [265] He has come tonight to uncork suppressed creativity that has fermented into agony within his chest. [281]

[296] Mounted directly into the jagged amethyst wall is a heavy wooden frame housing a painting with something behind it.

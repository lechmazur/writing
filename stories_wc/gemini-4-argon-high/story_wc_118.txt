[20] This was the era after the last human dream is dreamt, when the sleepers had finally vanished and left the waking stone behind. [90] He did not gather books for their own sake, as mere paper only recorded what people wished to claim about themselves. [155] Without the rhythm of night and day, human circadian tides had broken down, stealing their capacity for REM sleep long before the species quietly faded out. [346] The fire had consumed the rest of the paper, leaving the boy's vision unrecorded in ink.

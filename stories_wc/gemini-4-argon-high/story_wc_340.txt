The brass lift shudders to life at the docking level, beginning its slow vertical journey through the core of the icebound spire above the clouds. [25] Standing perfectly straight at the center of the rising cage, Barrow adjusts the silver tray resting upon his gloved palms. [45] He is, in both humble station and literal trajectory, an ascending servant, rising from the steaming kitchens of the lower rock into the frozen pinnacle where his master keeps solitary vigil. [76] As the elevator climbs ten floors, the temperature inside the open latticework shaft drops by twenty degrees, turning a valet's breath into transient ghosts of pale mist. [103] Barrow does not mind the chill, nor does he absorb the heavy melancholy that clings to the fortress like the rime on its outer stones. [128] Thirty years of domestic service have cultivated in him a state of affectionate detachment, allowing him to tend to Lord Cassian's jagged grief without ever being cut by it himself. [158] He cares deeply for the old aeronaut, yet he remains an observer on the shore of another man's tempest, keeping the tea hot and the silver gleaming regardless of the weather within. [190]

As the bronze indicator arrow clicks past the second floor, Barrow's eyes drift to the two items resting beside the porcelain teapot. [212] The first is a pair of airship captain's goggles, recently retrieved from the workroom where Barrow spent the morning buffing soot from their brass frames and faceted amber lenses. [241] The second is a narrow packet of vellum that slipped from the lining of Cassian's flight coat during the brushing, representing an unexpected encrypted diary discovery that has occupied Barrow's thoughts for the past hour. [276] By nature, the valet is warmly inquisitive, possessing a gentle curiosity that never seeks leverage or scandal, only a fuller comprehension of the human beings he serves. [303] Since the wreck of the sky vessel Halcyon five winters ago, Cassian has spoken only of the terrible silence of the glaciers and the crewmen he could not save. [332] Yet Barrow's quiet mission in this frozen house has always been to seek out the joy beneath anguish, convinced that no man builds a life among the stars without keeping some bright ember hidden from the frost. [369]

Passing the fourth floor, the lift emerges from the solid stone foundation into the glassy midsection of the tower, where arched lancet windows encased in six feet of blue glacier ice face the open sky. [404] With five floors remaining before his arrival at the observatory suite, Barrow sets the tray upon the narrow folding ledge of the cage wall. [428] His deft fingers reach out to unfold the stiff, pleated vellum packet, which opens like a paper concertina across the silver salver. [450] Down in the lamplight of the butler's pantry, the pages had appeared to be an impenetrable cipher of overlapping crimson and indigo lines, scrawled over heavy black entries detailing the Halcyon's final, agonizing descent. [484] As the car cables groan past the sixth level, however, a brilliant shaft of high altitude sun strikes the spire's frozen shell. [506] Dazzling beams fracture through the external hoarfrost and wash over the tray, causing the amber lenses of the goggles to cast a peculiar, geometric shadow directly onto the open vellum. [536]

Comprehending the mechanism in an instant, Barrow lifts the goggles to his eyes and peers at the document through scattered light refracting off the prism of the glacier wall. [565] The polarized crystal filters out the dark ink of the tragedy and the chaotic indigo grid, leaving only the crimson strokes glowing like threads of fire as the seventh and eighth floors slip beneath his boots. [601] Where the naked eye saw only a record of ruin, the optical cipher reveals Cassian's secret annotations written during those same final hours. [624] The captain had recorded the transcendent laughter of his helmsman as they surfed the upper aurora, the sublime warmth of shared brandy in the freezing wheelhouse, and an undying gratitude for forty years of boundless sky. [660] Cassian had hidden his happiness inside a fortress of sorrow, protecting his most precious memories behind lenses tuned exclusively to the glare of the high clouds. [686]

A faint, satisfied smile touches Barrow's lips as the ninth floor falls away. [699] He carefully presses the vellum pleats back together and rests the goggles directly atop the packet, angling the brass frames toward the eastern window so the master will not miss the alignment. [731] He does not need to speak of what he has seen, nor interfere with the old man's proud solitude; it is enough to know the warmth is still there, preserved beneath the ice. [764] The tenth floor bell chimes softly, the iron gates slide open, and Barrow lifts his tray to step composedly into the light. [786]

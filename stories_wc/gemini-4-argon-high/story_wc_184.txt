[15] Tonight, his desk was an oiled workbench inside the university clock tower mechanism room, surrounded by the rhythmic, brassy breathing of the great pendulum. [264] He withdrew a diplomat's wax seal impression, preserved in hardened vermilion resin and resting inside a cedar box. [282] Unlike standard seals that celebrated completed treaties, this artifact had been severed from its parchment in 1714 so that the clandestine negotiations could be hidden from invading armies. [310] Under his magnifying lens, the concave relief did not depict a family crest, but rather a meticulous diagram of interlocking knots and numerical ratios. [334] The university founders had not destroyed the peace archive; instead, they had folded the room into the mechanical cadence of the tower's timekeeping apparatus.

[34] The yellow fabric served as a vital filter against the golden motes floating in the stale air. [51] Those glowing particles were not mere detritus, but actual atoms of nostalgia left behind by a dead empire. [69] Instead of carving ink into stone, the ancients had distilled their fondest memories into physical matter. [85] Inhaling even a single unrefined grain would trap a scholar in a fatal daydream of someone else's idealized past. [104] Yet Kael had descended into the deepest restricted sector, driven to pursue forbidden knowledge that his superiors had outlawed for centuries. [125] The ruling synod claimed the ancients vanished in a sudden cataclysm during their golden age of peace. [142] Kael suspected a darker truth lay hidden across the end of innocence, that undocumented era when the empire realized its utopia was built on a lie. [168]

[239] As the amber beams refracted through the angled metal roofs, the surrounding dust surged toward the monument. [256] Phantom projections of laughing children and lush gardens began to coalesce in the refracted glow. [271] Kael knew these idyllic visions were a defensive mirage. [280] Left undisturbed, the atoms of nostalgia naturally organized themselves into symmetrical lattices of comfort, masking painful realities behind pleasant illusions. [300] To witness the authentic collapse of Oros, he needed to purify the memory cloud of its deceptive yearning. [318]

[332] Because false nostalgia thrived on rhythm, symmetry, and familiar cycles, a historian could only shatter the illusion by avoiding patterns. [397] Immediately, he launched into a frantic, erratic percussion across the metallic eaves. [466] Whenever his wrists drifted toward a recognizable melody, the golden mist thickened menacingly, whispering promises of warmth and forgotten youth. [486] He broke the emerging rhythm with a discordant clash, forcing the particulate cloud to fracture. [501]

[640] A wave of anguished joy crashed through his chest, bringing him to his knees on the stone floor. [658] He wept for the unbearable sorrow of a people who had murdered their own tomorrow, even as his heart soared at the transcendent nobility of their choice. [685] The synod had banned this history not because the ancients were monsters, but because their compassion was too devastatingly pure for a selfish age to bear. [711] As the last crimson ray vanished from the fissure, the prism went dark and the silver motes settled silently onto Kael's bare skin. [734] His own youthful certainty was gone forever, replaced by the heavy, luminous burden of a truth he would carry back to the surface. [757]

Oren crouched upon a pedestal of cracked marble, his matted fur stiff with rime as he surveyed the frozen garden in mist. [22] As a feral library guardian, he no longer wore the neat robes of an archivist, having shed civilized habits when the Great Index shattered. [46] Instead, he existed in a state of foggy lucidity, his thoughts drifting like vapor while his predatory instincts remained razor sharp. [67] He perceived the dead crystalline roses and ice coated statues through the veil of sleep, a perpetual half dream that blurred the centuries into one endless night. [94] Beneath his ribs ached a hollow pull, for he was dimensionally homesick, yearning for the geometric perfection of the Seventh Shelf where he had been born. [120] That realm of pure, warm parchment was gone, leaving him stranded in this cold courtyard between realities. [137]

With no scrolls left to protect, his only remaining directive was to wait. [150] He bided his time among the frostbitten hedges, listening for the footfalls of readers who would never arrive. [168] Clutched tightly in his clawed paw was a cultist's pendant, a jagged prism of black glass hung upon a tarnished silver chain. [190] He had torn it from the throat of a zealot who had breached the courtyard decades ago, seeking to burn the last remnants of recorded truth. [216] The zealot had perished in the frost, but the amulet still pulsed with a faint, violet luminescence. [233] Oren used this tiny glow to study the boundaries of light, watching where the violet rays surrendered to the surrounding gray fog. [255] In the old library, illumination had meant absolute knowledge, a neat division between what was cataloged and what did not exist. [276] Here, however, the edges of the glow were soft and porous, bleeding into the vapor without a clear border. [295]

Because the physical books had dissolved into the atmosphere during the collapse, Oren had developed a desperate method for preserving the world: interiorizing exteriors. [319] Every hour, he inhaled the freezing vapor and stared at the brittle ivy until the garden became mapped inside his own nervous system. [342] He swallowed the geometry of the leafless oaks, storing their tangled branches within his memory as if they were ink upon vellum. [364] By turning the landscape inward, he made himself the repository, though the process left him shivering and wild. [382] Yet no matter how much of the courtyard he absorbed, the ache for his native plane persisted. [399] The Seventh Shelf had been a place of absolute surety, where every cause sat neatly beside its effect. [417] This frozen expanse, conversely, shifted whenever the wind stirred the fog, refusing to stay indexed in his mind. [435]

He raised the dark prism to his snout, watching its violet gleam falter against the encroaching white emptiness. [453] The zealots had crafted the talisman to blind watchmen, designing it to show how fragile illumination truly was against the infinite dark. [475] For years, Oren had hated the trinket, viewing its dim circumference as an insult to the boundless lanterns of his lost home. [497] Tonight, as his consciousness floated deeper through the veil of sleep, the barrier between his memories and the garden began to thin. [519] He realized that his torment did not stem from the cold, nor from the savagery that had overtaken his posture. [539] His agony came from demanding that this misty purgatory behave like a cataloged volume. [553] If he wished to survive his exile, he needed a new purpose: to make friends with uncertainty. [570]

He looked again at the boundaries of light cast by the cultist's pendant, noticing how the mist danced most gracefully right where the visibility failed. [595] The unknown was not an enemy eating away at the archive; it was simply unprinted space waiting for new meaning. [615] Loosening his grip on the silver chain, Oren allowed the prism to swing gently from his claws like a pendulum. [635] He stopped trying to force the shifting fog into rigid mental categories. [647] Instead of merely memorizing the static ice, he began interiorizing exteriors that were entirely formless, drawing the unpredictable drift of the vapor into his spirit. [672] The sharp pang of being dimensionally homesick softened into a quiet, wistful acceptance as the unmapped mist filled the hollow spaces inside him. [695] He was still a beast in a ruined sanctuary, and his mind still swam in that strange, dreamy clarity. [714] Yet as he settled back onto the marble pedestal to wait, the shadows beyond the violet glow no longer felt like a threat, welcoming him like an open page. [743]

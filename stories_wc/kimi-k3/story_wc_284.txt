<story>At the tilt of the earth toward spring, Zephyr Bluff wore its mist like a held breath. [17] The hush was silver because the thaw had not decided whether to speak. [30] Mara Vey came up the old deer path, a burned spy with her living name still low. [47] The agency had called her dead after the embassy fire, and death had fit better than mercy. [64] Under the third cairn stone, where old snow refused to leave, Orsina's map had promised a pillbox. [81] It lay cold in Mara's palm, engraved with a windrose, seven cells, and one door without a dose. [99] Around the rim ran the words, courage keeps where use forgets. [110] Mara did not believe in mottoes, but she believed in gauges. [121] The network's last method had been codes in accumulated snow, read by wind, depth, and melt. [137] She set the pillbox on the rail and opened its cells to the bluff. [151] While mist combed the grass, snow chose different hollows and left one shining cell empty. [166] By the windrose, the filled depths spelled bell, lamp, flood, traitor. [177] The empty cell pointed south, toward the rescue beacon that should already have tasted ice. [192] Mara found the mast below the bluff with its mercury tilt switch cracked like a tired spine. [209] If the ice dam let go, no bell would warn the mill town folding linen in the valley. [227] Someone had prudently cut the wire high, daring the weather to finish the murder. [241] The adjective almost made Mara smile, because prudent daring had been her old assignment. [255] Then boots pressed the crust behind her, and Caspian Rhee said her dead name gently. [270] He had burned her file for passage money and kept her face as a private weather. [286] In his glove gleamed the missing screw from the switch, bright as a tooth. [300] Let the river speak, he said, and my convoy crosses before anyone learns the names. [315] Mara thought of shooting, which was simple, and of fixing, which was not. [328] The unused cell in the pillbox caught her eye, a small room swept for a guest. [344] She understood then that unused courage waits wherever repair remains possible and unclaimed. [357] So she became prudently daring, offering Rhee her back while she pocketed snow from the rail. [373] With her scarred thumb she wrote depth marks across that snow, sending warden, ladder, noon. [388] The melt would carry the code downhill as bright beads into the ditch by Hester's gate. [404] Rhee laughed once, until the first distant crack moved through the ice like a bone. [419] Mara said, I vouchsafe you one useful hour, not forgiveness, not a grave. [432] Hold the ladder against the wind, or explain your screw to the water. [445] His pride wanted refusal, but his hands, being colder, chose the rungs. [457] Mara climbed into the blowing gray with the pillbox open between her teeth. [470] Its hinge wire came free with a kiss of pliers, long enough to bridge the cut. [486] She warmed solder in a tin spoon over her hand heater and fed the break until it swallowed. [504] When the mercury stood upright, the mast remembered its body and the bell found its throat. [520] Sound crossed the mist before the flood did, a dark coin spent for every door. [535] Below, Hester's wardens were already moving because snow had spoken and courage had answered. [549] Rhee let go of the ladder only after boots ringed the mast and names left his pockets. [566] He looked betrayed by arithmetic, as if one repaired switch should equal one erased sin. [581] Mara kept the empty cell empty when she shut the pillbox. [592] Some doses should wait for the next broken thing, the next prudent hand, the next thaw. [608] At noon the bluff exhaled, the valley stood wet and alive, and the silver hush kept. [624]</story>

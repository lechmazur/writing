In the verdant arbor at dawn, Tilda Wren, whimsical tinkerer, heard the old garden breathing like a patient engine again. [20]
She came seeking a remedy, not for rusted kettles, but for blessings people had dropped from their busy hands lately. [40]

Tilda unslung her brass story wheel; when cranked gently, it spins golden stories from clinks, seeds, weather, and names alike. [160]

Then the gathering hush opened into morning, not empty, not finished, but fertile as a held breath before shared bread. [700]

<story>After the carousel halted above the clouds, the lagoon kept moving with small bright teeth. [15] Mara Vale, resolute scavenger, crossed the brass slats before the music finished dying. [28] Stillness did not arrive whole, but broke around her boots in careful pieces. [41] Every plank held a voice that had missed its proper year. [52] Mara was chronologically confused, so tomorrow often tapped her shoulder while yesterday warmed her hands. [67] She had learned to live by collecting fragments of overheard stories. [78] The kinetic lagoon above the clouds traded in such debris, bright foam and sentences torn from riders. [95] Some fell from the painted horses, some rose from the green machinery under the tide. [110] In her pocket sat the wheel from a disposable camera, black and notched and thirsty. [125] Its film was gone, yet each turn still made a frame inside the air. [139] Mara wound it gently, and a nearby gull gave her the end of a bargain. [154] Take back the brave boy, said the gull, though the boy had been old for ten winters. [171] A drowned calliope answered with the first hello Mara ever gave the sea. [184] She kept both fragments, because regret needed accurate hinges. [193] Her ghost waited beneath the central pole, wearing her face before ambition eroded. [206] It was not dead, only refused, only the self that stayed mounted when Mara stepped off. [222] The carousel had promised height to anyone who fed it a plan. [234] Young Mara fed it her map of storm gates, then her brother Nero fed it his fear. [251] The horses rose, the clouds opened, and Nero became a rumor with wet sleeves. [265] Mara told herself rescue required stillness, and stillness required leaving. [275] That sentence had protected her for years, which meant it had imprisoned her for years. [290] Now the halted carousel ticked like a clock too proud to strike. [302] Mara turned the camera wheel and caught a vendor saying, the map had teeth. [316] She turned it again and heard Nero at seven, begging the tide to hurry. [330] The next notch delivered her own voice, older than bone, promising not to look down. [345] Chronology buckled, but motive stood upright. [351] She wanted to negotiate with her ghost, not to win, only to stop paying interest on one halted afternoon. [370] The ghost lifted a hand full of eroded ambitions, pale as ground shells. [383] You left me riding, it said through three borrowed mouths. [393] You called survival a ladder and kicked away the rungs. [403] Mara regretted then with her whole body, a clean motion like diving. [415] She did not defend the frightened girl who had chosen air over Nero. [428] Instead she offered fragments, one by one, arranging them around the pole like lanterns. [442] A sailor said Nero laughed before the water took his name. [453] A child said the carousel only ate what was already cracked. [464] The gull repeated, take back the brave boy, and this time Mara heard begin where she had heard end. [483] Order remained impossible, but pattern emerged, a spiral rather than a road. [495] Mara set the black wheel against the pole and turned it backward through empty exposures. [510] Each click made the lagoon shiver without breaking its held breath. [521] Ghost Mara reached for the notches and found no numbers left, only smooth worn edges. [536] I cannot return him, Mara said, and the words did not excuse her. [549] I can carry the beginning beside the ending until they stop biting. [561] The ghost considered this with Nero's eyes, with Mara's mouth, with the face ambition had before it learned hunger. [580] Above them the clouds pressed close, bright floor and witness. [590] The carousel gave one slow rotation, not revival, but release. [600] A painted horse lowered its wooden knee, and from its hollow neck poured maps soft as ash. [617] Nero did not step out, yet his fear loosened from the gears and joined the tide. [633] Mara gathered that too, because even mercy must be scavenged before it rusts. [646] She gave the ghost the empty camera wheel, and her ghost gave back the years out of order. [664] Together they set the fragments down where the lagoon could read them without swallowing. [678] When the music did not return, Mara finally heard the stillness stop fracturing. [691] It stayed broken in places, but every broken place held a name she could answer. [706]</story>

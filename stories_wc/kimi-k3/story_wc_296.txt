<story>After the magic trick fails, Maren does not run from the lake. [12] She pockets the cracked mirror, because its lie has chosen her face. [24] Around her the shore waits with torches, ropes, prayers, and the baron's thin justice. [38] A guarded druid learns early that doors open inward and mercy can be armed. [52] Maren finds lost things: buttons, vows, moles, names, and once a winter bee. [65] The gift hurts, pulling her toward the empty place where an object should sleep. [79] It is how she buried three husbands nobody admitted killing and found the fourth alive. [94] Tonight it pulls through fog to the heart of a cursed lake, where a swirling orchard vortex swallows its own apples. [115] Black water turns in slow rings; apple trees wheel around the eye like soldiers keeping peace. [131] Roots surface and sink, pale hands spelling no, no, no against the current. [144] The hush has a warlike serenity, yet Maren sets her feet softly, as if calm were a drawn bow. [163] In her palm sits a flesh-eating plant seed, white as a tooth and warm from her blood. [180] It feeds only on living flesh that has finished pretending. [190] Maren stole it from a saint's reliquary and named it Last Argument. [202] Years ago her brother Elo confessed to poisoning the root that fed the baron's golden apples. [218] The words froze before they touched air, a clear confession hanging under the ice like a second moon. [236] Then the orchard bent inward, and every mirror in the valley began to answer questions sideways. [252] The glasswright swore reflected moonfire would thaw that frozen confession before the burning. [265] Instead the mirror showed Maren's mouth shaping Elo's crime. [274] The baron smiled, because a reflected druid is easier to hang than a lake. [288] Soldiers on the shore lift their brands, but Maren raises one empty hand and steps into the first ring. [307] She does not fight the vortex; she gives it a circle wider than fear. [321] The water accepts her ankle, her knee, her oath, then opens a path through expanding circles. [337] Each ring finds what the valley lost and lays it cold against her skin. [351] The first circle returns Elo's bone whistle; the second returns the baron's scale weight; the third returns Maren's missing laugh. [371] The fourth brings a child's red ribbon, still tied in the shape of a promised future. [387] The fifth brings teeth from apples that were never fruit. [397] With every find, one bent branch unbends with a crack like a kneeling army rising. [412] The orchard does not forgive quickly; it remembers axes more kindly than kings. [425] At the eye stands Elo, bark grown over his mouth, eyes patient as winter seeds. [440] Mirrors show his guilt; they do not show Maren pouring black sap into the golden root to spare the tithed children. [461] They do not show Elo stealing her knife and naming the poison his. [474] They show the crime, never the bargain, never the children counting ribs beneath harvest songs. [489] That is why Maren came: to discover what mirrors don't show when they choose a sinner. [505] Maren presses the seed to her cut palm and speaks before fear can close her. [520] It was my hand, she says; Elo only loved me badly enough to die for it. [536] The seed bites, finds the lie still living in her silence, and eats that instead of bone. [553] Pain blooms white, then roots outward in circles that unbend tree after tree after tree. [568] Her left arm straightens last, finger by finger, until it points at the baron without hatred. [584] The frozen confession lifts from the ice and breaks into rain warm enough to taste. [599] Elo's mouth opens; no sound leaves, but the lost words enter Maren through the rings. [614] Forgive the mercy that looked like murder. [621] The soldiers hear it and lower their brands, ashamed of fire. [632] The baron shouts that reflections cannot testify, and the lake answers with his stolen scale weight. [648] Maren keeps her guard, because peace is not trust, yet she lets one soldier hold the ladder of roots. [667] Together they draw Elo down while the orchard exhales apples into the dark. [680] By dawn the lake is only water, and the vortex remains as seven faint circles on Maren's skin. [698] When mirrors are brought to her, she turns them face down. [709] She has learned what they refuse to show: a hand can sin, a heart can confess, and love can wear both names. [731]</story>

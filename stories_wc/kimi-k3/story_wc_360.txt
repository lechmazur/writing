<story>Between vanishing stars, the topaz workshop kept its windows open so the roof could listen. [15] Ilex Vale ran the letterpress there, cryptic as a locked chase, borrowing a walk from masons, a laugh from midwives, and a prayer from thieves. [40] People said she had no self unless someone else was warm enough to lend one. [55] She answered that a self was only type left standing after the easier letters had been melted for bells. [74] Her motive was not kindness but proof: to understand the mathematics of emotion before the last star forgot its number. [94] In the composing stick she arranged grief as ratios, joy as bright integers, and shame as fractions that would not hold. [115] Above the press lay a halo fragment, curved like a gold parenthesis and cold enough to make starlight stutter. [134] It had fallen from no church anyone remembered, yet every face near it leaned as if forgiven. [151] On the night the three eastern stars vanished, Casian came with his sister's unsent letter and a mouth full of borrowed silence. [173] He had killed her patience by living, he said, and now he wanted to know whether her love had carried a remainder. [195] Ilex borrowed his clenched shoulders before she borrowed his words, which made him step back from his own body. [214] Then she disoriented the room through deliberate ambiguity, setting one line twice so it read both I absolve you and I am you. [237] The halo fragment went into the chase where a spacer should be, tilting every sort toward an invisible center. [256] Ink took the topaz light and returned it darker, as if yellow glass had learned to bruise. [273] Ilex pulled the proof, and the sheet came up blank except for two thumbprints exchanging heat. [289] Casian laughed with her midwife's mercy, then hated himself for the gentleness he had not earned. [305] Ilex felt his sister open inside her like a window that had waited years for weather. [321] The transposed empathy did not speak; it measured, finding the distance between wound and witness exactly one body wide. [340] Outside, another star vanished, and the workshop floor forgot which shadow belonged to which machine. [355] Casian reached for the letter, but his hand moved with Ilex's thief prayer and stole only the seal. [373] Inside the wax was a single strand of hair, black as uninked night and curved like the fragment's twin. [392] Ilex understood then that the sister had hidden her equation in plain sight: love equals attention divided by time, plus what remains when time refuses. [417] The remainder was not forgiveness but the habit of returning to the same ache with cleaner hands. [434] To test it, she set Casian's name beside her own and left the verb empty. [449] Ambiguity did the work of mercy by refusing to decide who was being saved. [463] The press rolled, stars thinned, and the halo fragment lifted enough light to outline two faces wearing one expression. [482] Casian felt Ilex's borrowed personalities fall away, mason, midwife, thief, until the vacancy underneath touched him like snow. [500] He did not pity it, which was the first honest number she had ever been given. [516] In return he gave her his sister's last gesture, fingers smoothing an apron that was not there. [533] The gesture carried no absolution, only practice, and practice weighed more than gold. [546] Ilex saw that emotion solved nothing; it changed units, turning punishment into shelter when the denominator became shared. [564] She could have kept Casian's grief as a new coat, warm, expensive, and falsely fitted. [579] Instead she broke the form before dawn, scattering type so no court of feeling could reconvene. [595] The halo fragment cooled to plain glass, its curve no longer holy, only accurate. [609] Casian read the blank sheet once more and found his own pulse printed there in the bruise colored topaz. [628] He left without a personality for her to borrow, and the absence fit better than any voice. [645] Between the last two vanishing stars, Ilex inked one clean line from no donor: I am still counting. [663] The press answered with a tremor, lucid and turbulent, like truth learning rhythm. [676] She did not call it love, because love had become too large for one name and too exact for worship. [696] When the final star withdrew, the workshop remained under stars by remembering them, each dark socket a proof pressed into sky. [717]</story>

<story>At Pearl Bay at dawn, before everything shifts, Maelis Sorren came alone to the prism atrium in mist. [18]
She was the reflective harbor glazier, the woman who read between lines when other people read only bills. [36]
Her trade was fitting panes for trawlers, lofts, and the old lighthouse stair. [49]

She believed value hid in the mundane because the mundane was where hands spent their lives. [84]

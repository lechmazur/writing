Sen was a harmonic stone skimmer sworn to the towers of bone in a canyon of endless echoes where sound kept faith with dead hands and old vows. [28]
Historically reverent she polished each ancestor name through centuries of layered dust until the walls remembered footsteps that no living tribe recalled or dared to name aloud again. [56]
Her motive was plain and hard as flint to learn to navigate without moving because the canyon had broken every road and spared only listening for those remaining. [84]
Among her relics lay a stone arrowhead no larger than a tear notched by a maker who had hunted thunder before herds forgot the sky and lost meaning. [112]
The elders spoke of a pulse left wandering after the first silence cracked the tower hearts and sent their guarded rhythm begging through stone for a still witness. [140]
Sen meant to appraise that fugitive beat through the patterns in a flock of birds in flight and read where stillness could travel farther than feet ever again. [168]
Each dawn she climbed no stair yet set her spine against the wind with weathered resolve and listened for wings crossing the white throat of the canyon below. [196]
Cranes rose from the salt pools in braided waves and their turning bodies wrote arrows that vanished before the echo could agree to point for any living hunter. [224]

To chase it would scatter the music so she pressed her heels into dust and let stillness become the longest journey she could command without begging far horizons. [336]
The birds turned again and each wingbeat laid a bead upon the string that ran from her breastbone to the hidden heart of echoes beyond reach but answerable. [364]
The stone arrowhead warmed though noon had failed and Sen understood that appraisal was not price but permission for truth to approach and remain inside the listening circle. [392]
Centuries before a keeper had hidden the tribe's heartbeat inside the canyon so invaders would hear only emptiness and turn their spears toward rain instead of children's doors. [420]

Now the cranes folded east then west then inward and Sen read a spiral that asked not for footsteps but for an answering hum deep enough to hold. [476]

Sen touched the arrowhead to the dust and sent the pulse home through the spiral while cranes carved gentler laws across the echoing heights above bone city below. [644]

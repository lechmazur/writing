<story>Before the ritual, Sera Vail climbed with charcoal in her teeth and defiance bright as a wound. [17] She was the defiant artist the bone law wanted gagged, a girl with an innocently mischievous smile and paint under every nail. [39] The towers of bone in a canyon of endless echoes kept their counsel under a forged hush. [56] Each tower had been raised from a witness who would not stop repeating one sentence. [71] Their ribs curved into flutes, and every apology came back wearing another voice. [84] At the center stood a pale spire in mist, thin as a held breath and sharp as refusal. [102] There the Keepers meant to forgive Captain Dron for killing Lio, Sera's sister and the canyon's best liar. [120] The price of forgiveness was simple and obscene. [128] Speak the one true account, surrender it to the spire, and let every echo forget the rest. [145] Sera loved Lio too much to make her conveniently singular. [155] Lio had stolen bread, kissed a novice, mapped forbidden stairs, and died naming Dron while smiling. [171] No single sentence could hold her without becoming a cage. [181] So Sera planned mischief with the clean gravity of prayer. [191] All night she moved from tower to tower, beginning impromptu poetry sessions with the dead. [206] She tapped a knuckle on each skull and offered half a line like bread to birds. [222] The echoes, starved for play, finished her rhymes wrong on purpose. [233] Their wrongness pleased her, because every crooked answer made the truth harder to arrest. [247] A child's tower lisped that Lio had died forgiving Dron before she understood him. [261] A sentry's tower boomed that Lio died cursing his mother, his oath, and his boots. [276] Three small towers giggled that Lio had seen Dron weep into a bowl of ash. [291] None of them agreed, and therefore all of them were dangerous. [302] By dawn the mist around the spire had learned to glitter with alternatives. [315] Keeper Anh met Sera at the stair and called her mercy vandalism. [327] Sera bowed so sweetly that the old woman checked her own pockets. [339] Inside them were only bells, blue thread, and a tooth Lio had lost while laughing. [354] The bells were for rhythm, the thread for sutures, the tooth for proof that joy had once had teeth. [373] Anh said forgiveness had to be one clean cut or the wound would gossip forever. [388] Sera answered that a clean cut was how executioners complimented themselves. [399] Her motive was not revenge, though revenge kept offering to carry her bag. [412] She came to refract a single truth into many, because one beam can be aimed and many can fill a room. [433] The Keepers rang the spire with iron mallets, and the forged hush returned like a lid. [449] Dron climbed in chains, handsome with regret, already rehearsing the sentence that would save him. [464] The crowd leaned toward the relief of being done with Lio. [475] Sera felt hatred offer its pure white stone, smooth enough to swallow. [487] Instead she rang Lio's tooth against the spire and began without permission. [499] My sister is not your key, she said, and the nearest tower answered, your lock is late. [516] Dron said he had followed orders, and five towers sang five different names for fear. [531] Sera tossed them the word forgive, and it came back as forbid, forget, forbid again, then forgive wearing mud. [550] Through impromptu poetry sessions the spire began to refract, not light, but accountability. [563] One truth entered the mist and struck the bone, and many truths walked out looking for witnesses. [580] Lio was murdered, Lio was merciful, Lio was furious, Lio was hungry, Lio was unfinished. [595] The price changed hands before anyone could bless it. [604] If Dron wanted forgiveness, he would have to live beside every version of the girl he ended. [621] If the crowd wanted peace, it would have to stop mistaking sameness for healing. [635] If Sera wanted her sister back, she would have to spend the rest of her life returning splinters. [653] She paid first by opening her fist and letting Lio be larger than her grief. [668] Dron spoke then, not one account but seven, each uglier and more exact. [681] The spire did not erase them; it leaned in the mist like a bell learning shame. [697] When the hush finally broke, it made a sound like many doors agreeing to stay open. [713]</story>

<story>Under a fleeting golden sunset the drift ice marina breathed through cracks of blue black water. [103] Now the ice carried boots though no living fisherman would cross after the bells went thin. [273] She named none of them monsters because names were doors and doors were what they ate. [289] Instead she offered her own guilt the winter she let her sister row out alone. [522] Mira locked the crypt by humming the ward into ice until the key remembered only gratitude. [538] When sunset vanished the marina still held one gold line inside every plank and bone. [553] She climbed to the lamp room with thawing sleeves and set the azure coat to dry beside the glass. [572] Below her the sacred artifact kept time with the tide while the key in her pocket beat like a second heart. [593] Mira laughed once because logic had led her to mercy and mercy had answered in flesh. [609] Then the light turned over the dark water carrying absolution farther than any ship could swim.

<story>Elian entered the library by falling asleep beside its forbidden door. [11] He came through the veil of sleep with snow on his sleeves and a stolen thing burning cold in his fist. [32] It was the cultist's pendant, a black stone in a silver mouth that swallowed lamplight. [47] Beyond the windows waited a frozen garden in mist, where roses stood in ice and statues wore white masks. [66] Elian had expected silence inside, but the shelves breathed, growled, and shifted their teeth of books. [82] Then she dropped from the highest gallery and landed on all fours between him and the door. [99] She was the feral library guardian, barefoot, ink stained, and crowned with nesting bookmarks. [113] Her name, stitched on a ruined uniform, was Vess. [122] "You smell of another winter," she said. [129] Elian opened his fist, and the pendant dimmed every lamp along the reading hall. [143] The light did not vanish, but pulled back to a bright circle around their feet. [158] Vess stared at the shrinking edge and whispered that the boundaries of light were the only true walls here. [177] "Beyond them," she said, "books forget their pages and gardens forget their ground." [190] The cult had sent Elian in dreams because waking thieves always lost their hands to the door. [207] They wanted the pendant planted among the oldest shelves, where its hunger would eat the dawn from inside. [225] Elian had agreed because the cult promised to cure his sister of a fever that returned each night. [243] Now the stone showed him her face in its black center, younger and already buried. [258] He understood with foggy lucidity that the cult had paid him in a future meant to frighten obedience. [276] "Take it back," he said, and held the pendant toward Vess. [287] She recoiled, not from fear of the stone, but from fear of touching a hand offered freely. [304] For thirty years she had guarded alone, eating marginal notes, sleeping under desks, and warning visitors away with bites. [323] Loneliness had made her honest and terrible at mercy. [332] Still, she leaned close, sniffed the pendant, and said it would starve by sunrise if denied the shelves. [350] Elian asked what they would do until then. [358] "We wait," she said. [362] Waiting was not idleness in the library, but a discipline older than catalogues. [375] Shelves settled only when watched patiently, and lost chapters crept home only when no one chased them. [392] Vess led him through the narrowing light into the garden, because cold soil could slow the stone. [409] Under a rose arch, she buried the pendant in black soil and covered it with her scarred hands. [427] Cold climbed her wrists while the circle of light closed around them like a slow eye. [443] Elian asked why she did not throw the pendant beyond the wall and be rid of it. [460] She said thrown things in this place learned the way back with teeth. [473] He asked if sunrise was certain, and her ears flattened at the word. [486] "Certainty is a cage with clean bars," she said. [495] "I was taught to make friends with uncertainty, because uncertainty leaves a door unlocked." [509] Once, Vess had lived in a warm dimension where libraries grew like orchards and guardians sang instead of growling. [528] A misfiled atlas had stranded her here, dimensionally homesick ever since for rain that fell upward and mothers who shelved the moon. [550] She missed that world most when frost made this one resemble a page no hand would turn. [567] To endure exile, she had learned the guardian method of interiorizing exteriors. [579] She breathed in the endangered place until its rooms, paths, and weather lived behind her ribs. [595] Now she knelt in the mist and inhaled the garden slowly, taking in frozen roses, iron gates, and buried paths. [615] Each breath made her shiver, yet each shiver planted the outside safely within her memory and blood. [632] Elian copied her, breathing mist until his chest ached and the garden seemed to stand inside his thoughts. [650] For the first time since his sister sickened, he did not demand a promised ending from the dark. [668] He waited beside Vess, sharing the small light, naming each fear without obeying it. [682] Through the veil of sleep, hours passed while voices in the mist offered each of them a stolen home. [701] Each voice asked only one step beyond the light, and each time the other hummed until it thinned. [719] At last the pendant cracked, and returning light found the garden alive inside them both. [734] Elian woke at dawn holding a bookmark, carrying Vess and her garden behind his ribs. [749] He went home unsure of healing, yet ready to wait beside his sister without lies. [764]</story>

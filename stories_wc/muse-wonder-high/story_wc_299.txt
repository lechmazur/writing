The quarry office trailer sat at the edge of the pit, its thin walls humming each time a loader passed outside. [84]

Milo liked that comparison too much, so he opened his notebook and began insulating emotionally with facts. [117]
Item one, limestone core samples, no resale value. [125]
Item two, steel desk, circa nineteen eighty, dented left drawer. [135]
Before antiques, he had been a roadie for nine years, hauling amplifiers for a band that almost mattered. [153]

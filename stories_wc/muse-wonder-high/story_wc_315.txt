<story>Mara's watch truly begins after the radio static clears. [9] Before that moment, the speakers only hiss and spit while she stares into black cloud. [24] Then the hiss stops, and the silence lands on the platform like a held breath. [39] She works in the nocturnal arbor above the clouds, a ring of platforms lashed to branches wider than roads. [58] Below the woven floor, cloud hides the forest, the river, and every road home. [72] Above her, leaves turn in wind she cannot hear through the thick glass of the watch dome. [89] The crew calls her their midnight pattern seeker because she finds meaning in lights that others dismiss as chance. [108] Fireflies drift between the branches, rain beads slide down ropes, and distant beacons blink through breaks in the cloud. [127] Most nights the patterns mean nothing, and she lets the crew sleep. [139] That mercy is the watcher's burden, since each quiet hour asks her to carry fear alone so others can rest. [159] Two nights ago she woke Captain Ilya for a pattern that proved to be moths around a lamp. [177] No one punished her, but breakfast went silent when she entered, and that silence stayed with her. [194] Tonight her task is simple and hard at once, to preserve trust under pressure without letting a real call fade. [214] At midnight the lower beacons begin to blink in a sequence she has never seen. [229] Three short pulses rise through cloud, then two long ones, then three short pulses again. [244] She counts on her fingers, writes the times in her log, and waits for the pattern to break. [262] It does not break. [266] Instead the pulses sharpen, as if someone below the cloud is turning a lamp toward her with weak and careful hands. [287] Mara wants to wake Ilya at once, but she can already hear the quiet at breakfast grow colder. [305] She must revise her first impulse into a test that no tired mind can mistake for hope. [322] Being attentively inventive has saved her before, so she trusts the odd tools at hand more than loud alarms. [341] She takes the spare signal mirror, a coil of pale cord, and a lantern shutter from the repair bench. [360] First she answers the pulses with the shutter, three short and two long, and holds her breath behind the glass. [380] The cloud below swallows her reply, or seems to, and for ten slow counts nothing answers back. [397] Then a new pattern comes, weaker but nearer, moving from the south branch toward the old supply line. [415] Mara leans from the dome door and feels cold air press her face like a palm. [431] Out there the arbor is all black leaves, wet bark, and ropes humming without sound against the wind. [449] A faint tap reaches the platform through wood rather than air, three taps and then two. [465] She follows the tap to the south rail, where a supply parachute hangs torn in the branches below. [483] Its canopy has snagged on a limb, and its cargo pod swings each time the hidden climber pulls the lines. [503] Caught in the pod netting is Joren, the young courier who was due before dusk with medicine for Ilya's fever. [523] He cannot shout through the wind, so he has been striking the trunk and flashing his wrist lamp through cloud gaps. [544] The tapping stops at his end because one line has twisted around a metal edge near his hand. [562] Mara sees the glint and knows it at once, a parachute buckle half jammed with bark and ice. [580] If Joren pulls again, the buckle may tear free and drop him and the medicine into the cloud. [598] She cannot reach him from the rail, and the winch handle is locked inside the sleeping quarters. [615] Waking everyone now would bring hands and light, but also panic on wet ropes in the dark. [632] Waking no one would keep faith with her watch, yet leave Joren to trust a stranger to notice him. [651] She revises the choice itself, waking only Ilya and telling him the pattern, the tap, and the buckle in one calm breath. [673] Ilya listens without doubt, because proof has replaced guesswork, and his nod restores something small and vital between them. [692] He holds the lamp while she climbs beyond the rail with the pale cord looped across her chest. [710] On the limb her gloves slip, her lamp dies, and darkness takes even the shape of her hands. [728] Then remembering through muscle memory saves her, as old rescue drills guide her fingers to knot, brace, and test each hold. [749] She reaches the buckle, clears ice with her thumbnail, and turns it until the trapped line slides free without tearing. [769] Joren climbs into lamplight with the medicine intact, and behind Mara the whole arbor seems to exhale as one soundless echo. [790]</story>

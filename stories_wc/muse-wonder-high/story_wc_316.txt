<story>The floating rock dojo hung above the sea where no rewritten road could reach it. [15] Below it, while history rewrites itself, harbors became battlefields and then orchards before the spray settled. [31] Only the rock remembered every version, so the traveling court climbed its rope stairs at dawn. [47] Mara Venn arrived first, carrying her daughter's spare coat over one arm like evidence. [61] She was an ego death survivor, and she had not felt anything clearly since that night. [77] Three years earlier, in this same hall, meditation and fever had dissolved the self she called Mara. [94] She had watched it go without terror and returned to a body that seemed borrowed. [109] Since then food had texture but no taste, music had pattern but no pull, and love had duty but no heat. [130] Her fight was to defeat emotional numbness before it quietly took her daughter too. [144] That private motive, not pride, had forced her into this clone custody battle. [157] Her opponent arrived barefoot, weeping with relief at the sight of the coat. [170] She wore Mara's face, Mara's scar, and a name the laboratory had given her, Duplicate. [185] The lab had grown her from cells taken while Mara lay mindless after ego death. [200] They had wanted a second witness to consciousness, one who could report on the empty room from inside another skull. [220] Duplicate had escaped, built a life in the marsh towns, and learned to feel everything Mara could not. [238] Where Mara was winter, Duplicate was flood, and each privately envied the other her weather. [253] Between them walked Lian, seven years old, holding both their hands without choosing. [266] Lian had been born in a clinic week that kept changing its own records. [280] In one history Mara carried her alone after a donor died childless and unknown. [294] In another Duplicate carried her, fled the lab pregnant, and begged Mara for shelter. [308] Both women remembered labor, first steps, fevers, and songs in the dark as their own. [323] The rewrites had not invented those memories from nothing, because both had loved her in different timelines. [340] Arbiter Sorn set a wooden rule on the mat and said the court would hear conduct, not metaphysics. [358] Master Ivo, bald and patient, placed cushions in a circle and poured tea that smelled of smoke. [375] His whole teaching worked through complementary opposites, stillness against sparring, silence against confession, grief against laughter. [391] He believed a person became whole only when she stopped amputating the half she feared. [406] Mara spoke first because numbness had made her blunt, and her honesty slices through pretense without cruelty. [423] She said she fed Lian, taught her, guarded her sleep, and still sometimes felt like an actor reciting motherhood. [442] She said she wanted custody not to win, but to stay near the one person she was determined to learn to feel again. [465] Duplicate answered shaking, saying feeling too much could drown a child as surely as feeling too little. [482] She described nights when panic made her hide in the marsh while Lian made soup and comforted her. [500] Each confession exposed the other woman's missing discipline, and Ivo let the silence hold both. [515] Then Sorn demanded the sealed evidence the lab had surrendered after its records began changing. [530] Two novices carried in a brain in a jar labeled duplicate in red laboratory ink. [545] Lian went very quiet, so Mara and Duplicate knelt at once on either side of her. [561] Ivo lifted the jar, and the brain inside caught the morning light through its glass. [576] He explained that the lab had removed it from the dying first clone and kept it dreaming under glass. [595] Its attached ledger proved neither woman in the room was the laboratory's first copy or its untouched source. [613] Both were later generations grown from divided cells, each edited toward a trait the scientists wanted isolated. [630] Mara had been shaped for detachment, Duplicate for attachment, and the jar held the sister neither had been allowed to become. [651] The revelation should have made the case simpler, but it stripped away the last legal fiction that original meant real. [671] Sorn asked Lian what home felt like, and the girl answered with a child's merciless clarity. [687] She said Mara made fear small and Duplicate made joy large, and she did not want to cut herself in half. [708] Ivo then set the jar between the women and asked each to vivify what the other had buried. [726] Mara taught Duplicate to breathe through panic until her hands stopped trembling over the teacup. [741] Duplicate taught Mara a ridiculous marsh song until laughter surprised her like warmth through cracked ice. [757] It was not a cure, but feeling returned in a thin bright thread when she sang the wrong words on purpose. [778] Sorn granted joint custody on the rock, where two incomplete mothers could raise one whole child together while timelines settled below. [799]</story>

<story>Mira had been the root-bound visionary of the cobalt glade for eleven years, ever since roots claimed her ankles at dusk. [21] She could walk the paths between the blue trees, but never beyond the last stone, where her hidden roots pulled taut. [42] It happened a week after the future arrives early, when tomorrow began appearing in puddles before today had finished raining. [62] Clocks chimed tomorrow's hours, seeds sprouted showing next year's flowers, and dreams arrived still warm from nights not yet lived. [82] Mira was purposefully curious about those dreams because curiosity, chosen daily, kept her rooted life from becoming a cage. [101] Each evening she pressed her palms to the scarred trunks and listened for what the glade wanted her to heed. [121] Long ago the first keepers had made a covenant with the glade, promising never to cut its heart tree in exchange for visions. [144] Last winter a solar eclipse had passed over that promise, and the covenant went dark with the sun. [162] The elders called it the eclipsed covenant, still binding but hidden, like a road under snow that feet could miss. [182] Since then the visions had arrived broken, each showing only half a future and leaving Mira to guess the rest. [202] Her brother Jonah, who cut paths for the village, dreamed the other halves and woke shouting names she had only whispered. [223] He came to the cobalt glade at dusk with a maze topiary clipping curled tight in his fist, green and impossibly fresh. [245] The clipping came from the hedge maze behind their childhood home, which neither of them had visited since the roots took her. [267] Yet its leaves bore yesterday's frost and next week's blossom at once, and its stem pointed always toward Mira, never north. [288] Jonah opened his hand and showed a thin scar across his palm, shaped exactly like the clipping's twisted stem. [307] Mira looked down and found the same scar blooming under her own skin, though no blade had touched her in years. [328] They shared one impossible purpose, to find the intersection of dreams where her half visions met his sleeping halves. [347] If they found it, they might learn who had cut the heart tree and why the future had come unstuck. [367] Jonah believed the maze remembered the cutter because its hedges grew around every secret pressed into them. [384] Mira feared he was right, and that fear sat beside a bright anxious elation she could not separate from hope. [404] At dusk the glade turned deeper cobalt, and every trunk scar opened like a dark eye watching them decide. [423] Mira set the clipping on the ground between their four feet, two rooted and two restless, and waited. [441] The clipping shivered, uncurled, and grew three quick leaves that formed a tiny arch pointing into the trees. [459] Beneath the arch, the ground showed not soil but a shared dream, their childhood kitchen flooded with blue evening light. [479] In that dream their mother stood young again, holding an axe she had sworn in the covenant never to lift. [499] Mira wanted to look away, but she heeded the scar's sudden ache instead, because it pulsed exactly when mother lied. [519] In the dream she said she had cut nothing, while behind her the heart tree bled light from a fresh wound. [540] Jonah whispered that in his half she always confessed, begging someone beyond the kitchen door to forgive her bargain. [559] Only together did the lie and confession make sense, because their mother had cut the tree to trade its heart for Mira's life. [582] When Mira lay dying at nine, their mother gave the glade heartwood and begged it to root her daughter instead. [602] The eclipse had not caused the break, only shadowed it, so blame settled on strangers while their family guarded shame. [622] Now the clipping, grown from hedges their mother planted over the buried heartwood, had carried that buried truth home. [641] Mira took Jonah's scarred palm, and linking through scars they stepped together into the dream kitchen as one witness. [660] Their joined scars burned, then cooled, and the dream mother finally saw both children and told the whole undivided truth. [680] She had meant to renew the covenant at dawn, but death took her before she could confess or plant amends. [700] Mira understood that the intersection was not a place to find, but a promise two people could finally keep together. [720] They planted the clipping where the heart tree had stood, and each vowed aloud to heed the glade before taking from it. [742] The roots at Mira's ankles loosened, not vanishing but easing, as if trust could make even a binding into choice. [762] Above them the cobalt dusk steadied, tomorrow waited its turn again, and anxious elation gave way to quiet joy in being believed. [784]</story>

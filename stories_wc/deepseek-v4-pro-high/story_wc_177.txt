<story>Foresight Hindsight Only woke with the taste of ozone and a dream still clotting behind her eyes. [17] She lay in the vine-draped bunker hidden in the rewilded forest, listening to the storm grope through the canopy like a blind animal. [40] Her foresight said the storm would arrive within the hour; her hindsight said she had been expecting it for days. [60] The present was the one thing Foresight could not see. [70] She sat up and reached under the cot for the wafer seal. [82] The seal had come attached to the last directive, a brittle dispatch from before the old network decayed. [100] Now the directive was losing its words, eaten by moisture and something else that unspooled sentences into silence. [118] She could still read one phrase: "Transcend the path that walks." [129] The rest crumbled when she touched it. [136] Foresight did not know what the phrase meant, but her dreams had been interpreting it for three nights. [154] Those dreams set the same scene: a path through drowned ferns, a sealed door in the bunker's east wall, and the storm arriving with her feet already moving. [182] She called the direction "foresight" and the memory "hindsight," but both were just weather in her skull. [199] Her name, Foresight Hindsight Only, had been a joke once, precisely playful, but now it was a diagnosis. [217] She lit the storm lantern and moved to the east wall. [228] There the vines had split the concrete, and behind the moss the seam of a door showed faint as a scar. [249] The wafer seal warmed in her palm, not from her body but from recognition. [263] Foresight pressed it to the door. [269] Nothing happened, exactly as her foresight had predicted and her hindsight had already grieved. [283] She needed the present, and the present could only be met through interpreted dreams. [297] The dream had told her to press the seal not against the door but against her own sternum. [315] That was not a sane instruction, but the decaying directive had never promised sanity. [329] She did it. [332] The wafer seal adhered, cold and then burning, and the door opened inward with a sound of roots tearing. [351] Beyond it lay the rewilded forest, not as she remembered but as her dreams had drawn it: silver with coming rain. [372] The path was already there, curving away from the bunker. [382] She could not decide whether it had been waiting for her or making itself as she watched. [399] The storm's first gust hit the canopy, and leaves fell upward. [410] Foresight walked. [412] As she followed the path, the directive's words returned, not in the paper but in the landscape. [429] She saw the phrase in the spacing of ferns: "Transcend the path that walks." [443] A rabbit thumped and crossed in front of her, then doubled back, as if the path corrected its own direction. [463] The third dream had been clearest: she would reach a dry creek bed where the wafer seal would fall away. [483] There she would have to speak the answer to the question that had driven her from the bunker. [501] She had asked it for years: why do some paths choose their walkers? [514] The storm opened overhead, and rain came in warm, hard sheets. [525] The path led her to the creek bed just as the water began to rise. [540] The wafer seal fell from her sternum and cracked into two neat halves on the stones. [556] Inside the seal, words had been hidden under the wax. [566] They were not the directive's words but her own, carved by a hand she recognized as hers. [583] "You do not choose the path," she read aloud. [592] "The path is the shape of your walking." [600] She closed her eyes. [604] Between foresight and hindsight, a narrow present opened like a door in the rain. [618] She stepped into it, not to escape the storm but to meet it with a self that finally occupied the walking. [639] The path unraveled behind her and tightened ahead, no longer choosing her or chosen by her, simply being made. [658] The vine-draped bunker disappeared into the roar, and Foresight Hindsight Only was no longer only foresight and hindsight. [676] She had transcended the divide between what waited and what remembered. [687] She was the present, precisely playful at last, walking the path that walked her. [701]</story>

The cloud-shadow poet walked through a cobalt atrium by a frozen lake after the ban lifted. [16]
She had come to stride through boundaries once thought sacred, and the thought made her gloved hands unsteady. [34]
The humming quartz colonnade rose from the ice at the atrium's far edge, each pillar glowing like a held breath. [54]

It was not hope exactly; it was the abrupt knowledge that the colonnade would allow her through. [154]

Mira was a bunker builder's claustrophobic child, and she carried a tiny porcelain doll in her coat pocket. [18]
Her father made shelters where doors locked inward, and each sealed room pressed against her ribs like a slow fist. [38]
So she sought open heights, where air could move without apology, and she found the xenial trellis above the clouds. [58]
The trellis was a lattice of pale wood and glass, smiling with gentle hooks, and it hung over a sea of fog. [80]
Every morning, when gravity inverts for an hour, the trellis breathed upward and the fog became a ceiling of white petals. [101]
Mira used the inverted hour to collect the flavors of different silences, because each hush had a distinct taste. [120]
Some silences tasted of salt, others of cold iron, and one near the eastern hook tasted of gilded hush. [139]
The gilded hush was not a sound; it was an intangibly tangible film that gathered on her tongue when she stopped trying to hear. [163]
Her father had taught her the method: by limiting exposure to noise, the mind learns the shape of quiet. [182]
He had said it like a warning, because silence can be a wall as thick as concrete. [199]
But Mira loved walls that let light through, and she kept the doll pressed to her ear like a tiny listening stone. [221]
On the second day of inverted gravity, the porcelain doll began to sprout a green thread from its painted hair. [241]
The thread climbed her sleeve and wrapped around her wrist, cool as a secret kept by limiting exposure. [259]
She was not afraid, because the doll had always felt safer than the bunker rooms, where air ran out first. [279]
As the hour deepened, the xenial trellis above the clouds turned its hooks toward the sun, and the gilded hush thickened. [300]
Mira closed her mouth to taste it, and the flavor came down like honeyed rain over a dry field. [319]
In the hush, she heard the trellis breathe: a long inhale of clouds, a shorter exhale of gold. [337]
Then the doll sprouted again, this time a white flower with a face like a closed eye. [354]
The flower did not bloom loudly; it opened with the tone of stretching contracted fabric, careful and sudden. [372]
Mira understood that the gilded hush was not empty, but full of tiny roots waiting for a place to press. [392]
The doll wanted to collect silences too, she realized, and it used her fear of enclosed spaces as a kind of door. [414]
By limiting exposure to her father's locks, she had become a small open room, a place where quiet could grow. [434]
Her father's voice reached up from the far below, muffled and warm, asking if the air was still enough. [453]
Mira touched the porcelain face and answered, Yes, but I am learning to breathe in a different direction. [471]
The inverted hour began to tilt back, and the clouds slid downward like sheets being pulled from a bed. [490]
The gilded hush thinned, but it left a film on her skin, intangibly tangible and faintly sweet. [507]
The doll's flower closed, and its sprout wound around her finger like a ring made of soft green light. [526]
Mira realized she had collected more than flavor; she had carried a silence back from the clouds into her own chest. [547]
It sat there, a small garden behind her ribs, no narrower than a bunker door, but breathing with her. [566]
When the next inversion came, she would bring the doll upward, because by limiting exposure to fear she could make room. [587]
The xenial trellis above the clouds would wait, and the gilded hush would wait, and she would sprout a little more. [608]

Ora is the reluctant caretaker of a cursed orchard, a title she accepted only because the hill answered no one else. [21]
The orchard grows on a cliffside labyrinth patrolled by mechanical birds, a place of hedges, stone stairs, and thin paths that drop into the sea. [46]

Every morning she climbs the walls before the night frost forms, because the birds say the cold keeps the dead quiet. [92]

It was offhandedly exact, with every seam placed as if by a careless hand that had practiced forever. [212]
The blueprint's instructions said to assemble the path by leaving impressions without touching the living things around it. [230]

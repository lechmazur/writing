Elio had been admitted there after the blood work comes back, though no one in the haven spoke of failure, only of the distance between one breath and the next. [92]
Mara had come with a clear purpose: to execute a flawless goodbye, because a goodbye could be arranged like a chord, with each voice placed so that no one was left alone in the final measure. [128]
She was decisively kind, meaning that her kindness did not waver when a man asked for the impossible or when a daughter could not meet his eyes. [155]
In the haven, the sun did not burn, but it laid its gold across Elio's blanket like a patient hand, and every object hummed with a pulsing stillness that felt like listening. [187]
Mara sat beside him and began hymning the mundane, turning the sound of rain on a window, steam from a teacup, and the scratch of a chair into a gentle song. [218]

He reached into his shirt pocket and found the vinyl shard, a dark chip from a record that had cracked during the night his first home flooded. [279]
He had carried it for forty years, believing that if he could ever place it against a speaker, the missing half of his life would begin to speak. [307]
Mara did not stop her hymning, because she understood that the shard was not meant to be repaired, but to reunify the people around Elio into one shared circle of attention. [338]
His daughter wiped the bedside table, his son folded a blanket, and his wife poured water, each action rising like a note in a simple chord. [364]
Elio's eyes opened, and the pulsing stillness in the room became so complete that the cloud window seemed to breathe with him. [386]

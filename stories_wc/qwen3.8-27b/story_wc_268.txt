Its shelves rose like ribs of a sleeping god, and dust moved in slow orbits around reading stones. [34]
In the deepest aisle sat Veyra, a diminutive titan whose shoulders held the weight of old crowns with regally unpretentious grace. [55]

Veyra poured water from a clay jug, and the dim light made her look both small and unshaken. [367]

<story>I was buried with full honors before anyone admitted the revolution had failed. [13] They carved my name on the basalt memorial on Carrion Hollow and then left me to haunt it. [31] This was long after time becomes visible, when we could all see it drifting over the baroque asteroid like pale gold dust in chapel light. [56] Our homes were not mines or domes but gilded naves and curling stone stairways that spiraled outward from the old impact core. [78] Mara Voss had been my lieutenant, poetically inclined even when she primed demolition charges, and she found me first. [97] She said the Council had reopened the vaults under Saint Perpetua and the archaeological dispute had turned ugly. [115] Half our history depended on whether the first settlers arrived as pilgrims or as prisoners. [130] I laughed, and the sound rustled in my dead throat like paper. [142] I am a defunct revolutionary, I told her, not a scholar. [153] She laid a crystal vase shard on my chest, cool as a tooth, veined with blue fire. [170] It sang faintly when the visible currents of time passed through it. [182] Where did you get this, I asked, though I already knew the curve of that glaze. [198] From the Perpetua trench, she said, where the time dust settles thickest and the excavators argue with trowels in their fists. [219] The Traditionalists claim the shard is pilgrim work, proof of a sacred landing sanctioned by Earth. [235] The Reclamationists swear its clay holds prison grit, proof we were dumped here to die ornamental and obedient. [253] Whoever owns the shard owns the story of our baroque asteroid. [264] I sat up among the wreaths and felt my old ambition flicker, to reimagine an entire civilization from its broken dishes. [285] Mara wanted me to devise a test that would settle the dispute without blood. [299] The last debate over origins ended with a chapel burned and three archivists spaced. [313] I followed her through the colonnades while time slid past us in slow amber braids. [328] Children practiced covering mirrors during storms, draping black velvet over every reflective pane with practiced reverence. [344] We all learned that lesson after the first time storm shattered the Hall of Echoes. [359] Uncovered mirrors catch visible time and throw it back doubled, and men go blind staring at their younger faces. [378] The storm bells were ringing as we reached the vault, low and burnished like sorrow struck on bronze. [396] Mara drew the velvet across the observation mirrors while I examined the shard under lamplight. [411] The glaze held fingerprints, a maker mark half polished away, and a hairline of gold where some loving hand had mended it. [433] This is neither pilgrim porcelain nor prison ware, I whispered, it is both. [446] A prisoner who learned to paint like a pilgrim, or a pilgrim who learned to survive like a prisoner. [465] Mara frowned, for she wanted a clean verdict to carry back to the shouting scholars. [480] History does not give clean verdicts, I said, only vessels that held water for whoever was thirsty. [497] Outside the storm thickened, and time ran visibly down the walls in rivers of ash and honey. [514] I could see my own youth moving in it, shouting on barricades strung between angels of gilded stone. [532] Mara watched me and understood that my failure still ached with a burnished sorrow no pardon could polish away. [551] Devise something better than proof, she pleaded, devise a story we can live with. [565] So I took the shard and set it in the center of the table between the covered mirrors and the arguing doctors. [587] Let both sides be right, I said, let us be the children of prayer and of punishment, and let that double birth ennoble us. [611] No single origin can contain a people who carved chapels from a falling star. [625] The scholars jeered at first, then fell silent, because the shard kept singing through the storm. [641] When the bells stopped, we uncovered the mirrors together, and time looked a little less cruel in our faces. [660] I returned to my memorial before dawn and lay down, a revolutionary at last relieved of being right. [678]</story>

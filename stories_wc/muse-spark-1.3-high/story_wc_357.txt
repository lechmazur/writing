[62] She had one reason left, to find the source of resilience the old growers whispered about. [98] No map marked it, and compasses spun among magnetized stones, so Mara trusted navigating by water flow. [115] She followed irrigation runoff as it threaded leaf to leaf, branch to root, downhill toward some hidden heart. [154] The elders claimed the tip was fired in the first war over water, and where it landed the first orchard grew defiant. [701] The hour ended with a soft jolt as gravity righted itself, and water fell, and stones settled with a sigh. [721] Her skiff dropped beneath her and she landed hard on its wing, laughing as petals rained down in normal noise. [741] New green was already racing along the failing rows, buds swelling where black had been minutes before. [758] She flew home slowly through the arches, carrying no sample and no secret except the song now stuck in her teeth.

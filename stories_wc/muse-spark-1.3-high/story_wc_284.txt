<story>Iris Kade had been a burned spy for eleven months, and exile fit her like a borrowed coat. [18] At the tilt of the earth toward spring, she rented the keeper's cottage on zephyr bluff in mist, where the sea muttered below and the wind never quite settled. [47] She told the village she was fixing what's broken in maps, which was almost true, because she spent her days redrawing coastlines from memory and burning the drafts. [75] The truth was narrower and colder: she had to find where unused courage waits when a life has been used up by others. [98] Each morning brought a silver hush over the headland, light the color of spoons, and gulls crying as if through wool. [119] Iris walked the cliff path prudently daring, testing ice with her stick before trusting weight, yet always pushing farther than comfort advised. [141] In her coat pocket she kept an engraved pillbox left by her handler, its lid etched with a swallow over waves and her old cover name beneath. [168] Inside were not pills but three microfilm dots, too dangerous to carry and too guilty to destroy. [185] She had been exposed in Vienna, traded by a double, and her network had folded like a bad hand. [204] Two friends were dead, one was in prison, and Iris woke each night hearing doors close. [220] Her new contact, a young ferry clerk named Tomas, left no notes and made no calls. [236] Instead they used codes in accumulated snow, lines and dots brushed with a pine branch along the bluff fence, melted by noon and meaningless to walkers. [262] A short cross meant safe, a circle meant wait, three verticals meant run to the harbor. [278] Iris answered with her stick, adding a careful slash to vouchsafe receipt, a promise she was still listening. [296] It was tedious work for a woman who had once copied ciphers from memory in embassy bathrooms. [313] But tedium was the point, penance measured in cold fingers and slow trust. [326] Tomas wanted the microfilm to bargain for his sister, held in a detention center south of the border. [344] Iris wanted it gone, sunk or burned, because every copy multiplied risk. [356] Their needs rubbed like flint, and on gray afternoons they argued in low voices by the boathouse, mending a torn net just to have something for their hands. [384] You think caution will fix the dead, Tomas said, and Iris heard the age in his anger, twenty and already tired. [405] I think rushing will add to them, she answered, showing him how to knot where the net had frayed. [424] That evening the snow code changed, three verticals trembling on the fence rail, and below them a fresh circle she had not made. [447] Someone else was reading their weather, someone who knew the old language. [459] Iris felt the old burn crawl up her neck, shame and alertness braided together. [473] She did not run, though every trained nerve urged flight toward the ferry lights. [487] Instead she pocketed the pillbox and walked into the mist with Tomas, taking the lower path where the zephyr lifted spray over the stones. [511] We finish this together or not at all, she said, and was surprised by the steadiness of her voice. [530] At the boathouse a man waited, her former Vienna counterpart, coat damp, hands empty and open. [546] He offered papers for Tomas's sister and amnesty for Iris, in exchange for the dots, and his eyes were tired rather than cruel. [569] It could have been a trap, and prudence said to test the papers, delay, verify through cutouts. [586] But daring said the window would close with the thaw, and Tomas was watching her as if she were a compass. [607] Iris opened the engraved pillbox, tipped the dots into her palm, and held them toward the lamp so all three could see how small betrayal looked. [633] Then she crushed one under her thumbnail, a deliberate crack, to prove some things would not be traded whole. [652] Two for the girl, one destroyed for the dead, she said, and the man nodded slowly, accepting the altered price. [672] Tomas breathed for the first time in minutes, and the silver hush settled again, softer now, as mist thinned over the water. [694] Later, walking back alone, Iris understood she had not recovered her old courage but found where unused courage waits, in the choice to mend rather than to win. [722] Fixing what's broken, she thought, was not restoring the past but giving the future a steadier seam. [739]</story>

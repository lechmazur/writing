[107] Two years ago she had falsified a summit report after her brother Tomas fell on the north ridge. [252] At dawn the sky outside held faint green bands, though it was too far south for true northern lights. [271] Jonas called those false dawns his swaying certainties, forecasts that leaned but never quite fell.

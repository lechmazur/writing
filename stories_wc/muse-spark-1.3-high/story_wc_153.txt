<story>Mara had carried her unsent letters for three years in a waterproof satchel. [13] Each envelope held an apology she could not speak aloud to her drowned brother. [82] The trail opened onto a stilt hut wrapped in fogged glass windows. [94] Inside waited the cryptic medium everyone in the delta both feared and trusted. [107] The woman was old, with barnacle scars on her hands and moonlight caught in her hair. [123] You brought the shard, the medium said without looking up from her tea. [189] Since then it cut her palm whenever guilt grew sharp enough to bleed. [202] The medium touched the wound gently, with fierce tenderness that startled Mara. [214] Child, she whispered, some things only heal if you stop hiding them. [226] Mara said she heals in moonlight but never fully, never past midnight. [238] We do not speak here, she said, we listen to what the air writes. [252] As night fell, messages in condensation on windows began to form slowly. [592] I am sorry I did not follow you, the window wrote in her own hand. [607] I am sorry I loved you loudly only after silence took you, it continued.

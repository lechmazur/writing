[161] Mara stayed behind to discover meaning in the mess, cataloging each broken fin and shattered console in a salvaged ledger. [588] Through the cracked roof the first true stars pricked through, and she raised the little glasses to greet them. [607] Far across the flats other tired fighters saw the amber glow and turned toward it, wondering who had kept watch. [627] It was not a signal to attack, but a porch light left on after a long storm, promising soup and sleep. [648] Mara logged the night in her book with steady hands, noting voltage, tone, and the strange peace in her chest.

<story>The desert took the road and kept it. [8] Mara meant to reach Copper Flats by dusk but a wrong turn leads to a hidden oasis. [98] He was the silent carousel carpenter and his hands knew every joint. [110] He did not speak when Mara entered but pointed to a bench. [122] She sat because the quiet felt like an invitation rather than a wall. [135] His goal was to interpret silence as possibility and not as absence. [147] For three years he had worked without words after the circus folded. [159] He was quietly persistent and each morning he oiled hinges and carved feathers. [490] The carpenter gave Mara the sketch and bowed low like a ringmaster. [502] She left the oasis at dawn with sand in her boots and light in her throat. [518] Copper Flats could wait another day for her coins and news. [529] She had learned how quiet hands keep working until song finds them. [541] Years later travelers spoke of music near the black water at dusk. [553] They said a train glowed softly though no engine burned. [563] They said a silent man kept horses dancing without moving from his bench. [576] Mara never claimed the tale but each winter she returned with oil and strings.

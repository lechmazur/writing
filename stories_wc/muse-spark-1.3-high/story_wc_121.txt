<story>Mara waited on the glowing viaduct after rain for the lights to steady. [13] She was known in the lower districts as a gravity defier who never touched stairs. [57] She carried a satchel with an ancient clay tablet with runes she had stolen from the archive. [74] The archive priests said the tablet mapped fractured timelines that branched beneath the city. [88] Mara did not steal it for profit but to conquer the labyrinth of self that haunted her sleep. [106] Every night she dreamed of falling upward into versions of herself she had abandoned. [120] The tablet grew warm against her hip as the first drop fell from the arch and hung. [137] Time slowed and stretched thin between the drop and the ripple where it should have landed. [153] In that pause the runes ignited and projected a stairwell that was not there before. [168] The stairwell folded downward and upward at once, humming with a periodically eternal pulse. [182] Mara understood she would have to retrace every choice she had ever refused to make. [197] She stepped off the rail and let her body rise instead of fall into the light. [257] The child stared at her hovering boots with envy and fear mixed together. [270] Mara reached down and lifted the girl until both floated above the imagined water. [284] The arch dissolved into mist as the girl laughed and vanished into Mara. [297] In the second arch she faced herself at nineteen signing away her flight to the military. [313] The pen shook in that younger hand and the officer watched without blinking. [326] Mara whispered that obedience would clip her sky and begged her to refuse. [365] Her mother stood there younger than Mara remembered, kneading dough with tired hands. [378] Mara had left without goodbye and the guilt had anchored her flights for years. [420] The kitchen folded and the tablet burned hotter, showing deeper fractures in the timelines. [434] Mara saw a thousand selves branching, some cruel and some broken and some luminous. [476] You defy gravity because you fear weight, the reflection said with quiet contempt. [489] Mara wanted to argue but knew the labyrinth ended only with honest surrender. [502] She let her boots touch the floor for the first time since childhood and felt the shock. [519] Weight flooded her ankles and knees and spine, terrible and strangely comforting. [531] The door opened onto the viaduct again where the suspended drop finally trembled. [544] The darkening brightness had deepened to violet and gold as evening settled fully. [557] Mara stood grounded as rain began again, no longer floating above her life. [570] The drop fell and struck the puddle, sending a ripple outward through fractured timelines. [584] She felt all her other selves settle inside her like stones finding a riverbed. [598] The tablet cooled and its runes faded to ordinary scratches in drying clay. [611] Mara smiled and walked home heavy and light at once beneath the glowing arches. [625]</story>
